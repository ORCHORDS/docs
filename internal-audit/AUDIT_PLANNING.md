# Audit Planning

## Purpose

Define risk-based planning for individual audits and the broader internal audit program.

## Engagement plan

An audit plan SHOULD identify:

- objective;
- scope and period;
- applicable criteria/policies/obligations;
- key risks and controls;
- resources and competence needed;
- evidence sources and sampling approach;
- stakeholders;
- target reporting date.

## Risk-based depth

Audit effort should prioritize material uncertainty and risk, not simply equal coverage of every document or control.

## Changes

Material scope changes during fieldwork should be documented with rationale and impact on conclusions.
