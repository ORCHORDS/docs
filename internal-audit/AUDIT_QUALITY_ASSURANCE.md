# Internal Audit Quality Assurance

## Purpose

Evaluate whether internal audit work remains objective, evidence-based, useful, and consistent with the audit program.

## Quality checks

The audit function SHOULD review planning quality, evidence sufficiency, sampling limitations, finding clarity, linkage between criteria and conclusions, timeliness, action follow-up, independence concerns, and stakeholder feedback.

Quality review must not pressure auditors to weaken supported findings merely to improve satisfaction scores or closure rates.

Improvement actions should be tracked like other material governance actions.
