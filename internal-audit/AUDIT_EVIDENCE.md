# Audit Evidence

## Purpose

Define evidence expectations for defensible audit observations and conclusions.

## Qualities

Audit evidence SHOULD be sufficient, relevant, reliable, attributable, and traceable to the tested objective and period.

Evidence may include inspection, observation, inquiry corroborated by other records, reperformance, data analysis, configuration review, sampling, or technical test results.

## Sampling

Sampling methods and limitations should be documented. A small sample does not establish population-wide effectiveness without a justified basis.

## Sensitive evidence

Audit workpapers often contain sensitive operational, legal, personal, or security information and generally do not belong in this public repository.

NIST SP 800-53A Rev.5 may inform security/privacy control-assessment methods, but it is not represented here as a general internal audit standard.
