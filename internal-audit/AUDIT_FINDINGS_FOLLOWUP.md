# Audit Findings and Follow-up

## Purpose

Ensure internal audit findings result in accountable decisions and verified closure.

## Finding record

A material finding SHOULD identify criteria, observed condition, risk/impact, evidence, management response, action owner, target date, and closure requirement.

## Management response

Management may agree, partially agree, or formally accept the risk. Disagreement should include rationale and accountable approval rather than deleting the finding.

## Follow-up

Internal audit should verify material remediation using evidence appropriate to the finding. Completion statements without evidence are insufficient for high-impact issues.

## Escalation

Overdue significant findings or repeated ineffective remediation require escalation to appropriate governance authority.
