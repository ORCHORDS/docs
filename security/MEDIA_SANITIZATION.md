# Media Sanitization and Secure Disposal

## Purpose

Ensure information is rendered inaccessible at the end of its authorized storage lifecycle.

## Requirements

Sanitization method MUST be appropriate to media type, information sensitivity, reuse or disposal destination, and assurance needed.

Processes should distinguish logical deletion from verified sanitization. Reuse, repair, return, resale, and disposal may require different methods and evidence.

## Verification

Material sanitization activities SHOULD produce evidence sufficient to show media identity or scope, method, date, responsible party, and validation outcome without retaining sensitive contents.

## Guidance

Program design is informed by NIST SP 800-88 Rev.2.
