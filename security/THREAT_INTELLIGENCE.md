# Threat Intelligence

## Purpose

Use external and internal threat information to improve risk decisions without treating every alert as equally relevant.

## Sources

Threat information MAY include incident findings, vulnerability exploitation data, supplier advisories, trusted communities, public research, indicators, techniques, and authoritative catalogs such as CISA KEV.

## Analysis

Intelligence should be assessed for source reliability, timeliness, relevance to company assets, exposure, confidence, and required action.

## Sharing

Threat information sharing must protect confidential, personal, contractual, and investigation-sensitive information.

## Action

Relevant intelligence may trigger vulnerability reprioritization, monitoring changes, threat-model updates, control testing, supplier review, or incident investigation.
