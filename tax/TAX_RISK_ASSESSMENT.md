# Tax Risk Assessment

Tax risk assessments should identify material obligations, jurisdictions, transactions, data dependencies, filing complexity, interpretation uncertainty, control gaps, and potential financial or reputational exposure.

Material risks require owners, treatment, evidence, and review triggers.
