# Tax Governance Policy

ORCHORDS manages tax obligations through accountable ownership, timely compliance, documented judgment, segregation of duties, evidence retention, change monitoring, and escalation of material uncertainty.

Tax decisions should align with applicable law, commercial substance, financial reporting, and documented company governance.
