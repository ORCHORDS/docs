# Public Communications Governance

## Purpose

Ensure public statements are accurate, authorized, appropriately reviewed, and consistent with security, privacy, legal, and evidence requirements.

## Requirements

Material external communications SHOULD have a clear owner, intended audience, factual source, review appropriate to risk, and publication record.

Unverified assumptions, internal speculation, security-sensitive details, personal information, confidential partner/customer information, and unannounced commitments must not be published as fact.

## Corrections

Material inaccuracies should be corrected transparently when a silent edit would mislead the audience.

## High-risk statements

Security/compliance claims, incident statements, financial claims, legal positions, and commitments about future capability require stronger review.

See [Public Statement Review SOP](../sop/PUBLIC_STATEMENT_REVIEW_SOP.md).
