# Public Disclosure Boundary

## Purpose

Define categories of information that require deliberate approval before public release.

## Restricted public disclosure

Examples include credentials, recovery methods, private endpoints, network/facility layouts, customer or personnel data, internal identifiers, security bypasses, unresolved exploit details, non-public contracts, banking details, privileged legal material, and unannounced commercial plans.

## Approval

When uncertain, classify the proposed disclosure and consult the relevant owner before publication.

## Principle

Transparency should improve trust and accountability without materially reducing security, privacy, legal protection, or contractual confidentiality.
