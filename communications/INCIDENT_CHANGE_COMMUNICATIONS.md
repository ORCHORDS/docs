# Incident and Change Communications

## Purpose

Set principles for user-facing communication about outages, incidents, significant changes, maintenance, or deprecations.

## Principles

Communications SHOULD explain what users need to know, what action is required, important timing, known limitations, and when another update is expected when events are evolving.

Do not expose attacker-useful investigative details, private topology, credentials, customer-specific information, or unsupported root-cause conclusions.

## Consistency

Public incident/change statements should align with [Crisis Communications](../resilience/CRISIS_COMMUNICATIONS.md), [Deprecation and Sunset Policy](../product/DEPRECATION_SUNSET_POLICY.md), and approved incident records.
