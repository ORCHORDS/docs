# Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

Establish a consistent company-wide method to identify, assess, treat, accept,
monitor, and close material risk.

## Scope

This policy applies to security, privacy, operational, supplier, data, AI,
engineering, legal/compliance, continuity, and material business risks.

## Principles

- Risk is expressed as uncertainty that can affect objectives.
- Risk assessments consider likelihood, impact, exposure, detectability,
  reversibility, dependencies, and available mitigations.
- Severity labels support decisions but never replace judgment.
- Risks have a named owner and review date.
- Accepted risk is explicit, time-bounded where appropriate, and supported by
  rationale.
- Material changes in context trigger reassessment.

## Treatment options

| Treatment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Avoid | Stop or redesign the activity that creates the risk |
| Reduce | Add controls that lower likelihood or impact |
| Transfer/share | Allocate defined portions through contract, insurance or other arrangements |
| Accept | Approve the residual risk with named accountability |

## Minimum risk record

A material risk record includes condition, affected objectives, plausible
impact, existing controls, treatment decision, owner, due/review date, residual
risk, dependencies, and closure evidence.

## Escalation

Risks with potentially severe safety, security, privacy, legal, financial, or
business-continuity impact are escalated to the appropriate accountable leader.
Risk acceptance cannot be delegated below the level that owns the affected
objective.
