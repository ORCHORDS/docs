# Records Retention and Deletion

## Purpose

Define defensible retention, preservation, and deletion principles without
publishing internal retention schedules or sensitive storage details.

## Principles

- Retain records only for a defined business, legal, security, contractual, or
  evidentiary purpose.
- Minimize duplicate records and unnecessary copies.
- Apply retention rules consistently to authoritative records and governed
  backups where feasible.
- Suspend routine deletion when a valid legal hold, investigation, incident, or
  preservation requirement applies.
- Delete or irreversibly de-identify information when retention is no longer
  justified and no hold applies.
- Disposal methods must match the sensitivity of the information and the
  capability of the storage medium.

## Record ownership

Each record class has an accountable owner responsible for purpose, retention
basis, access expectations, and disposal criteria.

## Evidence

Retention decisions should be traceable to an approved schedule or documented
basis. Material deletion processes should produce evidence sufficient to
demonstrate completion without exposing sensitive record contents.
