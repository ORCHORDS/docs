# Decision Escalation Timeliness

Material decisions must not remain blocked indefinitely.

Escalation expectations are based on business impact, reversibility, urgency, uncertainty, and risk exposure. Repeated delay is itself a governance signal and must be recorded, assigned, and reviewed.
