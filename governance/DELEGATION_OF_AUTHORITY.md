# Delegation of Authority

## Purpose

Define how decision authority is delegated without obscuring accountability.

## Principles

Delegated authority MUST have a clear scope, accountable delegator, effective period, and conditions or limits appropriate to risk.

Delegation does not transfer ultimate accountability for policy ownership or risk acceptance unless governance explicitly reassigns that accountability.

## High-impact decisions

Security-risk acceptance, material public assurance claims, emergency authority, major release decisions, and irreversible data actions should have clearly defined approval authority.

## Review

Delegations should be reviewed when roles change, the business context changes, or repeated escalation indicates the delegated scope is unclear or inappropriate.
