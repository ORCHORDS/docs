# Risk Treatment Verification

Closing a risk treatment requires evidence that the planned action occurred and materially changed the stated risk condition.

Residual risk must be reassessed after treatment. Unsupported closure, expired evidence, or treatment that only shifts risk elsewhere must be challenged and reopened where appropriate.
