# Control Assurance and Metrics

## Purpose

Define how ORCHORDS evaluates whether important controls are not only required
by policy but are operating effectively.

## Assurance model

Control assurance distinguishes:

1. **Design evidence** — the control is defined and appropriate to the risk.
2. **Implementation evidence** — the control exists in the relevant scope.
3. **Operating evidence** — the control performed during the review period.
4. **Effectiveness evidence** — results indicate the control reduces the
   intended risk.

A policy statement alone is not implementation evidence.

## Metrics

Prefer metrics that support decisions, such as:

- overdue control reviews;
- exception age and recurrence;
- incident recurrence and time to containment;
- restoration-test success;
- access-review completion and revocation timeliness;
- vulnerability aging by exposure and severity;
- supplier-review coverage;
- failed-change and rollback trends;
- documentation review freshness.

Avoid vanity metrics that reward volume without measuring outcomes.

## Findings

Control gaps are recorded with severity, owner, corrective action, target date,
and closure evidence. Repeated findings trigger root-cause review.

## Public claims

External claims about assurance, certification, testing, or control
implementation require current evidence and approval under
[Document Control](./DOCUMENT_CONTROL.md).
