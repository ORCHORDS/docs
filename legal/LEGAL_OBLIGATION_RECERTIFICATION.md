# Legal Obligation Recertification

## Purpose
Keep material legal and contractual obligation records current and accountable.

## Requirements
- Material obligations must have a source, owner, status, and review cadence.
- Periodic recertification must confirm applicability and current status.
- Superseded or completed obligations must be closed with evidence.
- Unclear, disputed, or overdue obligations must be escalated for review.

## Evidence
Maintain dated recertification results, ownership changes, status decisions, and supporting records. This policy does not provide jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
