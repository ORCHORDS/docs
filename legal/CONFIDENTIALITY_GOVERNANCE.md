# Confidentiality Governance

## Purpose

Define company-wide expectations for receiving, using, sharing, and protecting confidential information.

## Requirements

Confidential information SHOULD be used only for authorized purposes, shared on a need-to-know basis, protected according to sensitivity, and retained only as long as required by business, legal, contractual, or evidentiary needs.

Confidentiality obligations apply regardless of whether information is received verbally, electronically, in writing, or through system access.

When disclosure is required, disclose only the minimum necessary and preserve applicable approvals, notices, or legal restrictions.
