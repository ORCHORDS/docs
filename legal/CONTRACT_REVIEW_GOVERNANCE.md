# Contract Review Governance

## Purpose

Ensure material contractual commitments are understood before approval and remain traceable to responsible owners.

## Review areas

Depending on scope, review SHOULD consider commercial obligations, security/privacy terms, data rights, intellectual property, service levels, liability, confidentiality, termination, audit/assurance rights, jurisdiction, subcontracting, incident notification, and record-retention requirements.

## Authority

Only authorized roles may bind the company to material commitments. Informal messages, vendor portals, click-through terms, or technical integrations can create obligations and must not bypass review when impact is material.

## Lifecycle

Contract owners should track important renewal, termination, notice, and obligation dates.
