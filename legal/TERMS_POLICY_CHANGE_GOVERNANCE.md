# Terms and Policy Change Governance

## Purpose

Control material changes to customer-facing or public legal terms and policies.

## Requirements

Material changes SHOULD identify owner, rationale, affected audience, legal or contractual implications, effective date, required notice, migration or grandfathering considerations, and authoritative published version.

Superseded terms should remain identifiable when needed to determine which version applied at a given time.

Product or operational behavior that depends on a policy change should not be assumed complete merely because text was published.
