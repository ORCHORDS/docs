# Legal Request Handling

## Purpose

Ensure subpoenas, court orders, regulatory demands, preservation requests, law-enforcement requests, and similar formal demands are handled consistently and lawfully.

## Requirements

Material legal requests MUST be routed to an authorized legal/governance owner rather than answered ad hoc by individual personnel.

The review SHOULD record source, authority, scope, deadline, jurisdiction, authenticity checks, data or records affected, confidentiality restrictions, preservation needs, and response approval.

## Data minimization

Respond only with information legally required and approved for release. Protect unrelated personal, confidential, privileged, or security-sensitive information.

## Records

Preserve the request, legal assessment, approved response, delivery evidence, and relevant deadlines in an authorized record system.

See [Legal Request SOP](../sop/LEGAL_REQUEST_SOP.md).
