# Legal Hold Release Verification

## Purpose
Ensure legal holds are released deliberately and affected records return to normal lifecycle rules only after authorized review.

## Requirements
- Hold releases require documented authority and scope.
- Affected custodians or record owners must receive clear release instructions where applicable.
- Release completion must be verified before normal disposition resumes.
- Conflicting obligations must be resolved before release is considered complete.

## Evidence
Retain release authorization, scope, notification, verification, and exception records.
