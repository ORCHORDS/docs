# Preservation and Discovery Governance

## Purpose

Preserve potentially relevant records when legal, regulatory, contractual, or investigative duties require it.

## Requirements

Material preservation actions SHOULD define scope, custodians or systems at an appropriate level, effective time, suspension of conflicting deletion where authorized, access restrictions, and release criteria.

Preservation should be proportionate and documented; it must not become an indefinite reason to retain unrelated information.

Detailed matter-specific instructions belong in restricted systems rather than this public repository.
