# Regulatory and Legal Request Escalation

## Purpose

Ensure formal legal, regulatory, investigative, or enforcement requests are recognized and routed promptly to accountable review.

## Requirements

Potentially binding requests SHOULD be preserved in their original form, timestamped, assigned an owner, and escalated without unauthorized substantive response.

Personnel should not improvise commitments, admissions, disclosure, deletion, or deadline changes outside approved authority.

Urgent or unclear requests should be treated conservatively until scope and obligations are understood.
