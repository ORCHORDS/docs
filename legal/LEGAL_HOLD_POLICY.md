# Legal Hold Policy

## Purpose

Suspend ordinary deletion when records may be required for litigation, investigation, regulatory inquiry, or another authorized preservation purpose.

## Requirements

A legal hold MUST identify authorized issuer, scope, record categories or custodians where relevant, effective date, preservation instructions, and release authority.

Normal retention and deletion rules are suspended only for the affected scope. Overbroad preservation should be avoided when a narrower defensible scope is available.

## Confidentiality

Hold details may be legally sensitive and must be restricted to personnel who need them.

## Release

Deletion or ordinary retention resumes only after authorized release and reconciliation of any affected records.
