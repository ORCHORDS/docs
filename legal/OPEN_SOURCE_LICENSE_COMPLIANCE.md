# Open-Source License Compliance

## Purpose

Ensure use and distribution of open-source software respects applicable license obligations.

## Requirements

Material open-source use SHOULD preserve license and notice information, identify obligations relevant to distribution or modification, and avoid incompatible licensing commitments.

Security or maintenance approval does not automatically establish license compliance.

## Distribution

Before distributing software that includes third-party components, confirm applicable attribution, source-offer, notice, modification, linking, patent, or redistribution obligations as relevant.

## Records

Dependency manifests, SBOMs, notices, and review records may support compliance evidence.

See [Open-Source Dependency Policy](../engineering/OPEN_SOURCE_DEPENDENCY_POLICY.md).
