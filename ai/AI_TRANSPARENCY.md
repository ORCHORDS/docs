# AI Transparency

## Purpose

Define when users, reviewers, or affected people should be informed about material AI involvement and limitations.

## Principles

Transparency should be proportionate to impact and should help a person understand how to use, question, or escalate an AI-assisted outcome.

Useful disclosures may include that AI is used, its role, important limitations, whether human review occurs, how to report harmful behavior, and when outputs should not be relied upon.

## Limits

Transparency does not require exposing private security controls, proprietary prompts, confidential model details, or information that would materially enable abuse.

## Claims

Avoid implying that an AI system is objective, unbiased, deterministic, or error-free without evidence supporting the scoped statement.
