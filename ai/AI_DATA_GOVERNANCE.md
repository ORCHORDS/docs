# AI Data Governance

## Purpose

Apply data and privacy governance to information used to train, fine-tune,
evaluate, retrieve for, or otherwise support AI systems.

## Requirements

AI-related data use SHOULD establish:

- permitted purpose;
- source and provenance where relevant;
- authorization to use the data;
- classification and sensitivity;
- personal-data considerations;
- quality and representativeness limitations;
- retention and deletion expectations;
- controls against unintended model or provider retention where relevant.

## Sensitive information

Restricted information must not be sent to an AI service unless the use is
authorized and appropriate contractual, privacy, and security controls exist.

## Evaluation data

Test and benchmark data should be protected from contamination where leakage
would invalidate evaluation quality.
