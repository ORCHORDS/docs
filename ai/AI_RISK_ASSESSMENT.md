# AI Risk Assessment

## Purpose

Define the minimum questions used to classify and govern material AI use.

## Assessment dimensions

Evaluate:

- intended purpose and users;
- level of autonomy and decision influence;
- safety, financial, legal, privacy, security, or rights impact;
- sensitivity and provenance of input/output data;
- model and supplier dependencies;
- expected failure modes, misuse, manipulation, and abuse;
- accuracy, robustness, bias, explainability, and uncertainty requirements;
- human oversight and ability to override;
- monitoring, rollback, fallback, and retirement;
- material changes after approval.

## Risk tiers

Organizations may use low, moderate, high, or prohibited tiers. The chosen tier
must drive review depth, evidence, approval, and monitoring rather than acting
as a label only.

## Evidence

Higher-risk use requires documented evaluation criteria, test results,
limitations, owner approval, monitoring plan, and defined conditions for
suspension or reassessment.
