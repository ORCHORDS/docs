# AI Version Traceability

## Purpose

Keep material AI behavior attributable to the model, provider, prompt/configuration, retrieval context, policy, and evaluation state in use at the time.

## Requirements

Material AI capabilities SHOULD record the relevant model or service version, configuration version, prompt or policy version, evaluation reference, and effective deployment period at an appropriate level.

Provider-side changes that are not directly versioned should still have detectable review triggers where practical.
