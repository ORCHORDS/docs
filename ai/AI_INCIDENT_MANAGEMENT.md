# AI Incident Management

## Purpose

Define how harmful, unsafe, insecure, privacy-impacting, or materially
misleading AI behavior is handled.

## Triggers

An AI incident may include:

- harmful automated action;
- sensitive information disclosure;
- security-control bypass;
- material systematic error;
- unsafe or discriminatory outcome;
- loss of expected human oversight;
- unexpected model or provider behavior that changes risk.

## Response

Use the general incident process while adding AI-specific analysis of model or
provider version, prompts or instructions, retrieval context, input data,
output, automated downstream action, and human review.

## Containment

Containment may include disabling a feature, narrowing permissions, reverting a
model or configuration, adding mandatory human confirmation, or stopping
affected data flows.

## Learning

Post-incident review should determine whether evaluation, monitoring, policy,
or human-oversight controls need revision.
