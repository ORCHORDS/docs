# AI Change Management

## Purpose

Govern AI behavior changes that can occur through model, prompt, retrieval, policy, data, tool, or provider updates.

## Material changes

A change is material when it can alter risk, user impact, permissions, data exposure, evaluation validity, or regulatory/contractual assumptions.

## Requirements

Material AI changes SHOULD define owner, reason, expected behavior, evaluation evidence, privacy/security impact, rollout controls, monitoring, rollback or disable path, and reassessment triggers.

Externally managed models may change without source-code changes; monitoring and provider-change review must account for that possibility.
