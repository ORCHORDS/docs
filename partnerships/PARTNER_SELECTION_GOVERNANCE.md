# Partner Selection Governance

Partner selection considers strategic fit, complementary capability, values, reputation, operational readiness, customer impact, conflicts, security/privacy posture, and credible mutual benefit.

Selection evidence should explain why the relationship is preferable to non-partnership alternatives.
