# Security and Privacy Due Diligence

Security and privacy diligence should assess governance maturity, material incidents, unresolved vulnerabilities or audit findings, identity and access practices, data governance, third-party exposure, privacy obligations, resilience, and credible remediation capability.

Public transaction records must not expose sensitive architecture, credentials, customer data, or exploitable findings.
