# Legal and Compliance Due Diligence

Legal and compliance diligence should identify material obligations, disputes, investigations, permits, contractual restrictions, governance issues, policy gaps, regulatory exposure, and remediation commitments relevant to the transaction.

Open interpretation issues should be documented rather than silently resolved by assumption.
