# Transaction Approval Authority

Transaction authority must follow documented delegation and segregation-of-duties principles.

Material commitments require approval at the appropriate level before binding action. Approval records should identify scope, conditions, exceptions, key assumptions, unresolved risks, and any requirement for subsequent approval.
