# Intellectual Property Due Diligence

Intellectual-property diligence should evaluate ownership, assignment, licensing, restrictions, open-source obligations, disputes, material dependencies, and the relationship between claimed value and defensible rights.

Unverified ownership or material licensing uncertainty must be reflected in the transaction decision.
