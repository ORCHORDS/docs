# Strategic Fit Assessment

Transaction proposals should explain which strategic objectives they advance, what capabilities or positions they change, and which assumptions must hold for the rationale to remain valid.

Assessments should compare the transaction with internal build, partnership, organic growth, postponement, and no-action alternatives where relevant.
