# Negotiation and Term Sheet Governance

Negotiation authority must be defined before material commitments are communicated.

Term sheets and similar instruments should identify binding and non-binding provisions, approval conditions, confidentiality, exclusivity where applicable, diligence dependencies, key economic assumptions, and unresolved issues requiring further authorization.
