# HSR Premerger Notification Governance — 2026 Status

Source: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program/hsr-notification-forms-instructions-guidance

## Current-source note

The FTC page updated March 23, 2026 states that a federal district court vacated the updated HSR form on February 12, 2026 and that, after an appellate stay was denied on March 19, 2026, the agencies are accepting the form and instructions that were in place before the February 10, 2025 rule took effect. The FTC also states that updated-form submissions may be accepted voluntarily.

## Governance

- Never rely on an old internal HSR checklist without checking the current FTC/DOJ filing instructions.
- Obtain transaction-specific legal advice on whether a filing is required and which form/instructions apply.
- Record the source version/date used for the filing decision.
- Keep deal-specific thresholds, counterparties, valuations, filing drafts, and legal advice out of this public repository.

## Review cadence

Because the 2026 form status is affected by litigation and possible further rulemaking, re-check the official FTC page before any transaction decision that depends on the filing form or procedure.
