# Financial Due Diligence

Financial diligence should evaluate earnings quality, cash generation, working capital, liabilities, commitments, accounting assumptions, concentration, forecast reliability, and material off-balance-sheet or contingent exposures where relevant.

Material adjustments and normalized measures must be documented and reconciled to source evidence.
