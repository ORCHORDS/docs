# Confidentiality and Information Barriers

Transaction information must be shared on a need-to-know basis and handled according to confidentiality, legal, records, and security requirements.

Access should be proportionate to role. Sensitive information must not be reused for unrelated purposes or disclosed publicly before authorized communication.
