# Separation and Divestiture Planning

Separations and divestitures require clear scope boundaries, asset and record identification, access transition, contractual dependency analysis, customer and supplier continuity, people impacts, data handling, security, legal obligations, transitional services, and completion evidence.

Residual obligations must have owners after separation.
