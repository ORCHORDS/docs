# Due Diligence Governance

Diligence scope should reflect transaction size, structure, complexity, known risk, integration intent, and decision needs.

Findings must distinguish verified facts, representations, estimates, unanswered questions, and assumptions. Material findings require ownership and an explicit effect on valuation, terms, treatment, integration planning, or the transaction decision.
