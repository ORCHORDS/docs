# Deal Team Governance

Each material transaction should have a defined sponsor, transaction lead, functional owners, decision forum, confidentiality expectations, issue-escalation path, and recordkeeping responsibility.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed and managed. Critical decisions should not depend on a single undocumented individual judgment.
