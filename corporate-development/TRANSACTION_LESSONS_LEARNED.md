# Transaction Lessons Learned

After material transactions, teams should assess decision quality, diligence effectiveness, valuation assumptions, negotiation outcomes, integration or separation execution, risk forecasting, governance effectiveness, and evidence quality.

Lessons should result in specific process or control improvements rather than retrospective narrative alone.
