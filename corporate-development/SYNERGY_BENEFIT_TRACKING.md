# Synergy and Benefit Tracking

Expected transaction benefits should have baselines, owners, timing assumptions, dependencies, costs to achieve, measurement rules, and evidence of realization.

Forecast synergies must remain distinguishable from achieved outcomes. Changes to definitions or baselines should be recorded to preserve comparability.
