# People and Culture Due Diligence

People diligence should assess organizational dependencies, leadership continuity, key-person risk, role duplication, employment obligations, incentive structures, skills, culture integration risks, and transition needs while respecting privacy and employment-law boundaries.

Sensitive personnel data must remain appropriately restricted.
