# Supplier and Dependency Due Diligence

Diligence should identify material supplier concentration, critical contractual dependencies, single points of failure, change-of-control effects, renewal exposure, subcontractor chains, exit constraints, and resilience assumptions.

Material dependency findings should feed integration and continuity plans.
