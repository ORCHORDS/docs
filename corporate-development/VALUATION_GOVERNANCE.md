# Valuation Governance

Valuation decisions must distinguish observed evidence, management assumptions, scenarios, sensitivities, expected synergies, and uncertainty.

Material valuation inputs should be independently challenged, version controlled, and reconciled to the transaction decision record. Valuation ranges must not be presented as certainty.
