# Post-Close Governance

Post-close governance should confirm transaction conditions, action ownership, integration or separation milestones, risk remediation, financial controls, reporting cadence, benefit tracking, unresolved diligence findings, and decision escalation.

Temporary governance arrangements should have clear exit criteria.
