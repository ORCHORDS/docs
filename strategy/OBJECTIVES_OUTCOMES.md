# Objectives and Outcomes

## Purpose

Connect strategic intent to measurable outcomes without creating incentives for misleading metrics.

## Rules

Objectives SHOULD describe the desired change or result, not merely an activity list. Measures should have owner, source, frequency, interpretation limits, and a clear relationship to the objective.

Avoid single metrics when they create obvious gaming or conceal important safety, quality, customer, financial, or risk trade-offs.

Targets are planning instruments and must not be presented as achieved results.
