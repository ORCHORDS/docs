# Strategy Governance Policy

## Purpose

Define how company-level strategic choices are framed, approved, reviewed, and changed.

## Requirements

Material strategy SHOULD identify intended outcomes, decision owner, planning horizon, major assumptions, dependencies, resource implications, principal risks, measurable indicators, and review triggers.

Strategic choices should distinguish evidence from belief and current facts from forecasts.

## Change

A significant assumption failure, material incident, regulatory change, market shift, or delivery constraint should trigger reassessment rather than automatic continuation.

See [Strategy Review SOP](../sop/STRATEGY_REVIEW_SOP.md).
