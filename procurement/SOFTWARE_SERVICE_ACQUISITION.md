# Software and Service Acquisition

## Purpose

Apply technical, security, privacy, data, AI, supplier, and operational review before introducing material third-party software or services.

## Assessment

Depending on risk, review SHOULD consider data access, privileges, integration scope, security posture, update/support model, licensing, AI/data-use behavior, resilience, portability, subcontractors, monitoring, and exit.

## Shadow acquisition

Personnel should not introduce unapproved high-risk services merely because procurement is slow or a free trial is available.

## Lifecycle

Approved acquisition must have an owner and renewal/termination path.

See [Software and Service Acquisition SOP](../sop/SOFTWARE_SERVICE_ACQUISITION_SOP.md).
