# Renewal and Termination Governance

## Purpose

Prevent automatic renewals or supplier termination from bypassing cost, risk, or continuity review.

## Renewal

Material renewals SHOULD review continued business need, actual usage/value, supplier performance, security/privacy posture, unresolved incidents, pricing/terms, concentration risk, and exit alternatives.

## Termination

Termination planning should address notice obligations, access revocation, data return/deletion, integration removal, financial reconciliation, replacement service, and continuity.

Use [Supplier Exit and Offboarding](../third-party/SUPPLIER_EXIT_OFFBOARDING.md) for risk-sensitive exits.
