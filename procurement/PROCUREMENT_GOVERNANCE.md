# Procurement Governance Policy

## Purpose

Ensure purchases and supplier commitments are authorized, necessary, risk-assessed, and lifecycle-managed.

## Requirements

Material procurement SHOULD identify business owner, need, alternatives considered where appropriate, budget/approval, supplier risk, security/privacy/data impact, contractual obligations, implementation owner, renewal model, and exit path.

## Independence

Personnel must disclose conflicts of interest and must not manipulate supplier selection for personal benefit.

## Evidence

Keep enough records to support approval, supplier selection, contract terms, payment authorization, and renewal/termination decisions.
