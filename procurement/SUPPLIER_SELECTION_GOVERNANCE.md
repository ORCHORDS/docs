# Supplier Selection Governance

## Purpose

Select suppliers using documented business, risk, quality, security, privacy, resilience, and commercial criteria rather than convenience alone.

## Requirements

Material selections SHOULD define need, evaluation criteria, decision owner, competing options where practical, significant risks, required due diligence, and reasons for the final choice.

Single-source decisions, unusual concentration, weak exit options, or material control gaps require explicit justification and risk acceptance.

Selection evidence should be sufficient to explain why the supplier was reasonable at the time of decision.
