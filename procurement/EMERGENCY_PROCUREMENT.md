# Emergency Procurement

## Purpose

Allow urgent acquisition during incidents or severe business disruption without abandoning minimum controls.

## Requirements

Emergency procurement MUST identify the emergency need, accountable approver, supplier, financial impact, data/access involved, minimum security/privacy constraints, duration, and follow-up review.

Temporary emergency purchases should be time-bounded where practical and must not silently become permanent high-risk services without ordinary review.

## Follow-up

After stabilization, reconcile contract terms, supplier due diligence, access, payment records, renewal settings, and termination plan.
