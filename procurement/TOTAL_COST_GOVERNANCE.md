# Total Cost of Ownership Governance

## Purpose

Evaluate material acquisitions using lifecycle cost rather than headline price alone.

## Requirements

Total-cost review SHOULD consider acquisition price, implementation, migration, training, support, usage growth, integration, security/privacy obligations, renewal terms, internal operating effort, switching, and exit costs where material.

Forecast precision should match evidence quality; uncertain costs should be shown as assumptions or ranges rather than hidden inside a single confident figure.
