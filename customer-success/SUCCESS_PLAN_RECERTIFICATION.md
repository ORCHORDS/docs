# Success Plan Recertification

## Purpose
Keep material customer success plans aligned with current goals, risks, responsibilities, and evidence.

## Requirements
- Active success plans must identify current objectives, owners, and measurable outcomes.
- Periodic review must confirm that assumptions and priorities remain valid.
- Stale, unsupported, or superseded plan elements must be updated or closed.
- Material risks or unmet commitments require escalation and revised actions.

## Evidence
Maintain dated plan reviews, customer-agreed changes where applicable, and action closure evidence without exposing customer-confidential information.
