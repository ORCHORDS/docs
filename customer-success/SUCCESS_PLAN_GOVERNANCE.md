# Customer Success Plan Governance

## Purpose

Maintain a shared record of intended customer outcomes and the path toward them.

## Plan content

A success plan SHOULD identify outcomes, current state, milestones, customer and company owners, dependencies, open risks, decision points, and evidence of progress.

Plans should be updated when customer priorities, commitments, product capability, or delivery assumptions materially change.
