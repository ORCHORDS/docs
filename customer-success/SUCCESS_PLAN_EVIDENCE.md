# Customer Success Plan Evidence

## Purpose

Keep success plans anchored to customer-agreed outcomes rather than internal activity metrics.

## Requirements

Material success plans SHOULD identify desired outcomes, accountable customer/internal stakeholders, baseline or starting context, milestones, dependencies, evidence sources, risks, and review cadence.

Activity counts, meetings, logins, or feature usage may be signals but are not automatically proof of customer value.
