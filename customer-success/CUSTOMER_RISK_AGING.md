# Customer Risk Aging

## Purpose
Keep unresolved material customer-success risks visible until they are mitigated, accepted, transferred, or closed.

## Requirements
- Material risks must have an owner, opened date, status, and next action.
- Aging must be reviewed using risk-appropriate thresholds.
- Overdue or repeatedly deferred risks require escalation and refreshed rationale.
- Closure must record the outcome and supporting evidence.

## Evidence
Maintain privacy-minimized risk registers, aging reviews, escalations, and closure evidence without exposing customer-confidential information.
