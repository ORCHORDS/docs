# Customer Onboarding

## Purpose

Translate approved commercial commitments into a clear, owned adoption path.

## Requirements

Onboarding SHOULD confirm customer goals, stakeholders, agreed scope, dependencies, access or data prerequisites, training/enablement needs, milestones, support route, and acceptance signals.

Do not collect credentials or sensitive data in ad-hoc onboarding records.

See [Customer Onboarding SOP](../sop/CUSTOMER_ONBOARDING_SOP.md).
