# Renewal and Retention Risk

## Purpose

Identify material risks to continued customer value early enough for honest action.

## Requirements

Renewal risk SHOULD distinguish evidence from speculation and identify owner, cause, customer impact, mitigation, dependencies, and next review date.

Do not conceal service, security, legal, financial, or delivery problems solely to protect a renewal forecast.
