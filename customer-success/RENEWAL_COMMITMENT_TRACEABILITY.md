# Renewal Commitment Traceability

## Purpose
Ensure material commitments influencing renewal decisions are accurate, owned, and traceable to approved sources.

## Requirements
- Material renewal commitments must have an accountable owner and source record.
- Status must distinguish agreed, planned, completed, changed, and withdrawn commitments.
- Unsupported or ambiguous commitments require clarification before reliance.
- Handoffs must preserve commitment context and current status.

## Evidence
Retain approved commitment records, status updates, changes, and closure evidence without publishing customer-specific terms.
