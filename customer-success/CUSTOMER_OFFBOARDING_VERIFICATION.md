# Customer Offboarding Verification

## Purpose
Confirm material customer-transition and offboarding actions are completed, evidenced, and handed off appropriately.

## Requirements
- Offboarding plans must identify accountable owners and required completion criteria.
- Material open actions must be resolved, transferred, or explicitly accepted before closure.
- Data, access, contractual, support, and records obligations must be coordinated with relevant owners.
- Final verification must document unresolved exceptions.

## Evidence
Maintain completion records, handoffs, exception approvals, and closure verification without publishing customer or system details.
