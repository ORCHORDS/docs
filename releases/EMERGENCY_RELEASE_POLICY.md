# Emergency Release Policy

## Purpose

Enable urgent remediation while preserving minimum safety, traceability, and accountability.

## Trigger

Emergency release may be used for severe security, availability, data-integrity, legal, or user-harm conditions where normal release timing would materially increase risk.

## Minimum controls

Even under urgency, the release MUST have an accountable approver, scoped change, targeted verification, rollback or containment plan, integrity controls, and post-release monitoring.

Controls may be compressed but not silently omitted. Any skipped ordinary gate should be recorded with rationale and reviewed afterward.

## Follow-up

Emergency releases require retrospective review of cause, testing gaps, documentation, and whether permanent corrective work remains.
