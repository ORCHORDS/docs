# Quality Objectives

## Purpose

Define measurable quality outcomes that connect policy expectations to evidence and improvement.

## Requirements

Material quality objectives SHOULD identify owner, intended outcome, measure, review cadence, target or decision threshold where useful, and action when performance is persistently outside expectation.

Metrics should support better decisions rather than reward gaming, hidden defects, or superficial closure.
