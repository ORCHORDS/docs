# Supplier Quality Management

## Purpose

Manage recurring quality risk introduced by suppliers whose outputs materially affect users, delivery, or operations.

## Requirements

Material supplier quality issues SHOULD be recorded with impact, evidence, recurrence, corrective action, owner, target, and effectiveness review. Repeated defects or service failures should inform supplier-risk, renewal, sourcing, and resilience decisions.

Supplier corrective action does not remove ORCHORDS accountability for validating outcomes that affect its users or commitments.

See [Supplier Quality Review SOP](../sop/SUPPLIER_QUALITY_REVIEW_SOP.md).
