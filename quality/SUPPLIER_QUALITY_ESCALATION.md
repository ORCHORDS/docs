# Supplier Quality Escalation

## Purpose

Escalate repeated or high-impact supplier quality failures beyond routine case handling.

## Requirements

Material escalation SHOULD identify failure pattern, business/user impact, evidence, supplier response, corrective-action expectations, owner, decision date, and potential renewal or exit consequences.

Repeated workarounds should not substitute indefinitely for supplier remediation.
