# Defect Trend Governance

## Purpose

Identify recurring quality problems that individual defect closure can hide.

## Requirements

Trend review SHOULD consider recurrence, severity, escape point, affected process or component, detection source, fix effectiveness, and concentration by cause or workflow.

Rising closure volume should not be treated as improvement if creation or recurrence is rising faster.
