# Quality Management Policy

## Purpose

Define a company-wide quality system focused on intended outcomes, evidence, customer impact, prevention, learning, and continual improvement.

## Principles

Quality work SHOULD:

- define requirements and acceptance criteria before claiming completion;
- measure outcomes that matter to users and the business;
- treat defects, incidents, complaints, audit findings, and near misses as learning inputs;
- distinguish symptom correction from durable corrective action;
- verify remediation rather than relying only on ticket status;
- address recurring systemic causes across teams or processes;
- preserve traceable evidence for important decisions.

## Quality objectives

Objectives should be measurable where practical, owned, reviewed, and connected to a decision or improvement action. Metrics must not create incentives to hide failures or user harm.

## References

The quality framework is informed by the currently published ISO 9001:2015 with Amendment 1:2024. The 2026 edition is tracked as under publication and is not treated as final in this corpus.
