# Quality Review Governance

## Purpose

Provide periodic management review of quality performance, systemic issues, and improvement priorities.

## Inputs

Quality review MAY consider:

- product/service defects;
- incidents and recurrence;
- customer complaints and feedback;
- accessibility issues;
- audit/control findings;
- supplier quality;
- CAPA status/effectiveness;
- quality objectives and measurement validity;
- process bottlenecks or recurring exceptions.

## Outputs

Review should produce decisions, owners, improvement priorities, resource needs, risk escalations, and follow-up dates rather than only reporting metrics.

## Standard tracking

The 2026 revision of ISO 9001 should be reassessed after formal publication before this corpus treats it as the current final quality-management standard.
