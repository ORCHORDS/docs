# Quality Sampling Governance

## Purpose

Use samples for assurance without implying more certainty than the sample supports.

## Requirements

Material sampling SHOULD document population, selection method, sample size rationale, known bias or exclusions, review criteria, and limits on conclusions.

Convenience samples may be useful for exploration but should not be presented as representative evidence without justification.
