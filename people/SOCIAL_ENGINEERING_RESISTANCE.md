# Social Engineering Resistance

## Purpose

Reduce the risk that urgency, authority, familiarity, or impersonation is used
to bypass normal controls.

## Expectations

Personnel SHOULD independently verify unusual requests involving:

- credential or recovery changes;
- payment or banking changes;
- release of sensitive information;
- privileged access;
- disabling security controls;
- urgent supplier or executive requests;
- unusual file execution or software installation.

Verification should use a trusted, previously established channel rather than
contact details supplied by the suspicious request.

## Safe reporting

Personnel must be able to report suspicious messages and mistakes quickly
without fear that honest reporting will be punished merely for revealing the
event.

## Drills

Simulations may be used for education, but they should measure resilience and
learning rather than humiliation or individual blame.
