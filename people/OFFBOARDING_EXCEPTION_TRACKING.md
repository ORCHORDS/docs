# Offboarding Exception Tracking

## Purpose
Ensure incomplete or delayed offboarding actions remain visible and are resolved promptly.

## Requirements
- Material offboarding exceptions must have an owner, opened date, reason, and target closure.
- High-risk incomplete actions require prompt escalation.
- Exception status must be reviewed until closure is verified.
- Repeated failure patterns must inform process improvement.

## Evidence
Retain exception and closure records without publishing identities, access details, devices, or internal system information.
