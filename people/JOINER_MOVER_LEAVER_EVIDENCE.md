# Joiner Mover Leaver Evidence

## Purpose

Ensure personnel lifecycle changes leave reviewable evidence that access, responsibilities, equipment, and required acknowledgements were handled.

## Requirements

Material lifecycle events SHOULD record the event type, effective date, accountable roles, required access changes, asset actions, policy/training actions, and completion evidence.

The public repository must not contain personal personnel records or private employment details.
