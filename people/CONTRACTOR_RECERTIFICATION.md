# Contractor Recertification

## Purpose
Confirm ongoing contractor engagements, responsibilities, access needs, and required controls remain current.

## Requirements
- Material contractor engagements must have a current sponsor and business purpose.
- Periodic review must confirm engagement status, role, required access, and applicable obligations.
- Expired or unnecessary engagements must be closed and associated access addressed.
- Exceptions require documented ownership and follow-up.

## Evidence
Retain sponsor attestations, review outcomes, status changes, and closure evidence while protecting contractual and personal information.
