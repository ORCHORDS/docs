# Training Exception Aging

## Purpose
Prevent overdue mandatory training exceptions from becoming unmanaged risk.

## Requirements
- Material training exceptions must record reason, owner, due date, and risk response.
- Aging must be reviewed at a defined cadence.
- Long-running exceptions require escalation or refreshed approval.
- Repeated exceptions should inform training design or workforce planning improvements.

## Evidence
Maintain exception records, reminders, escalations, approvals, completion, and closure evidence while protecting personnel information.
