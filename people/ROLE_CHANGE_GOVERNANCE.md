# Role Change Governance

## Purpose

Ensure internal role changes update access, responsibilities, conflicts, and knowledge transfer rather than only the job title.

## Requirements

Material role changes SHOULD review privileged access, group/team membership, approval authority, sensitive-data access, owned controls/services, delegated authority, conflicts of interest, required training, and knowledge-transfer needs.

Access no longer needed must be removed promptly; new privilege follows normal approval rather than inheriting everything from the prior role.

See [Role Change SOP](../sop/ROLE_CHANGE_SOP.md).
