# External Standard Adoption Decision

Before declaring an external standard mandatory internally, decision-makers SHOULD consider scope, applicability, maturity, cost, conflicts, implementation evidence, legal/contractual drivers, maintenance burden, and whether partial guidance is more appropriate than full adoption.
