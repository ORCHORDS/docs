# Singapore PDPA Notifiable-Breach Assessment Clock

**Issue:** A breach-response team can lose notification time while debating impact without recording when credible grounds to believe a breach occurred or when assessment began.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls

- Confirm PDPA applicability and current PDPC guidance with Singapore counsel.
- Open a documented assessment immediately when credible breach information arrives.
- Determine significant harm and scale using a repeatable decision record.
- Preserve discovery, assessment, containment, notification, and affected-individual communication timestamps.

## Verification

- Tabletop ambiguous discovery, delayed vendor notice, encrypted data, and evolving affected counts.
- Recompute the notification decision from retained evidence.
- Verify processor-to-organization escalation meets contract and response timing.

## Gotchas

- Containment completion does not stop the assessment or notification clock.
- Legal thresholds and required contents must be checked against current PDPC rules.
