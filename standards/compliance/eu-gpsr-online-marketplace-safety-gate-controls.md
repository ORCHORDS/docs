# EU GPSR Online Marketplace Safety Gate Controls

**Issue:** The General Product Safety Regulation gives online marketplaces specific product-safety duties, including Safety Gate registration, contact points, listing information, notice handling, and recall communication.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls
- Register marketplace contact details in Safety Gate and maintain authority and consumer contact routes.
- Block publication until required manufacturer/responsible-person, product identification, and warning information is present.
- Process product-safety notices within the applicable deadline and retain receipt, assessment, action, and authority response evidence.
- Detect and prevent reappearance of listings identified by enforceable orders.
- Maintain purchaser contact and recall-notification workflows.
- Localize warnings for the Member State where the product is offered.

## Verification
- Attempt listings with each required field absent or malformed.
- Replay a Safety Gate notice through removal, recurrence prevention, buyer notification, and evidence export.
- Measure the three-working-day notice path independently of other moderation queues.

## Gotchas
GPSR obligations build on, but are not replaced by, Digital Services Act processes. “Marketplace” scope and sector-specific product law require legal classification.
