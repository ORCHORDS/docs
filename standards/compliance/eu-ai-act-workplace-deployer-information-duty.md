# EU AI Act workplace deployer information duty

**Issue:** Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 includes duties for deployers of high-risk AI systems, including prior information to workers' representatives and affected workers when the system is used at the workplace, subject to scope and applicable Union or national rules.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls and implementation

Classify the system, high-risk basis, deployer role, workplace use, affected groups, representative body, timing, language, accessibility, and national employment-law path. Version notices against system purpose, capabilities, human oversight, data use, and effective date; preserve consultation and change evidence.

## Tests

Test pre-deployment notice, material model/purpose change, contractor and remote-worker populations, representative unavailability, multilingual access, withdrawal, and system disablement.

## Gotchas

An employee privacy notice or vendor manual is not automatically the required workplace information. Applicability and consultation consequences require legal and labour-relations review; notice does not cure a prohibited use.
