# EU Right to Repair Directive — Compliance Guide

**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Author:** the platform team
**Status:** published

## Symptom

Your organization manufactures or imports consumer products sold in the EU.
You do not offer repair services beyond the legal warranty period, spare
parts are unavailable or prohibitively expensive, and independent repairers
cannot access diagnostic tools. As of 31 July 2026, the EU Right to Repair
Directive requires you to change these practices.

## Context

The EU Right to Repair Directive (Directive (EU) 2024/1799) establishes
consumers' right to have products repaired beyond the legal warranty
period. It applies across all 27 EU member states from 31 July 2026. The
directive works alongside Ecodesign regulations (which set product-
specific repairability requirements) and the Consumer Sales Directive
(which governs warranty obligations).

## Key obligations

### 1. Obligation to repair

Manufacturers must repair products at the consumer's request when repair
is technically possible under Ecodesign rules. The repair must be:

- At a **reasonable price** — the manufacturer must provide a cost
  estimate before repair. The price must not be so high as to discourage
  repair in favor of replacement.
- Within a **reasonable timeframe** — without unreasonable delays.

### 2. Spare parts and tools

- Spare parts must remain available for the period specified by the
  applicable Ecodesign regulation (typically 7-10 years for major
  appliances, 5 years for smaller electronics).
- Repair manuals, diagnostic tools, and software must be accessible to
  **independent repairers**, not just authorized service networks.
- Spare parts pricing must be transparent and reasonable.

### 3. Prohibition of anti-repair practices

Manufacturers cannot use:

- **Software locks** that prevent third-party repairs.
- **Parts pairing** that degrades device functionality when non-OEM parts
  are installed (unless objectively justifiable for safety or
  cybersecurity).
- **Contractual clauses** prohibiting independent repair.
- **Warranty voiding** solely because a repair was performed by an
  independent repairer.

### 4. Warranty extension

When a consumer chooses repair instead of replacement during the warranty
period, the legal guarantee is extended by **12 months** from the date the
repaired product is returned.

## Repairability information

### European Repair Information Form

Manufacturers must provide a standardized repair information form within
5 working days of a consumer's repair request, containing:

- Nature of the defect (as described by the consumer).
- Proposed repair and its conditions.
- Price or estimation method.
- Estimated repair time.
- Availability of a temporary replacement (if applicable).

### Online repair platform

The directive mandates a European online platform connecting consumers
with repairers and sellers of refurbished goods, promoting repair as the
first option.

## Anti-patterns

- **Pricing repair above replacement** — setting repair costs at 80-90%
  of a new product's price to steer consumers toward buying new.
- **Proprietary diagnostic monopoly** — restricting diagnostic tools to
  authorized service centers while charging independent repairers
  licensing fees that make independent repair uneconomical.
- **Planned obsolescence via software** — releasing firmware updates that
  degrade older devices or discontinuing software support to force
  hardware replacement.
- **Fine-print warranty exclusions** — burying clauses that void warranty
  for third-party repairs deep in terms and conditions.

## Gotchas

- **Product scope** — the directive applies to products covered by
  Ecodesign regulations. The list of covered products is expanding — check
  the current Ecodesign product list for your product categories.
- **B2B exclusion** — the directive covers business-to-consumer sales
  only. Commercial/industrial products are not covered.
- **National transposition variations** — member states may add
  requirements beyond the directive's minimum. Monitor transposition in
  key markets.
- **Interaction with product liability** — if a third-party repair causes
  a safety issue, liability analysis becomes complex. Document OEM vs.
  third-party repair history.

## Verification

- Repair service is available and advertised to consumers.
- Spare parts catalog is published with availability timelines and
  pricing.
- Repair manuals and diagnostic tools are accessible to independent
  repairers.
- Software locks and unjustifiable parts pairing are removed.
- 12-month warranty extension is implemented for repair-over-replacement.
- European Repair Information Form process is operational (5-day
  response).
- Legal team has reviewed national transpositions in key markets.
