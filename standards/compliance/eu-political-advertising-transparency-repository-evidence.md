# EU Political Advertising Transparency Repository Evidence

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2024/900 requires political-ad labels, sponsor and campaign transparency, targeting disclosures, machine-readable notices, retention, and repository delivery.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls

- Classify political advertising and each provider, publisher, and sponsor role before acceptance.
- Bind creative to sponsor/controller, linked process, targeting status, spend/value, funding source, dissemination period, and every notice version.
- Keep labels prominent through redistribution and notices accessible, current, machine-readable, and accessible.
- Submit online ads and metadata to the applicable repository within the required path and deadline.
- Retain notices and changes for seven years and support noncompliance reports.

## Verification

- Trace an ad through intake, targeting, publication, repository lookup, correction, suspension, and retention.
- Test missing sponsor, changed funding, repost, accessibility, and repository submission.
- Reconcile repository records to delivery logs and invoices.

## Gotchas

Political opinion is not automatically political advertising; remuneration and scope matter. Repository publication does not transfer accuracy responsibility.
