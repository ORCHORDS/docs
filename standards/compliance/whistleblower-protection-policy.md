# whistleblower-protection-policy

**Issue:** Implementing whistleblower protection programs compliant with EU Whistleblower Directive and SOX
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** documented

## Symptom / Context
EU Whistleblower Directive (2019/1937) required member states to implement protections and companies with 50+ employees to establish internal reporting channels by December 2023. SOX Section 301 and 806 mandate protections for public company employees in the US.

## Pattern / Solution
EU Whistleblower Directive requirements for companies >50 employees:

Internal reporting channel requirements:
- Secure, confidential channel accessible to employees (and optionally external parties)
- Acknowledgment within 7 days of report receipt
- Follow-up and feedback within 3 months
- Records maintained (with confidentiality protections) for 5 years

Prohibited retaliation actions:
- Dismissal, demotion, salary reduction
- Negative performance evaluations
- Withholding training or promotion
- Blacklisting, defamation
- Any other form of adverse treatment

Protected reportable matters (EU Directive scope): breaches of EU law in areas including financial services, anti-money laundering, environmental law, public health, food safety, consumer protection, data protection.

Implementation checklist:
- [ ] Designate an impartial internal person or department to receive reports (e.g., Compliance, Legal, or Board Audit Committee)
- [ ] Provide web-based, phone, and in-person reporting options
- [ ] Non-retaliation policy documented and published
- [ ] Acknowledgment and follow-up process defined with SLAs
- [ ] Training for managers on non-retaliation obligations
- [ ] Anonymous reporting option (strongly recommended even if not mandated)
- [ ] External channel option (report directly to regulator; national competent authority)

Third-party whistleblowing platforms: EthicsPoint (NAVEX), Speakfully, WhistleB — provide anonymity, case management, multilingual.

## Gotchas
- EU Directive covers contractors, suppliers, and former employees — not just current employees
- Anonymous reports must still be investigated even if you cannot follow up with the reporter
- Retaliation against whistleblowers creates separate liability beyond the original misconduct
- US SOX protections apply to employees of publicly traded companies and their subsidiaries and contractors
