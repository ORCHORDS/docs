# EU Pay-Transparency HR Data Evidence Controls

**Issue:** Pay-transparency reporting and worker information rights depend on consistent worker categories, remuneration definitions, and explainable calculations across fragmented HR and payroll systems.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls

- Confirm national transposition, employer-size thresholds, dates, and worker scope with counsel.
- Version the methodology for total remuneration, categories of workers, hourly rates, variable pay, and gender-pay-gap calculations.
- Preserve lineage from reported aggregates to authorized payroll and HR records while restricting personal-data access.
- Record joint pay-assessment triggers, corrections, approvals, and employee-response workflows.

## Verification

- Recompute sampled aggregates from source payroll periods.
- Test joiners, leavers, part-time workers, bonuses, multiple contracts, and changed worker categories.
- Verify workers receive required information without disclosure of another individual’s pay.

## Gotchas

- The directive requires national transposition; implementation details can vary.
- Small samples can create re-identification risk even in aggregate reports.
