# eu-data-act-implementation

**Issue:** A connected-product or related-service team has no operational process for data access, sharing, and cloud switching obligations
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Symptom

Product, legal, and platform teams disagree about which data is generated by connected products, who may access it, how requests are fulfilled, or whether a customer can move workload data to another processing service.

## Root cause

The EU Data Act applies from 12 September 2025. It establishes rules around access to and use of data generated by connected products and related services, and includes measures intended to make switching data-processing services easier. Compliance work fails when it is treated as a one-time policy update instead of a product, data-governance, and operational capability.

**Source:** [European Commission — Data Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act) and [Data Act explained](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/data-act-explained).

## Fix

Create a cross-functional implementation record before asserting coverage:

- inventory connected products, related services, generated data classes, holders, recipients, locations, retention rules, and contractual roles;
- document the applicable scope analysis with counsel; do not assume all telemetry or all customers are covered;
- design a verified request path for access, export, and sharing, with identity checks, tenant authorization, rate limits, and an audit trail;
- define portable export formats, schema/version documentation, and deletion/transition steps for switching scenarios;
- align contracts, support runbooks, privacy notices, security controls, and incident handling with the implemented data flows;
- measure request fulfilment times, export failures, authorization denials, and outstanding exceptions, then review them regularly.

## Verification

- **Tabletop:** fulfill a simulated eligible access or portability request end to end without manual data discovery.
- **Authorization:** one tenant cannot request or receive another tenant's data.
- **Export:** the result is complete for the documented scope, machine-readable, versioned, and integrity-checked.
- **Governance:** legal, security, support, and engineering sign off on the scope record and evidence location.

## Gotchas

- The Data Act is not interchangeable with GDPR; the data types, roles, and obligations differ.
- A download button is insufficient if exports omit required datasets, lack authorization controls, or cannot be interpreted by the recipient.
- Scope and exemptions are legal determinations; record the rationale and have qualified counsel review it.
