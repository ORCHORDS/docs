# EU Data Governance Act data-altruism consent evidence

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2022/868 creates a framework for recognized data-altruism organisations and a European consent form. Applicability depends on role, recognition, data, purpose, and Member State supervision.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls

Record the recognised entity, declared general-interest purpose, data/rights holder, consent scope, withdrawal channel, processing systems, and downstream recipients; separate altruism from paid service consent.

## Verification

Exercise granular grant, refusal, modification, withdrawal, purpose change, recipient change, deletion/restriction, and evidence export in every supported language.

## Gotchas

Data altruism does not waive GDPR or intellectual-property rights. A generic terms acceptance is not automatically the prescribed consent evidence.
