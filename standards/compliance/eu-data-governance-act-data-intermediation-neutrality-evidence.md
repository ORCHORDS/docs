# EU Data Governance Act Intermediation Neutrality Evidence

**Issue:** A data intermediation service can undermine neutrality if it reuses exchanged data for unrelated purposes or mixes intermediation with other business activities.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls

- Confirm service classification, notification duties, and national competent authority with counsel.
- Separate intermediation activity, accounts, access roles, and data processing from other services.
- Document permitted transformations, metadata use, pricing, and purpose limitations.
- Retain participant instructions, consent or authority, access logs, and portability evidence.

## Verification

- Trace sampled data from provider to user and verify no unauthorized secondary use.
- Test role separation and financial/accounting segregation.
- Exercise participant exit and export without lock-in.

## Gotchas

- The DGA is distinct from the Data Act and GDPR; multiple regimes may apply.
- Neutrality is an operating constraint, not merely a contractual statement.
