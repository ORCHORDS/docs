# EU ESPR Digital Product Passport Data Governance

**Issue:** Digital product passports under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation require product-linked data that remains accurate, accessible, interoperable, and appropriately restricted.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls

- Confirm delegated-act product scope and applicable data fields with counsel.
- Assign a system of record, owner, update trigger, retention, and access class to every passport field.
- Bind passport identifiers to product/model/batch identity and preserve correction history.
- Test public, authority, supply-chain, and restricted access views separately.

## Verification

- Trace sampled passport values to source evidence.
- Correct a field and verify versioning plus downstream visibility.
- Test identifier collision, retired products, offline links, and unauthorized access.

## Gotchas

- The framework regulation does not make every product immediately subject to identical fields.
- Public transparency and confidential business data require different access paths.
