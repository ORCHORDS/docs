# EU AMLR Beneficial-Ownership Evidence Graph

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 creates directly applicable anti-money-laundering requirements, including identifying and verifying beneficial owners through ownership and control.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls
- Determine obliged-entity scope, application date, national dependencies, and customer risk with counsel.
- Represent direct and indirect ownership, voting rights, other control, intermediate entities, and calculation evidence as a dated graph.
- Retain source provenance, verification method, discrepancies, reviewer, and refresh trigger.
- Escalate complex, nominee, trust, foundation, and no-identifiable-owner cases under the regulation's rules.
- Recompute after corporate events and periodic/risk-triggered review.

## Verification
- Test multi-layer, cross-holding, split ownership/control, trust, stale register, and conflicting-source cases.
- Reproduce each reported beneficial owner from source documents and calculation steps.
- Assert changes trigger screening and due-diligence refresh.

## Gotchas
Registry data is evidence, not an automatic substitute for obliged-entity verification. Ownership percentage alone may miss control through other means.
