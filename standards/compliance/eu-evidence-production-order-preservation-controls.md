# EU e-Evidence Production and Preservation Order Controls

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2023/1543 establishes European Production and Preservation Orders for electronic evidence with defined recipients, deadlines, confidentiality, review, and data-handling duties.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls
- Route validated orders to a trained legal-response function and authenticate the issuing/transmitting authority.
- Classify subscriber, traffic, and content data and preserve only the identified scope.
- Freeze deletion without exposing the preservation action to unauthorized staff or users.
- Track receipt, applicable deadline, impossibility grounds, objections, transmission, and chain of custody.
- Separate preservation from production authorization and minimize exported data.
- Encrypt transfers and log every access and disclosure.

## Verification
- Simulate ordinary and emergency orders, wrong provider, wrong addressee, conflicting law, unavailable data, and revoked order.
- Reproduce the exported dataset from scope and audit trail.
- Verify preservation expires or is extended only through authorized process.

## Gotchas
A preservation order is not itself a command to disclose. Applicability and interactions with national law require counsel.
