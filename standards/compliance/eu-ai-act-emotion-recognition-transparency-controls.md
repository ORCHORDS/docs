# EU AI Act emotion-recognition transparency controls

**Issue:** Article 50 transparency duties may apply to emotion-recognition or biometric-categorisation systems, subject to scope and exceptions.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls and implementation

Classify system/use/role, notify exposed persons when required, preserve notices and legal assessment, minimize biometric data.

## Tests

Test notice timing/accessibility, disabled model, exception path, vendor change, record retention.

## Gotchas

Transparency does not legalize a prohibited or otherwise unlawful use; legal review decides applicability.
