# Controlled Definition Glossary

## Core definitions

- **Control:** an action, mechanism, or condition intended to manage risk or satisfy a requirement.
- **Evidence:** information supporting a factual claim about design, operation, outcome, or assessment.
- **Owner:** the accountable role responsible for maintaining a decision, document, risk, control, or process.
- **Exception:** an authorized departure from a defined requirement for a stated scope and period.
- **Assurance:** confidence supported by evidence and assessment, with strength dependent on scope and independence.

Domain documents may define additional terms but should avoid conflicting meanings without explanation.
