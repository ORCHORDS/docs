# De-identification and Re-identification Risk

## Purpose

Set expectations when personal data is transformed to reduce identifiability.

## Principles

Removing obvious identifiers does not automatically make data anonymous. Risk depends on remaining attributes, auxiliary data, population uniqueness, access context, and intended sharing.

## Requirements

Material de-identification decisions SHOULD document technique, intended use, recipients, residual re-identification risk, access restrictions, and whether re-identification is prohibited by policy or contract.

## Public release

Data proposed for public release requires stronger review because recipient controls may be unavailable and external auxiliary data can change over time.

## Reassessment

Re-identification risk should be revisited when datasets are linked, new attributes are added, sharing broadens, or external data availability materially changes.
