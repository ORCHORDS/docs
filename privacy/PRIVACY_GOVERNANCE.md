# Privacy Governance

## Purpose

Establish company-wide accountability for handling personal information and
privacy risk.

## Principles

- Collect and use personal information for defined, legitimate purposes.
- Minimize collection, access, sharing, and retention.
- Provide transparency appropriate to the context.
- Protect information according to sensitivity and plausible harm.
- Respect applicable individual rights and legal obligations.
- Build privacy considerations into design and change processes.
- Evaluate suppliers and new uses of data before material exposure occurs.
- Preserve evidence of material privacy decisions.

## Accountability

Material privacy decisions have an accountable business owner and privacy
review appropriate to impact. Legal obligations take precedence where they
impose stricter requirements.

## Privacy risk

Privacy risk assessment considers the individuals affected, sensitivity,
volume, linkability, context, unexpected use, retention, sharing, automation,
and consequences of error or misuse.

## Incidents

Suspected privacy incidents are handled through the incident process with
privacy review and legally required notification assessment where applicable.
