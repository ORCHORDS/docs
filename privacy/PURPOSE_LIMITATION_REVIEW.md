# Purpose Limitation Review

## Purpose

Prevent information collected for one reason from being reused for materially different purposes without appropriate assessment.

## Requirements

Before material secondary use, owners SHOULD assess compatibility with the original purpose, user expectations, sensitivity, contractual or legal restrictions, new harms, and whether additional notice, choice, minimization, or approval is required.

Convenience or technical availability alone is not a sufficient justification for expanded use.
