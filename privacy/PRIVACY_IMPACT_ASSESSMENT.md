# Privacy Impact Assessment

## Purpose

Define when structured privacy-risk analysis is required before a new or
materially changed processing activity.

## Triggers

Assessment SHOULD be considered when a change introduces:

- new categories of personal data;
- sensitive or vulnerable-person data;
- materially new purposes;
- large-scale monitoring or profiling;
- automated decisions with meaningful effects;
- new sharing or transfer patterns;
- new retention or re-identification risk;
- AI use involving personal data.

## Assessment

A privacy impact assessment should record purpose, necessity, data flows at an
appropriate level, affected people, risks to individuals, controls, residual
risk, owner, and review decision.

Privacy risk is not limited to confidentiality breaches. It can arise from
inappropriate processing even when security controls operate correctly.
