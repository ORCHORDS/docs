# Privacy Incident Response

## Purpose

Integrate privacy analysis into incident response when personal data may be
lost, altered, disclosed, accessed, unavailable, or processed inappropriately.

## Requirements

Potential privacy incidents require prompt assessment of:

- data categories and sensitivity;
- affected people and approximate scale;
- confidentiality, integrity, availability, or misuse impact;
- duration and exposure;
- containment actions;
- applicable notification or contractual obligations;
- evidence needed for legal or regulatory assessment.

## Coordination

Privacy, security, legal, operations, and communications responsibilities may
overlap. Use one coordinated incident timeline rather than competing records
where practical.

## Notification

Whether notification is required depends on applicable law, contract, facts,
and risk. Public policy must not invent a universal notification deadline.
