# Consent and Preference Governance

## Purpose

Govern collection, recording, change, and withdrawal of consent or user privacy preferences where these are used as a basis for processing or communication.

## Requirements

Material consent or preference records SHOULD identify the relevant purpose, user choice, effective time, source, applicable notice or version, and withdrawal/change history where needed.

Consent must not be inferred from silence where an affirmative choice is required.

Preference changes should propagate to dependent processing within a reasonable and documented period.
