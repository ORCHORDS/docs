# Privacy by Design

## Purpose

Integrate privacy requirements into new processes, systems, data uses, and
material changes before deployment.

## Review triggers

Privacy review is expected when work introduces or materially changes personal
information, sensitive data, profiling, automated decisions, new sharing,
cross-context reuse, retention, identity linkage, tracking, or high-scale
collection.

## Design questions

A review should determine:

1. What personal information is necessary?
2. What purpose justifies collection and use?
3. Can the objective be achieved with less data or less precision?
4. Who needs access and for how long?
5. What user expectations or rights are affected?
6. What supplier or transfer risks exist?
7. What happens if data is inaccurate, exposed, misused, or retained too long?
8. What evidence will demonstrate the approved decision?

## Preferred patterns

Prefer minimization, purpose limitation, short retention, access separation,
aggregation, de-identification where suitable, clear user communication, and
reversible choices.

## Approval

Higher-impact privacy risks require explicit acceptance by the accountable
owner and privacy reviewer before release or operational use.
