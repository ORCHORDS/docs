# Physical Access Security

## Purpose

Restrict physical access to company-controlled spaces, equipment, and sensitive records according to business need.

## Requirements

Physical access SHOULD be attributable where risk warrants it, limited to authorized people, reviewed after role changes, and revoked promptly when no longer needed.

Sensitive areas may require stronger controls such as escorts, additional approval, logging, alarms, or separation of duties.

## Public boundary

Facility layouts, access mechanisms, alarm details, camera locations, badge formats, and emergency bypass methods do not belong in public documentation.

## Incidents

Suspected unauthorized entry, tailgating into sensitive areas, lost physical credentials, or tampering requires prompt reporting and assessment.
