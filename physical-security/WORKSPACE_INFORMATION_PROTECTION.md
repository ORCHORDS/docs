# Workspace Information Protection

## Purpose

Prevent accidental exposure of company information in offices, shared spaces, homes, and travel environments.

## Expectations

Personnel SHOULD secure unattended screens and devices, avoid leaving restricted records visible or unattended, use appropriate disposal methods, prevent unauthorized photography or conversation exposure where material, and store physical sensitive records only where authorized.

## Printing

Print restricted information only when necessary and control collection, storage, transport, and disposal.

Also follow [Remote Work Security](../people/REMOTE_WORK_SECURITY.md).
