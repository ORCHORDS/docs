# email-subject-line-best-practices

**Issue:** Writing subject lines that improve open rates and avoid spam filters
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** documented

## Symptom / Context
Subject line is the primary open-rate lever; poor subjects kill otherwise good emails.

## Pattern / Solution
1. Length: 40-60 characters; most clients truncate at 60. Mobile shows ~30-40.
2. Avoid spam trigger words: "FREE", "100%", "Act Now", "Limited time", excessive punctuation.
3. Use personalization where meaningful: `{{firstName}}, your invoice is ready`.
4. Create curiosity or urgency without deception: "What you missed in March" not "URGENT: OPEN NOW".
5. A/B test subject lines on 20% of list before sending to remaining 80%.
6. Avoid ALL CAPS, excessive exclamation marks, and misleading preview text.

## Gotchas
- Re-using the same subject line pattern across sends trains readers to ignore them.
- Emojis improve open rate for some audiences and hurt for others; test before committing.
- "Re:" and "Fwd:" prefixes on non-reply/forward emails may improve opens temporarily but damage trust.
- CAN-SPAM requires subject lines to accurately reflect content.
