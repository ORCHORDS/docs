# Responsible Business Exit

Ending a supplier or business relationship can itself create adverse impacts.

Exit decisions should consider severity of the issue, leverage, remediation prospects, affected workers or communities, legal obligations, continuity needs, transition timing, and whether responsible disengagement measures are appropriate.
