# Human Rights Management Review

Management review should consider salient risks, severe allegations, supplier findings, grievance trends, overdue remediation, changing legal or geographic risk, training effectiveness, and decisions requiring additional leverage or exit planning.

Outcomes should identify owners and follow-up evidence.
