# Grievance Mechanism Governance

Grievance channels should be accessible, understandable, appropriately confidential, protected from retaliation, and capable of routing concerns to accountable reviewers.

Material cases should have documented ownership, status, outcome, and follow-up without unnecessary disclosure of personal information.
