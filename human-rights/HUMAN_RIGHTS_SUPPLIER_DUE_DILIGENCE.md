# Human Rights Supplier Due Diligence

Supplier diligence should prioritize salient risks based on sector, geography, workforce model, recruitment practices, subcontracting, product/service characteristics, public evidence, and prior findings.

Questionnaire responses should not substitute for relevant corroborating evidence when risk is material.
