# Human Rights Contract Requirements

Material contracts may include proportionate requirements for lawful labor practices, subcontractor oversight, incident notification, evidence cooperation, corrective action, and termination or remediation rights where relevant to risk.

Contract language should align with actual monitoring capability and leverage.
