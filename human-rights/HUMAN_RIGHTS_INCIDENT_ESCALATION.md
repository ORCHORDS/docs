# Human Rights Incident Escalation

Credible allegations of severe harm, forced labor, trafficking, child labor, violence, retaliation, or systemic abuse require prompt escalation to appropriate governance, legal, people, procurement, and leadership owners.

Immediate safety and preservation of relevant evidence take priority over routine review cadence.
