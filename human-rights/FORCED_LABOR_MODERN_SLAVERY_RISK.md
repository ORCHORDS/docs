# Forced Labor and Modern Slavery Risk

Forced labor, trafficking, coercion, withheld identity documents, debt bondage, and similar practices are unacceptable.

Risk reviews should consider recruitment practices, worker mobility, wage withholding, document control, subcontracting, high-risk geographies, grievance access, and remediation evidence.
