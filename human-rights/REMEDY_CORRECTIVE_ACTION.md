# Remedy and Corrective Action

Where ORCHORDS caused or contributed to a material adverse human-rights impact, remediation should be proportionate, timely, evidence-based, and attentive to the needs of affected people.

Supplier corrective actions should include effectiveness review rather than closure based only on stated intent.
