# SOP: Corrective and Preventive Action

## Trigger
Use for material recurring defects, incidents, complaints, audit/control findings, supplier failures, or other evidence that simple correction is insufficient.

## Procedure
1. Define the problem and supporting evidence.
2. Apply immediate containment/correction where needed.
3. Determine whether structured cause analysis is required.
4. Identify corrective and preventive actions linked to causes.
5. Assign owners and review dates.
6. Define effectiveness criteria before closure.
7. Implement actions.
8. Verify the intended outcome over an appropriate period.
9. Close only when effectiveness is demonstrated or residual risk is explicitly accepted.
