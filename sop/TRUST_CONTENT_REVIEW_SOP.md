# Trust Content Review SOP

1. Identify the statement, audience, owner, and represented scope.
2. Verify current evidence and source authority.
3. Check consistency with security, privacy, compliance, reliability, AI, accessibility, and communications material as relevant.
4. Apply qualifications and expiry triggers.
5. Approve, correct, or retire the content and preserve the record.
