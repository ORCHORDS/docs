# Trust Artifact Recertification SOP

1. Identify customer-facing trust artifacts due for review.
2. Verify owner, scope, evidence date, claims, qualifications, and continuing accuracy.
3. Correct, withdraw, or mark stale material before reuse.
4. Record review outcome and next expiry or review date.
