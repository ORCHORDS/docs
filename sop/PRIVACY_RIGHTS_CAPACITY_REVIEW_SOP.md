# Privacy Rights Capacity Review SOP

1. Review request volume, backlog, timeliness, error trends, ownership, and dependencies.
2. Compare capacity with expected demand and obligations.
3. Assign process, staffing, tooling, or escalation improvements.
4. Record results using privacy-minimized data.
