# Tax Position Expiry Review SOP

1. Review the material tax position, governing facts, assumptions, and prior analysis date.
2. Identify changes in law, transactions, or business circumstances.
3. Require refreshed specialist review when historical analysis is no longer current.
4. Record the decision privately where tax-sensitive.
