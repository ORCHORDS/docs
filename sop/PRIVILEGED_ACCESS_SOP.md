# SOP: Privileged Access

## Trigger
Use for new, changed, temporary, or emergency privileged access.

## Procedure
1. Confirm identity, business need, scope, and duration.
2. Select minimum necessary privilege.
3. Obtain approval appropriate to impact.
4. Require strong authentication and named accountability.
5. Prefer temporary elevation where practical.
6. Record the grant and expiry/review point.
7. Verify access without expanding scope unnecessarily.
8. Remove privilege when purpose ends.
9. Review emergency use retrospectively.

## Evidence
Record requester, approver, scope, dates, and removal or renewal decision without storing credentials.
