# Project Assumption Recertification SOP

1. Review material project assumptions against current scope, dependencies, resources, and timing.
2. Identify assumptions that are invalid, uncertain, or unsupported.
3. Assess impact on milestones, risk, acceptance, and benefits.
4. Assign actions and record the revised assumption state.
