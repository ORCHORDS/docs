# Complaint Action Closure SOP

1. Confirm complaint-related actions and promised follow-up are complete.
2. Review evidence of resolution, communication, and systemic corrective action where applicable.
3. Verify no material open commitment was lost in handoff.
4. Close using privacy-protective records.
