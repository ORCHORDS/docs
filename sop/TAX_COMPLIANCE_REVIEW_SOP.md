# Tax Compliance Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Confirm the applicable compliance calendar and accountable owner.
2. Verify required source data, reconciliations, approvals, and review status.
3. Identify overdue, incomplete, or uncertain items and escalate according to risk.
4. Confirm filing or payment completion through authorized evidence.
5. Record exceptions and follow-up without publishing tax identifiers, amounts, or returns.
