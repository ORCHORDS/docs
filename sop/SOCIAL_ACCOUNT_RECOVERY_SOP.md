# SOP: Social Account Recovery

## Trigger
Use for suspected takeover, lost recovery access, unauthorized posts, or compromised administrator access on an official communication account.

## Procedure
1. Preserve evidence of suspicious access/content.
2. Use the approved recovery path to regain control.
3. Revoke unknown sessions, tokens, or delegated access.
4. Reset authentication/recovery methods and review administrators.
5. Assess whether other company accounts or credentials are affected.
6. Remove or correct unauthorized content while preserving evidence.
7. Escalate to incident management when impact warrants it.
8. Publish a correction or notice if users were materially misled.
9. Review root cause and recovery resilience.
