# Marketing Incident SOP

1. Contain the affected campaign, content, audience, or account where necessary.
2. Establish facts and affected stakeholders.
3. Escalate privacy, security, legal, customer, or brand impact.
4. Correct or retract inaccurate material promptly.
5. Record root cause, actions, and lessons learned.
