# SOP: Launch Readiness

## Trigger
Use before broad launch of a material capability.

## Procedure
1. Confirm intended outcome and acceptance criteria.
2. Review test and quality evidence.
3. Review security, privacy, accessibility, AI, supplier, and legal impact as applicable.
4. Verify operations, monitoring, support, documentation, and capacity readiness.
5. Confirm migration, rollback/disable, and incident paths.
6. Record known limitations and residual risks.
7. Obtain accountable launch decision.
8. Define post-launch monitoring and review period.
