# SOP: Access Provisioning

## Trigger

Use when granting or materially changing access to company-managed systems or
information.

## Procedure

1. Identify the person or service identity and accountable requester.
2. Confirm business need and intended duration.
3. Select the least privilege sufficient for the task.
4. Obtain approval appropriate to sensitivity and privilege.
5. Provision access using a named identity where possible.
6. Require strong authentication appropriate to risk.
7. Record the grant in the authoritative access record.
8. Verify access works without granting unnecessary permissions.
9. Define review or expiry for temporary/high-risk access.

See [Access Management](../operations/ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md) and
[Access Review SOP](./ACCESS_REVIEW_SOP.md).
