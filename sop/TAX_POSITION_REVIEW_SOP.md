# Tax Position Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Define the material tax position and governing facts.
2. Gather current legal, accounting, transaction, and advisor inputs as applicable.
3. Document assumptions, uncertainty, decision authority, and required controls.
4. Identify review triggers such as law, fact, or business changes.
5. Retain confidential analysis in authorized systems rather than this repository.
