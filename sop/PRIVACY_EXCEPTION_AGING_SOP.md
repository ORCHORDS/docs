# Privacy Exception Aging SOP

1. Review privacy exceptions by owner, age, affected processing, risk, and target resolution.
2. Confirm compensating measures remain appropriate.
3. Escalate expired or repeatedly extended exceptions.
4. Record renewal, remediation, or closure.
