# SOP: Threat Intelligence Review

## Trigger
Use for material threat advisories, exploited-vulnerability intelligence, incident-derived intelligence, or scheduled threat review.

## Procedure
1. Record source, publication time, and confidence.
2. Identify affected technology, behavior, or business exposure.
3. Compare intelligence with known assets and dependencies.
4. Assess exploitation evidence, relevance, and potential impact.
5. Choose actions: monitor, hunt, patch, harden, notify owners, update threat models, or escalate incident investigation.
6. Protect restricted sharing information.
7. Record action owners and review date.
8. Close when relevance is resolved or action is verified.
