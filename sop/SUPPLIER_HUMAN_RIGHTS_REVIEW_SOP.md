# Supplier Human Rights Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Determine supplier criticality and relevant human-rights exposure.
2. Review current due-diligence evidence and its age, scope, and reliability.
3. Identify gaps, incidents, geographic risks, and subcontractor dependencies.
4. Define remediation, enhanced diligence, monitoring, or exit considerations.
5. Record the decision, owner, and next review date.
