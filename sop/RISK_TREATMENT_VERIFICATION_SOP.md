# Risk Treatment Verification SOP

1. Identify the approved risk and planned treatment.
2. Verify implementation evidence.
3. Reassess likelihood, impact, dependencies, and residual risk.
4. Confirm no material risk was merely transferred without review.
5. Close, reopen, or escalate with rationale and evidence.
