# Customer Transition Exception Aging SOP

1. Review onboarding, handoff, renewal, and offboarding exceptions by customer impact, owner, and age.
2. Confirm open commitments and dependencies.
3. Escalate overdue transition gaps.
4. Record closure or renewed treatment without exposing customer-confidential data.
