# Portfolio Assumption Recertification SOP

1. Review portfolio assumptions about capacity, demand, timing, value, dependencies, and risk.
2. Compare assumptions with current evidence.
3. Identify impact on prioritization and allocation.
4. Record revised assumptions and decisions.
