# SOP: Third-Party Due Diligence

## Trigger

Use before a material supplier relationship and when significant supplier risk changes.

## Procedure

1. Define service scope, business owner, criticality, data, access, integration, and failure impact.
2. Determine assessment depth based on criticality.
3. Review supplier identity, ownership/control concerns where relevant, provenance, resilience, foundational security practices, and important supply-chain tiers.
4. Review privacy, data use, subcontracting, incident-notification, continuity, access, and exit considerations.
5. Validate available public or contractually supplied evidence.
6. Record gaps, compensating controls, contractual conditions, and residual risk.
7. Obtain required business, security, privacy, legal/commercial, or executive approval according to risk.
8. Define reassessment triggers and an exit plan.
9. Store the decision record in the approved system of record.

## Reference

Assessment dimensions are informed by NIST SP 1326.
