# Partner Due Diligence SOP

1. Classify the proposed partnership and intended activities.
2. Review identity, reputation, ethics, conflicts, capability, security/privacy, and financial or operational resilience as proportionate.
3. Record gaps and required conditions.
4. Obtain accountable decision approval.
5. Retain evidence and refresh triggers.
