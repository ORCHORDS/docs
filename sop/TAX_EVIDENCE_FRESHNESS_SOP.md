# Tax Evidence Freshness SOP

1. Identify evidence supporting material tax decisions or compliance.
2. Verify source, date, scope, owner, and limitations.
3. Refresh or qualify stale evidence before current reliance.
4. Retain tax-sensitive evidence outside the public repository.
