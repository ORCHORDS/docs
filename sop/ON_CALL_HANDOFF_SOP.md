# SOP: On-Call Handoff

## Trigger
Use at duty-period transition when material unresolved operational or security work exists.

## Procedure
1. Confirm incoming responder and covered scope.
2. Summarize active incidents and current severity.
3. Transfer important timelines, decisions, mitigations, and unknowns.
4. Identify noisy or suppressed alerts and temporary controls.
5. Highlight pending supplier, release, or recovery actions.
6. Confirm required access and escalation contacts are available.
7. Record ownership transfer in the incident or duty record.
8. Verify the incoming responder acknowledges critical items.

## Principle
A handoff should transfer decision context, not merely a list of ticket numbers.
