# Supplier Selection Exception Aging SOP

1. Review active supplier-selection exceptions for owner, age, rationale, risk, and evidence.
2. Escalate overdue or recurring bypasses.
3. Determine renewal, sourcing remediation, or closure.
4. Record the outcome.
