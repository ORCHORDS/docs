# Transaction Due Diligence SOP

## Procedure
1. Confirm transaction sponsor, strategic rationale, scope, and confidentiality boundary.
2. Assign diligence owners across relevant financial, legal, security, privacy, technology, people, supplier, IP, and commercial domains.
3. Record material findings, evidence limitations, dependencies, and decision impact.
4. Escalate unresolved critical findings before approval or closing.
5. Preserve deal-sensitive details only in authorized systems.
