# SOP: AI Change Review

## Trigger
Use for material model, prompt, retrieval, data, policy, tool, permission, or provider changes.

## Procedure
1. Describe change and owner.
2. Identify prior evaluation assumptions affected.
3. Reassess security, privacy, reliability, bias, misuse, and human-oversight risk.
4. Define targeted regression and adversarial tests.
5. Review data and supplier changes.
6. Set rollout, monitoring, and rollback/disable criteria.
7. Obtain approval proportional to impact.
8. Monitor after deployment and record outcome.

## Completion
Evidence supports the changed behavior for its approved use or the change is rolled back/disabled.
