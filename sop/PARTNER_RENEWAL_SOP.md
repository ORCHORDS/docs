# Partner Renewal Review SOP

1. Review original value hypothesis and current outcomes.
2. Reassess risk, unresolved commitments, incidents, dependency, and customer impact.
3. Confirm future objectives and resource needs.
4. Compare continuation with change or exit alternatives.
5. Record renewal, renegotiation, reduction, or exit decision.
