# SOP: Media Sanitization

## Trigger
Use before reuse, transfer, repair return, resale, or disposal of media containing company information when sanitization is required.

## Procedure
1. Identify media or storage scope and data sensitivity.
2. Determine destination and required assurance.
3. Select an approved sanitization method appropriate to media type.
4. Perform sanitization using controlled tooling/process.
5. Validate outcome proportionally to sensitivity and method.
6. Record media identity, method, date, operator, and validation without copying sensitive contents.
7. Escalate failed sanitization for destruction, alternative treatment, or risk decision.

## Guidance
Method selection is informed by NIST SP 800-88 Rev.2.
