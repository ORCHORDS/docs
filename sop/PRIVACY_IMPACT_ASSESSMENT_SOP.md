# SOP: Privacy Impact Assessment

## Trigger

Use when a material change may create new privacy risk or when
[Privacy Impact Assessment](../privacy/PRIVACY_IMPACT_ASSESSMENT.md) identifies
a review trigger.

## Procedure

1. State the purpose of processing.
2. Identify affected people and data categories.
3. Describe collection, use, sharing, retention, and deletion at an appropriate
   level.
4. Assess necessity and proportionality.
5. Identify risks to people, including misuse beyond security breach.
6. Document security, privacy, and governance controls.
7. Assess residual risk and unresolved legal or contractual questions.
8. Obtain the required owner decision before launch or continuation.
9. Record review triggers for future change.
