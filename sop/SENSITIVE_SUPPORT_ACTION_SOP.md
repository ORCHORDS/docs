# SOP: Sensitive Support Action

1. Classify the requested action and potential impact.
2. Perform the required identity verification using approved methods.
3. Confirm support tooling privilege is sufficient but not broader than needed.
4. Record reason and evidence; obtain secondary review when policy/risk requires it.
5. Perform only the approved action.
6. Notify/escalate unusual indicators, attempted bypass, or suspected compromise.
7. Preserve the minimum necessary audit record.
