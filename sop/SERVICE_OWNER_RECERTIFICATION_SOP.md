# Service Owner Recertification SOP

1. Review material services for current accountable ownership.
2. Confirm responsibilities for risk, incidents, changes, continuity, documentation, and dependencies.
3. Resolve ownership or coverage gaps.
4. Record the result without publishing private service topology.
