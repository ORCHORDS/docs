# SOP: Project Handover and Closure

## Procedure

1. Confirm acceptance criteria and receiving owner.
2. Transfer operating/support documentation and escalation paths.
3. Record known limitations, residual risks, open actions, dependencies, and monitoring needs.
4. Confirm required access, recovery, and maintenance responsibilities.
5. Measure outcome against original objectives where possible.
6. Capture lessons and unresolved benefit measurements.
7. Close temporary project access and records according to policy.
