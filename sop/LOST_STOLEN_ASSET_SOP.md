# SOP: Lost or Stolen Asset

## Trigger
Use when a company-controlled device, media item, credential, document, or other sensitive asset is lost, stolen, or unaccounted for.

## Procedure
1. Record asset and last known custody/location.
2. Identify data sensitivity and access capability.
3. Revoke or disable credentials/access where applicable.
4. Use approved remote lock/wipe or containment where justified.
5. Assess whether incident, privacy, insurance, or legal escalation is required.
6. Preserve available evidence and police/report identifiers where applicable.
7. Update inventory and replacement actions.
8. Close after access risk and record obligations are resolved.
