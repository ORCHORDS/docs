# SOP: Research Participant Consent

## Procedure

1. Identify what the participant needs to understand before participation.
2. Explain purpose, activity, recording, data use, retention, incentives, and material risks.
3. Explain voluntary participation and applicable withdrawal limits.
4. Confirm consent before the relevant collection begins.
5. Record the consent version/date using an authorized system.
6. Respect later withdrawal or preference changes where applicable.
