# SOP: Patch Management

## Trigger
Use for security updates, unsupported-version remediation, or material vulnerability fixes.

## Procedure
1. Identify affected asset, owner, and exposure.
2. Review exploitability, impact, fix availability, and change risk.
3. Select patch, upgrade, mitigation, isolation, or replacement path.
4. Test proportionally where safe and practical.
5. Define rollback or containment.
6. Deploy according to urgency and change controls.
7. Verify version, health, and security outcome.
8. Track deferrals with owner, rationale, controls, and review date.
9. Retest material findings when applicable.

## Completion
Evidence confirms the affected scope is remediated, mitigated, or explicitly risk-accepted.
