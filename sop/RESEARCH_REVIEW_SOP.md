# SOP: Research Review

## Procedure

1. Define research question and intended decision use.
2. Review method, recruitment, participants, incentives, and known sampling limitations.
3. Identify data collected, consent, recording, privacy, retention, and risk needs.
4. Confirm proportionality and required approvals.
5. Conduct research using the approved plan.
6. Report method, evidence, limitations, and interpretation separately.
7. Dispose of or retain research data according to the approved plan.
