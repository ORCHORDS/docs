# AI Oversight Capacity Review SOP

1. Review human-review workload, competence, coverage, escalation, and unresolved queues.
2. Compare actual capacity with oversight commitments and risk.
3. Assign improvements or reduce scope where coverage is inadequate.
4. Record the decision.
