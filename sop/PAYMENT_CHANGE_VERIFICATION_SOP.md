# SOP: Payment Change Verification

## Trigger
Use for a new or changed beneficiary, banking, payout, wallet, or other payment destination when the change is material.

## Procedure
1. Record requester and requested change without copying unnecessary secrets.
2. Independently verify identity and change using a trusted pre-established channel.
3. Compare against known supplier/customer records and contract context.
4. Escalate unusual urgency, secrecy, new channels, or inconsistent details.
5. Obtain approval proportional to financial impact.
6. Update the authoritative payment record.
7. For high-risk changes, consider a secondary review before first payment.
8. Preserve verification evidence and date.
