# SOP: Pilot Review

## Procedure

1. Define pilot objective, owner, scope, participants, duration, and success criteria.
2. Identify data, access, security, privacy, legal, support, and operational implications.
3. Define boundaries, rollback/exit criteria, and data/access cleanup.
4. Obtain required approvals before launch.
5. Monitor agreed measures and material incidents.
6. Close the pilot with results, lessons, residual risks, and next decision.

## Completion

Temporary access, data, and configuration are removed or transferred into an approved ongoing arrangement.
