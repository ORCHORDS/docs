# Tax Exception Aging SOP

1. Review material tax-control exceptions by jurisdiction, owner, age, and risk.
2. Validate due dates, dependencies, and required specialist review.
3. Escalate overdue items.
4. Record remediation or renewal without exposing tax-sensitive details.
