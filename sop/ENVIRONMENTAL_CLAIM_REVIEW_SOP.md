# Environmental Claim Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Identify the proposed claim, audience, reporting boundary, and intended publication period.
2. Collect current methodology, source data, assumptions, and approvals.
3. Verify that estimates, supplier-derived inputs, and directly measured evidence are distinguished.
4. Confirm the wording does not overstate scope, certainty, certification, or performance.
5. Record reviewer decisions, required qualifications, and expiry or review date.
6. Approve, revise, or reject the claim and retain the review record.
