# SOP: Commercial Forecast Review

## Procedure

1. Review material opportunities and renewal assumptions against defined stage/confidence criteria.
2. Separate contracted value from forecast, upside, and unqualified potential.
3. Identify delivery, legal, product, security, procurement, timing, and customer-decision dependencies.
4. Challenge stale stages, double counting, unsupported close dates, and hidden renewal risk.
5. Record material assumption changes and forecast movement.
6. Escalate conflicts between target pressure and evidence rather than weakening the criteria.
