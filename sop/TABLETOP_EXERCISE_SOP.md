# SOP: Tabletop Exercise

## Trigger
Use for scheduled resilience testing, major process changes, or lessons from incidents.

## Procedure
1. Define objective and scenario.
2. Select participants and facilitator.
3. Record assumptions and success criteria.
4. Introduce realistic events and uncertainty.
5. Require participants to state decisions, information needs, and escalation paths.
6. Capture policy, coordination, tooling, and knowledge gaps.
7. Assign actions with owners.
8. Verify material follow-up before closure.

## Evidence
Preserve scenario, participants, major decisions, findings, and action status.
