# SOP: Gift and Hospitality Review

## Trigger
Use when a proposed gift, hospitality, travel, entertainment, discount, or similar benefit requires review because of value, timing, recipient, or decision context.

## Procedure
1. Identify giver/recipient, purpose, approximate value, timing, and related business decision.
2. Assess government/public-official involvement where relevant.
3. Assess procurement, negotiation, approval, or conflict context.
4. Determine whether the benefit is lawful, modest, transparent, and free of improper influence.
5. Approve, decline, return, reimburse, or impose conditions.
6. Record the decision where required.
