# SOP: Business Impact Analysis

## Trigger

Use for a new material capability, major dependency change, scheduled
resilience review, or after evidence that existing recovery assumptions are
wrong.

## Procedure

1. Define the activity and accountable owner.
2. Identify important users, obligations, information, and dependencies.
3. Describe credible disruption scenarios.
4. Assess impact as disruption duration increases.
5. Define minimum acceptable operating capability.
6. Propose recovery priorities, sequencing, and objectives where useful.
7. Record assumptions and single points of dependency.
8. Review with operations, security, privacy, and business owners as relevant.
9. Approve actions for material gaps.
10. Revisit after major change or recovery-test failure.

## Evidence

Use [BIA Template](../templates/BUSINESS_IMPACT_ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md).

## Completion

The assessment is complete when impact, priorities, assumptions, owners, and
follow-up actions are explicit and reviewable.
