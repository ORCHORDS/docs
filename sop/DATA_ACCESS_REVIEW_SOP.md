# SOP: Data Access Review

## Procedure

1. Identify the governed dataset/data class and authoritative owner.
2. Review identities/roles with access, purpose, privilege level, and downstream use.
3. Confirm access remains necessary and consistent with classification, privacy, contract, and retention rules.
4. Pay additional attention to bulk export, privileged analytics, cross-domain access, and dormant access.
5. Remove or reduce unnecessary privilege promptly.
6. Record exceptions separately with owner and expiry where necessary.
7. Preserve review evidence appropriate to the data sensitivity.
