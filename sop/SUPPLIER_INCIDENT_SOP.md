# SOP: Supplier Incident

## Trigger
Use when a supplier incident may affect company services, information, users, or obligations.

## Procedure
1. Identify supplier, relationship owner, affected capability/data, and known facts.
2. Preserve supplier notices and relevant internal evidence.
3. Assess independent company impact and containment options.
4. Invoke company incident management when thresholds are met.
5. Review contractual notification/cooperation rights.
6. Coordinate privacy, legal, security, resilience, and communications as applicable.
7. Track supplier remediation and evidence.
8. Reassess ongoing supplier, concentration, and exit risk.

## Closure
Record residual risk and any contractual or supplier-governance follow-up.
