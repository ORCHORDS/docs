# SOP: Pricing and Discount Review

1. Record requested pricing/discount and rationale.
2. Confirm scope, duration, renewal assumption, and non-price concessions.
3. Review approval authority and margin/delivery impact.
4. Identify precedent, operational burden, or bundled commitments.
5. Route legal/security/privacy/product/delivery/finance review when triggered by the full commitment.
6. Record decision, conditions, and expiry/review point.
