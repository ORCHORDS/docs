# SOP: Customer Assurance Response

## Procedure

1. Classify the request by security, privacy, compliance, resilience, accessibility, or operations topic.
2. Reuse an approved current answer where available.
3. Validate factual claims against current public-safe evidence.
4. Escalate requests for sensitive evidence or unsupported assurance.
5. Mark limitations, scope, and dates clearly.
6. Obtain functional review for material claims.
7. Record the approved response and source review date.

Never convert policy intent into an implementation claim without evidence.
