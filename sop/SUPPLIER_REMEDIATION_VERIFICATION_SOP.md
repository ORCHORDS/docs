# Supplier Remediation Verification SOP

1. Identify the supplier issue and agreed remediation.
2. Obtain evidence proportionate to materiality.
3. Validate scope, completion, and remaining exposure.
4. Confirm whether compensating measures can be retired.
5. Close, extend, or escalate with evidence.
