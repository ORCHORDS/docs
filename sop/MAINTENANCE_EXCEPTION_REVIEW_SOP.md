# Maintenance Exception Review SOP

1. Review deferred or nonstandard maintenance decision, owner, rationale, and risk.
2. Reassess dependencies and target resolution.
3. Escalate repeated deferral or unacceptable exposure.
4. Record approval, remediation, or closure.
