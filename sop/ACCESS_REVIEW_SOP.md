# SOP: Access Review

## Trigger

Perform on the cadence defined for sensitive systems and after major
organizational or role changes.

## Procedure

1. Export or enumerate current identities and privileges from the authoritative
   source.
2. Identify privileged, dormant, shared, service, and external identities.
3. Map each privilege to a current owner and business need.
4. Remove access with no confirmed need.
5. Reduce excessive privilege.
6. Check service identities for owner, purpose, scope, credential lifecycle,
   and last use.
7. Confirm departed or transferred personnel no longer retain obsolete access.
8. Escalate unresolved privileged access to Security Lead.
9. Record reviewer, date, removals, exceptions, and follow-up.

## Evidence

Keep the review record and resulting remediation actions. Avoid copying
sensitive credential material into the record.
