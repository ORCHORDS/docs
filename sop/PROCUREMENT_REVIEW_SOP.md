# SOP: Procurement Review

## Trigger
Use before a material purchase or supplier commitment.

## Procedure
1. Confirm business need, owner, and budget authority.
2. Identify supplier, alternatives, cost/commitment category, and renewal model.
3. Assess supplier/data/access/AI/security/privacy risk proportionally.
4. Check conflicts of interest.
5. Review required contract and payment terms.
6. Obtain financial, procurement, specialist, and signatory approvals as applicable.
7. Record purchase/contract and key renewal/termination dates.
8. Ensure supplier onboarding requirements are completed before sensitive access/integration.
