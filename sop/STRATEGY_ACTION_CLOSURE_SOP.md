# Strategy Action Closure SOP

1. Confirm the strategic action, originating decision, owner, and expected outcome.
2. Review completion evidence and changed assumptions.
3. Determine whether the intended decision outcome was achieved or requires reassessment.
4. Record closure and remaining strategic follow-up.
