# SOP: Defect Escape Review

1. Describe the escaped defect and actual impact.
2. Identify lifecycle points where detection/prevention was reasonably possible.
3. Review assumptions, coverage, data, review, monitoring, and contributing conditions.
4. Assess whether similar defects could exist elsewhere.
5. Define corrective action proportionate to the cause, not merely the symptom.
6. Verify effectiveness and feed systemic findings into CAPA when appropriate.
