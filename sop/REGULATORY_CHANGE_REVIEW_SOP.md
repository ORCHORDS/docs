# SOP: Regulatory Change Review

## Trigger
Use for a material new or changed law, regulation, contract requirement, or authoritative standard.

## Procedure
1. Record authoritative source and status.
2. Distinguish final obligations from proposals or drafts.
3. Determine applicability and effective date.
4. Identify affected policies, controls, products, suppliers, or records.
5. Assign accountable interpretation and implementation owners.
6. Define actions and evidence needed.
7. Track completion or approved non-applicability.
8. Update public claims/documentation only when facts support them.
