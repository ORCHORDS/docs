# AI Use Case Recertification SOP

1. Confirm use-case purpose, owner, lifecycle state, risk classification, data use, and human oversight.
2. Review provider, model, configuration, monitoring, and dependency changes.
3. Decide whether the use case remains approved, needs reassessment, or should be retired.
4. Record evidence and follow-up actions.
