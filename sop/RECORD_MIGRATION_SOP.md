# Record Migration SOP

1. Define authoritative source, target, scope, metadata, and owners.
2. Map records and retention requirements.
3. Migrate through a controlled process.
4. Reconcile completeness, integrity, readability, and exceptions.
5. Approve source retirement only after verification and recovery needs are met.
