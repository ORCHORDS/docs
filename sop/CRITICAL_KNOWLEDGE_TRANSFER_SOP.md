# SOP: Critical Knowledge Transfer

## Procedure

1. Identify critical responsibilities and knowledge at risk of loss.
2. Identify receiving owner or backup role.
3. Review authoritative documents, current decisions, risks, commitments, and recurring tasks.
4. Demonstrate tacit or operational knowledge where documents alone are insufficient.
5. Confirm access to authorized knowledge stores.
6. Record gaps and follow-up actions.
7. Validate transfer through questions, rehearsal, or supervised execution where appropriate.
