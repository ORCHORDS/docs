# Liquidity Assumption Review SOP

1. Review material liquidity assumptions, commitments, dependencies, and scenarios.
2. Compare them with current evidence and cash-flow behavior.
3. Update planning or risk decisions when assumptions change.
4. Keep balances and financing details private.
