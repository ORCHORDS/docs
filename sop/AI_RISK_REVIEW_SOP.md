# SOP: AI Risk Review

## Trigger

Use before material AI use, after a material model/provider/data change, or
after an AI incident.

## Procedure

1. Define intended use and prohibited use.
2. Identify affected users and decision impact.
3. Record model/service, data, permissions, and downstream automation at a
   public-safe or internal level as appropriate.
4. Assess security, privacy, safety, bias, reliability, misuse, and dependency
   risks.
5. Define human oversight and escalation.
6. Set evaluation criteria and representative tests.
7. Define monitoring, rollback, and provider-change handling.
8. Record residual risks and accountable approvals.
9. Reassess when a trigger invalidates prior evidence.
