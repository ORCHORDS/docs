# Governance Calendar Recertification SOP

1. Review scheduled governance reviews, attestations, policy cycles, audits, and risk activities.
2. Identify missing, duplicated, or obsolete events.
3. Confirm owners and timing.
4. Record calendar changes and dependencies.
