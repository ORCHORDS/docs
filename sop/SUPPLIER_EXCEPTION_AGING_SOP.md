# Supplier Exception Aging SOP

1. Review supplier-risk exceptions by criticality, owner, age, evidence, and treatment.
2. Reassess material service, incident, or dependency changes.
3. Escalate expired or repeated exceptions.
4. Record renewed acceptance, remediation, reassessment, or exit action.
