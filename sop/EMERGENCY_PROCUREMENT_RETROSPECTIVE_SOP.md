# Emergency Procurement Retrospective SOP

1. Review emergency purchase rationale, approvals, bypassed controls, and evidence.
2. Assess pricing, supplier, security, privacy, legal, and continuity implications as applicable.
3. Assign missing post-purchase controls.
4. Capture lessons and close actions.
