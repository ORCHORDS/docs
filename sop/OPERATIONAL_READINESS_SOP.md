# SOP: Operational Readiness Review

1. Confirm accountable owner and launch/change scope.
2. Review support/escalation, monitoring, capacity, backup/recovery, rollback, maintenance, runbooks, security/privacy, dependencies, alerts, and known limitations.
3. Confirm handover and documentation are usable by operators who did not build the change.
4. Record gaps, owners, and acceptance/mitigation decisions.
5. Reassess after material scope or risk changes before launch.
