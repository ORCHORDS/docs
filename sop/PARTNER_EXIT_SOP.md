# Partner Exit SOP

1. Define exit scope, owners, obligations, and timeline.
2. Identify affected customers, data, access, integrations, brand use, financial items, and joint communications.
3. Execute transition and revoke unnecessary access.
4. Verify return, deletion, transfer, or archival obligations.
5. Record closure evidence and unresolved residual items.
