# Record Retention and Disposition SOP

1. Identify the record class and approved retention basis.
2. Check legal holds, audits, investigations, disputes, and continuing business need.
3. Approve eligible disposition scope.
4. Execute the authorized disposition method.
5. Verify completion and retain disposition evidence.
