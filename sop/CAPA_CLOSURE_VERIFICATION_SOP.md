# CAPA Closure Verification SOP

1. Confirm corrective and preventive actions were completed as approved.
2. Review evidence against root cause and intended outcomes.
3. Evaluate effectiveness after an appropriate observation period where needed.
4. Close, extend, or reopen the CAPA with documented rationale.
