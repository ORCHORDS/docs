# Financial Obligation Verification SOP

1. Review material obligation source, owner, status, due date, and completion criteria.
2. Verify completion or identify outstanding, disputed, or superseded items.
3. Escalate missed obligations.
4. Record evidence without publishing amounts or counterparties.
