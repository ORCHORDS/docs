# Procurement Exception Closure SOP

1. Review the procurement exception, owner, rationale, risk, and temporary safeguards.
2. Confirm normal controls are restored or a new approved state replaces the exception.
3. Verify supplier, contract, finance, security, and privacy follow-up as applicable.
4. Record closure or renewed approval.
