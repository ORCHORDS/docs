# Reconciliation Exception Closure SOP

1. Confirm the reconciliation difference, owner, investigation, and resolution.
2. Review evidence supporting the correction or accepted explanation.
3. Verify recurring root causes have appropriate follow-up.
4. Record closure without publishing amounts, accounts, or transaction details.
