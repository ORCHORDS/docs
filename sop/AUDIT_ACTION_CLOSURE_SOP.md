# Audit Action Closure SOP

1. Review the finding, agreed action, owner, due date, and submitted evidence.
2. Confirm the evidence addresses the stated finding and scope.
3. Preserve audit independence when deciding whether closure is supported.
4. Record validation outcome, residual concerns, and follow-up if needed.
