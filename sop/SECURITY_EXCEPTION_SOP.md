# SOP: Security Exception

## Purpose

Allow a time-bounded deviation from a security requirement without silently
weakening policy.

## Required record

- requirement being excepted;
- reason normal compliance is not practical;
- scope;
- risk and affected data/users;
- compensating controls;
- owner;
- approver;
- start date;
- expiry date;
- remediation plan.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the request cannot be satisfied through a safer normal design.
2. Assess threat, exposure, blast radius, and business need.
3. Define compensating controls.
4. Set the shortest practical expiry.
5. Obtain approval from Security Lead and accountable business/technical owner.
6. Track remediation to remove the exception.
7. Review before expiry.
8. Close, renew with fresh justification, or escalate.

## Rules

- Exceptions do not become precedent automatically.
- Expired exceptions are non-compliant until renewed or remediated.
- Critical controls may be designated non-exceptionable.
