# Cryptography Exception Review SOP

1. Review exception scope, owner, rationale, duration, and risk.
2. Confirm compensating safeguards and current technical constraints.
3. Reassess after material change or expiry.
4. Approve, remediate, or close without exposing key or configuration details.
