# SOP: Customer Success Review

## Procedure

1. Review intended customer outcomes and current milestones.
2. Review adoption/health signals and their limitations.
3. Review open support, delivery, security, operational, or commercial issues.
4. Identify renewal/retention risks and accountable actions.
5. Update the success plan, owners, and dates.
6. Escalate material blockers to the appropriate function.

Use [Customer Success Plan Template](../templates/CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_PLAN_TEMPLATE.md).
