# SOP: Contract Review

## Trigger
Use before accepting a material new agreement, amendment, renewal, or non-standard terms.

## Procedure
1. Identify business owner, counterparty, purpose, and requested decision date.
2. Confirm authorized contracting entity and signatory path.
3. Review commercial and operational commitments.
4. Review security, privacy, data, supplier, intellectual-property, service-level, liability, termination, and audit terms as relevant.
5. Record non-standard risks and required specialist review.
6. Negotiate or explicitly accept material deviations.
7. Obtain authorized approval/signature.
8. Record key obligations, notice dates, renewals, and owner.
