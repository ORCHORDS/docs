# Sustainability Evidence Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Identify the decision or disclosure supported by the evidence.
2. Verify source, date, scope, methodology, owner, and limitations.
3. Check consistency across related evidence and public statements.
4. Mark stale, incomplete, or unsupported evidence for refresh or qualification.
5. Record review outcome and next review trigger.
