# Sustainability Target Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Confirm the target owner, baseline, scope, timeframe, and measurement method.
2. Reassess assumptions, dependencies, feasibility, and available evidence.
3. Review progress and material changes affecting the target.
4. Distinguish aspirational targets from evidenced outcomes.
5. Document changes, continued approval, or retirement of the target.
