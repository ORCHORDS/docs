# Human Rights Remedy Closure SOP

1. Confirm the remedy or corrective action and intended affected-person outcome.
2. Review protected evidence of completion and effectiveness.
3. Identify unresolved harm, recurrence risk, or follow-up needs.
4. Record closure or further action with appropriate confidentiality.
