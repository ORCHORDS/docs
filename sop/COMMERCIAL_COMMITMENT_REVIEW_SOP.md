# SOP: Commercial Commitment Review

## Trigger

Use before accepting a material or unusual customer commitment.

## Procedure

1. Record requested commitment and customer outcome.
2. Identify factual claims, assumptions, dependencies, exclusions, and dates.
3. Confirm accountable delivery owner.
4. Obtain required security, privacy, legal, finance, operations, product, or delivery review.
5. Identify unsupported or ambiguous language and correct it.
6. Record approval, conditions, residual risk, and expiry/review trigger.
7. Hand approved commitments to delivery using the agreed record.

## Evidence

Use [Commercial Commitment Template](../templates/COMMERCIAL_COMMITMENT_TEMPLATE.md) or equivalent controlled record.
