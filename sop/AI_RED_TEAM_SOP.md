# SOP: AI Red Team

## Trigger
Use before high-impact AI launch, after material AI changes, or when incidents reveal new abuse paths.

## Procedure
1. Define authorized scope and safety constraints.
2. Identify realistic harms, attacker goals, and integration trust boundaries.
3. Build scenarios covering misuse, instruction injection, data leakage, unsafe tools, privilege, harmful output, and weak oversight as relevant.
4. Execute tests without unnecessary impact on users or live systems.
5. Record reproducible evidence and severity.
6. Apply mitigations or explicit risk decisions.
7. Retest material findings.
8. Update evaluation, monitoring, and threat models.
