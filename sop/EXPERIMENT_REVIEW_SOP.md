# SOP: Experiment Review

## Trigger
Use before a material user-facing or operational experiment.

## Procedure
1. State hypothesis, owner, population, and decision metric.
2. Identify important guardrail metrics and user harms.
3. Review privacy, security, accessibility, and AI impact as relevant.
4. Define allocation, duration or stopping rule, rollback, and monitoring.
5. Verify required notices or consent where applicable.
6. Approve proportionally to risk.
7. Run without changing success criteria silently after results are known.
8. Record result, limitations, and decision.
