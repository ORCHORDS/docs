# Customer Success Action Closure SOP

1. Confirm the customer-success action, expected outcome, owner, and commitment context.
2. Review completion evidence and customer-impact status.
3. Transfer any remaining obligations before closure.
4. Record the outcome without exposing customer-confidential information.
