# Tax Calendar Recertification SOP

1. Review tax calendar obligations, jurisdictions, owners, dependencies, and due dates.
2. Remove superseded items and add new obligations.
3. Confirm escalation for uncertainty or missing ownership.
4. Record changes without publishing tax-sensitive details.
