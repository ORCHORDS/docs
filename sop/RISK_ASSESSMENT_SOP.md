# SOP: Risk Assessment

## Trigger

Use for material new risks, major changes, supplier decisions, AI use, incidents, exceptions, or periodic reassessment.

## Procedure

1. Define the objective and risk condition.
2. Identify affected people, data, services, obligations, and dependencies.
3. Describe plausible causes and consequences.
4. Record existing controls and evidence.
5. Assess likelihood and impact using the organization's approved scale.
6. Consider uncertainty, exposure, reversibility, concentration, and detection.
7. Choose treatment: avoid, reduce, transfer/share, or accept.
8. Define actions, owner, due date, residual risk, and reassessment trigger.
9. Escalate risks beyond the owner's authority.
10. Record approval and closure evidence.

## Evidence

Use [Risk Assessment Template](../templates/RISK_ASSESSMENT_TEMPLATE.md) or an equivalent governed record.

## Completion

A risk is not closed until treatment is complete or acceptance is explicit and the residual risk owner is recorded.
