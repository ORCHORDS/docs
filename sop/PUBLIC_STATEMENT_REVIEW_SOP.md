# SOP: Public Statement Review

## Trigger
Use before publishing a material external claim, incident statement, compliance/security claim, financial statement, legal position, or future commitment.

## Procedure
1. Record exact proposed wording and audience.
2. Identify factual/evidentiary sources.
3. Review scope, date, assumptions, and limitations.
4. Check security, privacy, legal, contractual, and confidentiality boundaries.
5. Obtain appropriate owner/specialist approvals.
6. Publish through an authorized channel.
7. Preserve final wording and publication time.
8. Correct material inaccuracies when discovered.
