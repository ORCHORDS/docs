# Launch Exception Aging SOP

1. Review launch-readiness exceptions for user impact, owner, age, mitigation, and closure criteria.
2. Reassess after material scope or dependency changes.
3. Escalate overdue exceptions before further reliance.
4. Record remediation or renewed acceptance.
