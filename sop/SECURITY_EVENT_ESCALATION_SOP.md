# SOP: Security Event Escalation

## Trigger
Use when monitoring, personnel, suppliers, or tools identify a potentially material security event.

## Procedure
1. Record time, source, scope, and confidence.
2. Preserve relevant evidence.
3. Assess privilege, sensitive data, persistence, exposure, and potential impact.
4. Apply safe immediate containment when warranted.
5. Escalate to incident management when material impact is plausible or uncertainty is high.
6. Assign investigation owner and communication path.
7. Record decisions and important unknowns.
8. Close as benign, tracked finding, or incident with evidence.

## Principle
Do not wait for perfect certainty before containing credible high-impact risk.
