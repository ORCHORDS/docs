# Stage Gate Governance

## Purpose

Use explicit decision points for material initiatives where continued investment or exposure requires renewed evidence.

## Gate questions

A gate SHOULD assess outcome evidence, unresolved risks, assumptions, budget/resource position, quality, security/privacy/compliance needs, dependency status, customer impact, operational readiness, and whether continuation remains justified.

Possible decisions include continue, continue with conditions, re-scope, pause, or stop.

A gate is a decision mechanism, not a ceremony; weak evidence should remain visible.
