# Strategy Review Cadence

## Purpose

Ensure strategy is reviewed often enough to learn without changing direction reactively.

## Review layers

Use periodic operating reviews for execution signals, scheduled strategic reviews for assumptions and allocation choices, and event-driven reviews after material shocks or evidence changes.

A review should compare outcomes with expectations, test key assumptions, identify emerging constraints, and decide whether to continue, adapt, stop, or investigate further.
