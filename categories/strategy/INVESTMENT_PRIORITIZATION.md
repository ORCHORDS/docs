# Investment Prioritization

## Purpose

Create a repeatable basis for allocating scarce time, money, and attention across material initiatives.

## Decision factors

Consider strategic fit, expected customer or operational value, evidence strength, cost, time to value, reversibility, risk, legal/compliance implications, capability dependencies, and opportunity cost.

High uncertainty should reduce confidence in projected return rather than being hidden inside precise-looking estimates.
