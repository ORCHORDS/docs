# Customer Health Data Governance

## Purpose

Use customer-health signals responsibly, accurately, and with appropriate privacy and access controls.

## Requirements

Health models SHOULD document inputs, intended decision use, important limitations, access permissions, retention, and how manual judgment can correct misleading signals.

Sensitive support, security, billing, or personal information should not be broadly replicated into customer-success tooling merely because it may improve a score.

Customer-health data remains subject to [Data Governance](../data/README.md) and [Privacy](../privacy/README.md).
