# Adoption and Customer Health Signals

## Purpose

Use multiple signals to understand whether customers are progressing toward intended outcomes.

## Signals

Signals may include milestone completion, use of agreed capabilities, support friction, unresolved defects, stakeholder engagement, outcome measures, service incidents, and explicit customer feedback.

Health scores should document inputs and limitations. A numerical score is a decision aid, not objective truth.

ISO 10004:2018 may inform customer-satisfaction monitoring and measurement.
