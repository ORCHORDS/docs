# Data Lifecycle Governance

## Purpose

Govern information from collection through use, sharing, retention, archival,
and deletion.

## Lifecycle principles

1. **Define purpose before collection.**
2. **Minimize** the data, precision, copies, and access required.
3. **Validate quality** where decisions depend on accuracy.
4. **Control use and sharing** according to purpose, classification, privacy,
   contract, and legal requirements.
5. **Retain deliberately**, not indefinitely by default.
6. **Delete or de-identify** when purpose and retention requirements end.
7. **Preserve when required** for valid legal hold, investigation, incident, or
   evidentiary need.

## Ownership

Material data collections have an accountable owner who understands purpose,
classification, authoritative source, permitted use, retention basis, and
downstream dependencies.

## Changes

New data uses, material integrations, or repurposing of existing information
trigger privacy, security, and risk review appropriate to impact.
