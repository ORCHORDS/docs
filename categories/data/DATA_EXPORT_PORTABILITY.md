# Data Export and Portability

## Purpose

Govern extraction or transfer of material data for user rights, migration, backup, interoperability, supplier exit, or business continuity.

## Requirements

Exports SHOULD preserve required integrity and context, apply authorization appropriate to sensitivity, minimize unnecessary data, protect transfer, and define retention or deletion of temporary export copies.

## Format

Where practical, choose documented and reasonably interoperable formats rather than unnecessary proprietary lock-in for data expected to be portable.

## Security

Large exports can concentrate sensitive information and therefore may require stronger approval, logging, temporary storage controls, and expiry.
