# Data Correction Governance

## Purpose

Correct material data errors without destroying traceability or propagating inconsistent values.

## Requirements

Corrections SHOULD identify authoritative source, affected records/systems, reason, approval where needed, downstream propagation, validation, and whether historical/audit evidence must be preserved.

For personal data, applicable privacy-rights and legal requirements also apply. Corrections must not be used to rewrite evidence of prior transactions or control activity improperly.
