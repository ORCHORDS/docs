# Data Inventory Governance

## Purpose

Maintain enough knowledge of material information holdings to support classification, privacy, retention, security, and lifecycle decisions.

## Record expectations

Material data holdings SHOULD identify accountable owner, purpose, broad data category, classification, authoritative source, retention basis, important recipients or processors, and lifecycle state.

## Scope

The public repository defines inventory requirements but does not publish the operational data inventory.

## Quality

Inventory should be reconciled with actual processing and storage where practical. Unknown, duplicate, or ownerless data stores require investigation.

## Change

New data categories, major sharing changes, new suppliers, or material AI use should trigger inventory review.
