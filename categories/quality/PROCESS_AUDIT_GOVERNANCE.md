# Process Audit Governance

## Purpose

Evaluate whether important operational processes are followed, effective, and producing intended evidence.

## Requirements

Process audits SHOULD define scope, criteria, evidence, sampling approach where used, findings, owner responses, and follow-up.

Process audits complement internal audit and control testing; they should not create duplicate assurance without a defined purpose.
