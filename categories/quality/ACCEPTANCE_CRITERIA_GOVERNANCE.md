# Acceptance Criteria Governance

## Purpose

Make material acceptance decisions repeatable and evidence-based.

## Requirements

Acceptance criteria SHOULD be defined before final approval where practical, reflect intended user/business outcomes and risk, and identify evidence needed to show the criteria were met.

Criteria should not be weakened after failure merely to make an item appear complete without documented approval of the changed expectation.
