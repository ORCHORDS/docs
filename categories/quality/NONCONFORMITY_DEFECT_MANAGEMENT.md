# Nonconformity and Defect Management

## Purpose

Provide a consistent way to manage work, products, services, controls, or records that do not meet defined requirements.

## Requirements

Material nonconformities SHOULD identify requirement, observed condition, impact, scope, owner, containment, disposition, corrective action where required, and verification.

## Disposition

Depending on context, disposition may include correction, rework, rollback, replacement, user communication, accepted deviation, withdrawal, or retirement.

## Trends

Repeated similar defects should be aggregated for root-cause or problem-management review rather than treated as unrelated tickets.

## Closure

A nonconformity is closed when the immediate disposition is complete and any required systemic action has a verified owner/outcome.
