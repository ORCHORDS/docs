# CAPA Effectiveness Review

## Purpose

Verify that corrective and preventive actions actually reduced the targeted cause or recurrence risk.

## Requirements

Material CAPA closure SHOULD define effectiveness evidence, review timing, owner, residual risk, and what would indicate recurrence.

Completing an action item is not automatically evidence that the underlying problem was corrected.
