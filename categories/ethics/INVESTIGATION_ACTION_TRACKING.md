# Investigation Action Tracking

## Purpose
Ensure corrective actions arising from ethics or conduct investigations are completed and verified.

## Requirements
- Material actions must have an accountable owner, target date, and completion criteria.
- Status must distinguish planned, in progress, completed, and verified actions.
- Overdue actions require escalation and updated rationale.
- Closure must verify that the intended corrective outcome was achieved where practicable.

## Evidence
Maintain action registers, status reviews, verification records, and approved closure decisions with restricted access as appropriate.
