# Conflicts of Interest

## Purpose

Ensure personal, financial, family, outside-work, supplier, investment, or other interests do not improperly influence company decisions.

## Requirements

Personnel SHOULD disclose actual, potential, or reasonably perceived conflicts to an authorized governance owner before participating in a material affected decision.

A disclosed conflict is not automatically misconduct. Treatment may include recusal, additional review, ownership transfer, divestment, limits on access, or a documented finding that no material conflict exists.

## Sensitive handling

Conflict reports may contain personal information and should be restricted to those who need it.

See [Conflict Disclosure SOP](../sop/CONFLICT_DISCLOSURE_SOP.md).
