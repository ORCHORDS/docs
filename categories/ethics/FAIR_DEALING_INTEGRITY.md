# Fair Dealing and Integrity

## Purpose

Set baseline expectations for honest dealing with users, suppliers, partners, personnel, competitors, authorities, and the public.

## Principles

Personnel SHOULD communicate material facts accurately, avoid deliberate deception, honor approved commitments, respect confidential information, and avoid manipulating records or processes to create a misleading result.

## Competition and market conduct

Do not obtain competitor or partner information through theft, misrepresentation, unauthorized access, or inducement to violate confidentiality obligations.

## Metrics and reports

Do not knowingly falsify security, financial, product, performance, audit, or operational evidence to achieve a target or approval.
