# Knowledge Freshness and Review

## Purpose

Prevent authoritative guidance from remaining discoverable after it becomes materially wrong.

## Requirements

Critical knowledge SHOULD have a named owner, review trigger/cadence, source context, and clear status. Change, incidents, repeated support friction, ownership transfer, or standard updates can trigger early review.

Superseded guidance should be removed, redirected, or clearly deprecated rather than left as a competing source of truth.
