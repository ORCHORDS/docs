# AI Red Teaming and Adversarial Evaluation

## Purpose

Use structured adversarial testing to identify material misuse, safety, security, privacy, and reliability failures before and after deployment.

## Scope

Testing SHOULD focus on realistic harms and integration-specific attack surfaces, including instruction injection, unsafe tool use, data exfiltration, privilege escalation, policy bypass, harmful content, misleading outputs, and weak human oversight.

## Rules

Red-team activity must be authorized, scoped, and designed to avoid unnecessary impact on users or live systems.

## Evidence

Record scenarios, assumptions, observed behavior, severity, mitigations, residual risk, and retest results.
