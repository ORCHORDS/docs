# Human Override Governance

## Purpose

Ensure designated humans can stop, correct, decline, or reverse AI-supported actions when consequence warrants intervention.

## Requirements

Material AI use SHOULD define who may override, what can be overridden, how escalation works, whether the action is reversible, and what evidence is retained.

Human review must be meaningful; requiring approval without sufficient context, time, or authority is not effective oversight.
