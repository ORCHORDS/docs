# Merger Potential Entry Review

## Purpose

Provide public-safe governance for assessing whether a proposed transaction could remove a meaningful potential entrant from a concentrated market.

## Regulatory context

The 2023 U.S. Merger Guidelines state that mergers can create competitive concern when they eliminate a potential entrant, including circumstances where likely future entry or the perceived possibility of entry exerts competitive pressure.

## Review guidance

- Identify credible evidence of planned, probable, or strategically plausible entry by either party.
- Review internal strategy, product-development, capacity, customer, and investment evidence relevant to future competition.
- Consider whether market participants currently respond to the possibility of entry.
- Distinguish speculative possibilities from evidence-supported entry scenarios.
- Evaluate how concentration and barriers to entry affect the significance of losing a potential entrant.
- Escalate material findings to qualified antitrust counsel before transaction approval or commitment.

## Evidence examples

Evidence may include board materials, strategy documents, product roadmaps, customer requests, entry studies, investment plans, capacity analysis, and contemporaneous market evidence.

## Verification

Confirm that the review considers both current facts and credible future competition and that conclusions are documented with supporting evidence rather than assumptions alone.
