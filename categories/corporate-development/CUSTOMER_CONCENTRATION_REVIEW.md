# Customer Concentration Review

Material transaction reviews should assess revenue and relationship concentration, renewal timing, customer-specific commitments, change-of-control provisions, dependency on individual accounts, and evidence supporting retention assumptions.

Customer identities and confidential commercial terms must not be published in this repository.
