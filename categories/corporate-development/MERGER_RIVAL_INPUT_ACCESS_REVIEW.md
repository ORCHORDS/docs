# Merger Rival Input Access Review

## Purpose

Provide public-safe governance for assessing whether a transaction could give the combined firm the ability or incentive to limit rivals’ access to important products, services, routes to market, or competitively sensitive information.

## Regulatory context

The 2023 U.S. Merger Guidelines state that a merger may create competitive concern when the merged firm can limit access to products or services rivals use to compete, including by weakening rivals, increasing access to sensitive information, or deterring investment.

## Review guidance

- Identify products, services, platforms, data, infrastructure, distribution channels, or other inputs that rivals materially depend on.
- Assess whether the combined firm could degrade access, raise costs, worsen terms, delay supply, or otherwise disadvantage rivals.
- Evaluate whether the transaction changes access to rivals’ competitively sensitive information.
- Consider alternative suppliers, switching costs, capacity constraints, technical dependencies, and realistic time to substitute.
- Review internal plans and incentives rather than relying only on stated transaction intent.
- Escalate material concerns to qualified antitrust counsel.

## Verification

Confirm that the review addresses both ability and incentives, uses current market evidence, and records credible alternatives and dependencies rather than assuming substitution is easy.
