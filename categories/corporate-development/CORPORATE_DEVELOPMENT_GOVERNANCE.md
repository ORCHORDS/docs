# Corporate Development Governance

Strategic transactions require documented sponsorship, decision rights, confidentiality, proportionate diligence, risk review, approval, and post-transaction accountability.

Transaction activity must remain aligned with enterprise strategy, legal obligations, financial controls, information security, privacy, people governance, and records requirements.
