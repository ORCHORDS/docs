# HSR Current Form Governance — 2026

## Purpose

Provide public-safe governance for determining which Hart-Scott-Rodino (HSR) notification form and instructions are current before preparing a U.S. premerger filing.

## Current official state

The Federal Trade Commission states that a federal district court vacated the HSR form that had taken effect on February 10, 2025. The U.S. Court of Appeals denied the FTC’s motion for a stay on March 19, 2026. The FTC is currently accepting filings using the form and instructions that were in place before February 10, 2025.

## Governance guidance

- Reconfirm the current FTC form and instructions immediately before filing preparation begins and again before submission.
- Do not rely on archived internal copies without validating them against the FTC Premerger Notification Program.
- Record the form version, instruction version, verification date, and source used for the filing package.
- Escalate material ambiguity to qualified antitrust counsel or the FTC Premerger Notification Office as appropriate.
- Keep confidential transaction facts outside public documentation.

## Verification

Confirm that the filing package uses the form and instructions currently accepted by the FTC on the intended submission date.
