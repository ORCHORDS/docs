# Definitive Agreement Readiness

Before definitive commitments, teams should confirm authorized terms, material diligence disposition, required approvals, financing or funding readiness, integration assumptions, conditions precedent, key representations, risk allocation, and record completeness.

Unresolved material issues should be visible to the approving authority.
