# Transaction Decision Record

The final transaction decision should record strategic rationale, alternatives, valuation basis, material diligence findings, key risks, assumptions, conditions, approvals, integration or separation expectations, and reasons for proceeding or declining.

Decision records should remain distinguishable from advocacy materials.
