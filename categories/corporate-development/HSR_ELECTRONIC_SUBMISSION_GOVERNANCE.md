# HSR Electronic Submission Governance

Source: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program/hsr-notification-forms-instructions-guidance

The FTC's current HSR guidance states that filings must be submitted electronically through Kiteworks and that the Premerger Notification Office no longer accepts hard-copy or DVD submissions.

## Governance

- Verify the current official submission mechanism immediately before filing.
- Restrict filing access to authorized deal/legal personnel.
- Keep credentials, uploaded filing packages, counterparties, and transaction details outside this public repository.
- Retain internal evidence of submission and receipt according to legal-record requirements.
- Do not treat successful electronic upload as proof that a filing was required, complete, accepted, or that a transaction was cleared.

## Public boundary

This document describes a public FTC process only. It does not state that ORCHORDS has a reportable transaction, has submitted an HSR filing, or has received agency clearance.
