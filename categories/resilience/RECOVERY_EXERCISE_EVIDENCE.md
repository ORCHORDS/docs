# Recovery Exercise Evidence

## Purpose

Define evidence expectations for business-continuity and recovery exercises.

## Requirements

Material exercises SHOULD record scenario, scope, roles, assumptions, objectives, observed results, dependencies, decisions, gaps, and corrective actions.

Exercise records should distinguish activities that were demonstrated from activities that were only discussed or assumed.

Lessons and unresolved gaps should have accountable owners and feed future resilience planning and review.
