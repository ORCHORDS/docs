# Emergency Operations

## Purpose

Define how normal process may be safely compressed during severe disruption without abandoning accountability.

## Principles

Emergency authority MUST be explicit, time-bounded, and limited to actions needed for containment, safety, continuity, or recovery.

Emergency changes should still record who acted, why, scope, important evidence, and what must be reviewed afterward.

## Control preservation

Do not disable security, privacy, or evidence controls merely because an event is urgent unless the tradeoff is understood and authorized. Where a control must be bypassed, use the narrowest practical exception and restore normal control promptly.

## Transition

When the emergency stabilizes, transition back to normal change, access, and release governance and reconcile temporary actions.
