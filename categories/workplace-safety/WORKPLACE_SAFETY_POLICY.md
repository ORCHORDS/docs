# Workplace Safety Policy

ORCHORDS manages workplace safety through hazard identification, proportionate risk controls, clear responsibilities, training, incident learning, emergency readiness, and periodic review.

People are expected to report unsafe conditions promptly and may escalate material concerns without retaliation.
