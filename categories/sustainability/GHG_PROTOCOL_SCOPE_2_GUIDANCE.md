# GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for accounting and reporting greenhouse gas emissions associated with purchased or acquired electricity, steam, heat, and cooling.

## Baseline

The GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance supplements the Corporate Standard and standardizes how organizations measure and report Scope 2 emissions.

## Governance guidance

- Document the energy sources, facilities, reporting boundaries, and data systems used for Scope 2 accounting.
- Apply the required location-based and, where applicable, market-based accounting methods consistently.
- Retain evidence supporting contractual instruments, supplier-specific factors, residual mix data, and other market-based inputs.
- Separate calculation assumptions from verified source data.
- Review changes in energy procurement, utility service, renewable instruments, and applicable emission factors each reporting period.
- Avoid claims that exceed what the underlying accounting method and evidence support.

## Verification

Confirm that reported Scope 2 totals use current data, documented methodology, and traceable contractual or factor evidence and that method-specific disclosures are not combined in a misleading way.
