# Sustainability Claims Integrity

Environmental and sustainability claims must be specific, supportable, current, appropriately scoped, and understandable to the intended audience.

## Minimum evidence

Claims should identify the subject, boundary, period, baseline where relevant, methodology, important exclusions, and whether information is measured, modeled, supplier-provided, or independently assessed.

Absolute terms must not be used when evidence supports only a narrower conclusion.
