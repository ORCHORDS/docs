# Waste and Circularity

Waste governance follows a prevention-first hierarchy where practical: avoid, reduce, reuse, repair, recover, recycle, then dispose responsibly.

Records should support material disposal decisions, specialized handling, vendor responsibility, and any diversion or recycling claims. Security and privacy requirements remain mandatory during reuse, transfer, or disposal.
