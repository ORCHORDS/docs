# EPA GHG Inventory Management Plan

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for documenting and maintaining a greenhouse gas inventory management plan.

## Baseline

EPA’s Center for Corporate Climate Leadership recommends an Inventory Management Plan (IMP) to formalize the procedures used to develop and maintain an organizational GHG inventory. EPA provides an IMP checklist and simplified form to support consistent inventory development.

## Governance guidance

- Define responsible roles for data collection, calculation, review, approval, and change control.
- Document organizational and operational boundaries, data sources, calculation methods, emission factors, and assumptions.
- Record procedures for estimating missing data and resolving data-quality issues.
- Maintain version history for methodology, factor, source-system, and boundary changes.
- Define quality checks and reconciliation procedures before public or management reporting.
- Retain evidence supporting material calculations and adjustments.

## Verification

Confirm that the IMP reflects current systems, boundaries, emission sources, responsible roles, and calculation methods, and that inventory outputs can be reproduced from retained evidence.
