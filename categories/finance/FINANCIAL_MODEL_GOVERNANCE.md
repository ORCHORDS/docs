# Financial Model Governance

## Purpose

Keep material forecasts, budgets, valuations, and decision models understandable, reviewable, and explicit about uncertainty.

## Requirements

Material models SHOULD identify owner, purpose, key assumptions, authoritative inputs, significant formulas or transformations, scenario sensitivity, review date, and known limitations.

Model outputs must not be presented as certain when they depend on uncertain assumptions.

Material changes to assumptions or structure should be attributable and subject to review proportional to decision impact.
