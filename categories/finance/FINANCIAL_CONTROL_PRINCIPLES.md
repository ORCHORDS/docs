# Financial Control Principles

## Purpose

Define baseline controls for company financial actions without publishing thresholds or account details.

## Principles

Material financial actions SHOULD use appropriate authorization, traceable records, separation between request and approval where practical, reconciliation, and independent verification for unusual changes.

No single communication channel should be treated as sufficient proof for an unexpected high-impact payment or beneficiary change.

## Access

Financial-system and banking access follows least privilege, strong authentication, privileged-role responsibilities, and prompt revocation after role changes.

## Records

Financial records must be accurate, timely, reviewable, and retained according to applicable obligations.
