# Financial Record Integrity

## Purpose

Maintain trustworthy financial records and traceability for material transactions and adjustments.

## Requirements

Material records SHOULD identify source, date, amount or financial effect, counterparty or category as appropriate, approver, and supporting evidence.

Adjustments must not erase historical evidence in a way that prevents reconstruction of material changes.

## Reconciliation

Periodic reconciliation should compare authoritative records with external statements, payment processors, bank records, invoices, or other independent evidence appropriate to the process.

## Access

Changes to sensitive financial records require access controls and auditability proportional to impact.
