# Asset Inventory Policy

## Purpose

Define inventory expectations for company-managed technology and material
information assets without publishing the inventory itself.

## Requirements

Material assets SHOULD have:

- unique or unambiguous identity;
- accountable owner;
- lifecycle state;
- business purpose;
- sensitivity or criticality where relevant;
- support or maintenance status;
- relationship to important dependencies where useful.

Unauthorized or unmanaged assets create risk because ownership, patching,
monitoring, and recovery may be unclear.

## Lifecycle

Inventory records should be created near acquisition or creation, updated after
material change, and retired when the asset is disposed of or no longer under
company control.

## Reconciliation

Periodic reconciliation should compare authoritative records with observed
assets or service usage where practical. Gaps require investigation rather than
automatic assumption that one source is correct.
