# Onboarding and Offboarding Policy

## Onboarding

Before access is granted:

1. confirm role and manager;
2. identify required systems and privilege level;
3. provide security and acceptable-use expectations;
4. grant only required access;
5. confirm ownership of assigned responsibilities;
6. record completion of required training.

## Role changes

When responsibilities change, review existing access before adding new access.
Remove obsolete privilege rather than only accumulating permissions.

## Offboarding

At departure or contract end:

- revoke interactive access promptly;
- revoke or rotate credentials controlled by the departing person;
- transfer ownership of repositories, documents, domains, services, and
  recovery methods;
- preserve required business records;
- remove unneeded group memberships and privileged roles;
- confirm completion with the accountable manager.

Sensitive or involuntary departures may require coordinated, immediate
revocation.
