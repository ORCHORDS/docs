# Capacity Management

## Purpose

Ensure material services have sufficient resources and scaling plans for expected demand and credible failure conditions.

## Expectations

Capacity planning SHOULD consider normal growth, peak demand, maintenance windows, dependency limits, recovery operation, abuse or denial-of-service scenarios, and lead time for expansion.

## Signals

Useful indicators may include saturation, queue growth, throttling, resource exhaustion, error rate, processing delay, and supplier quota consumption.

## Action

Capacity limits with material impact require an owner, threshold or forecast, and action plan. Repeated emergency expansion is evidence that planning or architecture needs review.

Capacity targets are operational objectives, not public service guarantees unless explicitly approved as such.
