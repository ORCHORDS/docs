# Capacity Threshold Governance

## Purpose

Turn capacity signals into planned action before they become service failures.

## Requirements

Material capacity measures SHOULD define meaningful thresholds or review triggers, owner, expected lead time for mitigation, and known scaling constraints.

Thresholds should reflect user impact and operational behavior rather than arbitrary percentages alone.

Repeated emergency scaling should trigger review of forecasting and architecture assumptions.
