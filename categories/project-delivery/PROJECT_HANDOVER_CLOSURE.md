# Project Handover and Closure

## Purpose

Close projects without abandoning ownership, support, evidence, residual risk, or lessons.

## Handover

Before operational transfer, confirm accountable owner, acceptance criteria, operating documentation, support/escalation path, known limitations, unresolved risks, monitoring needs, required access, and rollback or recovery considerations where applicable.

## Closure

Record outcome against objectives, unresolved actions, lessons, decisions about retained records, and any benefits that require later measurement.

See [Project Handover SOP](../sop/PROJECT_HANDOVER_SOP.md).
