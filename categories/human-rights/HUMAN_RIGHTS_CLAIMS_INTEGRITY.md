# Human Rights Claims Integrity

Human-rights and responsible-sourcing claims must be specific, current, evidence-based, and appropriately scoped.

Policies, commitments, assessments, supplier representations, and independently verified outcomes are different evidence types and must not be presented as interchangeable.
