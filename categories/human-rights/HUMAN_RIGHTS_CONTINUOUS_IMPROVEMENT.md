# Human Rights Continuous Improvement

Human-rights governance should improve based on grievance outcomes, incidents, supplier findings, stakeholder feedback, changing external evidence, regulatory developments, and effectiveness reviews.

Repeated ineffective controls should trigger root-cause analysis and escalation.
