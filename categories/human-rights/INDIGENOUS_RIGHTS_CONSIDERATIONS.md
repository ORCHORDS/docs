# Indigenous Rights Considerations

Where activities could materially affect Indigenous peoples or their rights, decision processes should identify applicable legal obligations, recognized rights, consultation expectations, cultural considerations, and credible impact evidence.

This document does not provide jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
