# High-Risk Geography Review

Geographic risk assessments may consider conflict, weak labor protections, trafficking prevalence, migrant-worker vulnerability, corruption, enforcement conditions, and constraints on civic freedoms.

Geographic risk is a screening input, not proof of misconduct by an individual supplier or worker.
