# Product Risk Review

## Purpose

Integrate security, privacy, accessibility, resilience, supplier, AI, and user-harm considerations into material product decisions.

## Triggers

A review SHOULD be considered for new high-impact capabilities, sensitive data use, material automation, externally exposed interfaces, major supplier dependencies, irreversible user actions, or significant changes in legal or contractual assumptions.

## Review

Define intended outcome, affected users, important failure modes, dependencies, safeguards, evidence needed before launch, and residual risks requiring acceptance.

## Proportionality

Small low-risk changes should not require heavyweight governance. Review depth should scale with impact and uncertainty.

## Ownership

Product owns the user/business decision while specialist leads own advice within their domains.
