# User Safety and Harm Risk

## Purpose

Consider foreseeable user harm beyond narrow technical failure.

## Review areas

Depending on capability, assess misuse, confusing or irreversible actions, financial harm, privacy exposure, discrimination, accessibility barriers, deceptive behavior, unsafe automation, abusive content, and risk to vulnerable users.

## Controls

Risk treatment may include clearer consent, confirmation, rate or action limits, human review, user controls, warnings, monitoring, support paths, or refusing unsafe functionality.

## Evidence

High-impact risk decisions should record expected benefit, identified harms, controls, residual risk, and accountable approval.
