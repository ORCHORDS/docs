# Cross-Border Data Transfer Governance

## Purpose

Require deliberate review when governed information is transferred, accessed, or supported across national or regional boundaries.

## Requirements

Material transfers SHOULD identify data categories, source and destination context, purpose, recipient or service role, applicable contractual safeguards, security expectations, retention, onward-transfer considerations, and any required legal review.

This public policy does not state that any specific transfer mechanism is universally sufficient; applicable requirements depend on the relevant jurisdictions and processing context.
