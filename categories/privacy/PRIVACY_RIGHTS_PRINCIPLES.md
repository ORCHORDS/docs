# Privacy Rights Principles

## Purpose

Define public principles for handling individual requests and rights relating
to personal information without claiming a universal legal right in every
jurisdiction.

## Principles

Where applicable, ORCHORDS processes requests to access, correct, delete,
restrict, object to, or receive information about personal data in a secure and
verifiable manner.

## Handling expectations

- Verify identity to a level proportionate to the request and risk.
- Collect only the information needed to process the request.
- Avoid revealing information about another person.
- Track deadlines and legal exceptions applicable to the request.
- Coordinate deletion or correction across relevant authoritative records and
  governed downstream processes where required.
- Record the decision and completion evidence.
- Escalate disputed or legally complex requests for appropriate review.

## Security

Request handling must not become an account-recovery bypass or source of
unnecessary personal-data exposure.
