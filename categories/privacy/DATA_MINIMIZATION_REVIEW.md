# Data Minimization Review

## Purpose

Reduce privacy, security, and operational risk by limiting collection and retention to information that is genuinely needed.

## Requirements

Material data flows SHOULD periodically review whether each collected field, event, derived attribute, attachment, or log remains necessary for the intended purpose.

Information that is unused, duplicated, overly granular, or retained by habit should be removed, reduced, aggregated, or shortened where practical.

Minimization decisions should consider downstream copies and exports, not only the original source.
