# Visitor Security

## Purpose

Manage visitor access without unnecessary exposure of people, information, or restricted areas.

## Requirements

Where applicable, visitors SHOULD have an identified host, access limited to the purpose of the visit, clear restrictions for sensitive areas, and temporary credentials or badges that expire or are returned.

Visitors must not receive unattended access to restricted information or systems unless explicitly authorized.

## Records

Visitor records should collect only information needed for security, safety, or legal purposes and follow retention/privacy requirements.

See [Visitor Access SOP](../sop/VISITOR_ACCESS_SOP.md).
