# Trial-to-Contract Governance

## Purpose

Prevent pilots, free trials, proofs of concept, or informal use from bypassing procurement and supplier controls when they become material dependencies.

## Requirements

Before a trial converts to ongoing material use, owners SHOULD complete required security, privacy, legal, financial, supplier-risk, data-handling, and exit reviews.

Trial data and credentials should be removed or migrated deliberately rather than becoming unmanaged production assets by default.
