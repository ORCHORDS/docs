# Procurement Conflict Governance

## Purpose

Prevent personal, financial, or relationship conflicts from distorting supplier evaluation and purchasing decisions.

## Requirements

Material procurement participants SHOULD disclose relevant conflicts before influencing selection, negotiation, approval, or renewal.

A disclosed conflict does not automatically prohibit participation, but mitigation may require recusal, independent review, additional competition, or documented approval.

Undisclosed benefits, side agreements, or preferential treatment are inconsistent with fair procurement governance.
