# Purchase Approval

## Purpose

Ensure material purchases are authorized and connected to a legitimate business need before financial commitment.

## Requirements

A purchase SHOULD identify requester, business purpose, supplier, cost/commitment category, budget owner, required reviewers, payment terms, and contract/order evidence.

Approval thresholds and account details remain private operational controls.

## Fraud prevention

Unusual supplier or payment changes follow [Payment Change Verification](../finance/PAYMENT_CHANGE_VERIFICATION.md). Approval must not rely solely on the same person who initiated a high-impact transaction when separation is practical.
