# Supplier Renewal Value Review

## Purpose

Require material supplier renewals to be evaluated as fresh decisions rather than automatic continuation.

## Requirements

Before renewal, owners SHOULD review actual usage, realized value, unresolved incidents or defects, control evidence, pricing changes, lock-in, alternatives, concentration risk, contractual obligations, and exit feasibility.

Unused capacity, duplicated services, weak adoption, or unresolved control gaps should trigger renegotiation, reduction, remediation, or exit consideration.

Renewal approval should record the decision basis and material conditions for the next term.
