# Supplier Exit Cost Planning

## Purpose

Make switching and termination costs visible before they become barriers to sound supplier decisions.

## Requirements

For material services, exit planning SHOULD consider data export, migration effort, replacement capability, contract termination terms, internal transition work, overlapping service periods, support continuity, credential/access removal, and retained records.

High exit cost is a risk factor that should influence selection and renewal decisions rather than being discovered only after termination is necessary.
