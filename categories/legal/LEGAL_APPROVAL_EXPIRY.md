# Legal Approval Expiry

## Purpose
Prevent historical legal approvals from being reused after their assumptions, scope, or governing circumstances have changed.

## Requirements
- Material approvals must record scope, assumptions, owner, and decision date.
- Time-sensitive approvals must define an expiry or review trigger.
- Material changes in facts, law, product, geography, or commitment require reassessment.
- Expired approvals must not be treated as current authorization.

## Evidence
Retain approval records, review triggers, renewals, superseding decisions, and closure evidence.
