# Legal Approval Records

## Purpose

Make material legal approvals attributable and reviewable without exposing privileged advice publicly.

## Requirements

A material approval record SHOULD identify subject, decision, approving authority, date, scope, conditions, expiry or review trigger where relevant, and reference to restricted supporting material when appropriate.

The public repository must not contain privileged analysis, confidential negotiations, personal information, or matter-specific legal strategy.

Conditions attached to approval should remain visible to the teams responsible for implementation.
