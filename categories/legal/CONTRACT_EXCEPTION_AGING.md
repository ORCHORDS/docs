# Contract Exception Aging

## Purpose
Ensure nonstandard contract positions and temporary exceptions remain visible, owned, and time-bounded.

## Requirements
- Material contract exceptions must record rationale, owner, risk, and review date.
- Temporary exceptions must include an expiry or renewal trigger.
- Aging exceptions require reassessment before continued reliance.
- Repeated exceptions should inform standard-term or process improvement.

## Evidence
Retain exception decisions, periodic reviews, renewals, and closure records without publishing confidential contract terms.
