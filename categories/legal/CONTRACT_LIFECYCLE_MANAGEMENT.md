# Contract Lifecycle Management

## Purpose

Govern contracts from intake and review through approval, performance, amendment, renewal, and termination.

## Requirements

Material contracts SHOULD have an accountable owner, approved version, effective and renewal dates, obligations that require operational follow-up, amendment history, and a defined exit or termination path.

Operational teams must be able to identify obligations that affect security, privacy, service levels, data handling, reporting, or delivery without relying on memory.

Superseded drafts and expired obligations should not remain authoritative by accident.
