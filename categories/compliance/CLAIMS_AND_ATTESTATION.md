# Claims and Attestation

## Purpose

Prevent unsupported public or contractual claims about security, privacy,
compliance, testing, certification, or operational capability.

## Rules

A material assurance claim MUST identify:

- exactly what is being claimed;
- scope;
- applicable period or date;
- evidence owner;
- supporting evidence;
- limitations or exclusions;
- approval authority.

Terms such as "certified", "compliant", "audited", "penetration tested", or
"fully implemented" must not be used unless they are accurate for the stated
scope and period.

## Framework references

Using a framework to design policy does not establish conformance. Mapping
controls to a standard does not establish implementation. Passing an automated
scan does not establish broad security assurance.

## Expiry

Time-sensitive claims must be reviewed before reuse. Expired evidence cannot
support a present-tense claim without revalidation.
