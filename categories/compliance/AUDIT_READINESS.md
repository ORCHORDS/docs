# Audit Readiness

## Purpose

Maintain a reviewable control environment without creating a last-minute
evidence exercise before an assessment.

## Expectations

Audit readiness SHOULD include:

- current policy ownership;
- mapped obligations and controls;
- current evidence for recurring controls;
- traceable exceptions and risk acceptances;
- closed-loop remediation records;
- clear explanations of system or process scope;
- accurate statements of what is and is not implemented.

## Reviewer interaction

Responses to assessors must be factual. Personnel must not invent evidence,
backdate records, or represent a planned control as operating.

When a request is ambiguous, clarify the control objective before producing
large evidence sets.

## Findings

Assessment findings should be tracked by severity and root cause, not merely by
document count. Repeated findings indicate a control-system problem and require
management attention.
