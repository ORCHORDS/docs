# Evidence Freshness and Validity

## Purpose

Prevent stale or out-of-scope evidence from supporting current assurance claims.

## Requirements

Evidence SHOULD identify the control/assertion supported, source, scope, period, collection date, owner, and material limitations. Reuse is appropriate only while the evidence remains relevant to the current system/process state.

Material changes, incidents, control redesign, expired attestations, or scope changes may invalidate older evidence before its scheduled refresh.

Evidence retention follows applicable records and legal-hold requirements.
