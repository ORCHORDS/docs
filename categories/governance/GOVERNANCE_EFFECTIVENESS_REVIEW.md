# Governance Effectiveness Review

Governance mechanisms must be reviewed for whether they produce timely, accountable, evidence-based decisions.

Reviews consider overdue decisions, unresolved actions, repeated escalations, unclear ownership, ineffective forums, and stale control assumptions.

Improvement actions require an owner, target date, and later effectiveness check.
