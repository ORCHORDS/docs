# Issue Escalation Governance

## Purpose

Ensure important unresolved issues reach the authority needed for timely decision.

## Escalation factors

Escalate based on user harm, security/privacy impact, financial or legal exposure, uncertainty, recurrence, blocked remediation, cross-team dependency, and proximity to a critical date.

## Principles

Escalation is not failure; it is a control when local authority or information is insufficient.

Escalations SHOULD state the decision needed, current facts, unknowns, options, recommendation, owner, and deadline or consequence of delay.

## Closure

The receiving authority should record a decision, request additional evidence, or reassign ownership rather than allowing indefinite unresolved escalation.
