# Control Owner Accountability

## Purpose

Ensure material controls have an accountable owner who understands the intended outcome, evidence, dependencies, and failure response.

## Requirements

A control owner SHOULD know what the control is meant to prevent/detect/enable, how effectiveness is evidenced, what systems or teams it depends on, what known exceptions exist, and how a failure is escalated.

Ownership is not proof of implementation. A named owner cannot substitute for current evidence that the control operates as intended.

Ownership changes require explicit transfer and update of authoritative records.
