# Risk Appetite and Tolerance

## Purpose

Define how leadership expresses boundaries for acceptable business, security, privacy, operational, supplier, and product risk.

## Principles

Risk appetite is directional; tolerance defines more specific boundaries or escalation triggers. Neither replaces case-specific risk assessment.

Higher potential harm to users, sensitive data, critical operations, or legal obligations requires stronger evidence and more senior acceptance.

## Use

Risk tolerance should guide prioritization, exception approval, launch decisions, supplier choices, recovery investment, and remediation timelines.

## Review

Tolerance statements should be revisited after material incidents, strategic changes, major new obligations, or evidence that current boundaries produce unacceptable outcomes.
