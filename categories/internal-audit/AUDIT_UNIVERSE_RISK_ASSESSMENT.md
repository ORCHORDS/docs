# Audit Universe and Risk Assessment

## Purpose

Keep internal-audit planning focused on material risk instead of mechanically rotating through areas at equal frequency.

## Approach

The audit universe SHOULD consider major processes, control domains, third parties, strategic initiatives, material systems/data classes, prior findings, incidents, change velocity, compliance obligations, and management concerns.

Risk assessment should consider impact, likelihood/uncertainty, control maturity, time since meaningful independent review, and significant change.

The universe is a planning aid and may be updated when new risks emerge.
