# Auditor Independence

## Purpose

Protect the credibility of internal audit conclusions by managing conflicts, self-review risk, undue influence, and access restrictions.

## Requirements

Auditors SHOULD disclose actual or perceived impairments to objectivity before or during an engagement.

A person should not be the sole independent auditor of a control they designed, operate, approve, or materially own unless the impairment is explicitly disclosed and compensating review is arranged.

## Access

Internal audit requires sufficient access to relevant records, personnel, and systems within the authorized scope. Unreasonable restrictions should be documented and escalated.

## Reporting

Significant disagreements about findings or scope should remain visible in the audit record rather than being resolved by silently weakening conclusions.
