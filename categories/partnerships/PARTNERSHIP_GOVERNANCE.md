# Partnership Governance Policy

Material partnerships require a defined strategic rationale, accountable owners, documented rights and responsibilities, measurable outcomes, risk review, and an explicit renewal or exit path.

Partnership status must not imply endorsement, exclusivity, capability, or commitment beyond approved evidence.
