# Support Governance

## Purpose

Provide safe, consistent, evidence-based customer support while protecting accounts, privacy, sensitive information, and internal systems.

## Principles

Support personnel SHOULD:

- verify identity before sensitive account actions;
- collect only information needed to diagnose or resolve the issue;
- avoid requesting credentials or reusable authentication secrets;
- record material actions and customer-visible commitments;
- escalate security, privacy, fraud, legal, accessibility, or safety issues to specialist processes;
- use support tools under least privilege;
- distinguish known facts from troubleshooting hypotheses.

## Quality

Support interactions are an important source of product defects, complaints, security reports, and user-harm signals and should feed quality/product governance where material.
