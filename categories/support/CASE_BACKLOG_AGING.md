# Support Case Backlog Aging

## Purpose
Identify customer-impacting delays and unmanaged risk in long-running support cases.

## Requirements
- Backlog aging must be segmented by risk or priority where meaningful.
- Long-running high-impact cases require clear ownership and escalation.
- Aging trends must be reviewed for staffing, process, product, or dependency causes.
- Closure quality must not be sacrificed solely to reduce age metrics.

## Evidence
Retain aggregate backlog reviews, escalation decisions, and corrective actions without publishing customer data.
