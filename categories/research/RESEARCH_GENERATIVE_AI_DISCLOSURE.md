# Generative AI Disclosure in Research

Source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf

NIST AI 600-1 recommends considering disclosure of generative-AI use to end users in relevant contexts, taking account of the purpose, context, audience, risk and frequency of disclosure.

## Governance

- Decide disclosure requirements during research design, not after publication.
- Make the disclosure proportionate to how AI influenced data collection, analysis, synthesis, moderation, or presentation.
- Avoid vague statements such as "AI assisted" when a clearer description is necessary to interpret the evidence.
- Do not use disclosure as a substitute for validation, human review, consent, privacy controls, or provenance records.
- Keep externally published statements consistent with the actual research method.

## Evidence

Research records should identify where GAI was used, its role, material limitations, and the disclosure decision. Public disclosure must not expose confidential prompts, personal data, credentials, or private system details.
