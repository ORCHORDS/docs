# Research Data Handling

## Purpose

Apply data classification, minimization, retention, and privacy principles to research records.

## Requirements

Collect only data needed for the research question. Separate participant identity from research material where practical. Limit access, protect recordings and notes according to sensitivity, define retention before collection, and remove data when its purpose expires.

Published findings should avoid re-identification risk and should not imply population-wide conclusions beyond the method and sample.
