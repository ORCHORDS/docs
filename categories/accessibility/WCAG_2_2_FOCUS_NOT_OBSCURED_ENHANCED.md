# WCAG 2.2 Focus Not Obscured (Enhanced)

Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#focus-not-obscured-enhanced

WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.4.12 is Level AAA. When a user-interface component receives keyboard focus, no part of the component is hidden by author-created content.

## Governance

- Treat this as a stronger target than Level AA 2.4.11.
- Review persistent headers, footers, overlays, drawers, banners, and floating controls at every supported responsive breakpoint.
- Verify focus visibility after scrolling and after dynamic content opens.
- Do not advertise AAA conformance unless the complete applicable conformance claim is evidenced; meeting one AAA criterion is not equivalent to WCAG AAA conformance.

## Evidence

Manual keyboard testing and visual evidence should demonstrate complete visibility of the focused component for the tested state.
