# WCAG 2.2 Focus Appearance

Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#focus-appearance

WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.4.13 is Level AAA. It defines minimum area and contrast characteristics for a visible keyboard focus indicator.

## Governance

- Design focus indicators deliberately; do not rely on a tiny or low-contrast visual change.
- Check focus indicators across light/dark surfaces, high-density controls, custom widgets, and state changes.
- Do not remove user-agent focus styling unless the replacement is at least as usable and tested.
- Include focus styling in component design-system acceptance criteria.

## Evidence

Where this AAA criterion is adopted as an internal target, retain measurements or repeatable test evidence for indicator area and the required contrast relationship. Meeting this criterion alone is not a WCAG AAA conformance claim.
