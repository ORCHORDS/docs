# Accessibility Issue Management

## Purpose

Prioritize and remediate accessibility barriers using user impact rather than cosmetic severity.

## Prioritization

Consider whether the issue blocks an essential task, affects keyboard or assistive-technology users, creates safety or legal risk, has a practical workaround, or occurs across a reusable component.

## Remediation

Material issues require an owner, corrective action, and verification. Component-level fixes should be preferred when they eliminate the same barrier across many experiences.

## Exceptions

If immediate remediation is not practical, document an accessible alternative or workaround when possible, residual impact, owner, and review date.

## Learning

Repeated accessibility defects should feed design-system, testing, and content guidance improvements.
