# Security Training Effectiveness

## Purpose

Evaluate whether awareness and role-based training changes behavior and decision quality rather than merely recording attendance.

## Requirements

Material training programs SHOULD define intended behaviors, audience, completion evidence, practical assessment or reinforcement where useful, recurring error patterns, and improvement actions.

Completion rate alone is not sufficient evidence that training is effective.
