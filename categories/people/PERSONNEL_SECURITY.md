# Personnel Security

## Purpose

Define public-safe personnel security principles across joining, role change,
and departure.

## Principles

- Access follows current duties and least privilege.
- Sensitive responsibilities may require additional review or separation of
  duties.
- Role changes trigger access reassessment.
- Departures trigger timely access revocation and return or transfer of
  company-controlled information and assets.
- Confidentiality and information-handling obligations survive role changes
  where applicable.
- Personnel investigations and records must be handled lawfully and with
  appropriate confidentiality.

## High-risk roles

Roles with privileged access, financial authority, key custody, sensitive
data access, or control over critical changes should have stronger approval and
review controls proportional to risk.

See [Access Management](../operations/ACCESS_MANAGEMENT.md).
