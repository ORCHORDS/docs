# Insider Risk Principles

## Purpose

Reduce harm from misuse, error, coercion, compromised accounts, or inappropriate privilege without creating a culture of indiscriminate surveillance.

## Principles

Controls SHOULD emphasize least privilege, segregation where useful, strong authentication, logging proportional to risk, reporting channels, timely lifecycle changes, and fair investigation processes.

Monitoring must respect privacy, employment obligations, and proportionality.
