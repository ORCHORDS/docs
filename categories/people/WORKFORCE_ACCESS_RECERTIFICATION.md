# Workforce Access Recertification

## Purpose
Confirm workforce access remains aligned with current roles and business need.

## Requirements
- Access reviews must use current role and employment or engagement status.
- Material role changes must trigger reassessment of relevant access.
- Reviewers must identify unnecessary, excessive, conflicting, or orphaned access.
- Findings must be assigned and tracked through closure.

## Evidence
Maintain dated review results, accountable reviewers, exceptions, and remediation evidence without publishing personnel or access details.
