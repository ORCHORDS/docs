# Security Awareness and Training

## Purpose

Ensure personnel understand the security and privacy responsibilities relevant
to their work.

## Program expectations

Training should be role-aware and cover, as applicable:

- phishing and social engineering;
- authentication and account recovery;
- information classification and sharing;
- incident and suspicious-activity reporting;
- secure collaboration and remote work;
- privacy and personal-data handling;
- software and engineering responsibilities for technical roles;
- supplier and third-party risk for purchasing roles;
- AI-use risks for personnel using AI-enabled services.

## Timing

Baseline training should occur near onboarding and recur at a risk-appropriate
interval. Material policy or threat changes may trigger targeted training.

## Effectiveness

Completion alone is not sufficient evidence of effectiveness. Useful measures
include scenario performance, reporting quality, recurring incident causes,
and remediation of observed knowledge gaps.

Training metrics must not encourage concealment of honest mistakes.
