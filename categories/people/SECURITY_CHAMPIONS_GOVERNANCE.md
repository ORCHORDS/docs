# Security Champions Governance

## Purpose

Define a lightweight distributed security-support role without transferring formal security accountability away from designated owners.

## Requirements

Champion programs SHOULD define role expectations, training, escalation paths, time commitment, scope boundaries, and feedback mechanisms.

Champions may help teams identify risk and improve practices, but they do not replace required specialist review or accountable control ownership.
