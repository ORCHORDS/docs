# Privileged Duty Rotation

## Purpose

Reduce concentrated knowledge and access risk in high-impact operational responsibilities.

## Requirements

Where practical, critical privileged duties SHOULD have trained backup coverage, periodically exercised handoff, and review of persistent exclusive access or knowledge.

Rotation must not create unnecessary privilege; access should remain role-justified and time-appropriate.
