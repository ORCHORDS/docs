# NIST CSF 2.0, ERM, and Workforce Management

## Purpose

Provide public guidance for linking cybersecurity outcomes, enterprise risk management, and workforce capability using NIST CSF 2.0 and the NICE Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity.

## Baseline

NIST provides a CSF 2.0 Quick Start Guide showing how CSF outcomes and NICE Framework concepts can be used together to address cybersecurity risk and workforce needs.

## Governance guidance

- Identify cybersecurity outcomes that depend on specific workforce knowledge, skills, roles, or capacity.
- Assign accountable owners for capability gaps that create material cybersecurity risk.
- Use role-based training and workforce planning instead of treating cybersecurity awareness as the only people control.
- Include contractors and other external personnel when their work affects cybersecurity outcomes.
- Reassess capability needs when technology, threats, operating models, or regulatory obligations change.
- Connect workforce planning to risk-treatment priorities and measurable outcomes.

## Evidence examples

Evidence may include role definitions, skills assessments, training records, staffing plans, succession plans, contractor requirements, and risk decisions tied to workforce constraints.

## Verification

Confirm that material cybersecurity outcomes have responsible roles, required capabilities are identified, gaps are tracked, and training or staffing claims are supported by current evidence.
