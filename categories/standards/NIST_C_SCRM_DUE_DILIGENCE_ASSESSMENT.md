# NIST C-SCRM Due Diligence Assessment

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for supplier due diligence using NIST SP 1326, the Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management Due Diligence Assessment Quick-Start Guide.

## Baseline

NIST describes due diligence as researching pertinent information about a supplier or product so informed acquisition and existing-system decisions can be made. SP 1326 is scoped to ICT suppliers and is intended to support C-SCRM programs operating with SP 800-161 Rev. 1.

## Governance guidance

- define the supplier, product, service, and decision being assessed;
- gather current information from authoritative and corroborated sources;
- assess the SP 1326 due-diligence components proportionate to the acquisition and risk;
- distinguish verified facts from unresolved questions and assumptions;
- document risk findings, decision owners, exceptions, and required follow-up;
- refresh due diligence when ownership, product provenance, security posture, supply-chain structure, or material risk changes.

## Verification

Confirm that supplier decisions are supported by traceable evidence, identified uncertainties are visible to decision-makers, and due diligence is not treated as a one-time questionnaire exercise.
