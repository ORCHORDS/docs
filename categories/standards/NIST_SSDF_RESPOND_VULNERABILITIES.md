# NIST SSDF — Respond to Vulnerabilities

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for the **Respond to Vulnerabilities (RV)** practice group in NIST SP 800-218 SSDF Version 1.1.

## Baseline

Respond to Vulnerabilities covers identifying, confirming, remediating, learning from, and communicating about vulnerabilities discovered in released software.

## Governance guidance

- maintain channels and processes for receiving vulnerability reports and relevant external intelligence;
- triage reported vulnerabilities according to severity, exposure, exploitability, and affected users;
- develop, verify, and distribute remediations through controlled release processes;
- communicate material security information to affected users or stakeholders when appropriate;
- analyze root causes and recurring defect patterns rather than treating every vulnerability as isolated;
- feed lessons learned back into design, coding, testing, dependency, and development-environment practices;
- retain evidence supporting material response and exception decisions.

## Verification

Confirm that reports are tracked to disposition, remediation is verified before release, affected versions are identified accurately, and root-cause findings produce measurable development-process improvements where warranted.
