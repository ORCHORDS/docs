# Proposal and Claim Accuracy

## Purpose

Ensure proposals, questionnaires, presentations, and other pre-contract statements are accurate, supportable, and appropriately qualified.

## Rules

Claims SHOULD be traceable to current evidence or clearly identified as target, estimate, planned work, or third-party dependency.

Avoid absolute security, availability, compliance, performance, accessibility, or delivery claims unless they are supported by current evidence and approved for that use.

Reusable claims should have an owner and review date because factual accuracy can decay over time.

## Conflicts

When a customer request conflicts with approved policy or known capability, escalate rather than silently weakening the policy or inventing a workaround.
