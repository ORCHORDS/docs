# Customer Assurance Responses

## Purpose

Govern responses to customer security, privacy, compliance, resilience, accessibility, and operational assurance questions.

## Requirements

Responses MUST distinguish policy requirement from verified implementation and must not claim certifications, audits, testing, controls, or service characteristics without current evidence.

Use approved public-safe evidence where possible. Sensitive evidence may require controlled sharing, redaction, confidentiality terms, or refusal.

Repeated questions should feed a maintained answer library with owner, source, and review date.

See [Customer Assurance Response SOP](../sop/CUSTOMER_ASSURANCE_RESPONSE_SOP.md).
