# Trial and Pilot Governance

## Purpose

Keep trials and pilots bounded, measurable, reversible, and explicit about what they do and do not prove.

## Requirements

A material pilot SHOULD define objective, scope, participants, data categories, duration, success criteria, owner, support expectations, security/privacy review needs, exit criteria, and disposition of pilot data or access.

Pilots must not bypass normal approval for sensitive data, privileged access, legal commitments, or production-impacting changes.

## Completion

At closure, record results, unresolved risks, follow-up decisions, and whether any pilot-specific access, data, or configuration must be removed.

See [Pilot Review SOP](../sop/PILOT_REVIEW_SOP.md).
