# Commercial Handover

## Purpose

Transfer approved customer commitments into accountable delivery and operations without losing assumptions or exceptions.

## Handover content

Material handover SHOULD identify agreed scope, dates, dependencies, service expectations, custom terms, data/security/privacy obligations, unresolved risks, owners, acceptance criteria, and customer communication commitments.

## Confirmation

Delivery owners should confirm that the handover is understood before execution begins. Ambiguous commitments are clarified before they become operational incidents.

Commercial acceptance does not override security, privacy, legal, or risk controls.
