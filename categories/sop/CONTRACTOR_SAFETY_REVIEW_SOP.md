# Contractor Safety Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Confirm contractor scope, sponsor, locations, and relevant hazards.
2. Review applicable safety expectations and required competence or evidence.
3. Identify interface risks between contractor and company activities.
4. Record exceptions and required mitigations before work proceeds where applicable.
5. Review incidents, changes, and completion evidence at an appropriate cadence.
