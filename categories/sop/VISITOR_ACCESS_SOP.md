# SOP: Visitor Access

## Trigger
Use when a visitor requires access to a company-controlled workspace or restricted area.

## Procedure
1. Identify host, visitor purpose, expected time, and access needed.
2. Verify identity proportionally to risk where required.
3. Issue temporary access limited to the visit purpose.
4. Communicate applicable restrictions.
5. Escort or supervise access to sensitive areas where required.
6. Prevent unattended access to restricted records or systems.
7. Recover or expire temporary credentials at departure.
8. Record security incidents or exceptions.

## Privacy
Collect only visitor information needed for security, safety, or legal purposes.
