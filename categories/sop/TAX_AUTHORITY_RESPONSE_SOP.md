# Tax Authority Response SOP

## Procedure
1. Validate the request, deadline, jurisdiction, and authorized response owner.
2. Preserve relevant records and coordinate legal or advisor support when needed.
3. Review response accuracy, scope, confidentiality, and approval authority.
4. Track commitments, follow-up requests, disputes, and deadlines.
5. Keep authority correspondence and tax-sensitive information outside the public repository.
