# SOP: Role Change

1. Confirm old role, new role, effective date, and accountable manager/owner.
2. Review existing access, privileged roles, approvals, data access, owned controls/services, and delegated authority.
3. Remove access no longer required before adding new privilege where practical.
4. Grant new access through the normal approval process.
5. Transfer control/service ownership and critical knowledge explicitly.
6. Review conflicts, training, and outstanding responsibilities.
7. Record completion evidence.
