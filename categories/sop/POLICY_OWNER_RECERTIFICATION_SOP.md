# Policy Owner Recertification SOP

1. Identify controlled policies due for ownership review.
2. Confirm the current accountable owner, scope, review cadence, and open actions.
3. Resolve orphaned, duplicate, or obsolete ownership.
4. Record the attestation, changes, reviewer, and next review date.
