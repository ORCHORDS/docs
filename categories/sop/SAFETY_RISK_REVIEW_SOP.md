# Safety Risk Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Identify the activity, hazard, affected groups, and accountable owner.
2. Review existing controls and current conditions.
3. Assess residual risk using the approved risk method.
4. Define additional treatment where risk is not acceptable.
5. Assign actions, review triggers, and evidence requirements.
6. Protect personal and facility-sensitive information in public records.
