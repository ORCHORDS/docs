# AI Exception Aging SOP

1. Review active AI governance exceptions for scope, age, owner, risk, and mitigation.
2. Reassess material model, provider, data, or use-case changes.
3. Escalate overdue exceptions for renewal, remediation, or retirement.
4. Record the outcome and next review.
