# Supplier Owner Recertification SOP

1. Identify material supplier relationships due for review.
2. Confirm internal owner, criticality, obligations, incidents, risk, performance, and exit responsibilities.
3. Reassign orphaned relationships and record open follow-up.
4. Set the next review date.
