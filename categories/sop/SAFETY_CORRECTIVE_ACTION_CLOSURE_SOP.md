# Safety Corrective Action Closure SOP

1. Confirm required corrective action and responsible owner.
2. Review evidence that the action was implemented.
3. Verify the relevant hazard or control weakness was reduced as intended.
4. Record closure while protecting personal health and facility-sensitive information.
