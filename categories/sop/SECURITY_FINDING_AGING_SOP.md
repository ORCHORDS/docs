# Security Finding Aging SOP

1. Review open findings by severity, age, owner, and dependency.
2. Validate remediation status and current exposure.
3. Escalate overdue or repeatedly deferred findings.
4. Refresh risk treatment where deadlines change.
5. Record decisions and next review dates.
