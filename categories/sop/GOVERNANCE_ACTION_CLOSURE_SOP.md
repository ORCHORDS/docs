# Governance Action Closure SOP

1. Confirm the action owner, intended outcome, due date, and source decision.
2. Review completion evidence and unresolved dependencies.
3. Verify the intended governance outcome was achieved or explicitly reassessed.
4. Record closure authority, date, residual actions, and evidence reference.
