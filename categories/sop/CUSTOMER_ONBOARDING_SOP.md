# SOP: Customer Onboarding

## Procedure

1. Review approved commercial handover and customer outcomes.
2. Confirm stakeholders, owner, scope, dependencies, milestones, and support route.
3. Confirm required access/data prerequisites through approved channels.
4. Define training or enablement actions.
5. Track onboarding milestones and material blockers.
6. Confirm completion and transition into ongoing success review.
