# SOP: Support Escalation

## Trigger
Use when ordinary support cannot safely or authoritatively resolve an issue.

## Procedure
1. Identify customer impact, urgency, and support actions already taken.
2. Remove unnecessary sensitive data before handoff.
3. Select specialist path: security, privacy, finance/fraud, legal, accessibility, product/quality, supplier, or incident management.
4. Provide relevant evidence and important unknowns.
5. Assign or confirm receiving owner.
6. Tell the customer what can reasonably be communicated about next steps.
7. Track handoff and outcome until responsibility is clearly transferred or resolved.

## Escalation quality
A specialist handoff should contain decision-relevant context rather than an unstructured transcript dump.
