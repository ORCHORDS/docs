# CI Failure Trend Review SOP

1. Aggregate recurring CI failures and flaky checks.
2. Separate product failures from infrastructure and test instability.
3. Identify bypasses, long-running failures, and ownership gaps.
4. Prioritize corrective work by impact on delivery confidence.
5. Verify trend improvement after changes.
