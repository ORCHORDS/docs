# Safety Incident Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Confirm immediate safety response and protected incident record.
2. Classify impact and determine escalation obligations.
3. Identify contributing conditions and control gaps.
4. Assign corrective actions and responsible owners.
5. Verify action completion and capture lessons without exposing personal health information.
