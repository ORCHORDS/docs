# SOP: Speak-Up Report

## Trigger
Use for good-faith reports of suspected misconduct, fraud, conflicts, retaliation, safety/security concerns, or material policy violations.

## Procedure
1. Record the concern and safe contact preference when available.
2. Assess immediate safety, security, evidence-preservation, or retaliation risk.
3. Assign an impartial reviewer without a material conflict.
4. Define investigation scope and confidentiality controls.
5. Gather and evaluate evidence fairly.
6. Protect reporters and witnesses from retaliation for good-faith participation.
7. Decide remediation, disciplinary, control, or referral actions based on evidence.
8. Communicate closure to the reporter where appropriate and lawful.
9. Preserve required records.

## Principle
Reports do not establish guilt; evidence does.
