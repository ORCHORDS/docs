# Knowledge Owner Recertification SOP

1. Identify critical and authoritative knowledge due for review.
2. Confirm current ownership, classification, freshness, and transfer readiness.
3. Reassign orphaned content and flag obsolete sources for retirement.
4. Record the outcome and next review date.
