# Support Exception Aging SOP

1. Review material support exceptions for customer impact, owner, age, and control gap.
2. Confirm temporary safeguards remain appropriate.
3. Escalate overdue or repeated exceptions.
4. Track resolution and lessons without publishing customer data.
