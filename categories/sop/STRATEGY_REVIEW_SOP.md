# SOP: Strategy Review

## Procedure

1. Review intended outcomes and current evidence.
2. Re-test major assumptions and dependencies.
3. Review material risks, constraints, and opportunity changes.
4. Compare actual allocation and progress with strategic intent.
5. Identify decisions requiring continue, adapt, stop, or investigate.
6. Record rationale, owners, measures, and next review trigger.
