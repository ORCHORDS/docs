# SOP: Service Decommissioning

## Procedure

1. Confirm accountable owner and approved retirement decision.
2. Identify users, dependencies, integrations, data, suppliers, and commitments.
3. Define communication and migration/alternative needs.
4. Determine retention, legal-hold, backup, and deletion obligations.
5. Revoke access, credentials, automation, and unnecessary exposure in a controlled sequence.
6. Update monitoring, inventories, documentation, support paths, and recurring spend.
7. Validate dependency removal and data disposition.
8. Retain required closure evidence and confirm no orphaned ownership remains.
