# SOP: Security Architecture Review

1. Define the material design/change and intended use.
2. Identify trust boundaries, identities/privileges, sensitive data, external exposure, third parties, recovery paths, and high-impact actions.
3. Review realistic threats and misuse cases.
4. Identify required safeguards and verification evidence.
5. Record residual risks, owners, and conditions for re-review.
6. Keep sensitive topology, secrets, and exploitable detail out of public records.
