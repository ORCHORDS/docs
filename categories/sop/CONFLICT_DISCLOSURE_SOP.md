# SOP: Conflict Disclosure

## Trigger
Use when a person identifies an actual, potential, or perceived conflict relevant to a material company decision.

## Procedure
1. Record the affected decision/relationship and nature of the interest.
2. Route disclosure to an authorized governance owner.
3. Restrict personal details to those needed for assessment.
4. Assess whether judgment or independence could reasonably be affected.
5. Select treatment: no action, disclosure, recusal, additional review, access restriction, ownership transfer, or other mitigation.
6. Record decision and review trigger.
7. Reassess if circumstances materially change.
