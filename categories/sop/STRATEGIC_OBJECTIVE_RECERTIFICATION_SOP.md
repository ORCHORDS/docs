# Strategic Objective Recertification SOP

1. Review the objective, owner, intended outcome, evidence, dependencies, and resources.
2. Test whether assumptions and strategic relevance remain valid.
3. Decide to continue, revise, replace, or retire the objective.
4. Record rationale and next review trigger.
