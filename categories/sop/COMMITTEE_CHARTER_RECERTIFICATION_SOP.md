# Committee Charter Recertification SOP

1. Review the forum mandate, decision rights, membership roles, cadence, and escalation boundaries.
2. Identify overlap, gaps, or obsolete responsibilities.
3. Approve changes, consolidation, or continued operation.
4. Record the decision and next review.
