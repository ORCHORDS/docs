# SOP: Post-Incident Review

## Trigger

Perform after material incidents, significant near misses, or repeated events that indicate a systemic control gap.

## Procedure

1. Confirm immediate remediation and evidence preservation are complete.
2. Build a factual timeline from reliable evidence.
3. Separate direct cause, contributing conditions, detection gaps, and response friction.
4. Identify what worked as well as what failed.
5. Avoid assigning blame where process, design, incentives, or controls are the more useful unit of improvement.
6. Create corrective actions with owner, priority, due date, and verification method.
7. Update risk records, policies, SOPs, monitoring, tests, or training where needed.
8. Review recurrence risk and residual exposure.
9. Close actions only with evidence.
