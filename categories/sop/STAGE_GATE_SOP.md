# SOP: Stage Gate Review

## Procedure

1. Confirm gate objective and decision authority.
2. Review progress against intended outcomes and acceptance evidence.
3. Review major risks, issues, changes, dependencies, cost/resource position, and unresolved assumptions.
4. Confirm required functional readiness and customer/operational impact.
5. Decide continue, continue with conditions, re-scope, pause, or stop.
6. Record rationale, conditions, owner, and next gate or review trigger.
