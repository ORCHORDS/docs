# Incident Trend Review SOP

1. Review incidents over the selected period.
2. Group recurring causes, dependencies, detection gaps, and change patterns.
3. Compare prior corrective actions with recurrence.
4. Assign systemic improvements separately from individual incident actions.
5. Track effectiveness at the next review.
