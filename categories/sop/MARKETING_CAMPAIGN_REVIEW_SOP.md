# Marketing Campaign Review SOP

1. Define campaign purpose, audience, channels, owner, budget, and success measures.
2. Verify material claims and evidence.
3. Review privacy, consent, accessibility, brand, partner, and legal implications as applicable.
4. Confirm approvals and stop criteria.
5. Retain the review record and material changes.
