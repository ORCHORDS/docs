# AI Inventory Recertification SOP

1. Review AI inventory entries for owner, use case, provider, lifecycle, and risk classification.
2. Identify orphaned, duplicate, changed, or retired entries.
3. Correct the inventory and assign follow-up.
4. Record the review outcome.
