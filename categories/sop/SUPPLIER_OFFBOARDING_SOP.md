# SOP: Supplier Offboarding

## Trigger

Use when a material supplier relationship ends, is replaced, or loses access to
company information or systems.

## Procedure

1. Identify relationship owner and termination scope.
2. Inventory material access, integrations, credentials, data, and dependencies.
3. Plan continuity or replacement if the supplier is operationally important.
4. Revoke access and disable integrations at the agreed point.
5. Rotate shared or supplier-accessible secrets where appropriate.
6. Complete data return, migration, retention, or deletion actions.
7. Capture contractual or incident follow-up.
8. Verify no expected dependency remains.
9. Record completion evidence and residual obligations.

Use [Supplier Due Diligence](THIRD_PARTY_DUE_DILIGENCE_SOP.md) for replacement
supplier review.
