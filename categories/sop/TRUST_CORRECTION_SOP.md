# Trust Correction SOP

1. Confirm the inaccurate, stale, or contradictory trust statement.
2. Assess affected channels and customer communications.
3. Establish corrected facts and approvals.
4. Publish corrections or retire material promptly and consistently.
5. Record cause, scope, evidence, and preventive actions.
