# Monitoring Exception Aging SOP

1. Review material monitoring gaps and exceptions for service impact, owner, age, and mitigation.
2. Confirm interim detection or operational safeguards remain effective.
3. Escalate overdue gaps.
4. Track remediation through verified closure.
