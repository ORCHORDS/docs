# Data Sharing Approval Recertification SOP

1. Review material sharing purpose, recipients, data scope, safeguards, retention, and owner.
2. Confirm approval remains necessary and accurate.
3. Withdraw or amend stale sharing arrangements.
4. Record the decision without publishing sensitive datasets or recipients.
