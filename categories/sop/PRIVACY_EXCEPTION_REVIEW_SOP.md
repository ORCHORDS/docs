# Privacy Exception Review SOP

1. Confirm scope, owner, rationale, and authority.
2. Review personal-data impact and compensating measures.
3. Check expiry, changed processing, suppliers, or legal assumptions.
4. Approve continuation, require remediation, or close the exception.
5. Record the decision and evidence.
