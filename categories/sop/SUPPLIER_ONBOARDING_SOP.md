# SOP: Supplier Onboarding

## Trigger
Use before a material supplier receives sensitive data, privileged access, critical dependency status, or production integration.

## Procedure
1. Identify business owner and purpose.
2. Classify data, access, and dependency criticality.
3. Complete due diligence proportional to risk.
4. Resolve required contractual security/privacy terms.
5. Define least-privilege integration and credential ownership.
6. Record monitoring, resilience, incident, and exit expectations.
7. Obtain approvals and accepted exceptions.
8. Provision access/integration only after required controls are complete.
9. Add supplier to authoritative inventory and review schedule.
