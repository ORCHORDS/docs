# Audit Universe Recertification SOP

1. Review current organizational, process, regulatory, technology, supplier, and strategic changes.
2. Compare them with the audit universe and risk assessment.
3. Add, merge, reclassify, or retire auditable areas as needed.
4. Record rationale while preserving internal audit independence.
