# SOP: Technical Debt Review

## Procedure

1. Review material debt items and recent incident/change evidence.
2. Confirm each item still describes the real constraint or risk.
3. Assess security, reliability, delivery, maintainability, cost, and compounding impact.
4. Identify debt that blocks strategic or operational work.
5. Reprioritize, accept for another review period, or plan repayment/removal.
6. Record owner, decision rationale, next review trigger, and dependencies.
7. Close only when the underlying debt is removed or no longer material.
