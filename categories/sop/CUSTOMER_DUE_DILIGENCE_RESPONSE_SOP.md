# Customer Due Diligence Response SOP

1. Classify the request and identify reusable approved evidence.
2. Validate scope, freshness, and confidentiality boundaries.
3. Escalate novel or sensitive questions to subject-matter owners.
4. Review the completed response for consistency and unsupported assurance.
5. Retain the response version and evidence references.
