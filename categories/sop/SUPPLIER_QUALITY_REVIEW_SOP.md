# SOP: Supplier Quality Review

## Procedure

1. Gather recurring defects, incidents, service failures, complaints, missed commitments, and corrective actions attributable to the supplier.
2. Assess trend, recurrence, user impact, operational impact, and whether prior corrective actions were effective.
3. Confirm issues are also reflected in third-party risk where appropriate.
4. Request or track corrective action for material recurring failures.
5. Decide whether monitoring, contractual action, renewal conditions, alternative sourcing, or exit planning should change.
6. Record the decision and next review trigger.
