# WCAG 2.2 Dragging Movements

Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#dragging-movements

WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.5.7 is Level AA. Functionality that uses dragging must also be achievable with a single pointer without dragging unless dragging is essential or controlled by the user agent.

## Governance

- Provide click/tap alternatives for reorder, move, resize, slider-like, map-like, or drag-and-drop workflows where the criterion applies.
- Make alternatives discoverable and keyboard-compatible where applicable.
- Do not treat touch support as proof that a drag interaction is accessible.
- Record any use of the essential-function exception with the specific rationale.

## Verification

For every product-controlled drag operation, attempt the same outcome with a single-pointer non-drag interaction and document the result. Include users with reduced dexterity in usability testing where feasible.
