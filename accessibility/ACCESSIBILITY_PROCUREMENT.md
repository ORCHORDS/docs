# Accessibility in Procurement

## Purpose

Consider accessibility before purchasing tools or services that materially affect users or personnel.

## Requirements

Relevant procurement SHOULD request evidence appropriate to the product, evaluate critical user journeys and known limitations, avoid relying solely on vendor conformance claims, and record material gaps/mitigations.

Accessibility evidence should be scoped and current. A vendor statement or report does not by itself prove every configured use is accessible.
