# Break-Glass Operations

## Purpose

Define operational use of emergency bypass mechanisms when normal control paths are unavailable or too slow for an active emergency.

## Requirements

Break-glass use MUST be attributable, time-bounded, limited to the emergency purpose, logged where technically possible, and followed by review of actions, residual access, credentials, and any bypassed control.

The mechanism should be periodically tested without exposing secret material or normalizing routine use.

See [Emergency Access Governance](../security/EMERGENCY_ACCESS_GOVERNANCE.md).
