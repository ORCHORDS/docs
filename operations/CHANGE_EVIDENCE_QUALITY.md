# Change Evidence Quality

## Purpose

Ensure material operational changes leave enough evidence to understand what was intended, approved, performed, and verified.

## Requirements

Change evidence SHOULD include scope, owner, risk, approval basis, verification result, material deviations, and follow-up actions.

A successful deployment status alone is not sufficient evidence of intended service behavior where additional validation is required.

Emergency changes should receive retrospective evidence review.
