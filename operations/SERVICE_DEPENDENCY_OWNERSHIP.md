# Service Dependency Ownership

## Purpose

Make operational dependencies visible enough for change, incident, capacity, and recovery decisions.

## Requirements

Material services SHOULD identify critical upstream and downstream dependencies, accountable owners, failure assumptions, and important recovery or escalation relationships.

Dependency records should be reviewed when architecture or supplier relationships materially change.

Unknown dependency ownership is itself an operational risk.
