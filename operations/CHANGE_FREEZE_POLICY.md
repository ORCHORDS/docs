# Change Freeze Policy

## Purpose

Temporarily reduce avoidable operational change during high-risk periods without blocking necessary security or recovery work.

## Freeze design

A freeze SHOULD define scope, start/end conditions, rationale, exception authority, allowed low-risk activity, and communication expectations.

Emergency security, incident containment, recovery, or legally required changes may proceed through the emergency-change path when delaying them creates greater risk.

A freeze should not be used to conceal weak change controls or become an indefinite substitute for normal release governance.
