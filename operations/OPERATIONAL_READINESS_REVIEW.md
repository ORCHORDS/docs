# Operational Readiness Review

## Purpose

Confirm a material capability can be operated, supported, observed, recovered, and changed safely before broad use.

## Readiness areas

Review SHOULD cover ownership, support/escalation, monitoring, capacity, backup/recovery, change/rollback, security/privacy, dependencies, runbooks, alert quality, maintenance, known limitations, and handover evidence.

Readiness is risk-based; not every change needs the same ceremony.

See [Operational Readiness SOP](../sop/OPERATIONAL_READINESS_SOP.md).
