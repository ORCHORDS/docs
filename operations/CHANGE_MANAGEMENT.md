# Change Management Policy

## Purpose

Make operational change deliberate, reviewable, observable, and reversible.

## Change classes

| Class | Typical characteristics | Minimum control |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Repeated, low-risk, proven procedure | Pre-approved procedure + verification |
| Normal | Material but planned change | Peer review + risk assessment + rollback |
| High risk | Large blast radius, sensitive data, irreversible migration, novel path | Senior approval + staged rollout + explicit rollback/containment |
| Emergency | Delay creates greater risk than immediate change | Fast authorization + evidence + retrospective review |

## Change record

Material changes should record:

- objective;
- owner;
- affected capability;
- risk and blast radius;
- dependencies;
- verification plan;
- rollout plan;
- rollback or containment plan;
- approval;
- outcome.

## Windows and freezes

Use maintenance windows or release freezes when they reduce risk, but do not
treat them as substitutes for testing and rollback.

## Failed changes

A failed change that causes material user or security impact is assessed under
the incident process. Repeated change failures require process or engineering
remediation, not only additional approvals.
