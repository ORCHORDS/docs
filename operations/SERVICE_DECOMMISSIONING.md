# Service Decommissioning

## Purpose

Retire services without leaving orphaned data, access, credentials, dependencies, monitoring, cost, or customer commitments.

## Requirements

A decommission plan SHOULD identify owner, affected users/dependencies, data disposition, retention/legal-hold constraints, access and secret revocation, supplier implications, communications, monitoring changes, rollback window if any, and final verification.

Retirement should remove unnecessary privilege, network exposure, credentials, automation, documentation, and recurring cost after required evidence and records are retained.

See [Service Decommissioning SOP](../sop/SERVICE_DECOMMISSIONING_SOP.md).
