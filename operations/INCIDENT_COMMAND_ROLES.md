# Incident Command Roles

## Purpose

Define clear accountability during material incidents without depending on individual names.

## Requirements

Material incidents SHOULD establish roles for coordination, technical response, communications, decision logging, and stakeholder escalation as appropriate to severity.

One person may hold multiple roles in a small event, but responsibilities should remain explicit.

Role transfer should include current status, decisions, open risks, and next actions.
