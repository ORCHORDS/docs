# On-Call Sustainability

## Purpose

Keep incident duty effective by treating chronic overload and avoidable interruption as operational risks.

## Requirements

On-call programs SHOULD review alert volume, actionability, repeat incidents, handoff quality, staffing coverage, unresolved toil, and patterns of after-hours interruption.

Persistent overload should trigger remediation of alerting, reliability, ownership, or staffing causes rather than normalization of fatigue.

Personal schedules and health information remain private.
