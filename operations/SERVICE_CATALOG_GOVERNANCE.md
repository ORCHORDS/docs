# Service Catalog Governance

## Purpose

Maintain a discoverable record of material services and ownership without publishing the private catalog itself.

## Catalog fields

Records SHOULD include service identity, owner, lifecycle status, purpose, support model, criticality where relevant, important dependencies, documentation location, and deprecation state.

## Quality

The catalog should be reconciled with observed assets, repositories, suppliers, or operational systems where practical. Unowned or duplicate entries require investigation.

## Public boundary

Actual service catalogs may expose internal identifiers and topology and therefore generally remain private. This document defines the governance expectation only.
