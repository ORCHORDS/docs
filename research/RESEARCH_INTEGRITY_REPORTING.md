# Research Integrity and Reporting

## Purpose

Keep research conclusions traceable to the actual method and evidence.

## Reporting rules

Reports SHOULD state research question, method, sample/recruitment, relevant dates, major limitations, uncertainty, and distinction between observation and interpretation.

Do not suppress inconvenient findings solely because they conflict with a preferred decision. Re-analysis is acceptable when methods and rationale remain visible.

ISO 20252:2019 may inform market/opinion/social research practice; its 2026 edition is under publication and must not be treated as final until published.
