# Research Participant Recruitment

## Purpose

Recruit participants fairly and transparently while avoiding hidden bias and inappropriate targeting.

## Requirements

Recruitment criteria SHOULD be linked to the research question. Record major inclusion/exclusion criteria, recruitment channel, incentives, and known sampling limitations.

Avoid exploiting unequal power relationships or using sensitive attributes without a justified research need and appropriate safeguards.
