# Research Bias and Quality Review

## Purpose

Make known limitations visible before research findings are used for decisions.

## Review areas

Research SHOULD assess recruitment bias, non-response, incentive effects, moderator/interviewer influence, question framing, survivorship, sampling limitations, measurement error, missing data, and whether conclusions extend beyond the evidence collected.

Disconfirming evidence and uncertainty should be preserved rather than removed for narrative simplicity.

A research result can be useful without being representative of an entire population when its scope is stated accurately.
