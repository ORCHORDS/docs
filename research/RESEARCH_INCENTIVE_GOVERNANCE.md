# Research Incentive Governance

## Purpose

Use participant incentives transparently without creating inappropriate pressure or hidden sampling bias.

## Requirements

Incentives SHOULD be proportionate to time/burden, disclosed before participation, separated from promises about research outcomes, and documented as part of recruitment methodology.

Researchers should consider whether incentive size or form may disproportionately influence vulnerable participants, encourage misrepresentation of eligibility, or distort the sample.
