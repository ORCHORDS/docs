# Research Governance Policy

## Purpose

Ensure research is purposeful, proportionate, ethical, privacy-aware, and methodologically transparent.

## Requirements

Material research SHOULD define question, method, participant population, recruitment approach, data collected, consent needs, incentives, risks, retention, owner, and intended use of results.

Research must not use deception, coercion, or unnecessary collection of sensitive information merely for convenience.

See [Research Review SOP](../sop/RESEARCH_REVIEW_SOP.md).
