# Generative AI Public Feedback Governance

Source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf

NIST AI 600-1 recommends prioritizing structured public-feedback processes based on risk assessment.

## Governance

- Define what feedback channels are in scope for an AI research/evaluation activity.
- Provide a route for affected users or external parties to report meaningful failures, harms, or unexpected behavior.
- Triage feedback according to potential impact and evidence quality rather than popularity alone.
- Protect reporter privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive submissions.
- Feed validated findings back into evaluation plans, risk registers, and corrective actions.

## Evidence

Retain aggregated, public-safe evidence of themes, disposition categories, and resulting actions. Do not imply that receiving feedback proves every report was validated or every risk has been eliminated.
