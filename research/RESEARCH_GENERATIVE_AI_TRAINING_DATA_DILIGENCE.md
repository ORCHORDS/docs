# Generative AI Training Data Diligence

Source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf

NIST AI 600-1 recommends diligence on training-data use to assess intellectual-property and privacy risks, including whether proprietary or sensitive data use is consistent with applicable law.

## Governance

- Identify the provenance and permitted use of training/fine-tuning/evaluation datasets where this information is available and material.
- Separate public, licensed, proprietary, personal, and sensitive data classes.
- Document unresolved rights or privacy questions rather than treating missing evidence as permission.
- Minimize personal/sensitive data used for research and evaluation.
- Apply deletion, retention, and access controls appropriate to the dataset.
- Review third-party dataset terms and material changes.

## Public boundary

This repository may describe the diligence process but must not publish non-public datasets, personal information, contract terms, credentials, or proprietary source material.
