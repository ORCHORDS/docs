# Conflict Recertification

## Purpose
Ensure material conflict-of-interest disclosures and mitigations remain current as circumstances change.

## Requirements
- Material conflicts must have a documented owner, assessment, and mitigation where needed.
- Active conflicts must be reviewed periodically and after relevant role or relationship changes.
- Recertification must confirm whether the conflict remains active, changed, or closed.
- Ineffective or expired mitigations require escalation.

## Evidence
Maintain disclosure, review, mitigation, recertification, and closure records while protecting personal information.
