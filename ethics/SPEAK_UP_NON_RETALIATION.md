# Speak-Up and Non-Retaliation

## Purpose

Encourage good-faith reporting of suspected misconduct, fraud, safety/security concerns, policy violations, conflicts, or legal concerns.

## Principles

Good-faith reporters SHOULD be able to raise concerns without retaliation for the act of reporting. This does not prevent action for knowingly false reports, misconduct, or unrelated performance issues.

Reports should be handled confidentially to the extent practical and lawful, with access limited to those needed for assessment and response.

## Investigation fairness

People named in a report should not be presumed responsible before evidence is assessed. Investigations should avoid unnecessary disclosure and conflicts of interest.
