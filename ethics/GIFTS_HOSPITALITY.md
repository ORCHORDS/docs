# Gifts and Hospitality

## Purpose

Prevent gifts, meals, travel, entertainment, discounts, or other benefits from creating improper influence or the appearance of compromised judgment.

## Principles

Gifts and hospitality SHOULD be lawful, modest, infrequent, transparent, connected to a legitimate business purpose, and never conditioned on a decision or advantage.

Cash or cash-equivalent gifts, secret benefits, or benefits offered during sensitive procurement/approval decisions require heightened restriction and generally should not be accepted without explicit governance approval.

## Review

Context matters, including recipient role, timing, value, frequency, local custom, government involvement, and ongoing negotiations.
