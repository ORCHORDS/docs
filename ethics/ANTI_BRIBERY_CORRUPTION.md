# Anti-Bribery and Corruption

## Purpose

Prohibit improper payments, benefits, or influence intended to obtain or retain an unfair business advantage.

## Requirements

Personnel and representatives acting for the company MUST NOT offer, promise, authorize, request, or accept bribes or disguised improper benefits.

Payments, discounts, commissions, donations, sponsorships, gifts, hospitality, travel, and third-party arrangements require additional scrutiny when they could influence a business or official decision.

## Third parties

The company should not use agents, consultants, resellers, suppliers, or other intermediaries to do indirectly what personnel may not do directly.

## Records

Financial and contractual records must accurately reflect the true purpose and recipient of material payments.

## Escalation

Unusual requests or suspected improper payments should be paused when safe and escalated for governance/legal review.
