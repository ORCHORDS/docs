# Retaliation Follow-Up Aging

## Purpose
Keep non-retaliation follow-up commitments visible until they are completed or formally closed.

## Requirements
- Required follow-ups must have an owner, due date, and protected record.
- Overdue follow-up must be escalated without exposing reporter identity unnecessarily.
- Closure must document completion and any resulting action.
- Trend review should identify recurring delays or control weaknesses.

## Evidence
Retain protected follow-up schedules, completion records, escalation evidence, and closure decisions.
