# Workplace Violence Prevention

Threatening, intimidating, or violent conduct is not acceptable in company-related workplaces or activities.

Concerns should be reported through appropriate channels and assessed proportionately with attention to immediate safety, confidentiality, non-retaliation, and escalation to relevant authorities when required.
