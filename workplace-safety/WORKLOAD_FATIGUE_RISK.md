# Workload and Fatigue Risk

Work design should consider sustained workload, extended hours, overnight work, travel, on-call demands, and recovery time where fatigue could materially increase safety or quality risk.

Escalation should focus on work conditions and controls rather than requiring public disclosure of personal health information.
