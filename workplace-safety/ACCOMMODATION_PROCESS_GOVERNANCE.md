# Accommodation Process Governance

Requests for reasonable workplace adjustments should be handled through a confidential, respectful process that identifies functional needs, feasible options, responsibilities, review points, and applicable obligations.

Only the minimum necessary information should be shared with people implementing an approved adjustment.
