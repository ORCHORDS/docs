# Supplier and Contractor Safety Requirements

Supplier and contractor safety expectations should reflect the work they perform, the environments they affect, and the degree of control ORCHORDS exercises.

Material safety requirements should be incorporated into selection, onboarding, work coordination, incident escalation, and performance review where relevant.
