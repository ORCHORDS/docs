# Commercial Commitments

## Purpose

Prevent commitments that are unclear, unsupported, unowned, or inconsistent with approved product, security, privacy, legal, operational, or delivery constraints.

## Requirements

Material customer commitments SHOULD identify scope, assumptions, dependencies, exclusions, accountable owner, evidence for factual claims, and any time-bound obligation.

Commitments involving security, privacy, legal terms, unusual service levels, custom development, material discounts, data handling, or operational support require review by the relevant accountable function before acceptance.

## Accuracy

Do not present planned capability as implemented, estimates as guarantees, or informal discussion as an approved commitment.

## Changes

Material changes after approval require re-review of affected assumptions, risk, delivery impact, and customer communication.

See [Commercial Commitment Review SOP](../sop/COMMERCIAL_COMMITMENT_REVIEW_SOP.md).
