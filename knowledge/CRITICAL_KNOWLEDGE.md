# Critical Knowledge Governance

## Purpose

Identify knowledge whose loss would materially impair security, operations, legal obligations, customer commitments, delivery, or recovery.

## Requirements

Critical knowledge SHOULD have an accountable owner, suitable storage location, backup or alternate holder where appropriate, review trigger, and transfer plan for role changes.

Sensitive operational knowledge should remain in authorized systems rather than being copied into public documentation.

See [Critical Knowledge Transfer SOP](../sop/CRITICAL_KNOWLEDGE_TRANSFER_SOP.md).
