# Knowledge Transfer

## Purpose

Transfer critical knowledge during onboarding, role change, leave, project handover, supplier transition, and offboarding.

## Requirements

Transfer SHOULD cover current responsibilities, recurring decisions, known risks, key records, operational rhythms, escalation paths, pending commitments, and areas where tacit knowledge needs demonstration rather than a document alone.

The recipient should be able to demonstrate sufficient understanding for material responsibilities.

See [Critical Knowledge Transfer SOP](../sop/CRITICAL_KNOWLEDGE_TRANSFER_SOP.md).
