# Knowledge Content Lifecycle

## Purpose

Keep governed knowledge accurate from creation through retirement.

## Lifecycle

Content SHOULD have purpose, owner, audience, classification, source/evidence where factual, review cadence, and a clear supersession or retirement path.

Duplicate authoritative sources should be avoided. When a document is superseded, update inbound references and remove or clearly archive the old source.

High-change content requires shorter review cycles or event-driven review triggers.
