# Control Testing Policy

## Purpose

Define how control design and operating effectiveness are evaluated.

## Testing dimensions

Control testing SHOULD distinguish:

- design: whether the control could reasonably achieve its objective;
- implementation: whether the control exists in the stated scope;
- operation: whether it performed over the relevant period;
- evidence quality: whether available records support the conclusion.

## Test design

Testing depth should reflect control importance, automation, frequency, population size, prior failures, and potential impact.

A document stating a control requirement is not evidence that the control operates.

## Findings

Exceptions must be evaluated for cause, frequency, impact, and whether they undermine the control conclusion. Repeated exceptions may indicate a design problem rather than isolated error.
