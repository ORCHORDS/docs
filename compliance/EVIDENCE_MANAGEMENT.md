# Evidence Management

## Purpose

Define what makes governance, security, privacy, operational, or engineering
evidence trustworthy and reviewable.

## Evidence qualities

Evidence SHOULD be:

- relevant to the control or decision;
- attributable to a source and time period;
- protected from inappropriate modification;
- sufficiently complete to support the claim;
- retained only as long as required;
- understandable by a reviewer who did not create it.

Screenshots alone are often weak evidence because context and provenance may be
missing. Prefer durable records or exports where practical.

## Sensitive evidence

Public documentation must never embed sensitive assurance evidence merely to
prove that a control exists. Sensitive records belong in authorized systems;
public docs describe the evidence expectation.

## Review

Stale evidence cannot support a current-state claim without justification.
Recurring controls should define an evidence freshness expectation.
