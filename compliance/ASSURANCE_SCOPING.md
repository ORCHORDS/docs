# Assurance Scoping

## Purpose

Define clear boundaries for internal reviews, external assessments, and assurance claims.

## Scope definition

An assurance scope SHOULD identify relevant services or processes, organizational boundaries, period, locations or environments where material, excluded areas, dependencies, evidence owners, and applicable criteria.

## Principles

Scope must not be narrowed merely to avoid known material risk while retaining broad public claims.

Dependencies outside direct control should be identified when they materially affect the control objective.

## Claims

Any statement derived from an assessment must reflect the actual assessment scope and period. A limited review must not be described as broad organizational assurance.
