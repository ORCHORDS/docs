# Compliance Monitoring Governance

Recurring monitoring SHOULD focus on material obligations, control failures, overdue remediation, evidence freshness, exceptions, regulatory change, and emerging risk. Monitoring scope and thresholds should be reviewed as obligations or business activities change.
