# ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System Template Governance

## Purpose
Establish the governance pattern for templating a Quality Management System (QMS) per ISO 9001:2015 (Quality management systems — Requirements), including process maps, quality objectives, and document control.

## Scope
Applies to every QMS element operated by the studio, including process definitions, quality objectives, internal audits, management reviews, and document control records.

## Workflow
1. Use a templated QMS document set with mandatory elements per ISO 9001:2015: quality policy, quality objectives, scope statement, process map, and documented information register.
3. Define each process with inputs, outputs, controls, responsibilities, and performance indicators; map each process to the relevant ISO 9001:2015 clause.
5. Maintain a documented information register that lists every controlled document, its owner, its revision, and its retention period.
7. Conduct internal audits on a planned cadence covering all QMS processes; record results, nonconformities, and corrective actions.
9. Conduct management reviews on a scheduled cadence covering audit results, customer feedback, process performance, and improvement opportunities.

## Controls and evidence
- Quality policy and objectives with owner, measurable target, and review cadence.
- Process map with clauses mapped to ISO 9001:2015 and performance indicators with thresholds.
- Documented information register with version, owner, retention period, and access control list.
- Internal audit and management review records with attendees, decisions, and follow-up actions.

## Validation
- Re-validate the documented information register against the latest internal audit findings and confirm all controlled documents are present.
- Verify that each quality objective has at least one performance indicator with a defined measurement frequency.
- Confirm that internal audit and management review records cover all QMS processes within the audit cycle.

## Failure correction
- **Nonconformity detected in internal audit** → open a corrective action record, document root cause analysis, and verify effectiveness within the agreed timeframe.
- **Quality objective below threshold** → initiate a management review of the objective, document the cause, and adjust the target or the underlying process.
- **Documented information missing or outdated** → suspend the use of the document, document the gap, and update or recreate the document.

## Limitations
- ISO 9001:2015 is a generic standard; sector-specific requirements (e.g., AS9100 for aerospace, ISO 13485 for medical devices) impose additional controls.
- Process effectiveness may require capabilities beyond ISO 9001:2015, such as statistical process control (SPC) tools.
- ISO 9001:2015 emphasizes risk-based thinking; integrate with ISO 31000:2018 risk management where applicable.
