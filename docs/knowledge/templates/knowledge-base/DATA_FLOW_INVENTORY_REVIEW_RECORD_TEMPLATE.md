## Purpose

Use this record to review how data moves through systems, products, services, third parties, and processing environments, and whether the documented inventory remains accurate.

## Review checks

- [ ] Systems and services that process data are inventoried.
- [ ] Owners/operators and third parties are identified.
- [ ] Data elements and data actions are current.
- [ ] Processing purposes are documented.
- [ ] Processing environments and geographic locations are represented where relevant.
- [ ] Data flows between components and third parties are mapped.
- [ ] New integrations, exports, storage locations, or processors are reflected.
- [ ] Unnecessary or undocumented flows are assigned for remediation.
