## Review checks

- [ ] Recording is supported by a documented privacy-risk assessment.
- [ ] Applicant notice occurs before a recorded session begins.
- [ ] Applicant consent is obtained before recording starts.
- [ ] Retention schedule and deletion process are published.
- [ ] Access to recordings is limited to approved roles and purposes.
- [ ] Deletion is testable and evidence is retained without keeping the deleted recording itself.
- [ ] Recording is not enabled merely as an undocumented default of the capture platform.
