# ISO 30300:2020 Records Management Overview Template Governance

## Purpose

ISO 30300:2020, "Information and documentation — Records management — Core concepts and vocabulary," is the entry-point standard for the ISO 30300 series. It defines the core concepts and vocabulary for records management, including the records management system, the policy, the records processes (capture, control, retrieval, use, retention, disposition), and the relationship to ISO 30301 (the requirements standard). This article governs the application of ISO 30300 as a template for an organization's records management documentation, so the organization's records management vocabulary and structure are aligned with the international standard.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization that creates and maintains records. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the core concepts (record, records management, records management system, records policy, records process), the vocabulary, and the structure of the records management system. It does not replace ISO 30301 (the management system requirements); it provides the vocabulary and concepts used by 30301.

## Workflow

1. Adopt the ISO 30300 vocabulary for the organization's records management documentation: policies, procedures, training materials, and records management system design documents should use the terms the standard defines.
2. Apply the records management framework: policy, processes, and the supporting records management system.
3. Identify the records the organization creates and maintains in the course of its business. Distinguish records from other information (a record is captured as part of a business activity and is retained for evidence or memory).
4. Document the records management processes the organization operates:
   - Capture: how records are captured as part of business activities.
   - Control: how records are classified, indexed, and protected from unauthorized change.
   - Retrieval and use: how records are made available to authorized users.
   - Retention and disposition: how long records are kept and how they are ultimately disposed of.
5. Document the records management policy and the records management system.

## Controls and evidence

Controls include the documented records management policy, the records management system design, the records processes, and the records themselves (as evidence of the system's operation). Evidence of disciplined records management includes a current inventory of record types, retention schedules, and records management procedures that match the standard's vocabulary.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the vocabulary is used consistently, the policy is in place, the records management processes are documented, and records management is integrated with the organization's operations. Periodic reviews of the records management system confirm the framework remains aligned with the organization's needs.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the vocabulary is used loosely (corrective: enforce the ISO 30300 vocabulary); records are captured but not classified or indexed (corrective: classify and index at capture); retention schedules are not applied (corrective: enforce retention schedules and document dispositions); records management is treated as an IT activity rather than a business activity (corrective: engage business owners and integrate records management with business processes).

## Limitations

ISO 30300 is a core concepts and vocabulary standard; it does not specify requirements (ISO 30301 does that). The standard does not prescribe retention periods or disposition methods. The standard does not address sector-specific records regulations; readers should overlay their sector requirements.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral use of ISO 30300:2020 as a template. It does not assert any specific organization's records management conformance or claim any certification outcome.
