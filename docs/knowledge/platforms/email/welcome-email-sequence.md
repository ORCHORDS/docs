# welcome-email-sequence

**Issue:** Designing and implementing an effective welcome email sequence for new users
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** documented

## Symptom / Context
First impressions matter; the welcome sequence sets expectation, drives first activation, and establishes the email relationship.

## Pattern / Solution
Recommended sequence:
| Timing | Email | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Immediately | Welcome + value prop | Confirm signup, set expectations |
| Day 1 | Getting started guide | First activation |
| Day 3 | Key feature spotlight | Deeper engagement |
| Day 7 | Social proof / case study | Build confidence |
| Day 14 | Check-in / offer help | Reduce churn risk |

Best practices:
- Immediate welcome should arrive within 60 seconds.
- Keep early emails short and action-focused.
- Segment by acquisition channel or use case if data is available.
- Exit sequence on first meaningful activation event.

## Gotchas
- Do not send welcome sequence to users who signed up and immediately cancelled.
- Welcome email open rates are highest; optimize for deliverability on this send.
- Reply-to a real person address in early sequence increases replies and trust.
