# ISO/IEC 27035-3:2023 Incident Response Exercises Governance

## Purpose
Establish the governance pattern for designing, executing, and learning from security incident response exercises per ISO/IEC 27035-3:2023 — Guidelines for ICT incident response exercises.

## Scope
Applies to every exercise — tabletop, walk-through, partial simulation, full simulation — used to validate the studio's incident response capability, regardless of the exercise scope or participant count.

## Workflow
1. Define exercise objectives using SMART (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound) criteria and align each objective to an ISO/IEC 27035-3 exercise category.
3. Design the exercise scenario with documented inputs, expected actions, success criteria, and injects.
5. Conduct the exercise with an experienced facilitator; capture all participant actions, decisions, and communications.
7. After the exercise, run a structured debrief within 5 business days and produce an after-action report with findings, root causes, and remediation actions.
9. Track remediation actions to closure with owner, due date, and verification; report closure status in the next quarterly security review.

## Controls and evidence
- Exercise plan with objectives, scope, scenario, success criteria, participants, and schedule.
- Exercise log capturing actions, decisions, communications, and timing against the scenario.
- After-action report with findings, root causes, and remediation actions.
- Remediation action tracker with owner, due date, status, and verification evidence.

## Validation
- Verify that every exercise has an objective linked to an ISO/IEC 27035-3 exercise category.
- Confirm that after-action reports are produced within 5 business days of the exercise.
- Validate that remediation actions from the previous two exercises are closed or have documented acceptance rationale.

## Failure correction
- **After-action report delayed beyond 5 business days** → escalate to the exercise sponsor and produce the report within 10 business days; document the cause of delay.
- **Remediation action open beyond target date** → escalate to the action owner and document the impact or compensating controls.
- **Exercise objectives not met** → plan a follow-up exercise addressing the unachieved objectives; document the lessons from the prior exercise in the follow-up plan.

## Limitations
- ISO/IEC 27035-3:2023 is the third part of the 27035 series; the first and second parts cover principles and incident response planning, respectively.
- Exercises test the documented process; they do not necessarily reveal gaps in undocumented processes.
- Some exercises require sensitive test data and live infrastructure; the associated risk must be managed.
