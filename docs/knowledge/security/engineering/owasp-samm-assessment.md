# OWASP SAMM 2.0 Assessment

## Pinned source and scope
OWASP SAMM **2.0**. This article uses the named version and identifiers; do not combine evidence from another edition without a migration record.

## Control interpretation
Assess the five business functions—Governance, Design, Implementation, Verification, and Operations. Each contains three security practices; each practice has two streams and three maturity levels. Score an activity only when its quality criteria are met. A higher maturity activity does not erase missing prerequisites in the same stream.

## Domain-specific procedure
Define organizational unit and product scope. For all 30 streams, interview practitioners and sample policies, repositories, tickets, pipeline results, threat models, defects, incidents, and metrics. Record Current score, Target score, evidence, confidence, and improvement activity. Do not average scores into a certification claim; SAMM measures capability maturity, not application conformance.

## Evidence and decision
For every SAMM stream retain interview notes and artifacts satisfying its quality criteria, then record current and target maturity independently. Reassessment must use the same organizational boundary.

## Failure modes
Policy existence without sampled operation, scoring aspirations as current practice, and averaging away an absent stream distort maturity.
