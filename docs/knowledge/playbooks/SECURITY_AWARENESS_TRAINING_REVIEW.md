# Security Awareness Training Review

1. Define audience and required outcomes.
2. Collect current training and completion evidence.
3. Compare against applicable NIST AT controls.
4. Record gaps, actions, owners, and due dates.
5. Verify closure and effectiveness.

## Source
NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 / Release 5.2.0: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final
