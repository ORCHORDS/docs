# Control Assessment Plan Review

## Procedure
1. Confirm scope, systems, and controls selected for assessment.
2. Review methods, evidence sources, and assessor roles.
3. Verify sampling and timing are adequate for risk.
4. Update the plan for material system or threat changes.
5. Record approvals and unresolved limitations.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and SP 800-53A.
