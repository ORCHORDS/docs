# Secure Development Practice Review

## Procedure
1. Review secure design, implementation, review, testing, and release practices.
2. Verify required tooling and evidence are actually used.
3. Review developer security training and ownership.
4. Identify bypasses or stale procedures.
5. Assign improvements and verify adoption.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 SA family and NIST SSDF.
