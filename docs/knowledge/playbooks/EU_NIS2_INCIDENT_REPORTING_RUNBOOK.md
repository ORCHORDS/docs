# EU NIS2 Incident Reporting Runbook

## Purpose
Execute Article 23 incident reporting obligations under the NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555) on 24-hour / 72-hour / one-month cadences to the competent authority / CSIRT.

## Procedure
1. Detect or receive notification of an incident affecting essential or important entity services.
2. Run impact assessment against Article 24 criteria: services affected; number of users; duration; geographical spread; impact on economic / societal activities.
3. Stage 1 — Early Warning (within 24 hours of awareness):
   - Indicate whether the incident is suspected of being caused by unlawful or malicious acts, or could have a cross-border impact.
   - Submit to the competent authority / CSIRT via the prescribed channel.
4. Stage 2 — Incident Notification (within 72 hours of awareness):
   - Update the early warning with severity, impact, indicators of compromise.
   - Apply any sector-specific reporting guidance (energy, transport, health, etc.).
5. Stage 3 — Final Report (within one month of Stage 2):
   - Detailed description: threat / root cause; applied / ongoing mitigations; cross-border impact; alignment to Article 21 controls.
6. Intermediate Reports on request: provide status updates, milestone changes, and any new indicators of compromise.
7. Maintain the Article 21 risk-management measures evidence: policies, MFA, network segmentation, supply-chain risk, encryption, training.
8. Coordinate with the ECCC (European Cyber Crisis Coordination) for cross-border incidents; align to ENISA technical guidance.
9. Capture lessons-learned; update Article 21 controls; document management-body accountability per Article 20.

## Source basis
- Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2 Directive).
- Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690.
- ENISA technical implementation guidance; sector-specific competent authority guidance.
- NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2 (incident-handling lifecycle reference).
