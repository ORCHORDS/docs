# Data Breach Response Playbook

## Trigger

Use when personal or sensitive information may have been accessed, stolen, exposed, or improperly published.

## Steps

1. Mobilize the response team and assign an incident owner.
2. Secure affected operations and stop additional data loss without destroying evidence.
3. Preserve logs, forensic evidence, and relevant system state.
4. Determine the affected systems, information types, people, service providers, and likely exposure window.
5. Change compromised credentials and restrict unnecessary access.
6. Identify and remediate the vulnerabilities or process failures that enabled the breach.
7. Consult legal and regulatory requirements for law-enforcement, regulator, partner, and affected-person notification.
8. Prepare accurate, plain-language communications and support guidance.
9. Verify remediation and monitor for further misuse or recurrence.
10. Record decisions, evidence, notifications, and follow-up actions.

## Completion criteria

The exposure is contained, investigation evidence is preserved, legal/notification decisions are documented, remediation is verified, and follow-up actions have owners.
