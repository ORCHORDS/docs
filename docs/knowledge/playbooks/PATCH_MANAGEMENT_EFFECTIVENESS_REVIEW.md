# Patch Management Effectiveness Review

## Procedure
1. Review patch intake, prioritization, testing, deployment, and verification.
2. Compare critical patch age with risk expectations.
3. Investigate failed, deferred, or unsupported systems.
4. Confirm emergency patch paths are controlled and evidenced.
5. Track material gaps and exceptions.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — SI family.
