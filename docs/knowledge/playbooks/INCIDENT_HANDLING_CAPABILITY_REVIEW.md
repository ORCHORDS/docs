# Incident Handling Capability Review

## Procedure
1. Review preparation, detection, analysis, containment, eradication, and recovery capabilities.
2. Verify tools, roles, contacts, and evidence sources are usable.
3. Review recent incidents and exercises for recurring weaknesses.
4. Assign improvements and owners.
5. Verify changes through exercise or incident evidence.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 IR family and SP 800-61r3.
