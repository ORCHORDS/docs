# Privileged Access Usage Review

## Procedure
1. Identify privileged roles and accounts in scope.
2. Compare usage with approved duties and change/incident records.
3. Review emergency or temporary elevation separately.
4. Investigate unexplained, excessive, or stale privilege use.
5. Remove or reduce unnecessary privilege and track exceptions.
6. Preserve review evidence and closure status.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — AC family.
