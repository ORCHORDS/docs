# Vendor Remote-Access Review Playbook

## Trigger

Use before granting, renewing, or materially expanding a vendor's remote access to business systems.

## Steps

1. Confirm the business owner, purpose, systems, data, and duration requested.
2. Verify the remote-access method is approved and supported.
3. Require named identities and MFA for sensitive access where available.
4. Reduce permissions to the minimum required for the vendor's task.
5. Check endpoint, encryption, logging, and monitoring requirements.
6. Confirm vendor contract terms cover relevant security obligations.
7. Set an explicit expiry or review date and an access-removal owner.
8. Record the approval decision and any conditions.
9. Revoke access promptly when the work or relationship ends.
10. Reassess after a vendor security incident or material scope change.

## Completion criteria

Remote access is attributable, least-privileged, time-bounded or periodically reviewed, protected by appropriate controls, and has a defined removal path.
