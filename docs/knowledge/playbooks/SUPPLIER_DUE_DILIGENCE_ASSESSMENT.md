# Supplier Due-Diligence Assessment Playbook

## Trigger

Use before onboarding a material ICT supplier and when a supplier's risk profile materially changes.

## Steps

1. Define the product/service and business dependency being assessed.
2. Gather public and supplier-provided evidence relevant to ownership/control, provenance, resilience, foundational cyber practices, and supply-chain tiers.
3. Record unknowns separately from confirmed findings.
4. Rate material risks according to the organization's risk method.
5. Define required mitigations, contract conditions, monitoring, or evidence refreshes.
6. Make an accountable approve, conditional-approve, reject, or reassess decision.
7. Set a review date or event-based trigger for reassessment.
8. Preserve the assessment and decision evidence.

## Escalation

Escalate suppliers that create critical concentration, unacceptable provenance uncertainty, weak foundational practices, or dependencies that cannot be adequately mitigated.

## Completion criteria

The acquisition decision is traceable to current evidence, material unknowns are explicit, mitigations have owners, and reassessment timing is defined.
