# Authenticator Lifecycle Review

## Procedure
1. Inventory authenticator types and lifecycle owners.
2. Review issuance, binding, reset, recovery, replacement, and revocation.
3. Verify compromised or lost authenticators are invalidated promptly.
4. Check dormant authenticators and recovery channels.
5. Record gaps, owners, deadlines, and closure evidence.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — IA family.
