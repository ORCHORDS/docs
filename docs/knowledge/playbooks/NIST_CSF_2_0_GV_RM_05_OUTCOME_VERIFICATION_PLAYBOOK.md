# NIST CSF 2.0 GV.RM-05 Outcome Verification Playbook

## Target outcome

**GV.RM-05:** Lines of communication across the organization are established for cybersecurity risks, including risks from suppliers and other third parties

## Preconditions

- a defined system, service, organizational process, or supplier relationship is in scope;
- an accountable owner is identified;
- current evidence can be inspected;
- the NIST outcome is applicable or requires an applicability decision.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the scope and owner.
2. Read the current NIST outcome and any relevant informative references.
3. Inventory the evidence that should demonstrate the outcome.
4. Inspect implementation and operating evidence rather than relying on declarations.
5. Exercise tests or review procedures appropriate to the outcome.
6. Record gaps and assign remediation owners.
7. Record justified non-applicability or exceptions separately.
8. Set the next event-driven or periodic verification trigger.

## Failure handling

If evidence contradicts the documented state, treat the outcome as unverified or partially verified, preserve the evidence, and route the gap through the appropriate remediation or risk process.

## Completion evidence

Scope, owner, source version, evidence reviewed, tests performed, findings, exceptions, remediation links, reviewer, and next trigger.
