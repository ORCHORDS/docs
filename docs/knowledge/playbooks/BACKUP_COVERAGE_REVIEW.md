# Backup Coverage Review

## Procedure
1. Compare critical systems/data with backup policy and job configuration.
2. Identify missing, stale, failed, or unprotected backups.
3. Review offsite/isolated protection where required.
4. Confirm representative restore evidence exists.
5. Remediate coverage gaps.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — CP family.
