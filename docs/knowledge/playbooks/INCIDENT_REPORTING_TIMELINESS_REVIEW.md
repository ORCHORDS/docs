# Incident Reporting Timeliness Review

## Procedure
1. Sample incidents or exercises from the review period.
2. Compare detection, escalation, and reporting times with requirements.
3. Investigate delayed handoffs, unclear ownership, or channel failures.
4. Update procedures and contacts.
5. Track corrective actions and retest.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 IR family and SP 800-61r3.
