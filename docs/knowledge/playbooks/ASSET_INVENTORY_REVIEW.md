# Asset Inventory Review Playbook

## Trigger
Use on a scheduled review cycle, after major environment changes, or when unmanaged/unknown assets are suspected.

## Inputs
- current asset and service inventory;
- ownership and classification data;
- discovery/monitoring outputs;
- retirement and procurement records.

## Steps
1. Define the review scope and authoritative inventory source.
2. Reconcile discovered hardware, software, services, accounts, and externally hosted dependencies with recorded inventory.
3. Investigate unknown, duplicate, unsupported, or ownerless assets.
4. Confirm business owner, technical owner, criticality, and data classification for material assets.
5. Remove or archive retired entries and record disposition evidence.
6. Escalate unmanaged, unsupported, or high-risk assets with accountable remediation.
7. Record material changes and review outcomes.

## Completion criteria
Inventory differences are reconciled, material assets have current ownership/classification, and unresolved gaps have owners and due dates.
