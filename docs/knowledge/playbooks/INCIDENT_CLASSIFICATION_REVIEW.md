# Incident Classification Review

## Procedure
1. Review incident categories and severity definitions.
2. Test representative scenarios against escalation thresholds.
3. Verify legal, regulatory, supplier, and customer notification triggers are considered.
4. Resolve ambiguous or overlapping classifications.
5. Update responder guidance and examples.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 IR family and SP 800-61r3.
