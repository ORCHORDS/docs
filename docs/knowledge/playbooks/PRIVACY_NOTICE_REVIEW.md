# Privacy Notice Review

## Trigger
New or materially changed data processing, product, service, or policy.

## Steps
1. Identify affected processing and audiences.
2. Compare notice statements with actual processing.
3. Check purpose, sharing, retention, and rights descriptions.
4. Record gaps and owners.
5. Approve and publish the corrected notice.

## Completion
Notice accurately reflects current processing and evidence is retained.

## Source
NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 PT controls.
