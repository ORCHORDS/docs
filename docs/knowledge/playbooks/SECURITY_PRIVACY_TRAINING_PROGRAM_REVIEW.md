# Security and Privacy Training Program Review

## Trigger

Use on the program's scheduled review cycle and after significant changes in threats, systems, roles, law, or observed staff behavior.

## Steps

1. Identify the audiences and role-specific learning needs.
2. Review current objectives, materials, delivery methods, and completion data.
3. Compare training topics with current organizational risks and recent incidents.
4. Check whether assessments measure understanding or behavior rather than attendance alone.
5. Identify groups with repeated gaps, missed training, or role changes.
6. Update materials and delivery cadence where needed.
7. Assign corrective learning and follow-up owners.
8. Record the review, changes, metrics, and next review date.

## Evidence

Keep versioned materials, attendance/completion evidence, assessment results, review decisions, and corrective actions.

## Completion criteria

The program reflects current risks and roles, material learning gaps have owners, and effectiveness can be measured over time.
