# MFA Coverage Review

## Procedure
1. Enumerate systems and user populations requiring strong authentication.
2. Compare policy requirements with actual MFA enforcement.
3. Review privileged, remote, recovery, and break-glass paths separately.
4. Identify bypasses, unenrolled users, and weak factors.
5. Remediate or formally track justified exceptions.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — IA family.
