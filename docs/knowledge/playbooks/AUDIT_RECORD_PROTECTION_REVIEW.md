# Audit Record Protection Review

## Procedure
1. Inventory audit record stores and administrators.
2. Review modification, deletion, and export permissions.
3. Verify integrity, backup, and separation-of-duties controls.
4. Test unauthorized alteration protections where feasible.
5. Remediate excessive access and weak protections.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — AU family.
