# Risk Assessment Refresh

## Procedure
1. Identify the change or review trigger.
2. Reassess affected assets, threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact.
3. Update treatments and residual risk.
4. Escalate newly material risk.
5. Preserve evidence and approval of the refreshed assessment.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — RA family.
