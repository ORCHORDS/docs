# Security Metrics Review Playbook

## Trigger
Use on the organization's measurement review cadence or when risk priorities, controls, data sources, or leadership information needs materially change.

## Inputs
- current security measures and definitions;
- targets/thresholds;
- data-source quality information;
- recent risk and incident trends;
- management decision needs.

## Steps
1. Confirm each measure has a documented decision purpose and accountable owner.
2. Check data completeness, timeliness, consistency, and calculation definitions.
3. Compare actual results with targets or thresholds and investigate material variance.
4. Determine whether each measure still reflects current risk priorities and produces useful decisions.
5. Add, modify, or retire measures where evidence shows poor utility or changed needs.
6. Record decisions, actions, owners, and due dates.
7. Preserve the review record so changes to measures remain traceable.

## Completion criteria
Measures are current, decision-useful, supported by reliable data, and linked to documented actions where needed.
