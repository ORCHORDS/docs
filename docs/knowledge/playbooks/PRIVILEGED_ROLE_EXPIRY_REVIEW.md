# Privileged Role Expiry Review

Review time-bound elevated roles before expiry or renewal. Confirm the original justification, current owner, scope, and whether a lower-privilege alternative now exists. Renew only with a documented need and new end date; otherwise remove and verify. Preserve reviewer, decision, expiry, and evidence references.

Sources: NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 AC family; SP 800-53A Rev. 5.
