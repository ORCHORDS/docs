# Contingency Communications Review

## Procedure
1. Identify communications required during disruption.
2. Verify current contacts and primary/alternate channels.
3. Include suppliers, responders, leadership, and customers where relevant.
4. Test representative fallback communications.
5. Correct stale or single-point-of-failure dependencies.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — CP family.
