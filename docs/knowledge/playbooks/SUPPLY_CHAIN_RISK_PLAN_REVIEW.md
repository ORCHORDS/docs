# Supply Chain Risk Plan Review

## Procedure
1. Identify critical suppliers, products, services, and dependencies.
2. Review supply-chain threats, ownership, and required controls.
3. Verify monitoring, incident, continuity, and exit expectations.
4. Reassess after supplier or architecture changes.
5. Track unresolved risk and escalation decisions.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — SR family; NIST CSF 2.0 Govern.
