# Post-Incident Review Playbook

## Trigger

Use after a material cybersecurity incident has been contained and recovery is stable enough for structured review.

## Inputs

- incident timeline and response record;
- technical and business impact evidence;
- containment, eradication, and recovery actions;
- communications and escalation records;
- unresolved risks and corrective actions.

## Steps

1. **Set the review scope.** Define which incident period, systems, decisions, and teams are included.
2. **Reconstruct the timeline.** Use evidence rather than memory alone to establish detection, escalation, containment, remediation, and recovery milestones.
3. **Identify contributing conditions.** Separate the initiating event from technical, process, communication, and governance factors that increased likelihood or impact.
4. **Assess the response.** Record what worked, what delayed response, where evidence was missing, and which assumptions proved wrong.
5. **Review recovery performance.** Compare actual restoration and data-recovery outcomes with relevant objectives and expectations.
6. **Create corrective actions.** Turn significant findings into specific actions with owners, priorities, due dates, and verification criteria.
7. **Update knowledge and procedures.** Revise playbooks, controls, training, detection, recovery procedures, and dependency information when evidence supports a change.
8. **Track to closure.** Do not close the review merely because the meeting occurred; verify corrective actions through the normal governance process.

## Review principles

Use a fact-based, learning-oriented review. The purpose is to improve resilience and response capability, not to replace accountability processes or hide material control failures.

## Completion criteria

The review is complete when the incident timeline and material findings are agreed, corrective actions have accountable owners and verification criteria, and required procedural or control updates are tracked.

## Sources

- NIST — SP 800-61 Rev. 3, Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final
- CISA — StopRansomware Guide: https://www.cisa.gov/stopransomware/ransomware-guide

## Scope note

Incident reviews can involve privileged, regulated, or sensitive information. Public or reusable versions should contain only sanitized, project-neutral lessons.
