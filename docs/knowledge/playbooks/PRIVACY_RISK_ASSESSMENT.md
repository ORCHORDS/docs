# Privacy Risk Assessment Playbook

## Trigger
Use before launching or materially changing a system, product, service, or data-processing activity that can affect individuals' privacy.

## Inputs
- processing purpose and data flows;
- data categories and affected individuals;
- third-party processing;
- existing privacy/security controls;
- applicable organizational risk criteria.

## Steps
1. Define scope, purpose, affected individuals, data, and processing activities.
2. Choose and document the privacy risk model and rating method.
3. Identify plausible privacy risk scenarios and affected individuals.
4. Assess likelihood/impact and existing controls.
5. Determine residual risk and select response: mitigate, accept, avoid, or transfer where appropriate.
6. Assign actions, owners, deadlines, and decision authority.
7. Record review triggers for material processing or risk changes.

## Completion criteria
Material privacy risks are identified, evaluated consistently, assigned a response, and linked to accountable actions.
