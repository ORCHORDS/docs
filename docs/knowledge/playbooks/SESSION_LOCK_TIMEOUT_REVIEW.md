# Session Lock and Timeout Review

## Procedure
1. Identify session-bearing systems in scope.
2. Compare inactivity lock, timeout, and termination behavior to policy.
3. Test browser, mobile, API, and administrative sessions where applicable.
4. Confirm reauthentication after sensitive or expired sessions.
5. Record exceptions and remediate weak settings.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — AC family.
