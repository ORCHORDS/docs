# Identity Proofing Review

## Procedure
1. Identify where identity proofing is required.
2. Review proofing evidence and verification methods.
3. Check remote and high-risk onboarding paths for stronger assurance.
4. Review fraud, duplication, and failed-proofing handling.
5. Track exceptions and corrective actions.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — IA family.
