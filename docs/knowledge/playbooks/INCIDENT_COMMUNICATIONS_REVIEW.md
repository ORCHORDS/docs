# Incident Communications Review

## Trigger
Run after material incidents and after exercises where stakeholder communication was part of the scenario.

## Purpose
Review whether incident communications were timely, accurate, audience-appropriate, and consistent.

## Steps
1. Collect the incident timeline and all material communications.
2. Identify intended audiences and required communication channels.
3. Compare message timing against incident milestones and escalation needs.
4. Check that confirmed facts, uncertainty, impact, and next steps were communicated clearly.
5. Identify inconsistent messages, stale updates, missing audiences, or unnecessary sensitive detail.
6. Record corrections and process improvements.
7. Update templates, contact lists, and escalation paths where needed.
8. Preserve evidence with the post-incident record.

## Completion criteria
- Communications timeline reconstructed.
- Gaps and inconsistencies identified.
- Corrective actions have owners and due dates.
- Contact/escalation references are current.
