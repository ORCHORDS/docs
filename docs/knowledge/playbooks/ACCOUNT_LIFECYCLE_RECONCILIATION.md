# Account Lifecycle Reconciliation

## Trigger
Run on a defined cadence and after major identity, HR, or platform changes.

## Procedure
1. Enumerate authoritative account and identity sources.
2. Compare active, disabled, suspended, and terminated identities across systems.
3. Identify orphaned, duplicate, stale, shared, or ownerless accounts.
4. Verify privileged and service-account ownership separately.
5. Record exceptions and assign remediation owners and deadlines.
6. Recheck closed actions and retain sanitized evidence.

## Completion criteria
All material mismatches are resolved or formally tracked.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 / Release 5.2.0 — AC and IA families.
