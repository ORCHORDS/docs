# Plan of Action and Milestones Review

## Procedure
1. Enumerate open remediation actions.
2. Confirm owner, priority, due date, and evidence for each.
3. Escalate overdue or repeatedly deferred high-risk items.
4. Close only with verifiable remediation evidence.
5. Preserve decisions and accepted residual risk.

## Source basis
- NIST Risk Management Framework and SP 800-53 CA family.
