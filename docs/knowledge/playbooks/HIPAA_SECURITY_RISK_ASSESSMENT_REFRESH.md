# HIPAA Security Rule Risk Assessment Refresh

## Purpose
Refresh the HIPAA Security Rule risk analysis and risk management on a defined cadence and after every material change affecting electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

## Procedure
1. Inventory ePHI assets: systems, applications, databases, storage, devices, paper, transmissions, vendors with Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).
2. Identify threats (NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 threat catalog; HHS OCR guidance) and vulnerabilities for each asset.
3. Determine likelihood and impact for each threat / vulnerability pair; produce risk ratings; align to the four safeguard categories (administrative, physical, technical).
4. Document risk treatment decisions (mitigate, transfer, accept, avoid) for every rated risk; capture acceptance rationale and sign-off.
5. Re-confirm addressable vs required specification decisions for each § 164.308–§ 164.312 standard; document why each addressable specification is implemented, addressed via alternative, or deemed not applicable.
6. Reconfirm BAA inventory and downstream subcontractor BAAs (§ 164.308(b)); refresh BAA contracts where required.
7. Reconfirm contingency plan (§ 164.308(a)(7)): data backup, disaster recovery, emergency mode operation; perform restore / failover exercises.
8. Capture incident-response plan harmonised with NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2; integrate HHS breach-notification rules (45 CFR §§ 164.400–414).
9. Update the risk assessment document; review and sign by the Security Official.

## Source basis
- 45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C (HIPAA Security Rule); Subpart E (Privacy Rule); §§ 164.400–414 (Breach Notification Rule).
- NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2; NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1; NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2.
- HHS / OCR risk-assessment guidance.
