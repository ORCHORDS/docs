# Risk Register Review

## Procedure
1. Review open risks and recent changes in systems, threats, suppliers, or obligations.
2. Add missing risks and retire invalid entries with rationale.
3. Reassess likelihood, impact, treatment, and residual risk.
4. Confirm accountable owners and next actions.
5. Escalate material untreated risk.

## Source basis
- NIST CSF 2.0 and SP 800-53 RA family.
