# Service Account Review

## Procedure
1. Inventory non-human accounts and owners.
2. Verify each account has a current purpose and least privilege.
3. Review credential storage, rotation, and revocation.
4. Identify stale, orphaned, shared, or interactive-use accounts.
5. Remove unnecessary accounts and track exceptions.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — AC and IA families.
