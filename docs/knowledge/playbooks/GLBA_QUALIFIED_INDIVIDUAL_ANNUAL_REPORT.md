# GLBA Qualified Individual Annual Report

## Purpose
Produce the annual information-security report to the board / governing body per the GLBA Safeguards Rule (16 CFR § 314.4(j)), signed by the Qualified Individual (QI).

## Procedure
1. Pull the current information security plan (ISP) version; confirm last review date and any in-flight changes.
2. Compile the year's risk-assessment findings and treatment decisions; align to § 314.4(b) elements.
3. Capture the status of each § 314.4 control: access controls, MFA scope, encryption in transit / at rest, data inventory, secure development, monitoring, training, disposal, change management, incident response.
4. Capture service-provider oversight (§ 314.4(i)): vendor inventory, due-diligence status, contractual safeguards, periodic assessment outcomes.
5. Capture the incident-response plan (§ 314.4(g)): incidents logged, response timelines, notification decisions (aligned to state breach-notification statutes and the FTC notification supplement where applicable).
6. Capture training completion rates (§ 314.4(h)) and any sanctions executed.
7. Capture effectiveness-assessment evidence (§ 314.4(d)): KPIs, KRIs, control-effectiveness results.
8. Produce the board / governing-body report: executive summary, KPIs, key findings, year-on-year trend, remediation roadmap, sign-off by QI.
9. Submit and present to the board / governing body; record approval and any follow-up actions in the minutes.

## Source basis
- 16 CFR Part 314 (FTC Safeguards Rule, as amended 2021).
- 12 CFR Part 30, Appendix B (interagency Safeguards Rule for depository institutions).
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5; NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1; NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2.
- State breach-notification statutes (CCPA / CPRA, NYDFS, etc.).
