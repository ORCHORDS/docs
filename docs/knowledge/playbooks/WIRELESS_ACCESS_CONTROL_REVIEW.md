# Wireless Access Control Review

## Procedure
1. Inventory authorized wireless networks and access points.
2. Verify approved authentication, encryption, and segmentation.
3. Detect unauthorized or unmanaged access points.
4. Review guest and administrative wireless access separately.
5. Remove obsolete configurations and track exceptions.
6. Retain sanitized review evidence.

## Source basis
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — AC and SC families.
