# Third-Party Offboarding Playbook

## Trigger
Use when a supplier, contractor, partner, or service-provider relationship ends, is suspended, or materially changes.

## Inputs
- contract/service scope;
- account and credential inventory;
- integrations and remote-access paths;
- data-return/deletion obligations;
- asset and incident records.

## Steps
1. Confirm relationship end/change date and accountable owner.
2. Identify all accounts, credentials, certificates, integrations, remote-access methods, and shared resources associated with the third party.
3. Disable or remove access and rotate shared secrets where needed.
4. Recover organization assets and disable integrations, routes, webhooks, or delegated permissions.
5. Execute contractual data return/deletion requirements and retain evidence.
6. Reconcile subprocessors, retained data, legal obligations, and open incidents/findings.
7. Record residual risks and obtain accountable acceptance where anything remains unresolved.

## Completion criteria
Access, credentials, integrations, assets, data, and continuing obligations are reconciled with retained evidence.
