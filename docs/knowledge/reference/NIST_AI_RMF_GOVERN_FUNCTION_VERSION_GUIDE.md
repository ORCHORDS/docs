## Scope

The Govern function establishes the organisational structures, policies,
processes, and culture for AI risk management. It underpins the other three
AI RMF functions (Map, Measure, Manage).

This guide captures the Govern subcategories, the AI culture and
accountability expectations, and how ORCHORDS profiles Govern.

## Plan

1. Define AI risk governance strategy at the executive level with clear
   mandate, scope, and decision rights.
2. Assign AI risk roles with documented accountability (AI owner, AI risk
   council, internal audit, legal).
3. Embed AI risk considerations into existing organisational risk and
   compliance committees.
4. Maintain a documented AI code of conduct with escalation and whistle
   blower protection.
5. Track Govern maturity through periodic internal reviews against ISO/IEC
   42001 plus AI RMF Playbook evidence.

## Inputs

- AI strategy and executive mandates.
- AI risk committee charter and minutes.
- AI policy library and version control.
- AI training records and culture metrics.
- Internal audit reports on AI controls.

## Implementation Notes

- Govern is a precondition to operating AI systems at ORCHORDS. No Map,
  Measure, or Manage evidence is accepted without an active Govern profile.
- Where Govern overlaps ISO/IEC 42001:2023, ORCHORDS maps the controls
  one-to-one and stores evidence in a single register.
- Cultural maturity (training, tone from the top, incident reporting
  frequency) is reported at the AIMS management review.
