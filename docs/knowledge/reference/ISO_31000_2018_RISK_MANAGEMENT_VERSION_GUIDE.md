# ISO 31000:2018 Risk Management Version Guide

## Scope

Reference card for ISO 31000:2018, *Risk management — Guidelines*. ISO 31000 is the umbrella risk-management standard referenced by ISO/IEC 27005 (information-security risk management), ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk management), ISO/IEC 27001, and many sector-specific standards. Profiles that govern risk-management policy should reference ISO 31000:2018 by version and bind it to the sector-specific companion standards.

## Plan

1. Reference ISO 31000:2018 by version whenever a profile governs a risk-management framework.
2. Establish the risk-management framework: mandate and commitment, design, implementation, evaluation, and improvement.
3. Establish the risk-management process: scope, context, criteria; risk assessment (identification, analysis, evaluation); risk treatment; monitoring and review; communication and consultation; recording and reporting.
4. Integrate with sector-specific companion standards (ISO/IEC 27005 for information security; ISO/IEC 23894 for AI).
5. Treat risk appetite and risk tolerance as explicit, board-approved statements.
6. Document deviations with the approver, scope, expiration, compensating controls, and review schedule.

## Inputs

- ISO 31000:2018 normative sections: 5 (principles), 6 (framework), 7 (process), annexes.
- ISO Guide 73 vocabulary.
- IEC 31010 risk-assessment techniques catalogue.
- Internal risk-management policy, risk-appetite statement, risk register, and risk-treatment plan.

## ORCHORDS Profile

ORCHORDS treats ISO 31000:2018 as the canonical umbrella reference for risk-management frameworks. Profiles that reference ISO 31000 should cite the version, identify the framework design in use, and bind to the sector-specific companion standards. A profile that references "risk management" without binding to a recognized framework is non-conformant.

Profiles that govern AI risk should bind to ISO/IEC 23894 in addition to ISO 31000. Profiles that govern information-security risk should bind to ISO/IEC 27005.

## Implementation Notes

- ISO 31000:2018 emphasizes the integration of risk management into the organization's overall governance, strategy, and decision-making rather than treating it as a separate compliance function.
- Risk appetite is a strategic statement; risk tolerance is an operational statement. Conflating them is a common defect.
- Risk-assessment techniques are catalogued in IEC 31010; choose techniques that match the risk type and the data available.
- Communication and consultation with stakeholders is a process step, not a one-time event.
- Recording and reporting supports the management review and the audit trail.
