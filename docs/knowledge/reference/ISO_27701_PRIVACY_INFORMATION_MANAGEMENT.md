# ISO/IEC 27701 Privacy Information Management Reference Card

## Profile

ISO/IEC 27701:2019 is an extension to ISO/IEC 27001 (ISMS) and ISO/IEC 27002 that provides a Privacy Information Management System (PIMS) framework and a set of PIMS-specific controls. The standard addresses the role of PII controller, PII processor, and the joint controller relationship; the controls cover data collection, processing, sharing, retention, and disposal. Profiles that govern personally identifiable information (PII) handling should cite ISO/IEC 27701 and bind to ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR, NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 privacy controls, and the data-classification guidance.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 27701:2019 was published in August 2019. The amendment ISO/IEC 27701:2019/A1:2024 added additional guidance for PII controllers and processors operating under multiple privacy regimes. No successor revision is published as of September 5, 2026.

## Governance pattern

1. Cite ISO/IEC 27701 in privacy policy, data-handling procedures, and PIMS documentation.
2. Establish the PIMS scope and the role(s) of the organization: PII controller, PII processor, or both.
3. Map ISO/IEC 27701 controls to ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls and to ISO/IEC 27002 implementation guidance.
4. Document the legal basis for processing for each PII category (for example, consent, contract, legal obligation).
5. Document cross-border transfer mechanisms (for example, Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, Binding Corporate Rules).
6. Document data-subject rights handling (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction).
7. Document data-breach response and notification procedures, binding to GDPR Article 33 where applicable.
8. Bind to ISO/IEC 27001 for the ISMS context.
9. Bind to GDPR for the EU regulatory context.
10. Bind to NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 privacy controls for the alignment with the NIST catalog.
11. Document deviations with approver, scope, expiration, compensating controls, and review schedule.

## PIMS-specific controls

- PII controller obligations: lawfulness of processing, data minimization, purpose limitation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality.
- PII processor obligations: processing only on documented instructions, ensuring confidentiality of personnel, subprocessor management, assistance with data-subject rights.
- Joint-controller relationships: defined roles and responsibilities, transparency to data subjects, contact point for data subjects.
- Privacy impact assessment (PIA) / Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for high-risk processing.
- Records of processing activities (RoPA) maintained by controllers and processors.

## Validation and evidence

Compliance evidence includes:

- PIMS scope statement and role definition.
- Statement of Applicability (SoA) mapping ISO/IEC 27701 controls to organizational controls.
- Records of processing activities (RoPA).
- Privacy impact assessment (PIA) or Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) records for high-risk processing.
- Data-breach response procedures and notification records.
- Data-subject rights handling records (access requests, erasure requests).
- Subprocessor list and data-processing agreements.

Evidence that omits the role definition, the SoA, or the records of processing activities does not establish ISO/IEC 27701 conformance.
