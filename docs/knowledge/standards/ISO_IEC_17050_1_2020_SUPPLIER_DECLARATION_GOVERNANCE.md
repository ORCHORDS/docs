# ISO/IEC 17050-1:2020 Supplier Declaration of Conformity Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 17050-1:2020, "Conformity assessment — Supplier's declaration of conformity — Part 1: General requirements," defines the general requirements for a supplier's declaration of conformity (SDoC). The standard specifies what information the supplier must provide, the requirements for supporting documentation, and the supplier's responsibility for the declaration. This article governs the application of ISO/IEC 17050-1 so a supplier's declarations of conformity meet the standard's requirements.

## Scope

The standard applies to any supplier making a declaration of conformity for a product, process, service, or system. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the supplier's declaration, the identification of the product, the identification of the relevant standards or regulations, the supporting documentation, and the supplier's responsibility for the declaration. It does not cover third-party certification; readers should consult ISO/IEC 17065 for that.

## Workflow

1. Identify the product, process, service, or system for which the supplier will make a declaration.
2. Identify the standards, regulations, or other normative documents against which the supplier is declaring conformity.
3. Prepare the declaration per the standard:
   - The declaration must identify the supplier (name, address, contact).
   - It must identify the product (model, batch, serial range, or other identifier).
   - It must identify the standards or regulations against which conformity is declared.
   - It must state the date and place of issue.
   - It must be signed by an authorized representative of the supplier.
4. Maintain supporting documentation:
   - The test results, inspection records, or other evidence supporting the declaration.
   - The records the supplier used to demonstrate conformity.
5. Retain the declaration and the supporting documentation per the standard and the applicable regulation.

## Controls and evidence

SDoC controls include the declaration document, the supporting test results, the inspection records, the supplier's authorization records, and the retention records. Each declaration should be traceable to the supporting evidence.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the declaration contains the required information, the supporting documentation is current and complete, the authorized representative has signed the declaration, and the retention discipline operates. Periodic review confirms the declaration remains accurate.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the declaration does not identify the product uniquely (correct: use a unique identifier such as model number or batch range); the standards are not specifically identified (correct: cite the standard number, edition, and date); the supporting documentation is missing (correct: maintain the supporting documentation per the standard); the declaration is signed by an unauthorized person (correct: verify the authorization of the signatory).

## Limitations

ISO/IEC 17050-1 is a standard for the supplier's declaration of conformity; it does not certify the product outside the supplier's declaration. The standard does not replace third-party certification where required by regulation. The standard does not address every product type; readers should consult the specific regulations for the product.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral standards use of ISO/IEC 17050-1:2020. It does not assert any specific supplier's conformance or claim any certification outcome.
