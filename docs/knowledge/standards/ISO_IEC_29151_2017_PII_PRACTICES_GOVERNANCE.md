# ISO/IEC 29151:2017 PII Protection Practices Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 29151:2017 establishes control objectives and controls for the protection of personally identifiable information (PII), based on ISO/IEC 27002 and aligned with ISO/IEC 27018. Governance ensures that organizations acting as PII controllers apply the control objectives, that implementation is consistent across business units, and that the controls are reviewed when the PII inventory changes.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 29151:2017 was published as a re-approval of ISO/IEC 29151:2013, retaining the same control objectives. The standard is a code of practice. ISO/IEC 29151 should be applied alongside ISO/IEC 27002 and ISO/IEC 27018 (for cloud PII processing). Validate the current ISO/IEC 29151 status before treating any control objective as a current requirement.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Maintain a PII inventory

Map every processing activity, the categories of PII processed, the legal basis, the recipients, the retention, and the cross-border transfer mechanism. Update the inventory when new processing activities are introduced.

### 2. Apply the control objectives

ISO/IEC 29151:2017 provides 26 control objectives grouped into the same categories as ISO/IEC 27002. Map the control objectives to your controls. Document implementation status and rationale for any objective not applied.

### 3. Manage consent

Where consent is the legal basis, design consent collection to be specific, informed, and freely given. Maintain a record of consent. Provide a withdrawal mechanism. Test consent flows annually.

### 4. Honor data subject rights

Implement data subject rights procedures: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, automated decision-making. Define response SLAs aligned with applicable law.

### 5. Apply pseudonymization and minimization

Apply pseudonymization and minimization techniques where appropriate. Document the technique, the residual risk, and the reversibility constraints.

### 6. Manage cross-border transfers

Document every cross-border transfer with the destination country, the transfer mechanism (adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules, derogation), and the supplementary measures.

### 7. Review and audit

Review the PII inventory and the control implementation at least annually. Audit PII processing activities. Report findings to senior management.

## Validation and evidence

- PII inventory.
- Statement of Applicability for ISO/IEC 29151.
- Consent record system.
- Data subject rights procedures with response evidence.
- Cross-border transfer register.
- Annual review and audit reports.

## Failure correction

Common defects include stale PII inventories, missing consent records, and slow data subject rights response. Corrective actions include an automated PII inventory check, a consent audit, and an SLA-breach report for data subject rights.

## Limitations

- ISO/IEC 29151 is a code of practice, not a certifiable management system standard.
- The standard does not override specific privacy regulations.
- Consent requirements vary by jurisdiction; tailor to applicable law.
- Cross-border transfer rules evolve; track EU, UK, and other regulator guidance.
