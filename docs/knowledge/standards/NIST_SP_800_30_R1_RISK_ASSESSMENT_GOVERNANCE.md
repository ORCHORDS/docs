# NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 Risk Assessment Governance

## Purpose

NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 (September 2012) provides guidance for conducting risk assessments of federal information systems and organizations. Governance ensures that ORCHORDS applies the threat, vulnerability, likelihood, impact, and risk determination process consistently across system, mission, and organizational assessments, and feeds the results into the SP 800-37 RMF.

## Current context and source status

SP 800-30 Rev. 1 is the current revision and is closely coupled with SP 800-39 (Risk Management Hierarchy) and SP 800-37 (RMF). Treat the threat and vulnerability catalogs as illustrative rather than exhaustive. Verify current revision status before relying on specific tables.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Prepare for the assessment

- Identify the purpose, scope, assumptions, and constraints of the assessment.
- Identify the information sources and the threat, vulnerability, and likelihood inputs.
- Define the risk model, analytic approach, and assessment approach (qualitative, quantitative, or semi-quantitative).

### 2. Conduct the assessment

- Identify threat sources and events using the catalog and current threat intelligence.
- Identify vulnerabilities and predisposing conditions affecting the system.
- Determine likelihood of occurrence and impact if the threat exploits the vulnerability.
- Determine risk as a function of threat, vulnerability, likelihood, and impact.

### 3. Communicate results

- Communicate the assessment results to the documented stakeholders.
- Document the assessment with assumptions, inputs, methodology, and findings.

### 4. Maintain the assessment

- Update the assessment on a documented cadence or when context changes materially.
- Feed results into the SP 800-37 RMF and into the enterprise risk register.

### 5. Risk determination tiers

- Apply Tier 1 (organization), Tier 2 (mission), and Tier 3 (system) perspectives as documented.
- Cross-reference findings across tiers to surface systemic risk.

## Validation and evidence

- Assessment plan with purpose, scope, inputs, and methodology.
- Threat and vulnerability catalogs used for the assessment.
- Risk determination records with likelihood, impact, and risk score.
- Communication records and stakeholder feedback.
- Update cadence and trigger records.

## Failure correction

Common defects include stale threat intelligence, missing tier-cross-references, and lack of assessment updates. Corrective actions include threat intelligence freshness check, tier-coverage audit, and assessment cadence review.
