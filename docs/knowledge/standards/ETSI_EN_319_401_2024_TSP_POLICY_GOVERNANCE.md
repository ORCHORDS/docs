# ETSI EN 319 401:2024 Trust Service Provider Policy Governance

## Purpose

ETSI EN 319 401:2024, "Electronic Signatures and Infrastructures (ESI); General Policy Requirements for Trust Service Providers," defines general policy requirements common to all trust service providers (TSPs) operating under the eIDAS Regulation (EU 910/2014). The standard covers general obligations (legal, regulatory, operational), TSP management, the policies and practices the TSP must publish, the audit and assessment framework, and the relationship between the TSP and the supervisory body. This article governs the application of EN 319 401 so a TSP's policies and operations align with the eIDAS requirements.

## Scope

The standard applies to all trust service providers operating under eIDAS, including providers of electronic signatures, electronic seals, time stamping, electronic registered delivery, and website authentication. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the general policy requirements, the TSP practice statement, the audit and assessment, the supervision framework, and the documentation of the TSP's policy. It does not cover the per-service requirements (readers should consult EN 319 411 for certificates, EN 319 421 for time stamps, EN 319 522 for electronic registered delivery, EN 319 612 for trust service status lists).

## Workflow

1. Establish the TSP's general policy: scope, the trust services provided, the eIDAS roles (qualified or non-qualified), the relationship to the supervisory body, and the legal context.
2. Apply the general obligations:
   - Legal and regulatory: operate within the applicable legal framework, including eIDAS and national law.
   - Information security: apply a documented information security system management.
   - Personnel: ensure competence and trustworthiness.
   - Operations: apply operational procedures and records.
   - Incident management: detect and respond to incidents; notify the supervisory body where required.
   - Termination: plan and document the termination of the trust service.
3. Document the TSP practice statement (PS) — the general policy applicable to all trust services.
4. Publish the practice statement and other relevant information (terms of use, certificates, status information).
5. Conduct the audit and assessment per the standard's framework:
   - Internal audit.
   - Self-assessment.
   - Conformity assessment by a conformity assessment body.
6. Cooperate with the supervisory body in its supervision of the TSP.

## Controls and evidence

TSP evidence includes the general policy, the practice statement, the operational records, the incident records, the audit records, the conformity assessment report, and the supervisory communications. Each trust service should be traceable through the general policy.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the general policy is documented and current, the practice statement is published, the operational obligations are met, the audit framework operates, and the supervisory body is informed as required. The conformity assessment confirms the TSP's eIDAS compliance.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the practice statement is not published or is outdated (correct: publish and update the practice statement per the standard); the audit is not performed (correct: schedule the audit per the standard); incident notification to the supervisory body is delayed (correct: rehearse the notification process); termination plan is missing (correct: develop the termination plan before it is needed).

## Limitations

ETSI EN 319 401 is a standard for TSPs under eIDAS; it does not certify the TSP outside the conformity assessment. The standard does not address every trust service (the per-service requirements apply); readers should consult the relevant per-service standard. The standard does not replace national law; the TSP remains subject to applicable law.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral standards use of ETSI EN 319 401:2024. It does not assert any specific TSP's conformance or claim any certification outcome.
