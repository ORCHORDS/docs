# NIST SP 800-100 R1 Information Security Handbook Governance

## Purpose

NIST SP 800-100 Rev. 1 (draft), "Information Security Handbook: A Guide for Managers," provides guidance for federal information security program managers on the development, implementation, and oversight of an information security program. The handbook covers governance, risk management, capital planning, awareness and training, contingency planning, incident response, access control, configuration management, and the system development life cycle. This article governs the application of SP 800-100 R1 so the handbook's guidance is applied systematically to a federal information security program.

## Scope

The handbook applies to federal information security program managers and the staff supporting them. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the handbook's chapters on governance, the system development life cycle, awareness and training, capital planning, incident response, and contingency planning. It does not cover the technical controls in detail; readers should consult the relevant SP 800 publications.

## Workflow

1. Establish the information security governance:
   - Roles and responsibilities.
   - Policies and procedures.
   - Oversight and reporting.
2. Apply the system development life cycle:
   - Initiation.
   - Development/acquisition.
   - Implementation.
   - Operation and maintenance.
   - Disposition.
3. Apply the handbook's guidance on awareness and training, capital planning, incident response, and contingency planning in coordination with the SP 800-53 controls and the SP 800-37 risk management framework.
4. Maintain the documentation: the policies, the plans, the records of training and exercises, the incident records, and the audit records.
5. Periodically review and update the information security program per the handbook and the applicable federal guidance.

## Controls and evidence

Handbook controls include the governance documentation, the SDLC artifacts, the awareness and training records, the capital planning records, the incident records, the contingency plan, and the audit records. The handbook's evidence base is the same as for any federal information security program: the system security plan, the contingency plan, the incident response plan, and the assessment reports.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the handbook's guidance is applied, the SDLC operates with security integrated, the awareness and training program is current, the incident response plan and contingency plan are tested, and the oversight function operates. Federal Information Security Modernization Act (FISMA) reporting and Office of Inspector General (OIG) audits confirm the program.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: awareness and training is one-time (correct: schedule recurring training and exercises); the contingency plan is not tested (correct: test the contingency plan annually); the SDLC is bypassed for system changes (correct: enforce the SDLC for all changes); the incident response plan is out of date (correct: review and update the plan per the handbook).

## Limitations

SP 800-100 R1 is a handbook for federal information security program managers; it does not specify technical controls. The handbook depends on the broader NIST SP 800 series; readers should consult the relevant publications for the technical controls. The handbook is not the same as the NIST SP 800-53 controls or the SP 800-37 risk management framework; readers should understand how the handbook fits with those publications.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral standards use of NIST SP 800-100 R1. It does not assert any specific program's conformance or claim any certification outcome.
