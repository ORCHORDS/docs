# ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Testing and Calibration Competence Governance

## Purpose

Govern the application of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (general requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories) so that testing results the studio produces or relies on are traceable, method-controlled, and defensible: produced by competent people using validated methods with measured uncertainty, rather than by ad-hoc procedure.

## Scope

Applies to every studio function that produces test or calibration results used for decisions — performance testing, security testing measurements, materials or device testing, and measurement activities. Covers method validation, uncertainty, traceability, and result reporting. Does not cover management system certification (17021-1) or inspection bodies (17020).

## Workflow

1. Define the laboratory's scope: which tests or calibrations are performed, by which methods (standard, modified, or in-house), and with what equipment; out-of-scope work is refused rather than improvised.
2. Validate every non-standard, modified, or in-house method before use per 17025 clause 7.2: validation records state the method's fitness for purpose, the validation approach, and the results.
3. Estimate and report measurement uncertainty per test where relevant; a result without uncertainty context invites over-confident decisions.
4. Maintain metrological traceability: equipment calibration chains to national standards where metrologically meaningful, recorded per instrument with calibration status visible at the point of use.
5. Control competence of personnel: qualification requirements per test method, training records, and authorization to perform specific tests; unauthorized personnel do not sign results.
6. Report results with required content per 17025 clause 7.8: identification, method, results with units, and where relevant uncertainty, sampling details, and deviations; results are reviewed and authorized before release.
7. Participate in proficiency testing or interlaboratory comparison where available for the test category, and treat unsatisfactory results as trigger for root cause analysis of the whole testing process.

## Controls and evidence

- Laboratory scope document listing tests, methods, and equipment.
- Method validation records for non-standard, modified, and in-house methods.
- Uncertainty budgets per test where relevant.
- Equipment calibration records with traceability chain and status visibility.
- Personnel qualification matrix with training records and authorizations.
- Result review and authorization records; proficiency testing participation and outcomes.

## Validation

- Sample 10 released results and confirm each contains the required report elements and an authorizing signature from competent personnel.
- Confirm every in-house or modified method in use has a validation record predating its use.
- Confirm equipment used in the sample has valid calibration status traceable through the chain.

## Failure correction

- **Result released without review or authorization** → quarantine the result, review retroactively, notify recipients of the correction, and close the release gate.
- **Method used without validation record** → stop testing, validate the method or revert to a validated one, and invalidate affected results where the method's fitness is uncertain.
- **Proficiency test unsatisfactory** → root-cause the testing process (method, equipment, personnel), correct, and re-verify before resuming result release for that test.

## Limitations

- 17025 governs testing and calibration laboratories; purely qualitative inspections fall under 17020.
- Accreditation to 17025 is method-specific; an accredited lab's results are trusted only within its accredited scope.
- Uncertainty estimation depth varies by measurement discipline; sector guidance governs the details.
