# ISO/IEC TS 27007:2020 Cybersecurity Controls Auditor Guidelines Governance

## 1. Scope

This card governs ORCHORDS adoption of ISO/IEC TS 27007:2020 ("Information technology — Security techniques — Guidelines for information security management systems auditing") for internal and external ISMS audits. It applies to first-party (internal), second-party (supplier), and third-party (certification) audits of the ORCHORDS ISMS.

## 2. Normative references

- ISO/IEC TS 27007:2020 (second edition).
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — ISMS requirements.
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 — Information security controls.
- ISO 19011:2018 — Guidelines for auditing management systems.
- ISO/IEC 27006-1:2024 — Requirements for bodies providing audit and certification.

## 3. Audit principles

1. **Integrity**: auditors remain impartial and disclose conflicts of interest.
2. **Fair presentation**: audit findings are supported by verifiable evidence.
3. **Due professional care**: auditors exercise judgment based on training, knowledge, and experience.
4. **Confidentiality**: audit evidence is protected with the same controls as the audited data.
5. **Evidence-based**: findings trace back to objective evidence captured during the audit.
6. **Risk-based**: the audit programme prioritises higher-risk controls and recent changes.

## 4. Audit programme structure

| Phase | Activity | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | Audit charter, scope, criteria, risk-based selection | Audit plan |
| Field | Evidence collection, interviews, sampling, observation | Working papers, evidence index |
| Report | Findings classified as NC, OFI, or conformant | Audit report |
| Follow-up | Verification of corrective actions | Closure notice |

## 5. Finding classification

- **Major nonconformity**: control objective unmet; requires management intervention and re-audit.
- **Minor nonconformity**: single control partially unmet; requires documented correction.
- **Opportunity for improvement (OFI)**: a suggested improvement, no obligation.
- **Conformant**: control objective met with sufficient evidence.

## 6. Sampling guidance

- For low-risk controls: 5-10 % sample of population.
- For high-risk controls: 25-50 % sample or full population for the period.
- Maintain a sampling rationale in the audit working papers.

## 7. Auditor competence

- Lead auditor: ISO 27001 Lead Auditor certification + ≥ 3 years of ISMS experience.
- Auditors: ISO 27001 Internal Auditor certification + ≥ 1 year of experience.
- Maintain a competency matrix and a continuing professional development log per auditor.
