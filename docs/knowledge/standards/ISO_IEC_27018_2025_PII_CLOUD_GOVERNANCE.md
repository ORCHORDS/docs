# ISO/IEC 27018:2025 Public Cloud PII Governance

## Status

ISO/IEC 27018:2025 is Edition 3 and was published in August 2025. It replaces the withdrawn ISO/IEC 27018:2019 edition.

Current privacy and cloud-processing guidance SHOULD cite the 2025 edition unless a historical control mapping explicitly requires an older edition.

## Scope

ISO/IEC 27018 provides guidance for protecting personally identifiable information in public cloud services when the cloud service provider acts as a PII processor.

The standard builds on ISO/IEC 27002 and aligns cloud-processing controls with privacy principles.

## Governance expectations

Evidence for a public-cloud PII processor SHOULD cover:

- Processing only under documented customer instructions.
- Transparency about relevant sub-processing and processing locations.
- Controlled disclosure of PII.
- Return, transfer, and secure deletion of PII.
- Access control and accountability for personnel handling PII.
- Logging and monitoring of security-relevant processing activity.
- Incident and breach responsibilities.
- Assistance needed for customer privacy obligations.

## Transition rule

Mappings that cite ISO/IEC 27018:2019 SHOULD be reviewed for the 2025 edition. A historical citation MAY remain when it is tied to a completed certification, contract, or assessment, but it must be labeled as historical.

## Relationship to other standards

Use ISO/IEC 27018 together with:

- ISO/IEC 27001 for security management.
- ISO/IEC 27002 for the general control framework.
- ISO/IEC 27017 for cloud-specific security controls.
- ISO/IEC 27701 for privacy information management.
