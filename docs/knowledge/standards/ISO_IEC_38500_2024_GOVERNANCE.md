# ISO/IEC 38500:2024 Corporate Governance of IT Governance

## 1. Scope

This card governs ORCHORDS adoption of ISO/IEC 38500:2024 ("Information technology — Governance of IT for the organization") for board-level and executive-level governance of information technology. It supersedes the 2015 edition and aligns with ISO/IEC 38500-1 (concepts) and ISO/IEC 38500-2 (framework and model).

## 2. Normative references

- ISO/IEC 38500:2024 (third edition).
- ISO/IEC 38505:2017 — Governance of data.
- ISO/IEC 38507:2022 — Governance implications of AI.
- ISO/IEC 30121:2015 — Governance of digital forensic risk framework.
- COBIT 2019 — ISACA's governance of enterprise IT framework (mapping reference).

## 3. Six core principles

1. **Responsibility**: individuals and groups understand and accept their responsibilities for IT and its impact.
2. **Strategy**: the organization's strategic use of IT is aligned with current and future business needs.
3. **Acquisition**: IT acquisitions are made for valid reasons, on appropriate terms, and at an acceptable cost.
4. **Performance**: IT performs as required to support the organization.
5. **Conformance**: IT complies with applicable laws, regulations, standards, and internal policies.
6. **Human behaviour**: IT policies, practices, and decisions respect human factors.

## 4. Governance model (Evaluate → Direct → Monitor)

| Step | Question | Typical artefact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluate | What is the current and future IT landscape? | IT strategy review |
| Direct | What must be done to align IT with the business? | Strategic plan, budgets, policies |
| Monitor | Are we delivering on the direction? | Performance reports, audits |

## 5. Board responsibilities

- Approve the IT strategy and the digital risk appetite annually.
- Receive quarterly dashboards covering IT performance, major incidents, audit findings, and cyber risk.
- Sponsor a rolling 3-year IT investment programme.
- Ensure that the Chief Information Officer has the authority and resources to deliver on the strategy.

## 6. Mapping to COBIT 2019

- Principle 1 (Responsibility) — COBIT EDM02 (Ensure Benefits Delivery).
- Principle 2 (Strategy) — COBIT EDM01 (Set Governance Framework).
- Principle 3 (Acquisition) — COBIT EDM04 (Ensure Resource Optimization).
- Principle 4 (Performance) — COBIT EDM03 (Ensure Risk Optimization).
- Principle 5 (Conformance) — COBIT MEA (Monitor, Evaluate, Assess).
- Principle 6 (Human behaviour) — COBIT APO07 (Manage Human Resources).
