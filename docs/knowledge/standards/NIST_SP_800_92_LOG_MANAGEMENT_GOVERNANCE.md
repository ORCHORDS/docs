# NIST SP 800-92 Cybersecurity Log Management Governance

## Status

NIST SP 800-92, Guide to Computer Security Log Management, is the current final publication and was published in September 2006.

NIST SP 800-92 Rev. 1, Cybersecurity Log Management Planning Guide, remains an Initial Public Draft. NIST states that it is addressing public comments on that draft.

Documentation MUST distinguish the final 2006 publication from the Rev. 1 draft and MUST NOT present the draft as final.

## Governance objective

A log-management program should ensure that useful cybersecurity records are generated, transmitted, stored, accessed, protected, retained, and disposed of through a deliberate organization-wide process.

## Required planning evidence

A log-management plan SHOULD identify:

1. Log sources and owners.
2. Security and operational use cases.
3. Collection and transport paths.
4. Time synchronization expectations.
5. Access restrictions.
6. Integrity protections.
7. Retention and disposal requirements.
8. Monitoring and analysis responsibilities.
9. Capacity and availability constraints.
10. Incident-response dependencies.

## Lifecycle rule

Treat log data as a managed information asset. Collection without retention, access, integrity, and disposal decisions is incomplete governance.

## Draft handling rule

The Rev. 1 draft MAY be used as forward-looking planning guidance when clearly labeled as draft. Normative claims that require a final NIST publication SHOULD remain anchored to the final SP 800-92 or another applicable final publication.
