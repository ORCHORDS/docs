# ISO 14064-1:2018 GHG Inventory Governance

## Purpose

Govern the application of ISO 14064-1:2018 (greenhouse gases — Part 1: specification with guidance at the organization level for quantification and reporting of greenhouse gas emissions and removals) so that the studio's organizational greenhouse gas inventory is built to a recognized specification: complete boundaries, consistent quantification, documented methodology, and disclosure that does not mislead.

## Scope

Applies to the studio's organizational GHG inventory covering direct emissions and the indirect emission categories the organization chooses to quantify. Covers boundary setting, quantification methodology, data quality, and reporting. Does not cover project-level quantification (14064-2), validation/verification bodies (14064-3, 14065), or product carbon footprinting (14067).

## Workflow

1. Set the organizational boundary per 14064-1's consolidation approach — control (operational or financial) or equity share — and apply it consistently to every consolidated entity; mixed approaches within one inventory are prohibited.
2. Establish the reporting boundary: direct emissions and indirect emissions from imported energy are quantified without exception, and each other indirect category (upstream and downstream per the standard's classification) is either quantified or excluded with a documented justification.
3. Quantify each emission source with a documented methodology: emission factor source, activity data source, and calculation approach recorded per source; methodology changes between years are disclosed as recalculations.
4. Manage data quality hierarchically: primary activity data preferred, estimates and proxies flagged with their basis; data quality limitations are stated in the report rather than hidden in footnotes.
5. Build the inventory with an uncertainty perspective: where uncertainty is quantified, report it; where it is not, state the qualitative limitations.
6. Apply the inventory's own recalculation policy: base-year and prior-year figures are restated when structural changes (acquisitions, methodology corrections, boundary changes) make them non-comparable, per a pre-defined significance threshold.
7. Disclose per the standard's reporting requirements: organization boundaries, emission data by category, methodologies, and a statement of the inventory's preparation basis; prepare for verification against 14064-3 where third-party assurance is sought.

## Controls and evidence

- Boundary decision record naming the consolidation approach and its consistent application.
- Reporting boundary register: each indirect category quantified or excluded with justification.
- Methodology documentation per emission source with emission factor and activity data provenance.
- Data quality assessment records and the report's data quality statement.
- Recalculation policy with significance thresholds and recalculation event records.
- GHG report prepared to the 14064-1 reporting requirements.

## Validation

- Confirm the consolidation approach is applied uniformly across all consolidated entities in the current inventory.
- Sample five emission sources and confirm each has a methodology record with emission factor and activity data provenance.
- Confirm every excluded indirect category carries a documented justification, and no excluded category is material without explanation.

## Failure correction

- **Boundary applied inconsistently across entities** → restate the inventory under one approach and disclose the restatement as a recalculation.
- **Emission source quantified without methodology record** → reconstruct the methodology from data provenance, document it, and re-verify the calculation.
- **Material exclusion without justification** → quantify the category or document the justification; silent exclusion of material categories is a reporting integrity failure.

## Limitations

- Organizational inventories depend on supplier and value-chain data the organization does not control; indirect categories carry higher uncertainty.
- Verification (14064-3) is separate: an unverified inventory may still be 14064-1-conformant in preparation but lacks third-party assurance.
- Sector-specific rules (e.g., GHG Protocol sector guidance, regulatory schemes) may impose additional requirements beyond 14064-1.
