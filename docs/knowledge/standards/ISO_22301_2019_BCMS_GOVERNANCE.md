# ISO 22301:2019 Business Continuity Management Systems Governance

## 1. Scope

This card governs the establishment, operation, and continual improvement of a Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) within ORCHORDS-managed environments in conformance with ISO 22301:2019. It applies to business continuity policy, impact analysis, risk treatment, continuity procedures, exercising, and management review for products and services that ORCHORDS operates or supports.

## 2. Normative references

- ISO 22301:2019 (Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems — Requirements).
- ISO 22300:2021 (Security and resilience — Vocabulary).
- ISO/TS 22317:2021 (Guidelines for business impact analysis).
- ISO/TS 22318 (Supply chain continuity).
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27031:2023.

## 3. Leadership and context

- Top management demonstrates leadership by integrating the BCMS with business strategy and resourcing continuity activities.
- The organisation determines the context of the organisation, the needs of interested parties, and the scope of the BCMS; these determinations are documented and reviewed annually.
- A business continuity policy is established, communicated, and available to interested parties as appropriate.

## 4. Planning elements

| Element | ORCHORDS artefact |
| --- | --- |
| Business impact analysis | Product and service BIA with recovery time and recovery point objectives. |
| Risk assessment | Disruption-risk register aligned with ISO 31000. |
| Continuity strategies | Documented strategies for people, technology, facilities, and suppliers. |
| Incident response linkage | Tie-in with ISO/IEC 27035-3 incident response operations. |

## 5. Support and operation

- Documented information is controlled, stored, and retained for the period required by regulation and contract.
- Competence, awareness, and communication requirements are met through training records and internal communications.
- Continuity procedures are documented for each critical product and service, including activation, operation, and stand-down criteria.

## 6. Performance evaluation

- Internal audits are conducted at planned intervals against the operative edition of ISO 22301.
- Management review considers audit results, incident outcomes, exercise results, and changes in context.
- Corrective actions are tracked to closure and verified for effectiveness.

## 7. Continual improvement

- Lessons learned from incidents, exercises, and audits feed the improvement plan.
- BIA outputs are refreshed when products, services, or critical dependencies change.
- Exercise scenarios are updated to reflect current threat intelligence and disruption scenarios.

## 8. Certification and assurance

- Certification is conducted by an accredited certification body operating under ISO/IEC 17021-1.
- The certification scope, statement of applicability, and surveillance dates are recorded in the BCMS register.
- Material changes to the scope or to the operative edition trigger an impact assessment and a transition plan.
