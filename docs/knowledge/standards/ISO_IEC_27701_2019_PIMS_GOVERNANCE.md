# ISO/IEC 27701:2019 Privacy Information Management Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 27701:2019 extends ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002 with privacy-specific requirements and controls, forming a Privacy Information Management System (PIMS). It addresses both PII controllers and PII processors. Governance ensures that the PIMS is integrated with the existing ISMS, that privacy controls are scoped correctly, and that certification reflects the actual PII processing scope.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 27701:2019 was published as the first edition. The standard is a certifiable extension to ISO/IEC 27001:2013 (and via ISO/IEC 27001:2022 amendments, to ISO/IEC 27001:2022). Certification bodies offer ISO/IEC 27701 certification alongside ISO/IEC 27001 certification. Verify the certification scope and the specific ISO/IEC 27001 version applicable to your certification.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Define PIMS scope

Define the PIMS scope as a subset of or equivalent to the ISMS scope. Document the role(s) the organization plays (PII controller, PII processor, or both). Map the applicable privacy regulations to the PIMS scope.

### 2. Apply the PIMS-specific requirements

ISO/IEC 27701 adds requirements to ISO/IEC 27001 clauses plus PIMS-specific clauses. Address each requirement with documented evidence: leadership commitment, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement.

### 3. Apply the PIMS-specific controls

The standard adds controls to ISO/IEC 27002 Annex A. Map each control to your implementation. Document the Statement of Applicability covering both ISO/IEC 27002 and the additional PIMS controls.

### 4. Conduct privacy risk assessment

Conduct a privacy risk assessment in addition to the information security risk assessment. Identify risks to data subjects, not just to the organization. Document the methodology and the outcomes.

### 5. Manage PII processing agreements

Maintain a register of PII processing agreements. For each agreement, record the parties, the role (controller/processor), the data categories, the purpose, the legal basis, and the transfer mechanism.

### 6. Manage data subject rights

Implement data subject rights procedures aligned with applicable law. Define response SLAs and escalation paths. Maintain evidence of each response.

### 7. Audit and review

Audit the PIMS separately or as part of the ISMS audit. Review PII processing activities at least annually. Report to senior management on PIMS performance.

## Validation and evidence

- PIMS scope document.
- Statement of Applicability covering ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27002, and ISO/IEC 27701.
- Privacy risk assessment report.
- PII processing agreement register.
- Data subject rights response log.
- PIMS audit and management review records.

## Failure correction

Common defects include PIMS scope that is narrower than actual PII processing, missing privacy risk assessments, and PII processing agreements without the required content. Corrective actions include a scope reconciliation review, a quarterly privacy risk assessment review, and an agreement-content checklist.

## Limitations

- ISO/IEC 27701 is an extension, not a standalone standard.
- The standard does not replace the need to comply with specific privacy regulations.
- Certification is contingent on ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
- The privacy controls are aligned with the 2013 ISO/IEC 27002 structure; verify mapping to 2022.
