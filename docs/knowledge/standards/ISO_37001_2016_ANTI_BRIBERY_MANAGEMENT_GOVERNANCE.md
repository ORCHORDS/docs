# ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System Governance

## Purpose

Govern the application of ISO 37001:2016 (anti-bribery management systems — requirements with guidance for use) so that the studio's anti-bribery controls operate as a management system: risk-based, with defined functions for bribery risk assessment, controls over gifts/hospitality and business partners, investigation handling, and continuous improvement — rather than as an isolated policy document.

## Scope

Applies to the studio's anti-bribery management system (ABMS) covering all business units and functions, including the activities of business partners acting on the studio's behalf. Covers risk assessment, control implementation, investigation, and review. Does not cover criminal law compliance determination (legal counsel's domain) or fraud controls beyond bribery.

## Workflow

1. Define the ABMS scope deliberately: activities, business units, and business partner categories covered; scope exclusions (e.g., certain jurisdictions) require documented justification and board-level approval.
2. Conduct bribery risk assessment per defined criteria: risk factors include jurisdiction, sector, transaction type, business partner exposure, and regulatory intensity; the assessment is refreshed on a defined cadence and on trigger events.
3. Implement due diligence proportionate to risk over business partners, agents, and intermediaries: risk-tiered depth, refreshed on cadence, with escalation for adverse findings.
4. Control gifts, hospitality, donations, and similar benefits through defined thresholds and approval chains; benefits above threshold without pre-approval are treated as control breaches regardless of intent.
5. Operate reporting and investigation: confidential reporting channels, protection against retaliation, investigation procedures with defined independence, and outcome records feeding back into risk assessment.
6. Measure and review: ABMS performance indicators (training completion, due diligence currency, investigation cycle times) reported to top management on the management review cadence.
7. Certify or self-declare deliberately: where third-party certification is pursued, the certification body operates under ISO/IEC 17021-1 and the audit treats the ABMS like any certified management system.

## Controls and evidence

- ABMS scope statement with exclusions and approval records.
- Bribery risk assessment with criteria, factors, refresh cadence, and trigger events.
- Risk-tiered due diligence records for business partners with refresh dates.
- Gifts and hospitality register with thresholds and approval chain records.
- Reporting channel operation records, investigation files, and retaliation-protection evidence.
- Management review inputs and ABMS performance indicators.

## Validation

- Sample 10 business partner relationships and confirm due diligence depth matches the risk tier and is current.
- Confirm gifts/hospitality entries above threshold carry pre-approval records.
- Confirm the risk assessment was refreshed on cadence and after trigger events in the period.

## Failure correction

- **Due diligence lapsed for active partner** → refresh immediately, assess the exposure during the lapse, and fix the refresh automation.
- **Above-threshold benefit without approval** → record the breach, investigate intent, apply disciplinary or remedial steps, and tighten the pre-approval gate.
- **Investigation independence compromised** → reassign the investigation, document the conflict, and review the procedure's independence safeguards.

## Limitations

- ISO 37001 addresses bribery; broader corruption and fraud typologies need additional controls (e.g., ISO 37301 compliance management).
- Legal definitions of improper advantage vary by jurisdiction; local legal input is required for control design.
- Certification does not certify absence of bribery; it certifies the management system's implementation.
