# ISO/IEC 31000:2018 Risk Management Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 31000:2018 provides principles, framework, and process for risk management. Governance ensures that ORCHORDS applies the principles and the six-stage process (communication and consultation, scope/context/criteria, risk assessment, risk treatment, monitoring and review, recording and reporting) consistently across business, security, and AI risk domains.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 31000:2018 supersedes the 2009 edition. The 2018 edition is principle-based rather than prescriptive and aligns with ISO/IEC 27005:2022 and ISO/IEC 23894:2023 for security and AI risk respectively. Verify current revision status before adopting a new revision.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Integration, structure, and customization

- Integrate risk management into all organizational activities.
- Customize the framework to the organization's context, risk profile, and culture.
- Apply the principles: integrated, structured, customized, inclusive, dynamic, best-available-information, human-and-cultural-factors, continual-improvement.

### 2. Scope, context, and criteria

- Define the scope of each risk activity and the internal and external context.
- Define risk criteria including impact, likelihood, and acceptance thresholds.

### 3. Risk assessment

- Identify risks across the in-scope activities, assets, and stakeholders.
- Analyze risks using the documented method for likelihood and consequence.
- Evaluate risks against criteria and prioritize for treatment.

### 4. Risk treatment

- Select treatment options: avoid, reduce, transfer, accept.
- Document treatment plans with owners, deadlines, and expected residual risk.
- Implement and validate the treatments.

### 5. Monitoring and review

- Monitor risks and treatments against criteria.
- Review risks when context changes and feed results into the next assessment cycle.

### 6. Recording and reporting

- Maintain a risk register with consistent fields.
- Report risk posture to the documented governance body on a documented cadence.

## Validation and evidence

- Risk policy and criteria with management approval.
- Risk register with current owners and treatments.
- Risk assessment reports and treatment status.
- Risk reports to governance with documented cadence.

## Failure correction

Common defects include inconsistent criteria, missing acceptance thresholds, and stale risk registers. Corrective actions include criteria completeness review, threshold alignment check, and register freshness audit.
