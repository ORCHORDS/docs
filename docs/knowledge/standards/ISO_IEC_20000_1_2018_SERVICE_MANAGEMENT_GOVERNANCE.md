# ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 Service Management System Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 specifies requirements for a service management system (SMS). The standard applies to organizations that manage services, including internal IT services and externally provided services. Governance ensures that the SMS scope reflects the actual service portfolio, that service management processes are integrated, and that continual improvement is documented.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 was published as the third edition, replacing ISO/IEC 20000-1:2011. The standard is a certifiable management system standard. Certification bodies offer ISO/IEC 20000-1 certification. Verify the current certification scope and the certified processes before treating any clause identifier as a current requirement.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Define the SMS scope

Define the scope of the SMS: which services, which organizational units, which locations. Document the scope and the boundaries.

### 2. Establish service management policy

Adopt a service management policy aligned with the organization's strategic direction. Document the policy. Communicate the policy.

### 3. Plan the SMS

Establish service management objectives. Plan actions to achieve them. Document the plan. Provide resources.

### 4. Implement service management processes

ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 includes clauses on service portfolio, service catalogue, service level management, service request management, incident management, problem management, change management, release management, configuration management, and others. Implement each process with documented procedures, roles, and records.

### 5. Document services

Maintain a service catalogue that describes each service, the service level, the owner, the customer, and the dependencies. Maintain a service portfolio that describes the strategic view of services.

### 6. Conduct service reviews

Conduct regular service reviews with customers. Review performance against service levels. Identify improvement opportunities.

### 7. Internal audit and management review

Conduct internal audits of the SMS. Conduct management reviews at planned intervals. Document findings and follow-up actions.

### 8. Continual improvement

Maintain a continual improvement register. Track improvement actions to completion. Use assessment and review results to drive improvement.

## Validation and evidence

- SMS scope document.
- Service management policy.
- Service catalogue and portfolio.
- Service level agreements and reports.
- Internal audit reports.
- Management review minutes.
- Continual improvement register.

## Failure correction

Common defects include SMS scope that doesn't match reality, missing service reviews, and improvement actions that don't close. Corrective actions include a scope reconciliation, a service review schedule, and an improvement action closure review.

## Limitations

- ISO/IEC 20000-1 is a management system standard; it does not prescribe specific tools.
- The standard is process-focused; outcomes depend on implementation quality.
- Some clauses overlap with ITIL; align carefully.
- Certification is a snapshot; ongoing conformance requires maintenance.
