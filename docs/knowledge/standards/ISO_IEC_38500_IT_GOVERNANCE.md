# ISO/IEC 38500:2015 IT Governance Governance

## Scope

This card governs how `orchords-docs` evaluates IT governance against ISO/IEC 38500:2015. It is the reference input for any KB card that touches board-level IT oversight, accountability, or the six principles.

## Why this card exists

ISO/IEC 38500:2015 is the canonical IT governance standard, superseding the AS8015:2008 Australian interim standard. Without an explicit card, the KB cites IT governance practices that do not align with the six principles (responsibility, strategy, acquisition, performance, conformance, human behavior).

## Six principles

ISO/IEC 38500 defines six principles:

1. **Responsibility** — clear accountability.
2. **Strategy** — IT aligned with strategy.
3. **Acquisition** — acquired responsibly.
4. **Performance** — fit for purpose.
5. **Conformance** — compliant with rules.
6. **Human behavior** — respectful of human factors.

## Model — Evaluate, Direct, Monitor

The governance model uses three tasks:

- **Evaluate** — assess current and future use of IT.
- **Direct** — assign responsibilities and direction.
- **Monitor** — ensure compliance and performance.

These three tasks apply at every level of the organization.
