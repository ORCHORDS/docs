# ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015 Certification Body Audit Governance

## Purpose

Govern the studio's engagement with certification bodies auditing under ISO/IEC 17021-1:2015 (conformity assessment — requirements for bodies certifying management systems) so that certification audits are planned deliberately, competence of the audit team is verified rather than assumed, and audit findings are resolved through the certification body's own rules.

## Scope

Applies to every management system certification the studio holds or pursues where the certification body operates under ISO/IEC 17021-1 (ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 22301, ISO 14001 certifications and their sector-specific derivatives). Covers audit planning, team competence, conduct, and finding resolution. Does not cover the management systems themselves or accreditation of the certification body (ISO/IEC 17011).

## Workflow

1. Verify the certification body's accreditation scope before contracting: the accreditation (by an ISO/IEC 17011 accreditation body) must cover the standard being certified and the sector; accreditation outside scope means the certificate is not worth its paper.
2. Confirm the audit team's competence per 17021-1 clause 6: the team must have competence in the standard, the sector, and audit methodology; request and review team composition before the audit window.
3. Plan the audit programme across the certification cycle (initial stage 1 and 2, surveillance years 1 and 2, recertification) so each audit's scope and objectives are known in advance and evidence preparation is scheduled, not scrambled.
4. Manage audit conduct: the studio appoints an audit coordinator, controls access to evidence, and records what was shown; open-ended access without a coordination record is prohibited.
5. Classify findings per the certification body's scheme: nonconformity (major/minor) and opportunities for improvement; each nonconformity gets root cause analysis, correction, and corrective action within the body's deadline.
6. Track certificate status changes (grant, maintain, suspend, withdraw) and record the conditions attached; suspension conditions trigger an internal escalation to management review.
7. Feed audit results into the certified management system's own improvement loop: findings, evidence gaps, and auditor observations enter the internal audit programme and management review inputs.

## Controls and evidence

- Accreditation scope verification record for each certification body engagement.
- Audit team competence review records per audit.
- Multi-year audit programme plan with scope per audit.
- Audit coordination log: what was shown, to whom, when.
- Nonconformity records with root cause, correction, corrective action, and closure evidence.
- Certificate status register with conditions and dates.

## Validation

- Confirm the current certification body's accreditation still covers the standard and sector held.
- Sample nonconformities from the last audit and confirm each closed with root cause evidence within deadline.
- Confirm the audit programme plan covers the full cycle with no unexplained gaps.

## Failure correction

- **Accreditation lapsed or out of scope** → escalate to management review, engage the body on remediation, and prepare a transition plan (new body or accreditation restoration) before certificate expiry affects commitments.
- **Nonconformity closed without root cause evidence** → reopen internally, redo the analysis, and submit the corrected response.
- **Audit team competence gap identified mid-audit** → raise with the certification body per its complaint process and record the issue; do not accept findings from demonstrably unqualified auditors without challenge.

## Limitations

- Certification audits sample; they do not certify every process instance, and a certificate is not a guarantee of conformity at any moment.
- 17021-1 governs bodies certifying management systems; product and personnel certification use 17065 and 17024 respectively with different rules.
- Accreditation verification is point-in-time; accreditation status can change between audits.
