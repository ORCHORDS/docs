# IEEE 7000-2021 Ethical Value-Based Engineering Governance

## Purpose

IEEE 7000-2021 provides a model process for addressing ethical concerns during system design. The standard extends IEEE 12207 (software life cycle) with explicit ethical consideration activities. Governance ensures that ethical considerations are integrated into the system design process, that value-based requirements are captured, and that ethical reviews are documented.

## Current context and source status

IEEE 7000-2021 was published as the first edition. The standard is a process model, not a certifiable standard. It is supported by the IEEE 7000 series of standards addressing ethical concerns in autonomous systems, transparency, bias, and child-student data. Verify the current IEEE 7000 series status before treating any standard identifier as a current requirement.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Adopt the value-based engineering process

Adopt the IEEE 7000-2021 process model for systems where ethical concerns are material. Examples include AI systems, biometric systems, autonomous systems, and systems processing sensitive personal data.

### 2. Identify stakeholders and values

Identify stakeholders (users, non-users, vulnerable groups, society) and the values that the system should respect (privacy, autonomy, fairness, transparency, accountability). Document the stakeholder-value map.

### 3. Translate values into requirements

Translate each value into explicit system requirements. For example, the value of transparency translates into explainability requirements, logging requirements, and disclosure requirements.

### 4. Document the ethical risk register

Maintain an ethical risk register that records:

- ethical concern;
- affected stakeholders;
- value at risk;
- likelihood and impact;
- mitigation;
- residual ethical risk;
- owner;
- review date.

### 5. Conduct ethical reviews

Conduct ethical reviews at key project milestones. Include diverse stakeholders. Document the review findings and the decisions made.

### 6. Audit and update

Audit the ethical risk register periodically. Update when context changes, when incidents occur, or when new ethical concerns emerge.

### 7. Report to governance

Report ethical risks and ethical reviews to senior management. For systems with material ethical impact, report to the board.

## Validation and evidence

- Stakeholder-value map.
- Ethical risk register.
- Value-based requirements specification.
- Ethical review records.
- Senior management reports.

## Failure correction

Common defects include stakeholder maps that omit vulnerable groups, ethical risks without owners, and ethical reviews that are not documented. Corrective actions include an inclusive stakeholder workshop, an owner assignment for every ethical risk, and a mandatory review template.

## Limitations

- IEEE 7000-2021 is a process model, not a certifiable standard.
- Translating values into requirements is qualitative and may produce disputes.
- The standard does not prescribe specific ethical theories.
- Ethical risks evolve with societal norms; the process requires ongoing attention.
