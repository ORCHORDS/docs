# NIST CSF 2.0 ID.RA-04 Outcome Governance Profile

## Governed outcome

**ID.RA-04:** Potential impacts and likelihoods of threats exploiting vulnerabilities are identified and recorded

## Governance requirements

1. Applicability must be decided for a defined organizational or system scope.
2. An accountable owner must be identified when the outcome applies.
3. Local requirements derived from the outcome must be testable or reviewable.
4. Evidence must be distinguishable from policy intent and planned work.
5. Exceptions must record rationale, owner, compensating measures when relevant, and a review trigger.
6. Material changes to scope, dependencies, technology, suppliers, mission, or threats trigger reconsideration.
7. Public statements must not imply certification or implementation solely from the existence of this profile.

## Review record

Record scope, applicability, owner, evidence sources, current status, unresolved gaps, exceptions, and next review trigger.

## Status boundary

The NIST CSF describes cybersecurity outcomes rather than prescribing one implementation. This profile therefore governs evidence and decision quality, not a single technical design.
