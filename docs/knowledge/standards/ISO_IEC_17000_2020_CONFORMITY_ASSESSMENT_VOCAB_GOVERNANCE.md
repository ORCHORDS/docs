# ISO/IEC 17000:2020 Conformity Assessment Vocabulary Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 17000:2020, "Conformity assessment — Vocabulary and general principles," defines the vocabulary used across the ISO/IEC 17000 series of conformity assessment standards. The standard provides the terminology for conformity assessment bodies, conformity assessment schemes, the different types of conformity assessment (testing, inspection, certification, accreditation), and the principles of conformity assessment. This article governs the application of ISO/IEC 17000 so vocabulary across conformity assessment activities is consistent.

## Scope

The standard applies to all conformity assessment activities covered by the ISO/IEC 17000 series. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the vocabulary for conformity assessment bodies, the types of conformity assessment, the terminology for the conformity assessment process, and the relationship between accreditation, certification, testing, and inspection. It does not cover the substantive requirements for any specific conformity assessment scheme; readers should consult the relevant standards.

## Workflow

1. Identify the conformity assessment vocabulary to be used in the organization's documentation:
   - Conformity assessment body (CAB), accreditation body, certification body, inspection body, testing laboratory.
   - Conformity assessment scheme, certification scheme, accreditation scheme.
   - First-party, second-party, and third-party conformity assessment.
   - Surveillance, audit, assessment, and review.
2. Apply the vocabulary consistently across the organization's policies, procedures, and external communications.
3. Align the organization's vocabulary with the relevant ISO/IEC 17000 series standards (17011 for accreditation bodies, 17020 for inspection bodies, 17025 for testing and calibration laboratories, 17050 for supplier's declaration of conformity, 17065 for certification bodies).
4. Document the vocabulary used and the standards it aligns with.

## Controls and evidence

Vocabulary controls include the documented vocabulary, the cross-references to the ISO/IEC 17000 series, and the policies and procedures that use the vocabulary. Each document should use the vocabulary consistently.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the vocabulary is consistent with ISO/IEC 17000, the organization's documentation aligns with the relevant 17000 series standards, and the vocabulary is applied across the organization. Internal review and external audit confirm the consistency.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the vocabulary is inconsistent across documents (correct: apply ISO/IEC 17000 vocabulary uniformly); the organization's terminology conflicts with the standard (correct: align with the standard); new terms are introduced without cross-reference (correct: cross-reference new terms to ISO/IEC 17000 or document the deviation).

## Limitations

ISO/IEC 17000 is a vocabulary standard; it does not specify substantive requirements. The standard does not cover every conformity assessment type; readers should consult the relevant standards for each. The standard is not a substitute for the relevant substantive standard.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral standards use of ISO/IEC 17000:2020. It does not assert any specific organization's conformance or claim any certification outcome.
