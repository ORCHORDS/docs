# ISO/IEC 27035-3:2020 Incident Response Exercises Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 27035-3:2020, *Information technology — Information security incident management — Part 3: Guidelines for ICT incident response operations*, supplements ISO/IEC 27035-1 with operational exercise guidance. The 2020 publication remains current as of September 2026. Profiles that operate an incident-response exercise programme should reference ISO/IEC 27035-3 and bind to the broader ISO/IEC 27035 series alongside HSEEP, NIST SP 800-84, and ISO 22398.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 27035-3:2020 is the current edition and remains in force; no successor revision is published as of September 2026. ISO/IEC 27035-1 was updated in 2023 and ISO/IEC 27035-2 was updated in 2023, but ISO/IEC 27035-3 was last revised in 2020. Profiles that integrate the three-part stack should call out that 27035-3 is the older revision.

## Governance workflow and controls

1. Establish the exercise programme: scope, objectives, frequency, scenarios, participants, success criteria, and the link to the incident-management plan.
2. Exercise types: tabletop (discussion-based), walkthrough (procedure walk-through), simulation (role-play with partial tooling), full rehearsal (live, real-tooling). Match the exercise type to the learning objective.
3. Scenario design: realistic, scoped, time-boxed, and aligned to the highest-likelihood incident scenarios in the threat model.
4. Exercise conduct: facilitator, observers, role players, injects, communications discipline, and safety rails that prevent production damage.
5. Exercise evaluation: observer notes, participant feedback, success criteria scoring, deviation log, and findings.
6. Exercise improvement: corrective actions, updates to the incident-management plan, updates to detection and response tooling, and updates to the training programme.
7. Treat exercises as a control with their own evidence: schedule, attendance, scenarios, outputs, and corrective-action tracking.

## Validation and evidence

- Exercise schedule with frequency, types, and scope.
- Scenario library aligned with the threat model and incident-management plan.
- Exercise outputs: facilitator notes, observer notes, participant feedback, deviation log, scoring, and findings.
- Corrective-action tracking that shows how exercise findings feed into the incident-management plan, detection tooling, and training.
- Periodic exercise debrief with named accountable owners and timelines.

Evidence that omits the corrective-action tracking or the link to the incident-management plan does not establish ISO/IEC 27035-3 conformance.

## Failure correction

Common defects include exercises that are not realistic, lack observer notes, or produce no corrective-action tracking. Corrective actions include scenario review against recent threat-model updates, mandatory observer coverage, and binding corrective-action tracking to the same workflow as audit findings.
