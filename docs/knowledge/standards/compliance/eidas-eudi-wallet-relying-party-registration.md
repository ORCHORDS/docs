# eIDAS EUDI-wallet relying-party registration

**Issue:** The amended eIDAS framework creates European Digital Identity Wallet roles and relying-party requirements whose operation depends on implementing acts and national registration.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls and implementation

Classify relying-party role, requested attributes/purpose, registration/certificate requirements, minimization, consent and withdrawal; track implementing acts.

## Tests

Unregistered party, excess attribute request, expired certificate, wallet refusal, pseudonymous flow, audit export.

## Gotchas

Wallet availability does not authorize collecting every attribute; applicability and rollout require legal review.
