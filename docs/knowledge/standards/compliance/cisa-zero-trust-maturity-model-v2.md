# CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model Version 2.0

## Publication and scope

This article operationalizes **CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model Version 2.0**. It describes governance evidence and decisions; it does not make the publication mandatory or claim certification. Applicability depends on the organization, system, contract, and legal context.

## Five pillars and maturity stages

ZTMM 2.0 uses Identity, Devices, Networks, Applications and Workloads, and Data pillars. Cross-cutting capabilities are Visibility and Analytics, Automation and Orchestration, and Governance. Each pillar progresses through Traditional, Initial, Advanced, and Optimal stages; maturity is described by functions within pillars rather than one enterprise score.

## Publication-specific workflow

Assess each function against observed behavior; capture Traditional-to-Optimal current states without averaging away gaps; define target states based on mission risk; sequence identity, inventory, telemetry, policy, and enforcement dependencies; fund a roadmap; pilot changes; measure deny decisions and policy drift; reassess pillar functions after implementation.

Assign named owners for each decision and define review triggers. Tailor implementation to mission impact and architecture, but preserve the publication's named concepts so reviewers can trace local practice back to the source. Document assumptions, exclusions, inherited capabilities, and residual risk rather than presenting partial coverage as full implementation.

## Evidence to retain

Keep function-level ratings, evidence links, pillar dependencies, target rationale, architecture, telemetry coverage, automated response safeguards, policy tests, roadmap milestones, funding decisions, exceptions, and reassessment results.

Evidence must identify scope, collection date, source, owner, and covered population. Preserve raw results separately from interpretation. When remediation occurs, retain the original finding and append verification rather than rewriting history.

## Review and metrics

Review after material system, supplier, threat, mission, or organizational changes and at the stated document cycle. Metrics must include denominators and blind spots. Track overdue high-impact decisions, evidence age, exceptions approaching expiry, failed tests, and time to verified closure. Management review should focus on consequences and unresolved risk, not a context-free completion percentage.

## Failure modes

Do not declare the whole organization Advanced from one strong pillar, treat product purchase as maturity, jump to automation without reliable data, ignore governance, or select Optimal as a universal target without mission justification.

Also avoid unsupported claims based only on policy text, a product purchase, or one convenience sample. If evidence is unavailable, record the unknown, affected scope, interim safeguard, accountable owner, and decision deadline.
