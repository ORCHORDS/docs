# ISO IEC 27701 2025 Standalone PIMS Transition

**Issue:** Organizations using the withdrawn 2019 privacy extension can misstate their baseline after ISO IEC 27701 became a standalone management-system standard in its 2025 second edition.

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls

- Record ISO/IEC 27701:2025 Edition 2 as the current published baseline and mark ISO/IEC 27701:2019 withdrawn.
- Define PIMS scope, interested parties, controller and processor roles, privacy objectives, accountable owners, and documented information.
- Map existing ISMS-integrated privacy controls to the standalone PIMS requirements without assuming the old extension structure remains sufficient.
- Plan transition through gap assessment, risk treatment, competence, operational controls, internal audit, management review, and corrective action.
- Align the PIMS with ISO/IEC 27001 where useful while preserving its ability to operate as an independent management system.

## Verification

- Trace sampled PII processing to controller or processor responsibility, risk, control, monitoring, and improvement evidence.
- Audit every certification or alignment statement for the cited edition and assessed scope.
- Run a formal 2019-to-2025 gap assessment and verify closure evidence before updating public claims.

## Gotchas

- Confirm the cited feature or standard edition remains current before relying on it.
- Keep secrets, personal data, and restricted evidence out of examples and logs.
- Reassess after scope, implementation, or policy changes.
