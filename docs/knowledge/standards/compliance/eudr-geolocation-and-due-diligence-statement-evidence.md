# EUDR Geolocation and Due-Diligence Statement Evidence

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 requires covered commodities and products to be deforestation-free, legally produced, and covered by due diligence, with plot geolocation central to traceability.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls
- Determine commodity, product, operator/trader role, country-risk status, and applicable dates with counsel.
- Link product lots to production-plot geolocation, production date/range, supplier identity, legality evidence, and risk assessment.
- Preserve the due-diligence statement reference and the evidence supporting negligible-risk conclusions.
- Prevent mixing or substitution from breaking lot-to-plot traceability.
- Reassess after supplier, origin, boundary, risk classification, or legal changes.

## Verification
- Trace sampled market units back to all relevant plots and source documents.
- Test polygon/point validation, coordinate precision, impossible locations, duplicates, and mass balance.
- Reproduce a submitted statement from retained evidence.

## Gotchas
A supplier declaration alone is not the operator's due diligence. Applicability dates and amendments must be checked against the consolidated text.
