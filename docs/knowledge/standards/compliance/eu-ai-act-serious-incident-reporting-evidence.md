# EU AI Act serious-incident reporting evidence

**Issue:** The AI Act establishes serious-incident reporting duties for providers of high-risk AI systems, with role, timing, and transition details requiring legal assessment.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls and implementation

Map system/classification/role, awareness clock, incident criteria, authority, evidence custody, corrective action and customer communication.

## Tests

Near miss, death/harm, rights impact, incomplete initial report, correction, vendor escalation.

## Gotchas

Operational severity and statutory serious incident are not interchangeable.
