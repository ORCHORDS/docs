# ccpa-privacy-policy-requirements

**Issue:** Structuring a CCPA/CPRA-compliant privacy policy for California consumers
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** documented

## Symptom / Context
CCPA (amended by CPRA effective 2023) requires businesses meeting revenue or data-volume thresholds to maintain a comprehensive privacy policy disclosing 12-month data practices and consumer rights.

## Pattern / Solution
Required sections:

1. Categories of personal information collected and purposes
   - Use CCPA statutory categories: identifiers, commercial info, internet activity, geolocation, biometric, inferences, sensitive PI
   - State business or commercial purpose for each category
   - Disclose if sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising

2. Consumer Rights section:
   - Right to Know (categories and specific pieces)
   - Right to Delete
   - Right to Correct
   - Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing (must include "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link)
   - Right to Limit Use of Sensitive PI
   - Right to Non-Discrimination

3. Submission methods: email, toll-free number, web form
4. Response timeframe: 45 days (extendable 45 days with notice)
5. Verification process description

Sensitive PI requiring opt-in or limitation notice: SSN, financial account credentials, precise geolocation, racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs, union membership, private communications, health data, sexual orientation, biometric for identification purposes.

Update policy at least annually. Date it.

## Gotchas
- "Sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising counts as sale even without money changing hands
- Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal must be honored as opt-out
- CPPA (not AG) now enforces CCPA/CPRA — enforcement started July 2023
- Employees and B2B data now have full CCPA/CPRA protections as of Jan 2023
