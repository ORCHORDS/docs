# California CPPA Cybersecurity-Audit Evidence Boundary

**Issue:** Businesses preparing for California privacy cybersecurity-audit requirements need reproducible scope, evidence, and executive attestation rather than a generic annual penetration-test report.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls

- Determine applicability, threshold dates, and final regulatory obligations from current CPPA regulations with counsel.
- Define the audit boundary across personal-information processing, service providers, systems, policies, incidents, and risk governance.
- Maintain evidence provenance, sampling rationale, exceptions, remediation ownership, and independence assessment.
- Keep regulatory submissions separate from privileged internal analysis according to counsel-approved handling.

## Verification

- Trace sampled audit conclusions to current technical and governance evidence.
- Reperform key controls and verify exceptions have owners and expiry dates.
- Test acquired systems and service-provider data flows for inclusion in the audit population.

## Gotchas

- A SOC 2 report or penetration test may be evidence but is not automatically the required audit.
- Implementation dates and thresholds must be checked against the current final text.
