# EU Forced-Labour Product Investigation Evidence

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2024/3015 prohibits products made with forced labour from the Union market and export, and empowers authorities to request product and supply-chain information.

**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** draft

## Controls
- Map products, components, upstream extraction/harvest/production steps, suppliers, and jurisdictions.
- Retain risk indicators, due-diligence actions, worker/stakeholder inputs, supplier evidence, and remediation outcomes.
- Build an investigation response package that identifies affected products, parts, manufacturers, suppliers, importers, and exporters.
- Make product identification and lot lineage sufficient to execute withdrawal, prohibition, or disposal decisions.
- Record new substantial information supporting any review request.
- Align readiness to the Regulation's application date and forthcoming guidance.

## Verification
- Trace a high-risk component through every upstream stage.
- Simulate an authority information request and cross-border product decision.
- Test recall/withdrawal scope using product and supply-chain identifiers.
- Challenge evidence for translation, provenance, gaps, and supplier self-attestation.

## Gotchas
The prohibition covers forced labour at any upstream product stage. This evidence program does not replace human-rights due diligence or prove absence merely because a supplier responded.
