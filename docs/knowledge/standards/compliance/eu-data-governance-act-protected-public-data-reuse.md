# EU Data Governance Act protected public-sector data re-use

**Issue:** Chapter II of Regulation (EU) 2022/868 governs re-use of certain public-sector data protected for commercial confidentiality, statistical confidentiality, third-party intellectual property, or personal-data reasons. It does not create a right to obtain every public dataset.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls

- Record the public-sector body, protected-data category, lawful re-use conditions, purpose, recipient, jurisdiction, retention, and applicable national procedure.
- Prefer anonymised or otherwise modified outputs and secure processing environments; prevent unauthorized download and re-identification.
- Apply confidentiality, intellectual-property, competition, and international-transfer review independently rather than treating one approval as universal clearance.
- Preserve application, decision, conditions, access, query, output-review, incident, withdrawal, and deletion evidence.

## Verification

1. Deny an unapproved user, purpose, field, query, export, and destination.
2. Exercise remote secure processing, output disclosure review, withdrawal, expiry, and breach containment.
3. Attempt linkage and re-identification with external datasets and verify technical and contractual controls.
4. Prove published metadata does not itself expose protected information.

## Gotchas

The Regulation harmonises conditions for re-use but does not require public bodies to allow it. Anonymisation claims require risk evaluation, and access inside a secure environment can still disclose data through queries or outputs.
