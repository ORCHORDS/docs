# iso-27001-management-review

**Issue:** Conducting ISO 27001 Clause 9.3 management reviews of the ISMS
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Status:** documented

## Symptom / Context
Clause 9.3 requires top management to review the ISMS at planned intervals (minimum annually) to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness. A superficial review without real decisions is a finding.

## Pattern / Solution
Management review agenda (required inputs per Clause 9.3.2):

1. Status of actions from previous review
2. Changes in external/internal context that affect the ISMS
3. Information security performance:
   - Monitoring and measurement results
   - Internal audit results
   - Fulfillment of information security objectives
4. Feedback from interested parties (customers, regulators)
5. Risk assessment results and risk treatment plan status
6. Opportunities for continual improvement

Required outputs (Clause 9.3.3):
- Decisions on continual improvement opportunities
- Changes to ISMS (scope, policy, objectives)
- Resources needed

Minutes format:
```
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Attendees: [CEO, CISO, CTO, Legal, ...]
For each agenda item: discussion summary + decision + owner + due date
Sign-off: [CISO signature]
```

Frequency: Annual minimum; recommend semi-annual for organizations under active certification.

## Gotchas
- Management review without top management attendance (CEO/COO/Board level) is a minor nonconformity
- Minutes must show actual decisions, not just acknowledgment — passive reviews are findings
- Action items must be tracked and closed before next review or escalated with explanation
- Certification auditors will read minutes verbatim — avoid generic statements
