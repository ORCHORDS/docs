# EU Data Act international government-access safeguards

**Issue:** Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 includes safeguards concerning international governmental access to and transfer of non-personal data held in the Union by data-processing service providers. Applicability depends on role, request, data, and law.
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls and implementation

Inventory provider role and data location; route foreign-government requests to legal review; assess international agreement, necessity/proportionality and addressee protections; minimize disclosure; preserve request, decision, challenge and notice evidence where lawful.

## Tests

Exercise emergency and ordinary requests, conflicting-law claims, broad and narrowed scope, secrecy order, customer notice, challenge, preservation and rejected disclosure.

## Gotchas

This is not a universal ban on transfers and does not replace GDPR rules for personal data. Technical access capability is not legal authority to disclose.
