# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS Version Transition Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, *Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection — Information security management systems — Requirements*, is the normative reference for an Information Security Management System (ISMS). The 2022 revision reorganized the controls into four themes (organizational, people, physical, technological) and reduced the Annex A control set to 93 controls from the 114 controls in ISO/IEC 27001:2013. Profiles that govern an ISMS should reference ISO/IEC 27001:2022 by version and bind it to ISO/IEC 27002:2022 (the controls), ISO/IEC 27003, ISO/IEC 27004, ISO/IEC 27005, and ISO 31000.

## Current context and source status

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 was published in October 2022 and supersedes ISO/IEC 27001:2013. ISO/IEC 27002:2022 was published in February 2022 and provides the implementation guidance for the Annex A controls. As of September 2026, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 remains the current edition; ISO/IEC 27003 (implementation guidance) and ISO/IEC 27004 (monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation) are also revised. Profiles should track the published revision of each companion standard.

## Governance workflow and controls

1. Establish the ISMS scope: organizational units, locations, assets, technologies, and the relationship to the business context.
2. Leadership: top-management commitment, policy, organizational roles, responsibilities, and authorities.
3. Planning: context analysis, risk assessment and treatment, statement of applicability, and risk-treatment plan.
4. Support: resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information.
5. Operation: operational planning and control, risk assessment, risk treatment, and the controls listed in Annex A.
6. Performance evaluation: monitoring, measurement, analysis, evaluation, internal audit, and management review.
7. Improvement: nonconformity and corrective action, continual improvement, and the actions arising from the management review.
8. Track the transition from ISO/IEC 27001:2013 to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 with a documented mapping and a transition plan.

## Validation and evidence

- ISMS policy aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 with the leadership commitment, scope, and Statement of Applicability.
- Records of risk assessment and treatment with the controls applied to each risk.
- Internal audit and management review records.
- Records of nonconformity, corrective action, and continual improvement.
- Mapping from the 2013 Annex A control set to the 2022 Annex A control set with the transition plan.

Evidence that omits the Statement of Applicability, the risk-treatment records, or the management review does not establish ISO/IEC 27001:2022 conformance.

## Failure correction

Common defects include missing Statement of Applicability, undocumented risk-treatment plan, and missing management review. Corrective actions include the explicit Statement of Applicability, the documented risk-treatment plan, and the management review with the actions arising from it tracked to closure.
