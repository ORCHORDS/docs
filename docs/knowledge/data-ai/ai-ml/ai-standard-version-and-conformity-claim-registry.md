# AI Standard Version and Conformity-Claim Registry

**Issue:** Teams can claim alignment with an AI standard without identifying its edition, status, scope, or evidence, making the claim impossible to verify.

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Author:** ORCHORDS
**Status:** documented

## Controls

- Maintain a registry of every adopted AI standard with publisher, exact edition, publication status, scope, owner, and authoritative URL.
- Separate informative guidance, consensus standards, regulatory requirements, and conformity-assessment schemes.
- Map each external requirement to internal controls and evidence without converting voluntary guidance into a certification claim.
- Track superseded and editorially updated editions and require an impact review before changing the organizational baseline.
- Phrase public claims narrowly enough to match the assessed systems, lifecycle stages, and evidence period.

## Verification

- Sample each alignment claim and reproduce the edition, mapped controls, evidence, and approval.
- Inject a draft, withdrawn, or superseded document and ensure the registry flags it before adoption.
- Compare product statements with the registry and reject claims broader than assessed scope.

## Gotchas

- Verify source maturity and product support before making a normative claim.
- Keep secrets, tokens, personal data, and restricted evidence out of examples and logs.
- Reassess after material changes to scope, dependencies, or enforcement.
