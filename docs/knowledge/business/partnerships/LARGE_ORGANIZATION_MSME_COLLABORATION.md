# Large-Organization Collaboration with MSMEs

## Purpose

ISO 44004:2021 gives guidance to large organizations that want to engage micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) within collaborative relationship programmes. Its focus is the large organization's side of the relationship, making it distinct from guidance aimed at building an MSME's own collaborative capability.

## Why a separate governance pattern matters

Large organizations can unintentionally make collaboration unattractive to smaller partners through procurement overhead, slow approvals, disproportionate reporting, one-sided risk transfer, or payment and decision processes designed for much larger counterparties.

A proportionate collaboration model should preserve necessary governance without removing the agility and specialist capability that often make an MSME relationship valuable.

## Practical controls

1. **Clarify the value case.** Identify why an MSME is being engaged and what capability, innovation, access, speed, or specialist knowledge the collaboration is expected to provide.
2. **Scale onboarding.** Apply due diligence and governance requirements proportionately to the relationship's actual risk, access, dependency, and value.
3. **Make decision paths usable.** Give the MSME clear owners, escalation routes, and realistic response times rather than forcing it through opaque enterprise structures.
4. **Avoid disproportionate transfer.** Review contractual, insurance, security, reporting, and operational requirements for burdens that are not justified by the underlying risk.
5. **Protect collaboration value.** Address intellectual property, knowledge sharing, confidentiality, data access, and commercialization expectations explicitly.
6. **Monitor relationship health.** Look for late approvals, payment friction, unmanageable reporting, repeated escalation failures, or other indicators that enterprise processes are eroding the relationship.
7. **Plan transition and exit.** Preserve required records and knowledge while avoiding dependencies that would make the MSME or the larger organization unable to exit cleanly.

## Standards status

ISO lists ISO 44004:2021 as the current published Edition 1. ISO 44003:2021 remains the complementary guidance aimed at MSMEs themselves.

## Scope note

This article summarizes general collaborative-management considerations. It does not prescribe procurement law, contracting terms, certification, or a universal definition of an MSME.
