# Partner Customer Complaint Handoff

## Practice
- Define which complaints the partner resolves and which must be handed back.
- Preserve context and customer history during transfer.
- Set escalation timelines for safety, fraud, accessibility, or regulatory complaints.
- Avoid forcing customers to repeat the same issue unnecessarily.
- Review repeated handoff failures.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA Business Guide, customer and operational management resources: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide
