# Partner Due Diligence Scope

## Practice
- Define due-diligence depth based on the partner's access, data, customer impact, and operational criticality.
- Review ownership, financial/operational capability, security, and legal obligations as relevant.
- Avoid one-size-fits-all questionnaires.
- Record unresolved risks and decision owners.
- Refresh due diligence after material changes.

## Source basis
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, Govern supply-chain outcomes: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
