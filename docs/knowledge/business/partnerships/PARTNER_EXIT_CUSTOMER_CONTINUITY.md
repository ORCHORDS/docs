# Partner Exit Customer Continuity

## Practice
- Plan customer-impacting handoffs before ending a partnership.
- Identify data, support, billing, entitlement, and communications dependencies.
- Set final responsibilities and cutover dates.
- Preserve required records and remove obsolete access.
- Review post-exit customer issues for unresolved dependencies.
