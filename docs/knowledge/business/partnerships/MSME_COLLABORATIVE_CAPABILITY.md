# MSME Collaborative Capability

## Purpose

ISO 44003:2021 gives guidance for micro, small and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) on using the twelve collaborative relationship principles from ISO/TR 44000 to improve their collaborative capability.

## Practical use

An MSME collaboration programme should:

1. identify why collaboration is needed and what value each party expects;
2. assess whether the organization has the governance, communication, and decision capacity to collaborate effectively;
3. define responsibilities and decision rights early enough to avoid ambiguity;
4. build trust through transparent commitments, evidence, and agreed escalation paths;
5. protect the MSME from disproportionate process overhead that removes the value of collaboration;
6. monitor relationship health, delivery, and emerging risks throughout the engagement; and
7. plan transition, renewal, or exit before dependency becomes difficult to unwind.

## Scale principle

The depth of governance should be proportionate to the relationship's complexity, risk, and strategic importance. Small organizations should not copy large-enterprise bureaucracy when a lighter mechanism provides equivalent clarity and accountability.
