# Partner Telemarketing Compliance Handoff

Define responsibilities when telemarketing activity or lead handling crosses partner boundaries.

## Checks
- Assign script approval, consent/do-not-call controls, payment restrictions, and recordkeeping.
- Define incident and complaint handoff.
- Require current compliance evidence from the performing partner.

## Source
FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule
