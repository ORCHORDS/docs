# Partner Marketing Claim Approval

## Practice
- Require approval for partner-created claims using shared branding or product facts.
- Provide current substantiation and mandatory disclosures.
- Prevent outdated claims from remaining live after product changes.
- Monitor high-risk campaigns and influencer arrangements.
- Correct misleading partner claims promptly.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising and Marketing Basics: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/advertising-marketing-basics
