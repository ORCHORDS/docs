# Ecosystem Collaboration Governance

## Purpose

ISO/TS 44007:2025 gives guidance for ecosystem participants on implementing the 12 collaborative-business principles in ISO/TR 44000. It applies across ecosystem configurations, including orchestrators and members with different roles and objectives.

## Practical governance

1. Define the ecosystem's shared purpose, expected value, and boundaries before assigning detailed work.
2. Identify the orchestrator or coordinating role where one exists and document how decisions, conflicts, and changes are handled.
3. Map participant roles, capabilities, dependencies, information exchanges, and material risks across the ecosystem rather than evaluating each relationship in isolation.
4. Establish common working principles while preserving clear accountability for each participant's own obligations.
5. Use shared performance, risk, communication, and improvement mechanisms proportionate to the ecosystem's complexity.
6. Plan participant entry, change, and exit so that collaboration can evolve without losing critical knowledge or operational continuity.

## Source

- ISO — ISO/TS 44007:2025, Collaborative business relationship management — Guidance for ecosystem collaborations: https://www.iso.org/standard/88453.html

## Scope note

ISO/TS 44007:2025 is guidance, not a claim of certification or conformity. Contractual, competition, privacy, security, and sector-specific duties require separate assessment.
