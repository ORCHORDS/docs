# Partner Roles and Responsibilities Charter

## Practice
- Define business owner, technical owner, escalation owner, and decision rights.
- Separate shared responsibilities from retained internal responsibilities.
- Document customer, security, and compliance handoffs.
- Review responsibilities when services or scope change.
- Keep the charter accessible to operational teams.

## Source basis
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, Govern: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
