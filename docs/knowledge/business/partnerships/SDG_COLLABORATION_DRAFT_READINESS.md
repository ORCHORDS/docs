# SDG Collaboration Draft Readiness

## Status warning

**ISO/DIS 44008 is under development and is not a published International Standard.** As of the current ISO catalogue entry, the Draft International Standard has completed its DIS ballot and is progressing through the standards-development process. Draft content can still change before publication.

This article is therefore readiness guidance only. It must not be cited as a final requirement, certification basis, or evidence of conformity.

## Purpose

ISO/DIS 44008 is being developed to provide guidance for governments, businesses, and other stakeholders that use collaborative-business-relationship principles to align joint aims and objectives with contributions to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

## Readiness practices

Organizations that want to prepare for the emerging topic without prematurely adopting draft requirements can:

1. Identify the sustainability or development outcome the collaboration is intended to support rather than beginning with a generic partnership label.
2. Map each party's capabilities, resources, incentives, constraints, and expected contribution.
3. Define joint objectives and evidence that could show whether the collaboration is producing the intended outcome.
4. Record dependencies, trade-offs, and possible adverse effects rather than reporting only positive contributions.
5. Establish governance, decision rights, escalation, information-sharing boundaries, and review points before relying on the relationship for public sustainability claims.
6. Keep claims about SDG contribution proportionate to evidence and distinguish an activity's intent from measured impact.
7. Re-check the final ISO publication, if and when issued, before describing any programme as aligned with ISO 44008.

## Draft lifecycle control

The ISO catalogue currently identifies ISO/DIS 44008 as under development. Documentation systems should therefore tag references to it as draft and track its publication status. When ISO publishes a final edition, compare the final text and catalogue status with any assumptions made during the draft period before updating controlled guidance.

## Related published principles

ISO's draft describes use of the twelve collaborative relationship management principles from ISO/TR 44000. ISO/TR 44000:2019 is a published Technical Report and can be referenced separately from the developing ISO 44008 document.

## Scope note

This article is deliberately non-normative readiness guidance for a draft standard. It does not assert that ISO 44008 has been published or that any organization conforms to it, and it does not validate sustainability or SDG impact claims.
