# Partner Warranty Obligation Allocation

Assign warranty responsibilities across manufacturers, sellers, service providers, and support partners before launch.

## Checks
- Map who publishes terms, receives claims, performs remedies, and reports failures.
- Prevent contradictory customer messaging.
- Define evidence handoff and escalation.

FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
