# Collaborative Working for MSMEs

## Purpose

ISO 44003:2021 gives guidance for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) on applying the fundamental principles of collaborative business relationships. ISO states that the guidance is applicable to MSMEs regardless of activity, location, operating environment, culture, social capital, or objectives.

## Practical use

For a smaller organization, collaborative capability should be kept proportionate and usable. A practical approach is to:

1. identify why a collaboration is needed and what each party expects from it;
2. select partners based on capability, compatibility, risk, and the value each side can contribute;
3. document responsibilities, decision rights, information boundaries, and escalation paths;
4. make commitments realistic for the organization's actual capacity and resources;
5. review whether trust, communication, delivery, and shared objectives remain healthy; and
6. plan how the relationship can change or end without losing important knowledge or creating unmanaged obligations.

## Avoiding over-complexity

Small organizations do not need to imitate the governance overhead of a large enterprise. Controls should be sufficient to make expectations and responsibilities clear while remaining proportionate to the collaboration's value, duration, dependency, and risk.

## Current status

ISO lists ISO 44003:2021 as published and under systematic review. Until ISO publishes a replacement edition, the 2021 edition remains the published reference.
