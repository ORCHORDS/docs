# Partner Change Notification Governance

## Practice
- Define material changes that require partner notification.
- Cover ownership, service scope, data use, security architecture, subcontractors, and customer-impacting changes where relevant.
- Route notifications to accountable reviewers.
- Reassess risk and contracts after material change.
- Record approvals and unresolved issues.

## Source basis
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, Govern supply-chain outcomes: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
