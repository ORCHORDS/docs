# Partner Data-Sharing Governance

## Purpose

Partnerships that exchange business, customer, operational, or technical data should define what is shared, why it is shared, how it is protected, and when access ends.

## Governance pattern

1. Identify the business purpose and minimum data needed for the collaboration.
2. Classify the information and document permitted uses.
3. Define approved transfer methods, storage locations, access roles, and retention periods.
4. Require partners to protect data in transit and at rest according to its sensitivity.
5. Define restrictions on onward sharing and subcontractor access.
6. Establish notification and coordination requirements for loss, misuse, or unauthorized access.
7. Revoke access and confirm deletion or return when the purpose ends or the partnership changes.
8. Periodically review whether the original sharing remains necessary.

## Risk principle

Data-sharing scope should be proportional to the relationship. A partner should not receive broad access simply because narrower access is operationally inconvenient.
