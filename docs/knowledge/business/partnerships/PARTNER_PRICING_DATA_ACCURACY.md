# Partner Pricing Data Accuracy

Govern shared price and fee data so partner-supplied values do not cause misleading offers or checkout totals.

## Checks
- Define data owners and refresh frequency.
- Validate mandatory/optional charge classification.
- Reconcile displayed and final payment values.

FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
