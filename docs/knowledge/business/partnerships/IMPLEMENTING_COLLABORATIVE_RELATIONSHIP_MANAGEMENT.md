# Implementing Collaborative Relationship Management

## Purpose

ISO 44002:2019 provides guidance for organizations implementing ISO 44001 collaborative business relationship management systems. ISO lists the 2019 edition as published and marked to be revised; Edition 2 is under development and has not replaced the published edition.

## Practical implementation approach

A reusable implementation programme should:

1. define why collaboration is being used and the outcomes the relationship is expected to support;
2. assign accountable leadership and relationship-management responsibilities;
3. assess existing collaborative capabilities and gaps against the chosen framework;
4. define governance, decision rights, escalation routes, information-sharing boundaries, and performance measures;
5. establish a relationship-management plan appropriate to the scale and risk of the arrangement;
6. monitor whether the collaboration is producing the intended value and whether risks or assumptions have changed; and
7. capture lessons when the relationship changes, renews, or ends.

## Proportionality

Implementation should be proportionate to the relationship. A strategic joint programme may require formal governance, shared objectives, risk registers, and recurring reviews, while a smaller collaboration may use lighter controls. The important point is that roles, objectives, boundaries, and review mechanisms are explicit enough to manage the relationship deliberately.

## Version status

Do not describe the Edition 2 work item as a published standard. ISO currently identifies ISO 44002:2019 as the published edition and the replacement edition as under development.
