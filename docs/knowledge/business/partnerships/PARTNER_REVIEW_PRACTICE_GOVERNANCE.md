# Partner Review Practice Governance

Control review-solicitation and reputation-management practices performed by agencies, affiliates, or other partners.

## Checks
- Contractually prohibit fake reviews, sentiment-conditioned incentives, and improper suppression.
- Review partner scripts and workflows.
- Require evidence and corrective action for violations.
