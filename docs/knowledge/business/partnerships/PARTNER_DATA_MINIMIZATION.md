# Partner Data Minimization

## Practice
- Identify the minimum data required for the partner's purpose.
- Avoid sharing unrelated customer or internal information.
- Review retention, onward sharing, and deletion expectations.
- Reassess data scope after feature or contract changes.
- Track exceptions and excess-data remediation.

## Source basis
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 and NIST Privacy Framework principles: https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework
