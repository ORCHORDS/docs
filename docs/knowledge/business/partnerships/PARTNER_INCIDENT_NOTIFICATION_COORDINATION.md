# Partner Incident Notification and Coordination

## Purpose

Collaborative relationships should define how material cyber or operational incidents are communicated between partners before an event occurs.

## Coordination controls

- Name the contacts and escalation paths each partner will use for urgent incidents.
- Define what events require notification and expected notification timing.
- Agree on the minimum information to share: affected service, known scope, containment status, customer impact, dependencies, and next update time.
- Preserve incident evidence while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of unrelated confidential information.
- Coordinate containment when one partner's action could disrupt another partner's systems or services.
- Track commitments, owners, and follow-up actions to closure.
- Review the notification arrangement after major incidents and material relationship changes.

## Contract and governance considerations

Incident-notification obligations should align with applicable contracts, regulatory duties, data-processing terms, and supply-chain risk requirements. Critical suppliers may need tighter notification windows and more explicit evidence requirements.
