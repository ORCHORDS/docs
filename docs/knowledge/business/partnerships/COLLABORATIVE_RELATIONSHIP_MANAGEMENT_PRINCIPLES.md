# Collaborative Relationship Management Principles

## Purpose

Collaborative business relationships benefit from a management approach that is grounded in explicit principles rather than relying only on contracts or informal goodwill. ISO/TR 44000:2019 introduces twelve collaborative relationship management principles that underpin ISO 44001 and related collaborative-management standards.

## Practice

Use the principles as a recurring review lens for important collaborations:

1. confirm that the relationship has a defined purpose and expected value;
2. check that governance and decision responsibilities are understood by the participants;
3. make information and knowledge needed for the collaboration available through agreed channels;
4. examine whether organizational culture, incentives, and behaviours support effective joint working;
5. review whether risks, opportunities, and expected benefits are being considered across organizational boundaries;
6. evaluate whether the relationship is still producing the outcomes that justified it; and
7. capture lessons and adjust the collaboration when circumstances, participants, or objectives change.

A principles review complements lifecycle, partner-selection, performance, and exit controls. It should not be treated as evidence of conformity with ISO 44001.

## Current standards context

ISO lists ISO/TR 44000:2019 as published. ISO 44001:2017 remains the current published requirements standard, while a replacement edition is under development as ISO/DIS 44001. Draft requirements should not be represented as final requirements.

## Scope note

This article summarizes a project-neutral management approach. The exact application of collaborative principles depends on the relationship, its risks, participants, and objectives.
