# Partner Subcontractor Visibility

Maintain visibility into subcontractors that materially affect service, security, privacy, or customer obligations.

## Checks
- Require notification of material subcontractors.
- Identify inherited obligations and dependencies.
- Review changes that alter risk or customer commitments.

## Source
NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/final
