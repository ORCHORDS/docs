# Partner Performance Review

## Practice
- Review agreed service, quality, customer, financial, and risk measures.
- Separate temporary incidents from persistent underperformance.
- Record improvement actions and accountable owners.
- Link renewal decisions to evidence rather than relationship history alone.
- Escalate material unresolved risk.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA Business Guide and business management guidance: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide
