# Census Business Builder for Market Planning

## Purpose

Census Business Builder (CBB) provides selected demographic, economic, and business statistics for entrepreneurs and small businesses evaluating markets, locations, customers, competition, and expansion opportunities.

## Practical use

Use CBB to compare locations and business conditions with public data rather than relying only on anecdotal demand estimates.

1. Select the business type or industry that best matches the planned activity.
2. Compare candidate geographies using population, income, employment, business-count, payroll, and related measures available for that geography.
3. Review local competitors and the characteristics of residents who may form the target market.
4. Use geographic comparisons and time-series views where available instead of treating a single-period value as a trend.
5. Export a report or underlying data for the assumptions section of a business plan or expansion analysis.
6. Record the dataset vintage because different CBB measures can come from different Census programs and reference periods.
7. Do not interpret correlation between local indicators and business density as proof of demand or profitability.

## Current tool context

The Census Bureau describes CBB as a tool for opening or expanding a business and for understanding local market conditions. Current 2026 materials highlight interactive maps, customizable reports, region building, local business trends, industry clusters, and population characteristics.

## Scope note

Census data supports market research but does not predict the performance of a specific business. Strategy decisions should combine public data with direct customer, cost, competition, and operational research.
