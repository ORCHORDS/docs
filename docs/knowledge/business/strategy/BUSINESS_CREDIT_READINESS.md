# Business Credit Readiness

## Practice
- Maintain accurate financial statements and business records.
- Separate business and personal financial activity where appropriate.
- Review credit profile, existing obligations, and repayment capacity before borrowing.
- Prepare consistent funding-use explanations.
- Correct known reporting errors before major applications.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA Business Guide, establish business credit and funding guidance: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide
