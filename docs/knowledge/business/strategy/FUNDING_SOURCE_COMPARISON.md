# Funding Source Comparison

## Practice
- Compare debt, equity, grants, owner capital, and other funding sources using consistent criteria.
- Consider cost, dilution, repayment, collateral, timing, and restrictions.
- Match funding duration to the use of funds.
- Document downside scenarios and covenant risk.
- Preserve the decision rationale.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA Business Guide, funding resources: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide
