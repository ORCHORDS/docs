# Market Entry Assumption Review

Make market-entry decisions explicit and testable before committing major resources.

## Checks
- Record demand, market size, customer, competitor, barrier, and channel assumptions.
- Identify evidence and confidence level for each assumption.
- Define triggers for revising or abandoning the plan.
