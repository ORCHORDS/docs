# Sales Forecast Planning

## Practice
- Build forecasts from explicit volume, price, channel, and timing assumptions.
- Separate historical performance from forward assumptions.
- Create conservative, expected, and upside views where useful.
- Tie forecasts to cash-flow and staffing decisions.
- Review forecast error and update assumptions.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA business planning and financial projection guidance: https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/
