# Expand to New Business Locations

## Purpose

SBA guidance treats expansion to a new location as a fresh market-entry decision rather than a simple copy of the existing operation. The business should update its marketing plan, confirm financial readiness, and account for location-specific legal and tax requirements.

## Practical steps

1. Reassess target customers, competitors, local demand, and advertising conditions for the proposed location.
2. Forecast the additional operating, marketing, staffing, occupancy, and launch costs.
3. Compare expected revenue with the capital required and confirm the balance sheet can support expansion.
4. Identify state and local registration, licensing, permit, zoning, and tax requirements.
5. Check whether foreign qualification or other registration is required when entering another state.
6. Define measurable go/no-go criteria and post-launch review points before committing major capital.

## Source

- U.S. Small Business Administration — Grow your business, including expansion to new locations: https://www.sba.gov/counseling/grow-your-business/

## Scope note

Location, licensing, tax, and registration requirements vary by jurisdiction. This article is general planning guidance, not legal or tax advice.
