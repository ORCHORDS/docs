# Gross Margin Sensitivity Analysis

Test how changes in revenue, direct costs, discounts, and volume affect gross margin.

## Checks
- Establish baseline assumptions.
- Model cost and price shocks.
- Identify breakpoints that require pricing, sourcing, or product changes.
