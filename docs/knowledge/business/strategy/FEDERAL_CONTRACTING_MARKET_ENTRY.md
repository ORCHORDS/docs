# Federal Contracting Market Entry

## Purpose

Selling to the U.S. federal government is a distinct market-entry path. SBA guidance recommends assessing readiness, meeting eligibility and registration requirements, understanding contracting rules, and selecting opportunities that fit the business's capabilities.

## Practical steps

1. Confirm the business can deliver the required product or service at the scale, quality, location, and schedule expected by government buyers.
2. Review applicable small-business size standards and socioeconomic program eligibility where relevant.
3. Complete required registrations and keep business information current before pursuing opportunities.
4. Learn the contract types, solicitation process, evaluation criteria, performance obligations, and subcontracting rules relevant to the target market.
5. Build a capability statement and evidence of relevant past performance or transferable capability.
6. Start with opportunities that match current capacity rather than treating government demand as unlimited expansion capacity.

## Source

- U.S. Small Business Administration — Federal contracting guidance within the SBA business guide: https://www.sba.gov/counseling/

## Scope note

Federal procurement requirements depend on the solicitation, agency, contract, and applicable regulations. This article is general market-entry guidance.
