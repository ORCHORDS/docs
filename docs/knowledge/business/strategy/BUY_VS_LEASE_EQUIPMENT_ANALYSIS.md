# Buy vs Lease Equipment Analysis

## Practice
- Compare upfront cash, financing cost, lifetime cost, maintenance, flexibility, and expected useful life.
- Consider early-termination and obsolescence risk for leases.
- Consider depreciation and ownership value for purchases.
- Model expected utilization before committing.
- Have unclear contractual terms reviewed appropriately.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA, Manage your business / buy assets and equipment: https://www.sba.gov/counseling/manage-your-business/
