# Working Capital Planning

## Practice
- Track short-term assets, liabilities, receivables, payables, and cash needs.
- Model timing gaps between spending and collections.
- Identify minimum liquidity needed for normal operations.
- Reassess before expansion or major purchases.
- Link shortfalls to financing or cost actions early.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA finance guidance: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/manage-your-finances
