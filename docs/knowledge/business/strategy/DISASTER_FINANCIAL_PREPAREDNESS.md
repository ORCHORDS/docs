# Disaster Financial Preparedness

## Practice
- Identify realistic hazards and estimate likely financial interruption.
- Document critical expenses, liquidity needs, insurance, and recovery funding options.
- Protect key financial records and contact information.
- Exercise the response plan with staff.
- Update the plan after major operational changes.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA, Prepare for emergencies: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/prepare-emergencies
