# Operating Expense Forecast

## Practice
- Categorize recurring and nonrecurring operating expenses.
- Separate fixed, variable, and discretionary costs.
- Link expense assumptions to sales and staffing plans.
- Stress-test major cost drivers.
- Compare actuals with forecast and explain material variance.
