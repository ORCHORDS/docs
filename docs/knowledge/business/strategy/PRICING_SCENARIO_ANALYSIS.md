# Pricing Scenario Analysis

Compare pricing scenarios against demand, costs, margin, positioning, and customer expectations.

## Checks
- Model multiple price points and volume assumptions.
- Include recurring/nonrecurring costs and fees.
- Document downside cases and decision thresholds.
