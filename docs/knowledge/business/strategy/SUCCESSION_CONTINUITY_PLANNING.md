# Succession Continuity Planning

Prepare ownership and operational continuity for planned or unexpected leadership transition.

## Checks
- Identify critical decision roles and knowledge dependencies.
- Document transition options and trigger conditions.
- Align financial, legal, customer, employee, and partner communications.
