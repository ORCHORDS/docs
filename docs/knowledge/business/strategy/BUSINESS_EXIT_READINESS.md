# Business Exit Readiness

## Practice
- Maintain current records of assets, liabilities, contracts, customer obligations, and intellectual property.
- Understand likely valuation approaches before a sale.
- Define ownership-transfer options and transition responsibilities.
- Prepare for data, employee, supplier, and customer continuity.
- Use professional review for transaction documents where appropriate.

## Source basis
- U.S. SBA, Manage your business / close or sell your business: https://www.sba.gov/counseling/manage-your-business/
