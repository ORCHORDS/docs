# ISO 44001:2017 Collaborative Business Relationship Governance

## Purpose

ISO 44001:2017, "Collaborative business relationship management systems — Requirements and framework," specifies the requirements for a collaborative business relationship management system (the "system") that organizations can use to identify, establish, manage, and improve business relationships with other organizations. The standard applies whether the relationship is with a supplier, customer, partner, or any other organization. This article governs the application of ISO 44001 so an organization can establish a relationship management system that drives measurable value from its collaborative relationships.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization engaged in business-to-business relationships. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the scope definition, the leadership and culture requirements, the relationship strategy, the relationship management processes (identification, recognition, evaluation, selection, governance, management, review, improvement), the operational and strategic layers of relationships, and the performance evaluation of the system. It does not replace commercial or contractual practices; it complements them.

## Workflow

1. Determine the scope of the relationship management system: which organizations, which relationships, which boundaries.
2. Establish leadership commitment to the system and the culture of working collaboratively. Top management is responsible for the system and its performance.
3. Develop the relationship strategy: identify the relationships that matter most to the organization's strategy, define the strategic objectives for each, and align the operational management of the relationship.
4. Operate the relationship management processes at both strategic and operational levels:
   - Identify potential relationship partners and recognize their strategic relevance.
   - Evaluate and select partners using criteria aligned with the relationship strategy.
   - Establish governance for each relationship: roles, decision-making, escalation, joint objectives, and joint review.
   - Manage the relationship through operational processes: planning, communication, performance measurement, and joint improvement.
   - Review and improve the relationship on a planned cadence.
5. Establish performance evaluation of the relationship management system itself: internal audits, management review, and improvement.

## Controls and evidence

Relationship management evidence includes the system scope, the leadership commitment, the relationship strategy, the partner evaluation and selection records, the governance arrangements for each significant relationship, the operational management records, the relationship review records, and the system-level review and improvement records. Each significant relationship should have a documented governance arrangement.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the system scope is documented, the relationship strategy is aligned with the organization's strategy, governance arrangements exist for significant relationships, joint reviews occur and produce improvements, and the system is reviewed on a planned cadence. Internal audits provide additional assurance.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the relationship strategy is unclear or not aligned with business strategy (corrective: derive the relationship strategy from business strategy and document the relationship); governance arrangements exist on paper but not in practice (corrective: require joint governance meetings and record decisions); operational management is left to the project teams without strategic oversight (corrective: separate the operational and strategic layers and review each on its cadence); the system is implemented for new relationships but not extended to existing ones (corrective: prioritize existing strategic relationships for system application and document the prioritization).

## Limitations

ISO 44001 specifies the requirements for the system; it does not guarantee the success of any individual relationship. The standard assumes the organization has a culture that supports collaboration; without it, the system is paperwork. The standard does not replace commercial or contractual practices.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral business use of ISO 44001:2017. It does not assert any specific organization's relationship management conformance or claim any certification outcome.
