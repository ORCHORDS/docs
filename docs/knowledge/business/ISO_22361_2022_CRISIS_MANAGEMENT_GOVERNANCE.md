# ISO 22361:2022 Crisis Management Governance

## Purpose

ISO 22361:2022, "Security and resilience — Crisis management — Guidelines," provides guidance for organizations to plan, establish, operate, monitor, review, maintain, and continually improve a crisis management capability. The standard addresses the crisis management system, the leadership and decision-making structure, the communication and stakeholder engagement, and the operational aspects of crisis response. This article governs the application of ISO 22361 so an organization can establish a crisis management capability that is integrated with its business continuity, emergency management, and incident response arrangements.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization that may face crises. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the crisis management system, the crisis leadership structure (the crisis management team), the crisis communication, the operational coordination with related disciplines (BCMS, security, emergency management), and the review of crisis management after events. It does not replace sector-specific crisis regulations (e.g., for financial services or critical infrastructure); readers should overlay their sector requirements.

## Workflow

1. Establish the crisis management policy and the scope of the crisis management capability. Align the policy with the organization's strategic objectives and with related policies (business continuity, security, emergency management).
2. Identify crisis scenarios using a structured approach: threats, vulnerabilities, impacts, and likelihood. Distinguish crises from incidents; crises are typically low-probability, high-impact events that exceed normal incident response.
3. Establish the crisis leadership structure: a crisis management team with defined roles, decision-making authority, and succession. The team should be able to convene quickly and operate under stress.
4. Develop the crisis communication plan: stakeholder identification, message templates, channels, spokesperson designation, and coordination with operational communications.
5. Develop the crisis management procedures: activation criteria, decision points, escalation, coordination with incident response, business continuity, and external responders.
6. Train and exercise the crisis management capability on a planned cadence. Exercises should include the leadership team and should stress-test communication and decision-making.
7. Review and improve after each event or exercise. Capture lessons, update plans, and document the improvement actions.

## Controls and evidence

Crisis management evidence includes the policy, the scenario register, the crisis management plan, the communication plan, the exercise records, the activation records (when crises occur), the after-action reports, and the improvement actions. The activation and after-action records are particularly important for demonstrating operational capability.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the policy and plan are current, the leadership team has been exercised, after-action reviews have produced concrete improvements, communication is rehearsed, and the crisis management capability is integrated with related disciplines. Periodic exercises provide the strongest evidence of operational capability.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: crisis management is reduced to a plan document without exercising the team (corrective: schedule exercises and rotate scenarios); the crisis management team is staffed but lacks decision authority (corrective: define the team's authority and have leadership delegate the necessary authority in advance); activation criteria are unclear (corrective: define trigger criteria with operational thresholds); after-action reviews produce no concrete changes (corrective: require a documented improvement plan after each exercise or event).

## Limitations

ISO 22361 provides guidelines; it does not specify a certification scheme. The standard does not replace sector regulations that may mandate specific crisis management arrangements. The standard does not guarantee an effective crisis response; it ensures the capability is designed and exercised.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral business use of ISO 22361:2022. It does not assert any specific organization's crisis management conformance or claim any certification outcome.
