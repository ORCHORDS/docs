# Reasonable Service Policy Modifications

## Purpose

Customer-facing policies can unintentionally block access for people with disabilities. Businesses covered by ADA Title III must make reasonable modifications when needed to provide access to goods and services unless the change would fundamentally alter the nature of what is offered.

## Review pattern

1. Identify the policy, practice, or procedure creating the access barrier.
2. Understand the customer's requested modification and the service outcome they need.
3. Look for a practical adjustment that preserves the essential service while removing the barrier.
4. Escalate only when the request affects safety, essential service characteristics, or creates a significant operational question.
5. Document the decision and communicate it clearly to the customer.
6. When a recurring request reveals a systemic barrier, update the standard policy rather than solving the same problem case by case.

## Examples

Reasonable modifications can include adapting one-person fitting-room rules for a customer who needs assistance or permitting a service animal despite a general no-pets policy.

## Boundary

A modification is not required when it would fundamentally alter the essential nature of the goods or services. Other ADA requirements may also apply.
