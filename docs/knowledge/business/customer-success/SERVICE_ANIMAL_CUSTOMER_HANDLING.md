# Service Animal Customer Handling

## Practice
- Train customer-facing staff on lawful service-animal handling.
- Avoid impermissible fees or blanket refusals.
- Escalate unusual safety or service conflicts rather than improvising exclusions.
- Keep policies consistent across locations and channels.
- Review complaints involving service animals for recurring failures.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov and U.S. Department of Justice Title III disability guidance: https://www.ada.gov/
- DOJ disability-rights cases and guidance: https://www.justice.gov/crt/disability-rights-cases
