# Customer Success Reference Customer Reciprocal Review

## Scope

This practice structures the reciprocal review that occurs when a customer who is publicly named as a reference for the supplier also asks the supplier to reciprocate by being a reference for the customer, so that the reciprocal review can be replayed, defended to both parties, and governed by an evidence trail that survives the people who prepared it. The framing is the anti-bribery perspective articulated in ISO 37001 on anti-bribery management systems, and is referenced here as a documented structure rather than as a binding rule; adopting it does not by itself establish conformance with ISO 37001 and does not replace the customer's contract, the supplier's anti-bribery policy, or any specialist legal, ethics, or regulatory advice. The practice applies whenever the supplier is asked to be a reference for a customer who is publicly named as a reference for the supplier, and applies whether the reciprocal request is for a public case study, a private prospect reference, a regulator-facing testimony, or an analyst-facing briefing.

It does not apply to one-way reference requests in either direction, which can be governed by the supplier's reference-management practice rather than by this reciprocal-review practice; the reciprocal framing matters specifically because the benefit flowing in either direction must be visible to both ethics contacts.

## Workflow or implementation guidance

Open the reciprocal review by capturing the customer's identifier, the supplier's identifier, the customer's request, the supplier's acceptance, the customer's ethics contact, the supplier's ethics contact, the public reference artefact in each direction, and the third-party attestation, if any, that each reference relies on. Pull the latest version of each artefact, the latest version of each party's anti-bribery policy, and the latest version of each party's disclosure rules. Reconcile the two requests against the two policies before any artefact is published, and confirm that each ethics contact has been informed of the reciprocal benefit.

Sequence the review so that the ethics contact's perspective leads, not the commercial interest. A workable order is: the customer's stated reference request and the supplier's stated reference request; the benefit flowing in each direction; reconciliation of the benefit against each party's disclosure rules; any third-party attestation required by either reference; the route for revising either artefact if either party disputes it; and the retention schedule that governs the reciprocal review. Close by stating explicitly which items remain open, which decisions are recorded, and what evidence will be re-presented if either party later questions the reciprocal review.

Authoring notes: separate what each party has stated, what each party has inferred from observation, what a third party has reported, and what each party is estimating. Where a benefit is reciprocal, label it as such in both directions and use the same vocabulary. Where a figure is estimated, label it as such and separate it from verified facts. Where a customer or supplier statement is paraphrased, prefer that party's own words for the part they authored.

## Controls

The controlled reciprocal review identifies each party, each party's request, each party's ethics contact, the benefit in each direction, the disclosure result, the third-party attestation, the reviewer, the retention schedule, and the route for revision. Give it one accountable owner and a named delegate on each side, with a named bridge owner for the reciprocal record. State the frequency of refresh, the approval authority for any change that affects commercial exposure, the data classification of every attached exhibit, the retention period, and the escalation route when either party contests the review.

Material changes to the reciprocal review require review by someone other than the author on each side before they are reused, particularly where the change affects the benefit in either direction. Restrict distribution to the audience agreed with each party and to each party's anti-bribery register, follow the approved retention schedule, and do not let informal conversations become the de facto basis for a reciprocal-review revision without being recorded. Where the review is automated, the inputs, thresholds, and human override must remain understandable.

## Validation evidence

Direct validation must reconcile a sample of reciprocal reviews against the two policies, the two ethics contacts, the two artefacts, and the disclosed benefit in each direction. Record the sample, the source, the expected result, the observed result, the reviewer, and the correction ticket. Reconcile the disclosed benefit against each party's anti-bribery register and include rejected, overdue, and overridden cases so testing cannot pass by examining only clean examples.

To validate the practice itself, trace a sample of reciprocal reviews and verify that the benefit in each direction was disclosed to the ethics contact in each direction before either artefact was published. Useful indicators of effectiveness include reciprocal reviews that both parties accept in writing, reciprocal reviews where the benefit is symmetrical on both sides, and reciprocal reviews where the supplier's policy and the customer's policy converge on the same disclosure route. Internal targets are management choices, not public guarantees unless formally authorised.

## Failure modes and correction

When the practice fails its own controls, protect each party's position and the integrity of the reciprocal relationship first. If a benefit in either direction has not been disclosed, surface the disclosure immediately to the ethics contact in the relevant direction and record it. If an artefact has been published without segregation of duties, treat the publication as an incident: name the customer impact, the supplier impact, the containment owner in each direction, the next update, the recovery deadline, and the closure test. Do not close because an internal ticket moved. If either party disputes the reciprocal review, preserve the disputed record and do not rewrite history.

Search for similarly exposed reciprocal reviews in the same portfolio, since a single unrecorded benefit usually signals a wider problem in the supplier's anti-bribery discipline. Where the failure relates to data classification or distribution, treat the disclosure channel as the affected control. Reassess the practice after a material incident, a change in either party's environment, or evidence that the current shape creates unintended outcomes.

## Limitations

This practice cannot prove causation, eliminate professional judgement, or repair unreliable source data. The ISO 37001 framing referenced here is a documented structure, not a binding rule; adopting it does not by itself establish certification, conformance, or legal compliance. Sampling can miss rare harms, lagging measures can reveal defects late, and the customer's own policies can drift between the review and the next refresh.

A documented reciprocal review can still fail in use. The review is a structure, not a substitute for ethical discipline, judgement, or a strong account team. Treat it as a starting point, and reassess after a material incident, a change in either party's environment, or evidence that the current shape creates unintended outcomes.
