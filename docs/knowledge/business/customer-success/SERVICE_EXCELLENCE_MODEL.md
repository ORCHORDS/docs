# Service Excellence Model

## Purpose

ISO 23592:2021 defines service-excellence terminology, principles, and a model aimed at outstanding customer experience and sustainable customer delight. ISO distinguishes service excellence from basic customer service and states that the standard applies to organizations delivering services, including commercial, public, and not-for-profit organizations.

## Practical use

A service-excellence programme should:

1. define what an outstanding experience means for the organization and its customers;
2. make service excellence an intentional leadership and operating objective rather than an isolated frontline initiative;
3. design service interactions around customer needs, expectations, and the value the service is meant to create;
4. give employees enough capability, information, and authority to deliver consistently;
5. measure outcomes and experience rather than relying only on activity volumes; and
6. use evidence from customers, employees, operations, and service performance to improve the model over time.

## Current status

ISO lists ISO 23592:2021 as published and under systematic review. A review status does not mean that a replacement edition has already been published.
