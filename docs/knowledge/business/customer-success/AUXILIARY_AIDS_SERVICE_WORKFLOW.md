# Auxiliary Aids Service Workflow

## Practice
- Provide an intake path for customers requesting communication assistance.
- Match aids or services to the nature, length, complexity, and context of communication.
- Consult the customer about effective communication needs.
- Record delivery, substitutions, and escalations.
- Reassess recurring failures or delays.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov, Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
