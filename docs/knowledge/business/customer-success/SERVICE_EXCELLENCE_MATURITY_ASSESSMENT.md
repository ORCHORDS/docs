# Service Excellence Maturity Assessment

## Purpose

ISO/TS 19387:2025 establishes principles and guidance for using a service excellence maturity model. ISO describes it as a way for service organizations to determine their maturity level and guide future improvement, aligned with ISO 23592.

## Practical use

A maturity assessment should:

1. define which service-excellence dimensions or elements are in scope;
2. use explicit evidence rather than reputation or self-description alone;
3. assess current capability consistently enough to compare results over time;
4. distinguish basic service reliability from higher levels of service excellence;
5. identify the few improvement areas with the greatest effect on customer experience;
6. assign measurable actions and owners; and
7. repeat the assessment after meaningful changes or on a defined cadence.

## Evidence discipline

Record the assessment scope, evidence reviewed, rating method, findings, improvement actions, and reassessment date. Do not present a self-assessment as independent certification or external assurance.
