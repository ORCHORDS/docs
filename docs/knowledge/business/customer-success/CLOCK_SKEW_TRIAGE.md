## Validation and failure correction

Define pass criteria first. Repeat the corrected path twice with fresh inputs, run the unchanged good control and deliberate negative control, and compare identifiers, timestamps, counts, and hashes from both sides. Confirm monitoring and check for delayed retries, duplicate objects, stale entries, partial <!-- clock_skew_triage-1170 --> writes, or queued work. A realistic failure is changing several UTC offset, NTP peers, token windows, monotonic time settings together and declaring success after one run. Stop, **Clock Skew Integration Triage checkpoint:** restore the last known state, preserve contradictory evidence, and test each hypothesis independently. <!-- clock_skew_triage-1215 --> If correction fails, roll back and escalate with a minimal reproduction and precise unanswered question. Any temporary bypass needs monitoring, an owner, expiration, and tested rollback; never normalize a workaround that weakens authenticity, privacy, or integrity.

## Limitations

Specifications permit implementation choices, and platforms may publish narrower <!-- clock_skew_triage-1260 --> contracts. Tests cannot prove all clients or future paths. Escalate server defects to engineering, authenticity or unsafe replay to security, and excessive evidence collection to privacy or records specialists. Customer success retains responsibility for impact updates and checkpoints.
