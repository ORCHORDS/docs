# Customer Accommodation Feedback Review

## Practice
- Collect feedback on accommodation and accessibility requests.
- Separate individual preferences from systemic service barriers.
- Identify recurring delays, refusals, or ineffective solutions.
- Assign improvement owners and track closure.
- Feed validated lessons into service training and policy updates.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov, Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
