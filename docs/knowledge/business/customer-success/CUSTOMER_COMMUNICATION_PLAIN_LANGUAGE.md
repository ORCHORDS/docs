# Customer Communication Plain Language

Make customer-facing service communication easier to understand, especially when explaining choices, obligations, or remedies.

## Checks
- Use direct wording and short steps.
- Remove unexplained jargon.
- Confirm translations or auxiliary aids preserve meaning.

## Evidence
Approved scripts/templates and review notes.

## Source
ADA Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
