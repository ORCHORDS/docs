# Mobility Device Customer Access

## Practice
- Define how customers using wheelchairs and other mobility devices access services.
- Avoid unnecessary refusals or surcharges tied to disability.
- Train staff on safe handling and reasonable modifications.
- Escalate operational constraints for individualized review.
- Track accessibility failures and corrective actions.

## Source basis
- U.S. Department of Justice, disability-rights guidance and cases: https://www.justice.gov/crt/disability-rights-cases
