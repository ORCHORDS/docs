# Disability-Related Privacy Handling

Handle disability-related service information on a need-to-know basis while still enabling effective service.

## Checks
- Record only information required for the requested aid, modification, or follow-up.
- Avoid unnecessary diagnostic detail.
- Restrict disclosure to staff who need it for service delivery.

## Source
ADA Title III business guidance: https://www.ada.gov/topics/title-iii/
