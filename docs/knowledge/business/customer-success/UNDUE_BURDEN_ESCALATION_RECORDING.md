# Undue Burden Escalation Recording

## Practice
- Escalate claims that a requested aid or service would create an undue burden.
- Consider the nature and cost of the aid relative to organizational resources.
- Document the decision basis and alternative steps considered.
- Avoid allowing frontline staff to make unsupported blanket denials.
- Periodically review decisions for consistency.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov, Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
