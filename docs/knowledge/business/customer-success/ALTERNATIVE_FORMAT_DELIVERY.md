# Alternative Format Delivery

Provide information in an effective alternative format when the ordinary format creates a communication barrier.

## Checks
- Assess nature, length, complexity, context, and normal communication method.
- Coordinate accessible electronic, large-print, captioning, interpreting, or other appropriate aids.
- Confirm the customer received usable information.
