# Relay Service Call Handling

## Practice
- Train staff to recognize legitimate telecommunications relay calls.
- Do not reject calls solely because a relay operator is involved.
- Apply normal identity and transaction controls without adding discriminatory barriers.
- Document repeated relay-call failures for remediation.
- Include relay handling in contact-center quality reviews.

## Source basis
- U.S. Department of Justice ADA technical assistance: https://www.justice.gov/crt/americans-disabilities-act-technical-assistance-letters-3
