# Effective Communication Method Selection

## Practice
- Match the communication method to the customer's disability and the interaction's complexity.
- Consider the person's normal method of communication.
- Do not assume a single aid works for every interaction.
- Reassess when communication is not effective.
- Record recurring service barriers and fixes.
