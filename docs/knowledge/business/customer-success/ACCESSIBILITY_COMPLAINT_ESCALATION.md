# Accessibility Complaint Escalation

## Practice
- Tag accessibility-related complaints for specialized review.
- Preserve the service context and requested accommodation.
- Escalate repeated or high-impact failures promptly.
- Avoid retaliatory or dismissive handling.
- Track corrective action and customer follow-up.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov, Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
