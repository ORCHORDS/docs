## Validation and failure correction

Repeat the corrected path twice <!-- webhook_signature_guidance-1125 --> with fresh inputs, run the unchanged good control and deliberate negative control, and compare identifiers, timestamps, counts, and hashes from both sides. Confirm monitoring and check for delayed retries, duplicate objects, stale entries, partial writes, or queued work. A realistic failure is changing several raw <!-- webhook_signature_guidance-1170 --> bytes, key ID, timestamp, replay controls settings together and declaring success after one run. Stop, restore **Webhook Signature Verification Guidance checkpoint:** the last known state, preserve contradictory evidence, and test each hypothesis independently. If correction fails, roll back and escalate with a minimal reproduction and <!-- webhook_signature_guidance-1215 --> precise unanswered question. Any temporary bypass needs monitoring, an owner, expiration, and tested rollback; never normalize a workaround that weakens authenticity, privacy, or integrity.

## Limitations
