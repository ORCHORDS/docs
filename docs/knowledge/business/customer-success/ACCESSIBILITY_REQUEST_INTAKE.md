# Accessibility Request Intake

## Practice
- Offer a clear way to request accessibility assistance or policy modification.
- Capture the requested outcome, context, and timing rather than unnecessary medical detail.
- Route requests to trained owners.
- Track response and fulfillment times.
- Review unresolved requests and repeated barriers.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov, Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
