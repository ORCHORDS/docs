# ISO 45003:2021 Psychosocial Risk Governance

## Purpose

ISO 45003:2021, "Occupational health and safety management — Psychological health and safety at work — Guidelines for managing psychosocial risks," is the first international standard on psychological health and safety at work. The standard provides guidelines for managing psychosocial risks within an occupational health and safety management system based on ISO 45001. This article governs the application of ISO 45003 so an organization can identify, assess, and control psychosocial risks as part of its overall OH&S management.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the psychosocial hazard categories the standard identifies, the integration with the ISO 45001 OH&S management system, the psychosocial risk assessment, the controls (elimination, substitution, engineering, administrative, PPE-equivalent in psychosocial context), the participation of workers, and the monitoring of psychosocial risks. It does not replace clinical or occupational health expertise; readers should consult qualified professionals where the standard indicates.

## Workflow

1. Establish the context for psychosocial risk management: integrate with the ISO 45001 OH&S management system; identify the relevant stakeholders (workers, managers, HR, occupational health, worker representatives); document the legal and regulatory context.
2. Identify psychosocial hazards. The standard groups them into: how work is organized (workload, pace, hours, autonomy); social factors (interpersonal relationships, recognition, support); work environment (physical environment, equipment, culture); and organizational factors (communication, leadership, change management).
3. Assess psychosocial risks for the identified hazards: likelihood, consequence, and the population affected. Use validated assessment tools where available.
4. Apply the hierarchy of controls: elimination (remove the hazard), substitution (replace with less harmful arrangement), engineering (reorganize work), administrative (procedures, training, support), and personal support (employee assistance, counseling).
5. Ensure participation of workers and their representatives throughout the process.
6. Operate the controls, monitor their effectiveness, and review the assessment periodically.
7. Investigate incidents and complaints, identify root causes, and take corrective action.

## Controls and evidence

Psychosocial risk evidence includes the integrated management system documentation, the hazard identification records, the risk assessment, the controls in place, the participation records, the monitoring records, the incident investigation records, and the review records. Specific evidence includes workload analyses, communication audits, supervisor training records, employee assistance program utilization, and incident trends.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the context is integrated with ISO 45001, hazards are identified across all four categories, workers and their representatives are participating, controls are implemented according to the hierarchy, monitoring is in place, and incidents are investigated and addressed. Periodic audits provide additional assurance.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: psychosocial risks are addressed through employee assistance programs only, ignoring organizational causes (corrective: apply the hierarchy of controls and address organizational causes); worker participation is nominal (corrective: define meaningful participation mechanisms and document them); risk assessment is a one-time event (corrective: review assessment on organizational change and on incident); controls are listed but not operational (corrective: for each control, document the implementation and the monitoring).

## Limitations

ISO 45003 is guidelines, not a management system standard; it does not offer certification. The standard does not replace occupational health or psychological expertise. The standard provides a framework; the substantive content (specific controls, support services, intervention models) is selected by the organization. Sector regulations may impose specific psychosocial obligations.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral business use of ISO 45003:2021. It does not assert any specific organization's psychosocial risk management outcomes or claim any certification.
