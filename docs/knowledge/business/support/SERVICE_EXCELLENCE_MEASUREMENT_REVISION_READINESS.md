# Service Excellence Measurement Revision Readiness

## Purpose

ISO/TS 23686:2022 is the published technical specification for measuring service-excellence performance. ISO/AWI 23686 is an Edition 2 project under development and is intended to replace the 2022 edition, but it is not yet a published replacement.

## Practical use

Organizations using service-excellence measures should:

1. retain the published edition as the current documented reference where applicable;
2. keep metric definitions, data sources, calculation methods, and ownership traceable;
3. distinguish service-excellence measures from ordinary activity or volume metrics;
4. document changes to measurement methods so trends remain interpretable;
5. monitor the Edition 2 project without adopting unpublished requirements as final; and
6. perform a controlled comparison when ISO publishes the replacement edition.

## Scope note

This article focuses on revision governance and measurement traceability. It does not claim conformity with ISO/TS 23686 or predict the final content of Edition 2.
