# Service Excellence Practice Catalogue

## Purpose

ISO/TR 7179:2023 provides practices for achieving service excellence and is intended to support use of ISO 23592 and ISO/TS 24082. A support organization can use such a practice catalogue to turn abstract service principles into repeatable operating behaviours.

## Practical use

Build and maintain a service-practice catalogue that:

1. connects each practice to a defined customer or service outcome;
2. identifies where the practice applies across channels, teams, and service journeys;
3. states the capability, information, authority, and tooling needed to perform it;
4. defines observable evidence that the practice is actually used;
5. captures exceptions and known constraints rather than hiding them;
6. links relevant measures and feedback to the practice; and
7. reviews practices when customer needs, channels, or operating conditions change.

## Improvement loop

Treat the catalogue as a living operating reference. Retire ineffective practices, strengthen those that correlate with better outcomes, and avoid measuring activity volume as a substitute for service quality.

## Scope note

This article summarizes a general service-excellence practice approach. It does not claim conformity with ISO/TR 7179, ISO 23592, or ISO/TS 24082.
