# Public Service Excellence

## Purpose

ISO 11367:2025 establishes principles and a model for public-service excellence. It is intended for public service organizations seeking outstanding stakeholder experience rather than only baseline service delivery.

## Practical use

Organizations operating public-facing support or service functions can adapt the guidance by:

1. identifying the citizens, communities, partner organizations, and other stakeholders the service exists to serve;
2. defining service-excellence outcomes beyond basic transaction completion;
3. aligning leadership, service design, people, and operational processes around those outcomes;
4. collecting evidence from stakeholder experience and service performance;
5. improving services using that evidence; and
6. keeping public-interest, accessibility, fairness, and accountability considerations visible in service decisions.

## Source

- ISO — ISO 11367:2025, Service excellence — Principles and model for public service organizations: https://www.iso.org/standard/83829.html

## Scope note

This article summarizes general public-service excellence concepts. It does not imply that a private organization is a public-service body or that any organization conforms to ISO 11367.
