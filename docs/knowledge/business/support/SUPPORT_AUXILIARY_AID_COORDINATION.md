# Support Auxiliary Aid Coordination

## Practice
- Give support agents a route to request interpreters or other auxiliary aids.
- Consider communication length, complexity, and customer preference.
- Avoid delaying urgent support because ownership is unclear.
- Track fulfillment and recurring availability gaps.
- Review vendor performance where aids are externally supplied.

## Source basis
- ADA.gov, Effective Communication: https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/
