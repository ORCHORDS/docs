# Measuring Service Excellence Performance

## Purpose

ISO/TS 23686:2022 specifies metrics and methods for measuring service-excellence performance. ISO currently lists the 2022 edition as published and marked to be revised; an Edition 2 work item is under development and has not replaced the published specification.

## Measurement approach

A reusable service-performance framework should:

1. define the customer and service outcomes that matter before selecting metrics;
2. combine experience measures with operational measures rather than optimizing one in isolation;
3. establish clear definitions, data sources, ownership, and review cadence for each measure;
4. distinguish leading indicators from lagging outcomes;
5. segment results carefully enough to expose material service gaps without collecting unnecessary personal data;
6. investigate changes and outliers before attributing them to a single cause; and
7. connect findings to specific improvement actions and later verify whether those actions changed outcomes.

## Version status

Do not treat the Edition 2 work item as final. ISO identifies ISO/TS 23686:2022 as the currently published specification and the replacement project as under development.
