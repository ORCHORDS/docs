# Remote Support Session Termination

Close remote-support sessions cleanly once the approved task ends.

## Checks
- Confirm the customer understands the session is ending.
- Terminate remote-control capability and temporary access.
- Remove temporary files/credentials where applicable.
- Record completion or unresolved work.
