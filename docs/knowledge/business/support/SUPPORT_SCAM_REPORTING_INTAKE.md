# Support Scam Reporting Intake

## Practice
- Provide a clear way for customers or staff to report suspected scams or impersonation.
- Capture sender/channel, requested action, payment method, and evidence.
- Avoid asking reporters to interact further with the suspected scammer.
- Escalate active impersonation campaigns promptly.
- Track recurring patterns and customer communications.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Scams and Your Small Business: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/scams-your-small-business-guide-business-0
