# Support Channel Authenticity Notice

## Practice
- Publish the official support channels customers should trust.
- State what support will not ask customers to do, especially around unusual payments or secrets.
- Keep channel information current across website, app, and social profiles.
- Use customer communications during active impersonation campaigns.
- Monitor confusing or spoofable legacy channels.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Scams and Your Small Business: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/scams-your-small-business-guide-business-0
