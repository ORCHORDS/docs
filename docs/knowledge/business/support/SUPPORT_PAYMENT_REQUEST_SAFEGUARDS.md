# Support Payment Request Safeguards

## Practice
- Prevent support staff from requesting payment through suspicious or prohibited channels.
- Treat urgency, gift-card, cryptocurrency, or unusual wire-payment demands as scam indicators.
- Direct customers to verified billing channels.
- Escalate unexpected payment requests for fraud review.
- Preserve evidence of suspected impersonation.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Scams and Your Small Business: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/scams-your-small-business-guide-business-0
