# Service Design Excellence

## Purpose

ISO/TS 24082:2021 provides principles and activities for designing excellent services that aim to create outstanding customer experiences. ISO currently lists the 2021 technical specification as published and marked for revision, while a replacement edition is still under development.

## Practical use

Support and service teams can use the published guidance to:

1. identify the experience the service is intended to create;
2. connect customer needs and expectations to service design decisions;
3. design service interactions, channels, handoffs, and recovery paths intentionally;
4. test whether the designed service can be delivered consistently;
5. use customer and operational evidence to improve the design; and
6. distinguish basic service adequacy from deliberate service-excellence objectives.

## Current-status note

ISO/TS 24082:2021 remains the published edition. ISO/CD 24082 is under development and should not be treated as a published replacement until ISO completes the publication process.
