# Warranty Support Eligibility Check

Give support staff a consistent method to determine warranty eligibility without changing published warranty terms.

## Checks
- Verify product, purchase, term, and claim conditions.
- Separate warranty, service contract, and goodwill remedies.
- Escalate unclear exclusions or repeated failures.

## Source
FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
