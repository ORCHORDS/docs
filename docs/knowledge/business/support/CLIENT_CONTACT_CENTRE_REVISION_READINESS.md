# Client Contact Centre Revision Readiness

## Purpose

ISO 18295-2:2017 remains the published international standard for organizations using customer contact-centre services. ISO currently marks it to be revised, while ISO/AWI 18295-2 is an Edition 2 project under development.

## Practical use

Organizations that procure, operate, or govern contact-centre services should:

1. keep ISO 18295-2:2017 as the published baseline where it is referenced;
2. maintain clear client responsibilities, service expectations, performance measures, and governance arrangements;
3. identify contracts, supplier requirements, and internal procedures tied to the 2017 edition;
4. monitor the Edition 2 project without treating draft work as final requirements;
5. prepare a traceable gap assessment method for the eventual published replacement; and
6. update governed references only after the replacement edition is officially published and reviewed.

## Scope note

This article is revision-readiness guidance. It does not represent the content of an unpublished replacement edition.
