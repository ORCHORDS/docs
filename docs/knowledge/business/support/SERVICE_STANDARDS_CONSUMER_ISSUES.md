# Service Standards and Consumer Issues

## Purpose

ISO/IEC Guide 76:2020 gives recommendations for addressing consumer issues when developing service standards. Its scope covers a broad range of services, including services where there is no formal contract or purchase price.

## Practical use

Teams defining support or service standards should consider:

1. the needs of the people who receive and use the service;
2. accessibility and usability across relevant service channels;
3. clear information about what the service provides and what users can expect;
4. fair handling of problems, complaints, and service failures;
5. risks created by vulnerable situations or unequal access to information; and
6. whether service requirements can be understood and evaluated by the people affected by them.

## Source

- ISO/IEC — Guide 76:2020, Development of service standards — Recommendations for addressing consumer issues: https://www.iso.org/standard/73717.html

## Scope note

This is project-neutral guidance for service-standard design. It does not replace sector-specific consumer-protection or accessibility requirements.
