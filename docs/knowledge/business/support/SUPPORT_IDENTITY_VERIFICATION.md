# Support Identity Verification

## Practice
- Define verification strength by the sensitivity of the requested support action.
- Avoid relying on easily guessed profile information alone.
- Use stronger verification before credential, payment, or account-control changes.
- Record failed or suspicious verification attempts.
- Escalate impersonation indicators.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Scams and Your Small Business: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/scams-your-small-business-guide-business-0
