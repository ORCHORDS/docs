# Refund and Cancellation Support Script

Standardize support explanations for refunds, cancellations, fees, timing, and next steps.

## Checks
- Match the current published policy and transaction facts.
- State material conditions clearly.
- Avoid inventing fees, deadlines, or restrictions.
- Record exceptions and approvals.
