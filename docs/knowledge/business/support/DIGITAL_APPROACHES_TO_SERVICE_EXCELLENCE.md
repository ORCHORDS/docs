# Digital Approaches to Service Excellence

## Purpose

ISO/TS 19384:2026 gives guidance for applying ISO 23592 service-excellence principles and models through digital approaches. ISO lists Edition 1 as published in February 2026 and applicable to organizations that use, or plan to use, digital approaches to achieve service excellence.

## Practical controls

A digital-service programme should:

1. define which customer outcomes the digital approach is intended to improve;
2. keep digital channels aligned with the broader service model rather than treating automation as an end in itself;
3. design handoffs between self-service, automated assistance, and human support so customers are not trapped in unsuitable channels;
4. monitor usability, service quality, failure modes, and customer outcomes;
5. provide escalation paths when a digital interaction cannot resolve the customer's need; and
6. review whether new technology changes risk, accessibility, privacy, or service expectations.

## Technology governance

Introducing AI, automation, chat, or self-service does not remove the need for accountable service ownership. Digital approaches should be assessed for their effect on the whole customer journey and on customers who may require alternative channels.

This article is project-neutral service guidance and does not prescribe a particular technology stack or claim conformity with ISO/TS 19384.
