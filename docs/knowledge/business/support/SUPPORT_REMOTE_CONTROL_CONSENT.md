# Support Remote-Control Consent

## Practice
- Explain why remote access is needed before starting a session.
- Obtain explicit customer authorization for the session.
- Limit remote control to the approved task and duration.
- End access when the task is complete.
- Escalate sessions involving unexpected payment or credential requests.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Scams and Your Small Business: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/scams-your-small-business-guide-business-0
