# Support Escalation Evidence Retention

## Practice
- Preserve the minimum evidence needed to understand high-risk escalations.
- Include timestamps, channel, agent actions, customer-impact summary, and decision rationale.
- Avoid retaining unnecessary sensitive information.
- Link evidence to fraud, accessibility, safety, or dispute workflows where relevant.
- Review retention rules periodically.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission small-business guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/small-businesses
