# ISO 37000:2021 Governance of Organizations Governance

## Purpose

ISO 37000:2021, "Governance of organizations — Guidance," provides universally applicable guidance on the governance of organizations, defining governance as the system by which organizations are directed and controlled. The standard identifies principles of good governance (effectiveness, accountability, responsibility, transparency, responsiveness, equitability, inclusiveness, rule of law, sustainability), and defines the functions of the governing body (direction, oversight, accountability). This article governs the application of ISO 37000 so an organization can assess and improve its governance arrangements using the principles and evaluation criteria the standard provides.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization regardless of form (public, private, third sector). Within this knowledge base, the article covers the governance principles, the governance objectives and functions, the structure and composition of the governing body, the relationship between the governing body and management, and the evaluation of governance against the standard's principles. It does not replace legal or regulatory governance regimes (corporate law, sector regulation); it provides a framework that complements them.

## Workflow

1. Apply the governance principles to assess the organization's governance. Each principle is supported by practices and verifiable behaviors the standard describes.
2. Establish the governance objectives: the purpose of the governing body, the value the governance system delivers, and the outcomes the governance system is intended to achieve.
3. Define the governing body's functions: setting direction, providing oversight, and being accountable. For each function, identify the activities the governing body performs.
4. Structure the governing body for effectiveness: composition, skills, independence, committees, succession, and evaluation. Define the relationship between the governing body and management.
5. Implement the governance practices: ethics policy, conflicts-of-interest management, stakeholder engagement, decision processes, and information flows.
6. Evaluate governance against the principles on a planned cadence. Use the findings to improve the governance system.
7. Report on governance to stakeholders. Transparency is one of the principles.

## Controls and evidence

Governance evidence includes the governance charter, the composition and committee structure of the governing body, the governance practices, the meeting records, the conflict-of-interest disclosures, the evaluation records, and the governance reports. Each principle should be supported by verifiable evidence.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the governing body operates under a documented charter, the composition supports effective decision-making, the principles are applied in practice, the evaluation occurs on a planned cadence, and governance reports are published to stakeholders. External reviews provide additional assurance.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the governing body is staffed but lacks the information or independence to function effectively (corrective: define information flows, independence expectations, and resource support); principles are stated but not applied (corrective: require practice-level evidence for each principle); evaluation is performed but not used (corrective: require an improvement plan after each evaluation); conflicts of interest are not actively managed (corrective: require periodic disclosure and recusal where appropriate).

## Limitations

ISO 37000 is guidance, not a management system standard; it does not offer certification. The standard does not prescribe a specific governance structure (board of directors, supervisory board, etc.); the organization chooses the structure that fits its legal form. The standard's effectiveness depends on the governing body's willingness to apply the principles honestly.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral business use of ISO 37000:2021. It does not assert any specific organization's governance conformance or claim any certification outcome.
