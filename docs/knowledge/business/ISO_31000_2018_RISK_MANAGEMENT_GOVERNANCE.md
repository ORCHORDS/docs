# ISO 31000:2018 Risk Management Governance

## Purpose

ISO 31000:2018, "Risk management — Guidelines," provides a framework for managing risk that is applicable to any organization regardless of sector, and that can be integrated with other management system standards. The framework is structured around principles, framework, and process, and emphasizes the integration of risk with strategy, decision-making, and performance. This article governs the application of ISO 31000 so an organization can design and operate a risk management approach that is aligned with the standard and integrated with its decision processes.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the principles of risk management, the framework (leadership commitment, integration, design, implementation, evaluation, improvement), the process (communication, scope, risk assessment, treatment, monitoring, recording), and the integration of risk with decision-making. It does not certify risk management; it provides a structured approach.

## Workflow

1. Adopt the principles of risk management as the foundation: integrated, structured, customized, inclusive, dynamic, best-available information, human-and-cultural-factors, and continual improvement.
2. Establish leadership and commitment: explicit endorsement of risk management by top management, integration into governance, and allocation of resources.
3. Design the framework for risk management: scope, roles, authority, integration with strategy and decision processes, and resources.
4. Implement the framework: integrate into processes, communicate, and operate the risk management process.
5. Apply the risk management process:
   - Communication and consultation with stakeholders throughout.
   - Establishing the scope, context, and criteria for each risk activity.
   - Risk assessment: identification, analysis (likelihood and consequence), and evaluation (comparison with criteria).
   - Risk treatment: avoid, reduce, transfer, accept — with documented treatment plans.
   - Monitoring, review, recording, and reporting of risks.
6. Evaluate and improve the framework periodically. Use findings to refine the framework and the process.

## Controls and evidence

Risk management evidence includes the documented principles, the framework design, the role assignments, the integration into processes, the risk criteria, the risk register, the risk treatment plans, the monitoring records, and the review records. Each decision at the strategic and operational level should reflect the framework's integration.

## Validation

Validation should confirm leadership commitment is documented, the framework is integrated with strategy and decision processes, the risk process is applied consistently, risk treatment plans are implemented, and the framework is reviewed on a planned cadence. The maturity of risk management can be assessed against the standard's components.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: risk management is a separate compliance activity rather than integrated with decisions (corrective: require risk inputs in the decision template for material decisions); risk assessments are performed but not used to drive treatment (corrective: require a treatment plan for each assessed risk and track it); the framework is designed but not evaluated (corrective: schedule periodic evaluation and use findings to improve); risk criteria are not aligned with strategy (corrective: derive risk criteria from the organization's strategy and risk appetite).

## Limitations

ISO 31000 is guidelines, not a management system standard; it does not offer certification. The standard does not prescribe specific risk metrics or risk models; the organization selects the approach that fits its context. The standard does not guarantee risk outcomes; it ensures risk is considered systematically.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral business use of ISO 31000:2018. It does not assert any specific organization's risk management conformance or claim any certification outcome.
