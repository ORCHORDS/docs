# Warranty Remedy Fulfillment

Turn promised warranty remedies into a trackable service process.

## Checks
- Verify eligibility and promised remedy.
- Track repair, replacement, refund, or other resolution to completion.
- Record delays, repeat failures, and escalations.

## Evidence
Claim record, timestamps, remedy proof, customer notice.

## Source
FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
