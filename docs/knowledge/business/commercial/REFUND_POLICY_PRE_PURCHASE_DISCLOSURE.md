# Refund Policy Pre-Purchase Disclosure

## Practice
- Make material refund, cancellation, exchange, or final-sale restrictions visible before payment.
- Keep policy wording consistent across sales channels.
- Train sales and support teams not to contradict written terms.
- Record exceptions and discretionary refunds for consistency review.
- Recheck policy accuracy when product or fulfillment models change.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Complying with the Telemarketing Sales Rule: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule
