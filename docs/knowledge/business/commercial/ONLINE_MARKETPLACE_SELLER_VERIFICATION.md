# Online Marketplace Seller Verification

## Purpose

The U.S. INFORM Consumers Act applies to covered online marketplaces where high-volume third-party sellers offer new or unused consumer products. FTC guidance explains that covered marketplaces must collect, verify, and in specified circumstances disclose seller information, keep information current, suspend non-compliant sellers, and provide a clear reporting mechanism for suspicious activity.

## Operational controls

A marketplace compliance workflow should:

1. determine whether the platform and seller meet the Act's definitions;
2. detect when a seller reaches the statutory high-volume threshold;
3. collect required identity, contact, tax, and payment-related information within the required period;
4. verify information using methods capable of reliably determining that documents and information are valid and correspond to the seller;
5. obtain periodic recertification and updates;
6. make required seller disclosures when the applicable revenue threshold is reached;
7. maintain a consumer reporting channel for suspicious activity; and
8. suspend future sales activity when the Act requires suspension for non-compliance.

## Data handling

Because the process can involve sensitive seller information, collection and verification should be limited to what is required, protected appropriately, and separated from public-facing disclosure fields.
