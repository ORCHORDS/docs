# Written Warranty Term Disclosure

Operationalize complete, understandable written warranty terms and pre-sale availability.

## Checks
- Identify warrantor, covered products, duration, remedies, exclusions, and consumer duties.
- Make terms available before sale where required.
- Keep the disclosed version tied to the product/offer.

## Evidence
Approved warranty text, publication proof, change history.

## Source
FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
