# Rebate Promotion Disclosure

## Practice
- State the consumer's before-rebate cost clearly.
- Disclose the rebate amount and material eligibility conditions.
- Explain purchase requirements, deadlines, fees, and expected fulfillment timing.
- Avoid presenting the post-rebate amount as if it were the immediate purchase price.
- Preserve promotion terms and evidence of fulfillment.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
