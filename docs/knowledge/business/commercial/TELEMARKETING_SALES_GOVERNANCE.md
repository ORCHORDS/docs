# Telemarketing Sales Governance

## Purpose

The U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Telemarketing Sales Rule (TSR), 16 CFR Part 310, establishes requirements for covered telemarketing. FTC guidance summarizes core obligations including material disclosures, prohibitions on misrepresentations, calling-time limits, company-specific do-not-call requests, National Do Not Call requirements, payment restrictions for certain transactions, and recordkeeping.

## Governance controls

A reusable telemarketing programme should:

1. identify whether a campaign, seller, telemarketer, call type, or transaction is within the TSR's scope;
2. maintain current suppression and consent data before calls are placed;
3. ensure required disclosures are delivered clearly and before the point at which the rule requires them;
4. prohibit scripts, training, or incentives that encourage false or misleading statements;
5. control calling windows and do-not-call requests;
6. retain required records for the applicable retention period; and
7. allocate recordkeeping responsibilities clearly when sellers and telemarketers work together.

## Current amendments

FTC amendments effective in 2024 expanded protections against deceptive business-to-business telemarketing and updated recordkeeping requirements. Current processes should therefore not rely on older assumptions that most B2B telemarketing is outside the rule's deception protections.

## Technology neutrality

Changing technology does not remove the need to assess the rule. FTC guidance has specifically emphasized that existing restrictions can apply when automated or AI-enabled calling techniques are used.

## Scope note

This article is general operational guidance and not legal advice. Coverage, exemptions, consent requirements, and calling restrictions should be assessed for the relevant activity and jurisdiction.
