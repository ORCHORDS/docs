# Telemarketing Call Recordkeeping

## Practice
- Define which telemarketing records must be retained for the business model.
- Preserve scripts, offers, seller information, and required transaction evidence.
- Keep records retrievable for complaint or compliance review.
- Protect retained records from unauthorized alteration.
- Review retention and deletion procedures periodically.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Telemarketing guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/telemarketing
