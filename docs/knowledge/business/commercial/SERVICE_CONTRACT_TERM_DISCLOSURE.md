# Service Contract Term Disclosure

Keep service-contract terms distinct from product warranties and clear before purchase.

## Checks
- State coverage, duration, exclusions, fees, claim process, and cancellation terms.
- Avoid presenting a paid service contract as a free warranty benefit.
- Retain the version accepted by the customer.

## Source
FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
