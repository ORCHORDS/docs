# Optional Ancillary Fee Disclosure

## Practice
- Separate mandatory charges from optional ancillary goods or services.
- Do not present optional fees as part of the mandatory total price.
- Describe optional add-ons clearly enough for informed selection.
- Ensure intermediaries receive accurate fee data when they display prices.
- Audit checkout flows for inconsistent fee treatment.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
