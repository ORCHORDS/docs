# Returns, Refunds, and Resolution Operations

## Purpose

A clear returns and refund process reduces disputes and helps staff resolve product or service problems consistently.

## Operating pattern

1. Publish return, exchange, store-credit, and refund conditions where customers can find them before and after purchase.
2. Record deadlines, eligibility conditions, warranty boundaries, and required proof of purchase.
3. Give support staff a consistent set of resolution options and escalation limits.
4. Preserve receipts, invoices, warranties, order records, and customer correspondence relevant to the case.
5. Distinguish seller obligations from manufacturer warranty processes.
6. Explain the selected resolution and any remaining customer options in plain language.
7. Track recurring defect, fulfilment, or service complaints and feed them back into product and operations reviews.

## Resolution options

Depending on the transaction and applicable law, outcomes may include a full refund, partial refund, exchange, store credit, repair, replacement, or other agreed remedy.

## Boundary

Return and refund rights vary by transaction type and jurisdiction. A voluntary store policy must not be represented as eliminating rights that applicable law provides.
