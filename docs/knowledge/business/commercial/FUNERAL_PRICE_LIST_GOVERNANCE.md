# Funeral Price List Governance

## Purpose

The FTC Funeral Rule requires covered funeral providers to give consumers accurate, itemized price information and specified disclosures. Commercial operations should govern the required price lists as controlled customer-facing records.

## Practical controls

1. Maintain a current General Price List with identifying information, effective date, required disclosures, and itemized prices.
2. Maintain Casket and Outer Burial Container price information in the permitted format when those goods are offered.
3. Make required lists available at the points and times specified by the Rule, including accurate telephone price information when requested.
4. Prevent bundling or sales practices that require consumers to buy goods or services the Rule permits them to decline.
5. Keep the Statement of Funeral Goods and Services Selected accurate and itemized for the consumer's actual selections.
6. Record effective dates, approvals, superseded versions, and evidence that staff received updated pricing and disclosure materials.

## Source

- Federal Trade Commission — Complying with the Funeral Rule: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-funeral-rule

## Scope note

This article applies only to businesses within the Funeral Rule's scope. State law can impose additional or stricter requirements.
