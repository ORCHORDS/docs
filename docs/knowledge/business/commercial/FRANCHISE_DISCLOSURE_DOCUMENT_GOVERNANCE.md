# Franchise Disclosure Document Governance

## Purpose

The FTC Franchise Rule requires covered franchisors to provide prospective franchisees with prescribed disclosure information before a franchise sale. Commercial operations should treat the Franchise Disclosure Document and its supporting records as controlled pre-sale materials.

## Practical controls

1. Determine whether the offering is within the Franchise Rule's scope and identify any applicable exemptions with documented legal analysis.
2. Maintain the required disclosure document with the mandated categories of information and current supporting records.
3. Control update, amendment, approval, and distribution processes so prospects receive the required current version within the applicable timing rules.
4. Keep financial-performance representations, if made, within the Rule's permitted disclosure framework and support them with appropriate records.
5. Train sales personnel not to contradict, omit, or supplement required disclosures with unsupported claims.
6. Retain evidence of the disclosure version and delivery associated with each prospective franchise transaction.

## Source

- Federal Trade Commission — Franchises, Business Opportunities, and Investments guidance hub: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/industry/franchises%2C-business-opportunities%2C-and-investments

## Scope note

Franchise regulation also exists at the state level and can impose registration, disclosure, relationship, renewal, or termination obligations beyond the federal rule.
