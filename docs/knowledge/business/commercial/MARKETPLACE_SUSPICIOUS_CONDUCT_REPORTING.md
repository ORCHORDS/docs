# Marketplace Suspicious Conduct Reporting

## Practice
- Provide a clear consumer mechanism for reporting suspicious seller conduct.
- Route reports to a defined review queue with ownership and escalation.
- Link suspicious-conduct handling with seller verification records.
- Preserve evidence needed to investigate repeated or coordinated abuse.
- Track response times and outcomes.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Online Advertising and Marketing: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/online-advertising-marketing
