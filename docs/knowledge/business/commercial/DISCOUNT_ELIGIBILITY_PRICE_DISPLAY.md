# Discount Eligibility Price Display

Make conditional discounts understandable without obscuring the price a customer actually qualifies to pay.

## Checks
- State eligibility conditions near the offer.
- Keep conditional pricing from overshadowing required total-price disclosure.
- Recalculate accurately when eligibility changes.
