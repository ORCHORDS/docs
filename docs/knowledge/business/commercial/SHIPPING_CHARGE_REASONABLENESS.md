# Shipping Charge Reasonableness

Govern excluded shipping charges so customers understand their nature, purpose, and amount before payment.

## Checks
- Identify shipping separately from mandatory non-shipping fees.
- Disclose the amount before payment.
- Reconcile displayed shipping with the final payment amount.

## Source
FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
