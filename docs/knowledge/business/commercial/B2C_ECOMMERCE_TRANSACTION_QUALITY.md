# B2C Electronic-Commerce Transaction Quality

## Purpose

ISO 10008:2022 provides guidance for planning, designing, developing, implementing, maintaining, and improving business-to-consumer electronic-commerce transaction systems. ISO lists the 2022 second edition as the current published edition; it replaced and withdrew ISO 10008:2013.

The public ISO description says the guidance is intended to support fair, effective, efficient, transparent, and secure B2C electronic-commerce transactions, with the aim of increasing consumer confidence and satisfaction.

## Transaction lifecycle

A useful B2C transaction model should consider the whole customer journey rather than treating checkout as an isolated page. Areas to design and review include:

1. **Pre-transaction information** — make the identity of the seller, material product or service information, pricing, applicable terms, and important limitations understandable before commitment.
2. **Customer decision and order formation** — make selections, quantities, charges, and the action that commits the customer clear enough to reduce avoidable mistakes.
3. **Confirmation and records** — provide an understandable record of what was ordered and the material transaction information the customer may need later.
4. **Fulfilment** — align delivery or service performance with the representations made during the transaction.
5. **Changes, cancellation, returns, and refunds** — communicate available processes and conditions clearly and apply them consistently with applicable law and contract terms.
6. **Support and complaints** — make post-transaction help discoverable and connect unresolved issues to complaint and dispute-resolution processes.
7. **Data and security** — protect transaction information using controls appropriate to the risks and applicable legal requirements.
8. **Measurement and improvement** — use failures, complaints, abandonment patterns, customer feedback, and operational data to improve the transaction system.

## Avoid dark or misleading transaction design

A technically functional transaction can still damage customer confidence if material information is hidden, choices are confusing, fees appear too late, or cancellation and remedy paths are unnecessarily difficult. Transaction-quality review should therefore include the overall customer experience and not only system availability or payment success.

## Edition control

ISO 10008:2013 is withdrawn. New documentation should cite ISO 10008:2022 unless a historical context specifically requires the earlier edition. When a standard is version-sensitive, record the edition so readers can distinguish current guidance from superseded material.
