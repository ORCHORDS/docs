# Business Opportunity Disclosure Governance

## Practice
- Determine whether the offering falls within applicable business-opportunity rules.
- Prepare required disclosures before accepting payment or commitment.
- Keep earnings or success claims supportable and consistent with disclosures.
- Prevent sales staff from improvising unsupported claims.
- Retain signed or delivered disclosure evidence.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising and Marketing Basics: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/advertising-marketing-basics
