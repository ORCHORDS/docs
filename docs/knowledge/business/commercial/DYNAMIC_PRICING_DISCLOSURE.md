# Dynamic Pricing Disclosure

Keep changing prices truthful and ensure mandatory charges are handled consistently with current FTC fee-disclosure requirements.

## Checks
- Show the applicable total price prominently where required.
- Timestamp or otherwise control fast-changing displayed prices.
- Prevent stale cart and checkout totals.

## Source
FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
