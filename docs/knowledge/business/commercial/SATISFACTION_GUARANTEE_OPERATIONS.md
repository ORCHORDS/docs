# Satisfaction Guarantee Operations

## Purpose
Translate advertised satisfaction or money-back guarantees into an operational process that can actually honor the promise.

## Practice
- Document the guarantee term, eligibility window, exclusions, and return method.
- Make material limitations clear before purchase.
- Ensure support and refund teams can recognize eligible claims.
- Track denied guarantee requests and reasons for consistency.
- Reconcile advertising language with the real refund workflow.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
- Federal Trade Commission, Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
