# Telemarketing Payment Restriction Review

## Practice
- Map payment methods used in telemarketing transactions.
- Check applicable restrictions before introducing new payment mechanisms.
- Prevent sales staff from steering consumers toward prohibited or high-risk payment methods.
- Review scripts and payment-processing integrations together.
- Record remediation when restricted payment paths are found.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Complying with the Telemarketing Sales Rule: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule
