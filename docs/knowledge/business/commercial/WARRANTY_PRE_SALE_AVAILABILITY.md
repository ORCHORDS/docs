# Warranty Pre-Sale Availability

## Practice
- Identify products covered by written warranties.
- Make warranty terms available before sale where required.
- Ensure online and remote sales provide a practical way to review the warranty.
- Keep advertised warranty language consistent with the written warranty.
- Periodically test that warranty links and copies remain accessible.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
