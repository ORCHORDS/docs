# Implied Warranty Limitation Review

Review commercial terms that limit or disclaim implied warranties before publication or sale.

## Checks
- Confirm wording is consistent with applicable warranty obligations.
- Check interaction with any written warranty or service contract.
- Escalate jurisdiction-specific questions for legal review.

## Evidence
Reviewed terms, approval, version history.

## Source
FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
