# Final Payment Amount Reconciliation

Verify checkout totals immediately before the customer is asked to pay.

## Checks
- Reconcile total price, permitted exclusions, selected optional goods/services, taxes, and shipping.
- Display final payment amount prominently.
- Block mismatches between UI, order record, and charge request.

## Source
FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
