# B Corp Certification Governance

## Purpose

B Corp Certification is administered by B Lab and assesses companies across five impact areas: governance, workers, community, environment, and customers. Governance ensures that an enterprise pursuing B Corp Certification understands the assessment requirements, that the assessment reflects actual practices, and that recertification is maintained.

## Current context and source status

B Lab publishes the B Corp Certification standards and assessment methodology. The current assessment includes the B Impact Assessment (BIA), the standards for specific topics, and the new requirements for governance, workers, human rights, and climate. Verify the current B Lab standards and the assessment version before treating any specific requirement as a current requirement.

## Governance workflow and controls

### 1. Determine readiness

Determine readiness for the B Impact Assessment. Review the assessment scope, the evidence requirements, and the minimum scores for certification.

### 2. Conduct the assessment

Conduct the B Impact Assessment with verifiable evidence. Address the five impact areas: governance, workers, community, environment, customers. Address the cross-cutting standards.

### 3. Address the climate requirements

Address the climate requirements. For companies subject to the climate requirement, document emissions, targets, and reduction plans.

### 4. Adopt the legal requirement

Adopt the legal amendment to the articles of incorporation or equivalent. The legal requirement aligns the company's purpose with stakeholder interests.

### 5. Verify the assessment

Submit the assessment to B Lab for verification. Provide supporting documentation. Address any verification findings.

### 6. Maintain certification

Maintain certification through the recertification cycle. Update the assessment when the company changes materially.

### 7. Disclose B Corp status

Disclose B Corp status with care. Avoid claims that overstate the certification scope.

## Validation and evidence

- B Impact Assessment responses with evidence.
- Legal amendment documentation.
- Verification report.
- Recertification cycle records.

## Failure correction

Common defects include assessment responses that are not supported by evidence, missing legal amendment, and missed recertification deadlines. Corrective actions include an evidence-claim alignment review, a legal amendment filing, and a recertification calendar.

## Limitations

- B Corp Certification is a private standard administered by B Lab.
- The assessment is self-reported with verification; verification depth varies.
- Recertification is required; failure to recertify results in loss of status.
- The standards evolve; track B Lab updates.
