# Prescription Drug Advertising Balance Review

## Scope and governing basis

This article governs prescription-drug promotion. Check the controlling source for coverage, definitions, exemptions, amendments, and jurisdiction before deciding a campaign. This is an operational control, not advice on particular facts. State, local, privacy, platform, and contractual duties may apply independently.

The topic-specific release rule is: Test truthfulness, indication boundaries, fair balance, material risk presentation, prominence, and adequate provision.

Open a record identifying advertiser, offer, product, audience, geography, channels, publication dates, landing pages, fulfillment path, and every party allowed to alter delivery. Assign a business owner and reviewer. Classify uncertain executions before creative production so a late disagreement cannot be resolved by relabeling the campaign.

## Workflow

Translate the communication into testable representations. Inventory headlines, body copy, prices, numbers, comparisons, images, demonstrations, audio, omissions, targeting criteria, and material conditions. Describe the reasonable audience takeaway in plain language. Link each takeaway to dated evidence that existed before publication and covers the same product, population, period, and conditions.

The specialist review must examine the operative system, not a mockup alone. Compare claims with source records, disclosures, application screens, scripts, and the post-click experience. Test common mobile and desktop views and audio sequences. Qualifications should travel with the triggering claim and remain noticeable under realistic rendering.

Create a locked release package containing final assets, disclosures, destinations, audience settings, schedule, and vendor instructions. Give it a version identifier and checksum or equivalent immutable reference. A second person compares trafficking with approval. Experiments, translations, dynamic fields, and generated variants require the same review as manually written copy.

## Controls

Maintain a topic owner who tracks the canonical source and starts reassessment when rules, guidance, product facts, or system behavior change. Set claim expiry to the shortest-lived input. Automated checks should block expired evidence, missing approval, changed destinations, and unauthorized audience expansion. Manual sampling includes low-volume variants and non-default paths.

Require agencies, affiliates, and platforms to use authorized assets, preserve identifiers, avoid independent claims, and report complaints or regulator contacts promptly. Contracts support audit, correction, withdrawal, and evidence return. Platform acceptance is not compliance proof because platform checks may address different requirements.

Separate creation, approval, trafficking, and verification where staffing permits. Restrict and log overrides. Reconcile advertised prices, eligibility, inventory, rankings, performance periods, or other changing facts to systems of record at launch and on a documented risk-based cadence.

## Verification evidence

Retain intake, classification, dated source copy, claim-evidence matrix, raw records, calculations, approvals, escalation, rendered captures, asset identifier, targeting export, vendor instructions, destination tests, start and stop times, monitoring, complaints, and corrective actions. Apply the governing retention schedule and legal holds rather than inventing a universal period.

An independent verifier should reconstruct what the audience encountered and why every material representation was supportable then. Replay links, forms, telephone numbers, disclosures, transaction logic, opt-outs or cancellations where relevant, and partner delivery. Record environment, timestamp, result, and defect disposition. A screenshot without source data or delivery configuration is incomplete evidence.

## Failure handling

Missing, stale, contradictory, or narrower evidence blocks release unless the claim is narrowed and reapproved. For a live defect, stop new delivery, preserve exact creative and settings, identify variants and syndication partners, and prevent caches or scheduled jobs from republishing it. Do not overwrite records needed to assess exposure.

The incident owner establishes audience, geography, dates, impressions, spend, transactions, and likely consumer effect. Qualified personnel determine whether correction, notice, refund, rescission, reporting, or regulator engagement is required. Correct source systems before restart, verify withdrawal independently, and document rationale. Recurrence triggers stronger gates, training, vendor remediation, or termination.
