# Warranty Limitation Advertising Disclosure

Ensure material warranty limits are not hidden when advertising warranty coverage.

## Checks
- Explain how consumers can obtain full warranty details.
- Disclose material time, return, eligibility, or other limits clearly.
- Keep advertising consistent with the written warranty.

FTC Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
