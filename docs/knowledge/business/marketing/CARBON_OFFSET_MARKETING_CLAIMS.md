# Carbon Offset Marketing Claims

## Purpose

The FTC Green Guides address carbon-offset claims because consumers can be misled about whether an offset represents a real, additional, properly accounted-for emissions reduction.

## Practical controls

1. Use competent and reliable scientific and accounting methods to quantify claimed emission reductions.
2. Prevent double counting or double selling of the same reduction.
3. Disclose when the underlying reduction will not occur for at least two years.
4. Do not claim an offset for activity that is already required by law.
5. Keep the project, methodology, quantity, timing, ownership, and retirement evidence tied to the exact marketing claim.
6. Avoid broad climate-neutral or equivalent claims when the evidence only supports a narrower offset statement.

## Scope note

The Green Guides are U.S. FTC guidance. Carbon-market rules, accounting standards, and environmental-claim laws can impose additional requirements.
