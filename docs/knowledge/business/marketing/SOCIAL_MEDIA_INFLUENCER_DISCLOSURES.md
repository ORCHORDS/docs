# Social Media Influencer Disclosures

## Practice
- Identify material connections between the brand and endorser.
- Require disclosures that are clear and hard to miss.
- Place disclosures where the endorsement is actually consumed.
- Review sponsored content before and after publication.
- Correct missing or ambiguous disclosures quickly.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Endorsements, Influencers, and Reviews: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews
