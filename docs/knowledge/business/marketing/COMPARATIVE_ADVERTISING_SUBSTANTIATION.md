# Comparative Advertising Substantiation

## Practice
- Write down the exact comparative claim being made.
- Preserve evidence supporting the compared products, conditions, and time period.
- Avoid cherry-picked or outdated comparisons.
- Review implied as well as express claims.
- Refresh substantiation when products or market conditions change.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising FAQs and truth-in-advertising guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
