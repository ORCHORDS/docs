# Satisfaction Guarantee Advertising Conditions

Use satisfaction or money-back guarantee claims only when operational terms support the advertised promise.

## Checks
- Confirm refund practice matches the claim.
- Disclose material conditions and limitations.
- Test campaign copy against actual returns workflow.

## Source
FTC Businessperson's Guide to Federal Warranty Law: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law
