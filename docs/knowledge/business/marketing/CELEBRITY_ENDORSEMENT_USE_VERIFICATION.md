# Celebrity Endorsement Use Verification

Keep celebrity endorsements truthful throughout the campaign lifecycle.

## Checks
- Confirm the endorsement reflects honest experience/opinion.
- Verify claimed product use when the ad implies use.
- Reconfirm continuing endorsement validity during long-running campaigns.

FTC Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
