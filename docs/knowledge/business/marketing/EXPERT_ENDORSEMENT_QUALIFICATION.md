# Expert Endorsement Qualification

Verify that an expert endorser has relevant qualifications and that the endorsement is based on an evaluation appropriate to the field.

## Checks
- Validate expertise.
- Retain testing/evaluation basis.
- Substantiate claims as if made directly by the advertiser.
