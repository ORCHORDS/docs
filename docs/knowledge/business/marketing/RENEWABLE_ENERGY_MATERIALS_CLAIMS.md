# Renewable Energy and Renewable Materials Claims

## Purpose

The FTC Green Guides give specific guidance for claims that products are made with renewable energy or renewable materials. These claims can mislead when marketers do not explain the source, extent, or accounting basis of the claimed benefit.

## Practical controls

1. Identify the renewable-energy source clearly, such as wind or solar, when needed to prevent a broader misleading impression.
2. Do not make an unqualified renewable-energy claim when significant manufacturing processes use fossil-fuel energy unless matching renewable energy certificates or equivalent evidence supports the claim.
3. Do not claim to use renewable energy that the organization generated if it sold the associated renewable energy certificates and no longer owns the claimed attribute.
4. For renewable-material claims, identify the material, why it is renewable, and the portion of the product or package it represents when needed for clarity.
5. Keep energy, certificate, sourcing, material-composition, and claim-approval evidence together.
6. Re-review claims when energy contracts, certificate ownership, suppliers, or material percentages change.

## Scope note

Renewable-energy certificate rules and environmental-claim laws can differ by jurisdiction. This article summarizes U.S. FTC-oriented marketing guidance.
