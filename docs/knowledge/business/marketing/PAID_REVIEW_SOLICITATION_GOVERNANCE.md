# Paid Review Solicitation Governance

## Practice
- Prohibit fake reviews and undisclosed incentives for positive sentiment.
- Review agency and vendor review-generation practices.
- Keep incentive terms neutral rather than conditioned on favorable ratings.
- Preserve evidence of compliant review campaigns.
- Remove or correct noncompliant content where possible.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Endorsements, Influencers, and Reviews: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews
