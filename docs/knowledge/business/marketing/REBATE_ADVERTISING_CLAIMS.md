# Rebate Advertising Claims

## Practice
- State the actual out-of-pocket cost before rebate.
- Disclose rebate amount, eligibility, purchase conditions, and timing.
- Avoid making the rebate look automatic when additional consumer action is required.
- Keep campaign creative consistent with fulfillment terms.
- Monitor complaint patterns for misleading presentation.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
