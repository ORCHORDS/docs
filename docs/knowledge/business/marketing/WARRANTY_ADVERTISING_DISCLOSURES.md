# Warranty Advertising Disclosures

## Practice
- When advertising a warranty or guarantee, explain how consumers can obtain details.
- Clearly disclose material limitations or conditions.
- Keep marketing language consistent with the written warranty.
- Avoid implying broader coverage than the actual warranty provides.
- Recheck links and referenced terms during campaigns.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
