# Digital Disclosure Proximity and Prominence

## Practice
- Put material disclosures close to the claim they qualify.
- Use presentation that is noticeable and understandable on the device or medium used.
- Avoid burying essential terms behind unrelated links or dense text.
- Test disclosure visibility on mobile and desktop layouts.
- Recheck disclosures after design changes.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Online Advertising and Marketing: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/online-advertising-marketing
