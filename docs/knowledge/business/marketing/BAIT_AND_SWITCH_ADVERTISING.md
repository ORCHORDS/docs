# Bait-and-Switch Advertising

## Purpose

Advertising should not promote an attractive product or price when the seller does not genuinely intend to sell the advertised offer and instead plans to steer customers to a different, usually more expensive, transaction.

## Control pattern

- Confirm advertised products, quantities, conditions, and prices are genuinely available as represented.
- Do not disparage or undermine the advertised item merely to redirect the customer.
- Do not create artificial obstacles to purchasing the advertised offer.
- Train sales staff that upselling is acceptable only when the original advertised offer remains honestly available on its stated terms.
- Preserve evidence supporting inventory, offer terms, and campaign dates.
- Correct or stop campaigns promptly when availability or terms materially change.

## Customer experience test

Ask whether a reasonable customer responding to the advertisement can actually buy the promoted item under the represented conditions. If the operational intent is to use the advertisement only as a lead generator for a different sale, the campaign presents a serious deception risk.
