# Typical Results Testimonial Disclosure

## Practice
- Determine whether a testimonial implies results consumers can generally expect.
- Support objective claims before publication.
- Disclose limited or atypical applicability clearly when necessary.
- Do not rely on vague phrases such as 'results may vary' to cure a misleading net impression.
- Revalidate claims when evidence changes.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Advertising FAQs: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business
