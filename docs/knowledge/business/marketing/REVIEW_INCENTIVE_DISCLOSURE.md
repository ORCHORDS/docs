# Review Incentive Disclosure

Govern review incentives so compensation or benefits do not mislead consumers about review independence or sentiment.

## Checks
- Never condition incentives on positive sentiment.
- Disclose material incentives where required.
- Keep campaign terms and review-solicitation instructions.

## Source
FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule Q&A: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers
