# Optional Fee Advertising Disclosure

Distinguish truly optional goods or services from mandatory charges in advertising and checkout messaging.

## Checks
- Make optional status clear.
- Avoid preselected options that create misleading price impressions.
- Keep optional charges separate from mandatory total-price requirements.

## Source
FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
