# Environmental Certification and Seal Claims

## Purpose

The FTC Green Guides explain that environmental certifications and seals can communicate implied environmental claims and may function as endorsements. A seal does not remove the marketer's responsibility to substantiate what consumers are likely to understand from it.

## Practical controls

1. Identify the specific environmental attribute or attributes the certification evaluates.
2. Disclose material connections to the certifying organization when they could affect credibility.
3. Avoid seals that imply broad environmental superiority when the certification covers only a limited attribute.
4. When the seal itself does not clearly state its basis, provide a clear and prominent explanation close to the claim.
5. Substantiate the express and implied environmental claims independently of the certification.
6. Re-review the claim if certification criteria, certifier independence, product composition, or claim context changes.

## Scope note

A certification or seal is not a general safe harbor for environmental advertising. Other endorsement, labeling, and jurisdiction-specific requirements may also apply.
