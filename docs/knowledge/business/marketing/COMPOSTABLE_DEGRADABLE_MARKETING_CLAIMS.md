# Compostable and Degradable Marketing Claims

## Purpose

The FTC Green Guides distinguish compostable and degradable claims and require evidence that matches how consumers are likely to understand the claim under customary disposal conditions.

## Practical controls

1. For compostable claims, retain competent and reliable scientific evidence that all materials will break down safely into usable compost in an appropriate timeframe.
2. Qualify compostable claims when the product cannot be composted safely or promptly at home.
3. Qualify claims about municipal or institutional composting when appropriate facilities are not broadly available to the intended market.
4. Make unqualified degradable claims only when the entire item will completely break down and return to nature within the applicable reasonably short period after customary disposal.
5. Do not imply landfill-disposed products will rapidly biodegrade when customary landfill conditions do not support the claim.
6. Keep disposal assumptions, test methods, timing, and claim wording linked in the marketing evidence record.

Environmental-claim rules can differ by jurisdiction and waste-management system. This article summarizes U.S. FTC-oriented guidance.
