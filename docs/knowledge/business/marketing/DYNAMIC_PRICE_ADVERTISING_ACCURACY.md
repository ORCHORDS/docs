# Dynamic Price Advertising Accuracy

Control fast-changing advertised prices so consumers see current, truthful price information.

## Checks
- Define price refresh and expiry behavior.
- Prevent stale ads from contradicting current offers.
- Preserve required total-price prominence and disclosures.

FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions
