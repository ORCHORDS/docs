# Review Platform Moderation Integrity

## Practice
- Moderate reviews using documented, viewpoint-neutral integrity rules.
- Detect clearly fake, manipulated, or inauthentic submissions.
- Avoid suppressing legitimate negative reviews merely because they are unfavorable.
- Keep moderation actions auditable.
- Review vendor moderation practices and appeals.

## Source basis
- Federal Trade Commission, Online Advertising and Marketing: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/online-advertising-marketing
