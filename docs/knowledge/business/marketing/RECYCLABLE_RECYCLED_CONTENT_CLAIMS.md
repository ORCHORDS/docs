# Recyclable and Recycled-Content Claims

## Purpose

The FTC Green Guides distinguish claims that a product or package is recyclable from claims about recycled content. Each needs evidence and qualifications that match the claim consumers receive.

## Practical controls

1. For recyclable claims, verify that the item can actually be collected, separated, or otherwise recovered for reuse through established recycling programs.
2. Qualify recyclable claims when appropriate recycling facilities are not available to a substantial majority of the consumers or communities where the item is sold.
3. Distinguish whether recycled-content claims refer to pre-consumer or post-consumer material when that distinction is material.
4. State percentages or other quantitative recycled-content claims accurately and retain the supply-chain evidence supporting them.
5. Do not use a recycling symbol or similar visual in a way that creates a broader recyclable or recycled-content impression than the evidence supports.
6. Re-review claims when material composition, suppliers, collection infrastructure, or recycling practices change.

Recycling infrastructure and labeling requirements vary by jurisdiction. This article summarizes U.S. FTC-oriented marketing guidance.
