# IAASB 2024 Quality Management for Audits Governance

## Purpose

The IAASB (International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board) standards ISQM 1, ISQM 2, and ISA 220 (Revised 2024) establish a quality management approach for firms that perform audits or reviews of financial statements, or other assurance or related services engagements. ISQM 1 requires firms to design, implement, and operate a system of quality management. ISA 220 (Revised) requires engagement-level quality management. This article governs how firms apply these requirements so audit quality is managed as a system rather than as a series of individual engagement checks.

## Scope

The standards apply to firms performing audit and assurance engagements. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the ISQM 1 components (governance and leadership, relevant ethical requirements, acceptance and continuance, engagement performance, resources, information and communication), the engagement-level responsibilities of ISA 220, and the documentation of quality management. It does not cover substantive auditing procedures or accounting topics; readers should consult the relevant ISA standards for those.

## Workflow

1. Design the firm's system of quality management. Identify the quality objectives for each ISQM 1 component, the risks to achieving those objectives, and the responses to those risks.
2. Implement the responses. Responses include policies, procedures, tools, training, monitoring activities, and engagement-quality reviews.
3. Operate the system continuously. The system operates through engagement performance and through the firm's monitoring activities.
4. Monitor the system. Monitor each ISQM 1 component on a planned cycle, evaluate findings, and identify deficiencies.
5. Evaluate deficiencies. Distinguish isolated deficiencies from systemic deficiencies and from significant deficiencies (per ISQM 1 criteria). Take root-cause action on systemic and significant deficiencies.
6. Document the system. ISQM 1 requires documentation sufficient to support the operation of the system and to enable external inspection.
7. Apply ISA 220 (Revised) at each engagement: engagement-quality reviews where required, the engagement partner's responsibilities, and the documentation of engagement-level quality.

## Controls and evidence

Quality management evidence includes the system's design documentation (quality objectives, risks, responses), the implementation records (policies, procedures, training records, monitoring activity records), the deficiency evaluations, the corrective actions, and the engagement-level records (engagement-quality review records, partner sign-offs). The annual ISQM 1 evaluation is a critical control.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the ISQM 1 system is designed and documented, the components operate, monitoring occurs on the planned cycle, deficiencies are evaluated against the standard's criteria, root-cause actions are taken on systemic and significant deficiencies, and engagement-level ISA 220 (Revised) requirements are met. External inspections provide additional assurance.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the ISQM 1 system is documented but not operating (corrective: review the operation against the documented design and address gaps); monitoring is performed but deficiencies are not evaluated (corrective: apply the standard's deficiency criteria to every monitoring finding); root-cause analysis is superficial (corrective: require root-cause analysis that addresses underlying causes, not just symptoms); engagement-level quality reviews are nominal (corrective: define review criteria, independence, and documentation requirements).

## Limitations

IAASB standards establish the quality management framework; they do not prescribe substantive auditing methods. The effectiveness of the system depends on the firm's culture, leadership, and resources. The standards require firms to design a system appropriate to their nature and circumstances; a small firm has a simpler system than a multinational firm, but both must meet the standard's requirements.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral business use of the IAASB 2024 quality management standards. It does not assert any specific firm's quality management conformance or claim any inspection outcome.
