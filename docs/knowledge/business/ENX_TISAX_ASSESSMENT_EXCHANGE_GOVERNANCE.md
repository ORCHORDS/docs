# ENX TISAX Assessment and Exchange Governance

## Purpose

Govern the studio's participation in TISAX (Trusted Information Security Assessment Exchange) governed by ENX Association so that assessments are scoped to the right assessment level for the business relationships served, conducted by qualified audit providers, and shared through the ENX platform under controlled access rather than ad-hoc certificate distribution.

## Scope

Applies to the studio's TISAX assessments and labels serving automotive industry information security requirements. Covers assessment level selection, audit provider engagement, assessment scope definition, finding handling, and label sharing. Does not cover the underlying information security management (ISO/IEC 27001 guidance covers that).

## Workflow

1. Determine the required TISAX assessment level (AL1, AL2, or AL3) and scope from the business relationships that demand it: customers specify the level and scope labels they require; building to a higher level than any relationship requires spends audit budget without purpose.
2. Register with ENX and define the assessment scope per the TISAX scope rules (locations, organizational units, and relevant processes) so the label's coverage matches the claims customers will rely on.
3. Select an audit provider from ENX-approved TISAX audit providers; the provider's qualification is ENX's gate, but availability, sector familiarity, and scheduling fit the studio's choice.
4. Prepare per the applicable TISAX catalog (the information security catalog based on the version in force for the assessment cycle) with self-assessment evidence: policies, controls implementation, and the ISMS context.
5. Handle findings per TISAX rules: the assessment report records findings and maturity levels; corrective action plans with deadlines are agreed where required, and follow-up assessment confirms closure.
6. Share results only through the ENX platform exchange: the TISAX label and report are shared with specific registered partners under access control; emailing assessment reports to unverified recipients defeats the exchange's trust model.
7. Track label validity and re-assessment windows: TISAX results carry a defined validity period, and re-assessment scheduling starts early enough to avoid a coverage gap that breaks customer requirements.

## Controls and evidence

- Assessment level and scope determination record tied to customer requirements.
- ENX registration and scope definition records.
- Audit provider engagement records with provider qualification confirmation.
- Self-assessment evidence package per the applicable catalog version.
- Assessment report, findings, corrective action plans, and closure confirmations.
- Label sharing records through the ENX platform (who accessed, when).
- Validity and re-assessment calendar.

## Validation

- Confirm the current label's scope covers the processes customers actually rely on — scope narrower than customer assumptions is a silent failure.
- Confirm sharing has occurred only through the platform exchange, with no email-distributed reports in the sample period.
- Confirm re-assessment is scheduled to complete before the current label's expiry.

## Failure correction

- **Scope gap discovered (process outside label scope)** → amend scope and re-assess the gap area before the customer relationship depends on it.
- **Corrective action overdue** → escalate internally, notify the audit provider per the plan terms, and close or renegotiate the deadline.
- **Report shared outside the exchange** → notify the recipient of the improper channel, re-share through the platform, and gate future sharing on the exchange process.

## Limitations

- TISAX is an automotive-industry mechanism; other sectors use different schemes and the label does not transfer.
- Assessment levels below AL3 involve progressively reduced examination depth; labels at lower levels support correspondingly narrower claims.
- The TISAX catalog versions evolve; assessments are conducted against the version in force at assessment time, and customers may specify versions.
