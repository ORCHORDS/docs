# OWASP JAAS Risk Ownership Review

## Purpose

Connect the **JAAS** security topic to accountable business risk decisions when it is relevant to a product, service, supplier, or operating process.

## Source

- Official OWASP guidance: https://github.com/OWASP/CheatSheetSeries/blob/master/cheatsheets/JAAS_Cheat_Sheet.md
- Source location reviewed: 2026-09-23

## Decision questions

1. Is the topic relevant to a revenue, customer, operational, legal, contractual, or trust-critical process?
2. Which service owner is accountable for the technical implementation decision?
3. What business impact could follow if the relevant control fails?
4. Are third parties or shared responsibilities involved?
5. Which evidence is required before a risk can be accepted, reduced, transferred, or closed?
6. Does a material change require renewed technical and business review?

## Risk record

Capture the affected process, technical owner, business owner, impact categories, current evidence, open remediation, exception expiry, and review trigger.

## Boundary

This record does not assign a severity or claim legal applicability automatically. Impact and obligations must be determined from the actual context and authoritative requirements.
