# ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 Service Management Audit Governance

## Purpose

ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018, "Information technology — Service management — Part 1: Service management system requirements," specifies requirements for a service management system (SMS) for the planning, design, transition, delivery, and improvement of services. The standard uses the ISO high-level structure shared with ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO 37301, supporting integration with these. This article governs the application of ISO/IEC 20000-1 so an organization's SMS is designed to meet the standard's requirements and can be audited effectively.

## Scope

The standard applies to any organization that provides or plans to provide services, and to the services themselves. Within this knowledge base, the article covers the SMS scope, the service management policy, the planning and service delivery processes, the performance evaluation, the internal audit, and the management review. It does not cover the supporting standards (20000-2, 20000-3); readers should consult those for guidance and terminology.

## Workflow

1. Determine the scope of the SMS: which services, which customers, which geographic boundaries, which organizational units.
2. Establish the service management policy at the appropriate level, supported by leadership, addressing the organization's commitment to service management and continual improvement.
3. Plan the SMS: define service objectives, identify risks and opportunities, plan changes, and plan resources.
4. Implement the SMS processes for the service life cycle: design and transition, service delivery, service control, and release.
5. Support the SMS with documented information, competence, awareness, and communication.
6. Monitor, measure, analyze, and evaluate the SMS: service-level reporting, internal audit, nonconformity and corrective action.
7. Conduct management reviews on a planned cadence.
8. Pursue certification audits where applicable; certification bodies audit the SMS against the standard's requirements.

## Controls and evidence

SMS evidence includes the scope, the policy, the service management plan, the service-level agreements, the service delivery records, the incident and problem records, the change records, the internal audit results, the management review minutes, and the corrective action records. The Statement of Applicability (where used) identifies which Annex A controls apply and how they are implemented.

## Validation

Validation should confirm the SMS scope is documented, the policy is supported, the service life cycle processes are implemented, internal audits cover the SMS, management reviews are conducted, corrective actions are tracked to closure, and the SMS is integrated with other management systems. Certification audits (where pursued) verify against the standard.

## Failure correction

Common failure modes: the SMS is implemented for one service and not extended (correct: prioritize services based on criticality and document the prioritization); service-level agreements are not maintained (correct: review and update SLAs at planned intervals); internal audits are nominal (correct: audit with specific objectives and follow up on findings); the SMS is not integrated with other management systems (correct: align with the ISO high-level structure and reuse compatible processes).

## Limitations

ISO/IEC 20000-1 specifies requirements; it does not prescribe specific service management tools. The standard does not guarantee service outcomes; it ensures the SMS is in place to manage services. Sector regulations may impose specific service management obligations beyond the SMS.

## Scope note

This article summarizes project-neutral operations use of ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018. It does not assert any specific organization's SMS certification or claim any service outcome.
