# Recovery Communications Governance

## Purpose

Coordinate accurate, timely, and audience-appropriate communications during material recovery activity.

## Requirements

Recovery communications SHOULD identify audience, owner, approved facts, uncertainty, next update trigger, and any required legal, privacy, security, or customer review.

Do not present estimates as guarantees or disclose sensitive details merely to appear transparent.

Communication cadence should reflect user impact and material change rather than arbitrary frequency.
