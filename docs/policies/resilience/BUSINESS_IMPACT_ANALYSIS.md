# Business Impact Analysis

## Purpose

Define how ORCHORDS identifies activities whose disruption could materially
affect customers, legal obligations, security, finances, reputation, or
recovery capability.

## Requirements

A business impact analysis (BIA) MUST identify:

- the activity or capability being assessed;
- accountable owner;
- material upstream and downstream dependencies;
- important information and records;
- credible disruption scenarios;
- impact over time;
- minimum acceptable operating capability;
- recovery priorities and sequencing;
- assumptions that could invalidate the assessment.

Recovery time and recovery point objectives MAY be defined where they are
useful, but they must reflect business impact and tested capability.

## Impact dimensions

Assess at least customer harm, security/privacy harm, regulatory or contractual
impact, financial impact, operational dependency impact, and reputation.
Ratings should explain the consequence rather than rely on a number alone.

## Review triggers

Reassess after a major business change, material incident, dependency change,
recovery-test failure, or significant change in obligations.
