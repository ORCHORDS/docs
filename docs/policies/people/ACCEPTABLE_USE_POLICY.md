# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

Set baseline expectations for safe and responsible use of company information,
devices, accounts, collaboration tools, and authorized services.

## Requirements

Personnel MUST:

- use access only for authorized purposes;
- protect authentication factors and recovery methods;
- avoid bypassing security controls without approved exception;
- use approved methods for sensitive information;
- promptly report suspected compromise, loss, or accidental disclosure;
- respect legal, contractual, privacy, and intellectual-property obligations.

Company resources must not be used for malicious activity, unauthorized access,
harassment, deliberate policy evasion, or storage of clearly unlawful content.

## Personal use

Limited personal use may be permitted when lawful, reasonable, low-risk, and
not disruptive, but it does not override security or record-handling
requirements.

## Monitoring

Authorized security and operational monitoring may occur for protection,
reliability, investigation, and compliance purposes subject to applicable
privacy and legal requirements.
