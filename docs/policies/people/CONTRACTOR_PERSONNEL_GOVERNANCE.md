# Contractor Personnel Governance

## Purpose

Apply appropriate personnel, access, confidentiality, training, and offboarding expectations to non-employee contributors.

## Requirements

Material contractor engagements SHOULD define sponsor, scope, required access, confidentiality obligations, applicable policies, security training where needed, review date, and end-of-engagement offboarding.

Contractor status must not be used to bypass least-privilege or lifecycle controls.
