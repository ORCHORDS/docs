# Critical Role Absence Coverage

## Purpose

Reduce key-person risk when critical responsibilities temporarily lack their usual owner.

## Requirements

Material roles SHOULD have appropriate backup coverage, documented escalation, access readiness where justified, and knowledge transfer sufficient for temporary continuity.

Coverage planning should avoid unnecessary standing privilege and should respect private leave and health information.
