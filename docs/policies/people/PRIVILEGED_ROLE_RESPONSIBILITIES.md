# Privileged Role Responsibilities

## Purpose

Define behavioral expectations for personnel with elevated technical, financial, security, privacy, or administrative authority.

## Responsibilities

Privileged personnel MUST use authority only for authorized purposes, protect elevated authentication material, avoid unnecessary standing privilege, preserve evidence for sensitive actions, and promptly report mistakes or compromise.

## Separation of duties

Where practical, a person should not independently request, approve, execute, and conceal a high-impact action. Controls should reflect actual risk rather than rigid ceremony.

## Accountability

Privileged status does not remove ordinary policy requirements. Emergency authority must be time-bounded and reviewed afterward.
