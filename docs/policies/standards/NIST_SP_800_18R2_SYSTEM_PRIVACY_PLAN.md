# NIST SP 800-18r2 System Privacy Plan

## Purpose

Summarize the system privacy plan element of NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2.

## Guidance

A system privacy plan should centralize the information needed to understand privacy risk within the system boundary, including data processing, responsible roles, applicable privacy requirements, relevant controls, and the system context in which those controls operate.

Document what data is created, collected, used, disclosed, retained, and disposed of, along with material data flows and dependencies that affect privacy risk. Keep plan content aligned with actual processing rather than treating it as a one-time compliance artifact.

## Evidence

Useful evidence includes the approved plan, data-flow documentation, processing inventories, privacy-control implementation details, role assignments, review records, and change assessments.

## Verification

Confirm that material changes to data use, system boundaries, integrations, controls, or responsible parties trigger plan review and risk reassessment.

## Source

- NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2, **Developing Security, Privacy, and Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management Plans for Systems**, final June 30, 2026: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/18/r2/final
