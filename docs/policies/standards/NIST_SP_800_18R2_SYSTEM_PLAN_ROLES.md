# NIST SP 800-18r2 System Plan Roles and Responsibilities

## Purpose

Summarize role-governance expectations associated with system security, privacy, and C-SCRM plans in NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2.

## Guidance

System plans should make responsibility visible. Identify who owns the system, who manages security, privacy, and supply-chain risk, who implements and assesses controls, who accepts risk, and who keeps plan content current.

Role descriptions should distinguish decision authority from implementation responsibility and should account for inherited/common controls and external service dependencies.

## Evidence

Useful evidence includes role assignments, approval records, plan-maintenance responsibilities, control ownership mappings, escalation paths, and authorization records.

## Verification

Confirm that every material plan section and control responsibility has an accountable owner and that changes in personnel, services, or governance trigger reassignment and review.

## Source

- NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2, final June 30, 2026, including supplemental system-plan role material: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/18/r2/final
