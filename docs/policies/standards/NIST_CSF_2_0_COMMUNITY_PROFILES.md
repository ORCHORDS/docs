# NIST CSF 2.0 — Community Profiles

## Purpose

Provide public guidance for using NIST CSF 2.0 Community Profiles to express shared cybersecurity priorities across organizations with common missions, sectors, technologies, threats, or requirements.

## Baseline

A Community Profile can help a community identify and communicate common cybersecurity outcomes while allowing individual organizations to tailor implementation to their own context.

## Governance guidance

- Define the community scope, participants, shared assumptions, and intended audience.
- Base profile outcomes on documented common needs rather than the preferences of a single participant.
- Distinguish shared target outcomes from organization-specific implementation choices.
- Record authoritative sources, dependencies, and known exceptions.
- Review the profile when common threats, technologies, regulations, or sector priorities materially change.

## Use

Community Profiles can support coordination, supplier expectations, sector guidance, benchmarking, and communication. They do not by themselves prove that any participant has implemented the outcomes in the profile.

## Verification

Confirm that the scope and participating community are defined, the selected outcomes are traceable to shared needs, and implementation claims remain organization-specific.
