# NIST CSF 2.0 — Small Business Quick Start

## Purpose

Provide public guidance for applying NIST CSF 2.0 concepts in smaller organizations with limited cybersecurity staff, budget, or formal process maturity.

## Baseline

NIST provides a CSF 2.0 Quick Start Guide specifically for small businesses with modest or no cybersecurity plans. The goal is practical risk reduction rather than implementing every framework outcome at once.

## Prioritization guidance

- Identify the business services, information, and technology whose loss would cause the greatest harm.
- Assign clear responsibility for cybersecurity decisions even when no dedicated security team exists.
- Focus first on high-impact basics such as account protection, software updates, backups, secure configuration, incident contacts, and supplier awareness.
- Use simple Current and Target Profiles to record what is in place and what should improve next.
- Reassess priorities after material business, technology, supplier, or threat changes.

## Governance

Small size does not remove the need for ownership, evidence, or review. Documentation can remain lightweight while still identifying responsible people, decisions, exceptions, and follow-up actions.

## Verification

Confirm that priority outcomes are linked to real business risks, owners are assigned, planned improvements are distinguished from current controls, and progress is periodically reviewed.
