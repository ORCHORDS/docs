# NIST SSDF — Prepare the Organization

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for the **Prepare the Organization (PO)** practice group in NIST SP 800-218 Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) Version 1.1.

## Baseline

SSDF Version 1.1 is the current final NIST publication. NIST also published an initial public draft of SSDF Version 1.2 in December 2025; that draft should not be represented as final guidance.

Prepare the Organization establishes the people, processes, technology, policies, and supporting resources needed to perform secure software development consistently.

## Governance guidance

- define and communicate secure-development requirements and responsibilities;
- assign accountable roles for secure-development activities and risk decisions;
- integrate security requirements into the software development life cycle;
- maintain secure-development environments, tooling, and supporting processes;
- provide role-appropriate training and capability development;
- define criteria for third-party and supplier contributions where they affect software security;
- periodically review practices as threats, technology, business needs, and NIST guidance change.

## Verification

Confirm that secure-development responsibilities, requirements, environments, and supporting capabilities are documented, current, and reflected in operating practice. Policy statements alone are not evidence that SSDF practices are implemented.
