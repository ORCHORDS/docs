# NIST SP 800-18r2 C-SCRM Plan

## Purpose

Summarize the cybersecurity supply chain risk management plan element of NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2.

## Guidance

A C-SCRM plan should describe how supply-chain risks are identified, assessed, treated, monitored, and communicated for systems and their dependencies. It should identify relevant suppliers, products, services, roles, controls, dependencies, and risk decisions within the system context.

Keep supplier-risk assumptions, control implementation details, provenance information, resilience considerations, and material dependency changes current enough to support acquisition and ongoing risk management.

## Evidence

Useful evidence includes the approved plan, supplier inventories, risk assessments, due-diligence records, control mappings, exception decisions, dependency documentation, and review records.

## Verification

Confirm that material supplier, product, service, ownership, provenance, or control changes trigger reassessment and plan review.
