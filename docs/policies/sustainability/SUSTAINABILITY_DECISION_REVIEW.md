# Sustainability Decision Review

Material sustainability decisions should record the business objective, environmental considerations, options evaluated, evidence quality, important tradeoffs, affected stakeholders, decision owner, and follow-up actions.

The process supports informed decisions; it does not require environmental considerations to override security, safety, legal, accessibility, or continuity requirements.
