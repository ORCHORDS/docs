# EPA GHG Organizational Boundaries

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for defining which entities and assets are included in an organizational greenhouse gas inventory.

## Baseline

EPA explains that organizational boundaries determine which entities and assets are included in Scope 1 and Scope 2 inventories. The selected consolidation approach should be applied consistently so reported emissions remain decision-useful and comparable over time.

## Governance guidance

- Document the consolidation approach used for subsidiaries, joint ventures, partnerships, facilities, vehicles, and other controlled or influenced assets.
- Apply the chosen approach consistently across the inventory unless a documented methodology change is approved.
- Record acquisitions, divestitures, reorganizations, and ownership changes that affect boundaries.
- Distinguish organizational boundaries from operational boundaries, which determine the emission sources included by scope.
- Define when historical inventories or base-year data require recalculation after structural changes.
- Retain evidence supporting material inclusion and exclusion decisions.

## Verification

Confirm that the inventory boundary reconciles to the current organizational structure and relevant assets, and that changes are documented with effective dates and methodology impact.
