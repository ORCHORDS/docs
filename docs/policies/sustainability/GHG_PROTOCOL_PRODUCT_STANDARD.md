# GHG Protocol Product Life Cycle Standard

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for product-level greenhouse gas accounting across a product’s life cycle.

## Baseline

The GHG Protocol Product Life Cycle Accounting and Reporting Standard provides a globally applicable method for measuring greenhouse gas emissions associated with a product across raw materials, manufacturing, transportation, storage, use, and end-of-life stages. GHG Protocol states that the standard is intended to help identify life-cycle emission hotspots and reduction opportunities.

GHG Protocol and ISO are currently working on an updated, harmonized product-level accounting standard. Until a replacement is finalized, current product accounting should clearly identify the standard and version actually used.

## Governance guidance

- Define the product, functional or reference unit, life-cycle boundary, and reporting period before calculation.
- Document material exclusions, allocation rules, assumptions, emission factors, and data-quality limitations.
- Prefer primary activity data where it materially improves reliability and decision usefulness.
- Distinguish measured product emissions from comparative or avoided-emissions claims.
- Maintain traceability from reported results to source data and calculation methods.
- Reassess methodology when product design, suppliers, logistics, use assumptions, or end-of-life pathways materially change.

## Verification

Confirm that the inventory boundary covers the intended life cycle, material assumptions and exclusions are disclosed, and results can be reproduced from retained calculation evidence. Do not use product-footprint results alone to claim that one product is environmentally superior to another without an appropriate comparative methodology.
