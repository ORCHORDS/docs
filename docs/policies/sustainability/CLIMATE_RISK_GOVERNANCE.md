# Climate Risk Governance

Climate-related physical and transition risks are assessed where they could materially affect operations, suppliers, customers, assets, or strategic decisions.

## Governance

- Assess exposure using plausible scenarios rather than a single forecast.
- Distinguish physical, regulatory, market, supplier, and reputational drivers.
- Record assumptions, uncertainty, time horizon, owner, and treatment decision.
- Reassess when material operating conditions or external evidence changes.
- Link material climate risks to enterprise risk and resilience processes.
