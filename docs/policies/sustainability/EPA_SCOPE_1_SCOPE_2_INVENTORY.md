# EPA Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG Inventory Guidance

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for calculating and maintaining Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas inventories.

## Baseline

EPA defines Scope 1 emissions as direct emissions from sources owned or controlled by an organization and Scope 2 emissions as indirect emissions associated with purchased electricity, steam, heat, or cooling.

## Governance guidance

- Identify in-scope stationary, mobile, fugitive, purchased-energy, and other relevant sources.
- Use documented activity data and current, appropriate emission factors.
- Record estimation methods for missing or incomplete data.
- Apply consistent units, global warming potentials, and calculation conventions within the reporting period.
- Review material source additions, retirements, and changes before finalizing the inventory.
- Reconcile reported totals to supporting facility or source-level data.
- Preserve the methodology and source evidence needed to reproduce reported results.

## Verification

Confirm completeness across material emission sources, calculation consistency, factor provenance, and reconciliation to source data before relying on inventory totals for targets or public claims.
