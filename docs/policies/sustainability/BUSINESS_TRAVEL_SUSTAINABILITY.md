# Business Travel Sustainability

Travel decisions should balance business value, employee wellbeing, security, cost, and environmental impact.

Where outcomes can be achieved effectively without travel, lower-impact alternatives should be considered. Any travel-footprint reporting must use consistent scope, activity data, factors, and treatment of multi-leg journeys.
