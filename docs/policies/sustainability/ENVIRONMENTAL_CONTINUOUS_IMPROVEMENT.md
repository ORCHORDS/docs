# Environmental Continuous Improvement

Environmental improvement opportunities may arise from incidents, reviews, metrics, supplier changes, new evidence, employee observations, legal changes, or strategic decisions.

Improvement actions should define expected outcome, owner, due date, evidence, and effectiveness check. Repeated ineffective actions should trigger root-cause review and escalation.
