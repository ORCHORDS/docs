# Privacy Rights Requests

## Purpose

Define a governed process for requests by individuals concerning their personal data where applicable rights exist.

## Principles

Requests SHOULD be authenticated to a level appropriate to the information requested without collecting excessive new personal data.

The process should identify applicable rights, scope relevant records, preserve legal or security restrictions, coordinate supplier-held data where necessary, and provide a clear response.

## Timing

Applicable response periods depend on law and context. The authoritative obligation for the request must be identified rather than relying on a universal public deadline.

## Records

Keep enough evidence to show receipt, identity-verification approach, decision, actions, response date, and any lawful restriction.
