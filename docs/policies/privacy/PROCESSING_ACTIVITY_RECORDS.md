# Processing Activity Records

## Purpose

Maintain enough information about material personal-data processing to support
privacy governance and change review.

## Record elements

A processing activity record SHOULD identify:

- purpose;
- responsible business owner;
- categories of people and personal data;
- broad source and recipient categories;
- retention basis or schedule;
- lawful or contractual basis where relevant;
- international or third-party transfer considerations;
- key security/privacy controls;
- related privacy assessment where applicable.

## Scope

This public policy describes record expectations only. Actual processing
records may contain sensitive operational, contractual, or personal
information and generally do not belong in this public repository.

## Maintenance

Records should be updated after material processing changes and reviewed
periodically for obsolete or unnecessary processing.
