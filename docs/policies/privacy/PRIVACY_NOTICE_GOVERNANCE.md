# Privacy Notice Governance

## Purpose

Keep public privacy notices aligned with actual material processing and applicable obligations.

## Requirements

Privacy notices SHOULD be understandable, accurate for their scope, versioned or otherwise reviewable, and updated when material processing changes make existing statements misleading.

Notice content may address data categories, purposes, sharing, retention principles, rights, contact paths, and other required disclosures appropriate to jurisdiction and context.

## Claims

A privacy notice must not promise technical or organizational controls that are not supported by evidence.

## Change coordination

Material product, supplier, data, or AI changes that alter disclosed processing should trigger privacy-notice review before or alongside launch.
