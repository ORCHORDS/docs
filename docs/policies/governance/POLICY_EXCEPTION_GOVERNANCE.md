# Policy Exception Governance

## Purpose

Ensure policy deviations are explicit, bounded, reviewed, and not silently normalized.

## Requirements

A material exception MUST identify requirement, scope, owner, reason, risk, compensating controls, approver, and expiry or review date.

Permanent exceptions should be rare. If the same exception recurs broadly, the underlying policy or operating model requires review.

## Closure

Close an exception when the normal requirement is restored, the policy is formally changed, or the affected activity ends.

Expired exceptions must not remain valid by inaction.

Security-specific deviations also follow [Security Exception SOP](../sop/SECURITY_EXCEPTION_SOP.md).
