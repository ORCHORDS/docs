# Account Recovery Trend Analysis

## Purpose
Use aggregate account-recovery patterns to identify control weaknesses, abuse signals, and customer-friction risks.

## Requirements
- Trend analysis must use privacy-minimized, aggregate information where possible.
- Material changes in recovery volume, failure, escalation, or abuse signals require review.
- Findings must distinguish control issues from normal variation where evidence permits.
- Corrective actions must have owners and follow-up.

## Evidence
Retain trend summaries, review decisions, and action records without publishing customer identifiers or recovery methods.
