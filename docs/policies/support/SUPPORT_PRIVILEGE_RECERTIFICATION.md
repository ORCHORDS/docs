# Support Privilege Recertification

## Purpose
Ensure support privileges remain necessary for current responsibilities and customer-safe operations.

## Requirements
- Elevated support privileges must have a named owner and role-based justification.
- Privileges must be reviewed periodically and after material role changes.
- Excessive, unused, or conflicting privileges must be removed or corrected.
- Exceptions require documented risk acceptance and review dates.

## Evidence
Maintain dated review outcomes, reviewer identity, exceptions, and remediation evidence without exposing internal access mechanisms.
