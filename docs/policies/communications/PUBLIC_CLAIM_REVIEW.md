# Public Claim Review

## Purpose

Prevent unsupported statements about security, privacy, compliance, performance, market position, accessibility, AI, reliability, or business capability.

## Review

A material claim SHOULD identify exact wording, scope, time period, evidence source, owner, assumptions, and limitations.

Absolute terms such as "fully secure", "always available", "zero risk", "100% compliant", or "unbiased" require extraordinary evidence and generally should be avoided.

## Reuse

Claims must be revalidated when evidence ages, scope changes, a referenced standard changes, or incidents contradict the statement.

Related assurance rules are in [Claims and Attestation](../compliance/CLAIMS_AND_ATTESTATION.md).
