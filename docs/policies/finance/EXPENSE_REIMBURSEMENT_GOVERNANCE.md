# Expense and Reimbursement Governance

## Purpose

Ensure business expenses are legitimate, proportionate, documented, and independently reviewed where appropriate.

## Requirements

Reimbursable expenses SHOULD identify business purpose, amount, date, supporting evidence, claimant, and approver.

Self-approval, split transactions intended to avoid review, duplicate reimbursement, and undocumented exceptions are not acceptable normal practice.

Policy exceptions should be explicit and reviewable rather than hidden inside ordinary expense descriptions.
