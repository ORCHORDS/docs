# Payment Change Verification

## Purpose

Reduce fraud risk from impersonation, compromised communications, or incorrect payment instructions.

## Requirements

New or changed beneficiary, banking, wallet, payout, or other payment destination information SHOULD be independently verified using a trusted channel established separately from the change request.

Urgency, executive impersonation, supplier familiarity, or a matching email thread must not override verification for material changes.

## Approval

High-impact changes may require dual approval or additional evidence based on financial risk.

## Sensitive information

Do not place real banking credentials, full account identifiers, recovery phrases, or private payment secrets in public documentation.

See [Payment Change Verification SOP](../sop/PAYMENT_CHANGE_VERIFICATION_SOP.md).
