# Ethics Case Aging

## Purpose
Promote timely, fair, and accountable handling of ethics concerns without sacrificing investigation quality.

## Requirements
- Open cases must have an owner, status, and next action.
- Aging must be monitored against risk and case complexity.
- Stalled or overdue cases require documented escalation or refreshed plans.
- Aging trends should be reviewed for resourcing, process, or control weaknesses.

## Evidence
Retain case-aging summaries, escalation records, and closure evidence using privacy-protective access controls.
