# Asset Loss and Theft

## Purpose

Reduce harm when a company device, media item, physical credential, document, or other controlled asset is lost, stolen, or cannot be accounted for.

## Response

Loss MUST be reported promptly when the asset may contain company information, provide access, or create safety/security risk.

Assessment SHOULD identify asset, last known custody, information sensitivity, authentication capability, encryption or other protections, remote containment options, and whether credential revocation or incident escalation is required.

## Evidence

Preserve enough information for investigation, insurance, inventory reconciliation, legal obligations, and recovery without exposing unnecessary personal information.

See [Lost or Stolen Asset SOP](../sop/LOST_STOLEN_ASSET_SOP.md).
