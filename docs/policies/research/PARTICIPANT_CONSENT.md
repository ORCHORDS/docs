# Research Participant Consent

## Purpose

Ensure participants understand what they are agreeing to and can make a meaningful choice.

## Consent information

Where consent is appropriate, explain purpose, expected activity, recording or observation, data use, retention, incentives, foreseeable risks, voluntary nature, withdrawal limits, and contact route.

Consent should be understandable to the participant population and should not bundle unnecessary permissions.

See [Participant Consent SOP](../sop/PARTICIPANT_CONSENT_SOP.md).
