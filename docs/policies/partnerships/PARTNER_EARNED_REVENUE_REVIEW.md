# Partner Earned Revenue Review

## Purpose

This policy establishes the quarterly review of revenue that is partner-sourced, partner-influenced, partner-delivered, or partner-co-sold. It ensures that such revenue is verified against independent sources, that attribution is consistent with the partnership agreement, that disputes are resolved on a documented basis, and that the forecast reflects the most current partner pipeline and program changes.

## Scope

This policy applies to all revenue recognized under a partnership arrangement, including direct sales, channel sales, referral fees, marketplace transactions, co-sell revenue share, services revenue, support renewals, and any rebate or incentive that affects the revenue line attributable to a partner. It covers verification, attribution, dispute resolution, forecasting, and the records that demonstrate review integrity. It does not cover internal sales performance management except where it interacts with partner attribution.

## Requirements

- The Partnerships Lead MUST convene a quarterly earned revenue review covering every active partner with revenue in the period under review.
- Each partner's earned revenue MUST be reconciled between the partner's reported figure and the organization's internal figure; variances exceeding an agreed tolerance MUST be investigated.
- Attribution MUST follow the definition set out in the partnership agreement and the supporting commercial terms; any deviation MUST be documented and approved by the Partnerships Lead and Finance.
- Disputes regarding attribution, calculation, or amount MUST be logged, severity-rated, and resolved within thirty business days; escalated disputes go to governance.
- The review MUST include a forward-looking forecast for the next two quarters, reconciled against the partner pipeline, the partner's stated capacity, and known program changes.
- The Partnerships Lead MUST publish the review outcome to Finance, Sales Operations, and the partner account owner.
- The organization SHOULD retain evidence of reconciliation for the period required by the records-retention schedule.
- The organization MAY require partner audit rights, certified revenue reports, or third-party reconciliation where the volume or risk warrants.

## Workflow

1. **Data collection.** The Partnerships Lead obtains the partner's reported revenue and the organization's internal revenue figure for the period.
2. **Reconciliation.** The figures are reconciled line by line; variances are documented.
3. **Variance investigation.** Variances exceeding tolerance are investigated, with root cause and corrective action recorded.
4. **Attribution validation.** The attribution method is validated against the agreement; deviations are documented and approved.
5. **Dispute handling.** Open disputes are logged, assigned owners, and tracked to closure; aging disputes are escalated.
6. **Forecasting.** A forward forecast is prepared, reconciled against the partner pipeline and program changes.
7. **Review meeting.** The Partnerships Lead convenes the quarterly review meeting, publishes decisions, and circulates minutes.
8. **Records.** Reconciliation evidence, decisions, and minutes are retained per the records-retention schedule.

## Controls

- A quarterly earned revenue review is scheduled, convened, and minuted for every active revenue-bearing partner.
- Reconciliation evidence exists for every line item and is retained per policy.
- A dispute log captures every open dispute with owner, deadline, and status.
- A forward forecast is published each quarter and reconciled against pipeline.

## Records and retention

The Partnerships Lead retains the following records for each quarterly review: the meeting agenda, the partner-reported revenue statement, the internal revenue report, the reconciliation worksheet, the attribution validation, the dispute log, the forward forecast, and the meeting minutes with attendees and decisions. Records are retained for the period required by the records-retention schedule and are accessible to authorized reviewers including Finance, Internal Audit, and external auditors under the standard audit cooperation clause. Where partner audit rights apply, the organization coordinates review with the partner and limits disclosure to information necessary to verify the figures under review.

## Risk and escalation

Material variances, persistent attribution disputes, or repeated forecast misses are escalated to the governance body with a recommended corrective action. Where the review identifies revenue recognition concerns, the Partnerships Lead coordinates with Finance and, where required, External Audit to determine whether a restatement or disclosure is necessary. Repeated failure of a partner to provide timely or accurate revenue reporting is itself a partnership-performance issue and is addressed under the partner-performance review policy.
