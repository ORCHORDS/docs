# Partner Training Currency

## Purpose

This policy establishes how the partner team maintains currency on product, security, privacy, compliance, and support topics so that the partner can represent the organization accurately, support customers competently, and deliver services that meet current standards. It ensures that training plans are documented, evidence of completion is retained, refresh cycles are observed, and that role-based training requirements are matched to the partner's actual responsibilities.

## Scope

This policy applies to all partners authorized to represent the organization in pre-sales, sales, post-sales, support, implementation, managed services, training delivery, or any other function in which the partner's competence is customer-facing or operationally significant. It covers training needs analysis, training plan definition, course assignment, completion evidence, refresher cadence, role-based requirements, re-certification, and the records that demonstrate currency. It does not cover general business-skills training that is not specific to the partnership.

## Requirements

- The Partnerships Lead MUST maintain a training plan for each partner that identifies required courses by role, completion deadlines, and refresher cadence.
- Training MUST cover at minimum product, security, privacy, compliance, ethics, support processes, and any function-specific topics relevant to the partner's scope.
- Completion of each required course MUST be evidenced by a dated certificate or equivalent record retained by the partner and the organization.
- Refresher training MUST be completed within the defined cadence; missed deadlines MUST trigger a remediation period not exceeding sixty days.
- The partner MUST ensure that customer-facing personnel hold the role-based certifications required for their responsibilities before engaging with customers.
- The Partnerships Lead MUST validate training currency at least annually and after any material change in product, security posture, or regulatory environment.
- Where partner personnel are certified by an external certification body, the partner MUST maintain certification in good standing and provide evidence on request.
- The organization SHOULD publish a training catalog with course descriptions, prerequisites, delivery modes, and language availability.
- The organization MAY require partner instructors to hold current instructor credentials where the partner delivers authorized training on the organization's behalf.

## Workflow

1. **Needs analysis.** The Partnerships Lead identifies the training requirements for each partner role using a role-competency matrix.
2. **Plan publication.** A partner-specific training plan is published, with required courses, deadlines, and refresher cadence.
3. **Assignment.** Partner personnel are enrolled in the courses appropriate to their role.
4. **Delivery.** Courses are delivered through the approved channels; completion is tracked.
5. **Validation.** Completion evidence is collected and validated; gaps are identified.
6. **Remediation.** Missed deadlines trigger a defined remediation period with retraining or reassignment.
7. **Refresher cycle.** Refresher training is scheduled and completed within the defined cadence.
8. **Annual review.** The Partnerships Lead reviews training currency annually and after material change.
9. **Records retention.** Training records are retained per the records-retention schedule.

## Controls

- A training plan exists for every active partner with customer-facing or operationally significant responsibilities.
- Completion evidence is current and reconcilable against the role-competency matrix.
- Refresher cadence is documented and missed deadlines trigger documented remediation.
- Annual currency review findings are reported to the Partnerships Lead and the governance body.

## Role-competency matrix

The Partnerships Lead maintains a role-competency matrix that defines, for each role performed by partner personnel, the required competencies, the courses that deliver them, the evidence of attainment, and the refresher cadence. The matrix is reviewed at least annually and whenever a material change in product, security posture, regulatory regime, or support process occurs. The matrix is the source of truth for assigning training and for validating currency; deviations from the matrix MUST be approved in writing.

## Language, accessibility, and delivery

Training SHOULD be available in the languages in which the partner operates, with accessibility accommodations consistent with applicable accessibility law. Delivery modes include self-paced e-learning, instructor-led virtual sessions, and in-person sessions where the audience size warrants. Where partner instructors deliver authorized training on the organization's behalf, the partner MUST maintain current instructor credentials and provide evidence on request.
