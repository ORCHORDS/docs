# Project Governance

## Purpose

Define decision rights and evidence expectations for material projects without prescribing a single delivery methodology.

## Requirements

Material projects SHOULD have a sponsor, delivery owner, objective, measurable outcomes, scope boundary, delivery approach, key dependencies, risk view, decision path, and closure criteria.

Governance depth should increase with cost, irreversibility, customer impact, security/privacy exposure, regulatory impact, or cross-functional dependency.

## Decisions

Important decisions should record context, decision, owner, date, trade-offs, and follow-up actions.

ISO 21502:2020 may inform project-management practice; inclusion does not imply certification.
