# Benefits Realization

## Purpose

Verify whether completed work produced the intended outcome rather than equating delivery with value.

## Requirements

Material initiatives SHOULD define expected benefits, baseline or comparison where practical, accountable benefit owner, measurement method, timing, dependencies, and conditions that could invalidate attribution.

Benefits that occur after project closure must transfer to an accountable operational or business owner.

See [Benefits Review SOP](../sop/BENEFITS_REVIEW_SOP.md).
