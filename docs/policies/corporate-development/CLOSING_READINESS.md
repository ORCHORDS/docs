# Closing Readiness

Closing readiness should confirm conditions, approvals, funding or consideration mechanics, required signatures, records, communications, access changes, continuity plans, legal deliverables, ownership of post-close actions, and escalation for unresolved conditions.

Readiness evidence should be retained with the transaction record.
