# 2023 U.S. Merger Guidelines Governance

## Purpose

Provide public-safe governance for using the 2023 U.S. Merger Guidelines when assessing acquisition and investment risk.

## Baseline

The Department of Justice and Federal Trade Commission jointly issued the 2023 Merger Guidelines on December 18, 2023. The Guidelines describe factors and frameworks the agencies often use when reviewing mergers and acquisitions. DOJ states that the Guidelines are non-binding and do not create independent rights or obligations.

## Governance guidance

- Treat the Guidelines as an enforcement-analysis framework, not a substitute for legal advice or transaction-specific analysis.
- Begin antitrust review early enough to influence structure, diligence, timing, and approval decisions.
- Preserve factual evidence relevant to competition, including current competition, potential entry, customer alternatives, supplier dependencies, and strategic documents.
- Avoid relying solely on market-share calculations when other competitive evidence is material.
- Reassess the antitrust view when transaction scope, market facts, or agency guidance changes.
- Escalate material concerns to qualified antitrust counsel.

## Verification

Confirm that antitrust review reflects the current Guidelines, current facts, and applicable law, and that internal summaries do not present agency guidance as binding law.
