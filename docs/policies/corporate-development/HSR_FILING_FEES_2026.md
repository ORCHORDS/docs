# HSR Filing Fee Governance — 2026

## Purpose

Provide public-safe governance for validating Hart-Scott-Rodino filing fees before a reportable U.S. transaction is submitted.

## Current official state

The FTC publishes annual adjusted filing-fee tiers. As of the FTC’s February 17, 2026 update, the applicable fee depends on the adjusted size of transaction and ranges across multiple statutory tiers.

## Governance guidance

- Obtain the current fee table directly from the FTC for the intended filing date.
- Determine the applicable size of transaction using qualified transaction and antitrust analysis.
- Record the calculation source, applicable tier, verification date, and approved payment responsibility.
- Do not hard-code annual thresholds or fees into standing policy without an expiry or refresh mechanism.
- Reverify the fee immediately before payment if the filing timetable crosses an annual adjustment or other regulatory change.
- Keep transaction values and payment details confidential where required.

## Verification

Confirm that the transaction-size calculation and selected fee tier match the FTC’s current published table and that the payment method and timing satisfy current filing instructions.

- FTC, **Filing Fee Information**, updated February 17, 2026: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program/filing-fee-information
