# HSR Premerger Notification Readiness

## Sources

- U.S. Federal Trade Commission — Premerger Notification Program: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program
- U.S. Federal Trade Commission — Current HSR thresholds: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program/current-thresholds
- U.S. Federal Trade Commission — HSR Rules: https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/hsr-rules
- U.S. Federal Trade Commission — Filing fee information: https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program/filing-fee-information

## Purpose

The Hart-Scott-Rodino Act establishes a U.S. federal premerger notification process for certain transactions. The Federal Trade Commission administers the program, and reportable transactions generally require filings with both the FTC and the U.S. Department of Justice before the applicable waiting period can be satisfied.

HSR jurisdictional thresholds and filing fees are adjusted periodically, including annual threshold adjustments. Filing forms, instructions, and procedural requirements may also change. Transaction teams therefore must use the current FTC materials rather than relying on values or forms copied from an earlier transaction.

## Readiness controls

For a transaction with a possible U.S. HSR nexus, the transaction file should record:

- the proposed transaction structure and expected closing date;
- the current size-of-transaction analysis;
- any size-of-person analysis where relevant;
- applicable exemptions considered;
- the current FTC threshold source and date checked;
- legal-review status for reportability;
- filing responsibility and required information owners;
- the expected waiting-period milestone;
- filing-fee responsibility where a filing is required; and
- any agency request, extension, or other condition affecting closing readiness.

## Current-source requirement

Do not hard-code historical HSR thresholds into a standing decision rule. Before relying on a threshold, confirm the value currently published by the FTC and record the source used for the transaction analysis.

Likewise, use the current FTC filing instructions and submission process. The FTC's premerger program materials can change in response to rulemaking, litigation, or administrative updates.

## Closing gate

A transaction that requires HSR notification must not be treated as ready to close solely because documents have been signed. The closing checklist should independently confirm that applicable notification and waiting-period requirements have been satisfied or that qualified legal review has concluded that no filing is required.

## Public boundary

Public documentation may describe the HSR governance process but must not disclose confidential transaction values, filing contents, counterparties, agency communications, or non-public closing plans.

This document describes governance only and does not assert that any ORCHORDS transaction is reportable, filed, pending, cleared, or exempt.
