# Commercial Due Diligence

Commercial diligence should test demand assumptions, market position, pricing, customer concentration, contract durability, sales pipeline quality, renewal behavior, channel dependencies, competitive exposure, and material commitments.

Forecast claims should be traced to evidence and separated from transaction advocacy.
