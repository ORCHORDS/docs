# AI Auditability and Logging

## Purpose

Preserve enough evidence to investigate material AI decisions, actions, changes, and failures without indiscriminate collection of user content.

## Requirements

Risk-appropriate records SHOULD capture relevant system/version context, high-impact tool or action outcomes, policy decisions, human overrides, material errors, and change events.

Logging design must follow privacy, security, retention, and minimization requirements.
