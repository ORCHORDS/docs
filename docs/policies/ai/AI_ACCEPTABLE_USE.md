# AI Acceptable Use

## Purpose

Set company-wide expectations for responsible use of AI-assisted tools.

## Permitted use

AI may support research, drafting, coding, analysis, summarization, testing, and
other work when the user remains accountable for accuracy, security, privacy,
licensing, and final decisions.

## Restricted use

Do not:

- provide secrets, restricted information, customer data, or sensitive personal
  information to an AI service unless the use is specifically approved;
- treat generated code, legal analysis, security findings, financial analysis,
  or factual claims as verified without appropriate review;
- use AI to bypass access controls, policy, or required human approval;
- represent generated material as independently validated evidence;
- deploy autonomous actions with material impact without defined guardrails,
  authorization, and recovery paths.

## Review

Higher-risk uses follow [AI Risk Assessment](AI_RISK_ASSESSMENT.md).
