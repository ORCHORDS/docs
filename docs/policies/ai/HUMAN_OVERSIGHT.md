# AI Human Oversight

## Purpose

Define meaningful human oversight for AI-enabled decisions or actions where failure could cause material harm.

## Requirements

Oversight SHOULD specify who reviews, what information they receive, what authority they have to stop or override an outcome, and when escalation is required.

A human approval step is not meaningful if the reviewer lacks time, context, competence, or practical ability to disagree with the system.

## Automation level

Higher-impact uses require stronger oversight, narrower automated authority, or stronger pre-deployment evidence.

## Monitoring

Track whether human reviewers routinely override, ignore, or rubber-stamp AI outputs. Those patterns may indicate poor interface design, inadequate training, or an inappropriate allocation of responsibility.
