# AI System Inventory

## Purpose

Maintain a governed record of material AI-enabled capabilities so risk ownership and reassessment remain possible.

## Record expectations

A material AI record SHOULD identify owner, intended use, affected users, model or service class, data categories, degree of automation, human oversight, suppliers, important permissions, evaluation status, monitoring, and lifecycle state.

## Public boundary

The inventory itself may contain sensitive architecture or supplier information and generally remains private. Public documentation defines the governance expectation only.

## Lifecycle

Inventory updates are required after material model, provider, permission, data, or use changes and at retirement.
