# Third-Party AI Use

## Purpose

Define additional review for externally provided AI models, APIs, hosted
services, and embedded AI capabilities.

## Review areas

Before material use, assess:

- supplier terms and data-use commitments;
- retention and training behavior;
- security and privacy controls;
- model/update transparency;
- regional or legal considerations;
- availability and exit risk;
- ability to monitor or constrain harmful behavior;
- human oversight needs;
- dependency concentration.

## Change risk

Third-party AI services can change behavior without an application release.
Where that change can affect material outcomes, monitoring and reassessment are
required.

Use [Third-Party Risk Management](../third-party/THIRD_PARTY_RISK_MANAGEMENT.md)
alongside AI-specific review.
