# AI Evaluation and Assurance

## Purpose

Define evidence expectations before AI-enabled capabilities are relied upon for
material use.

## Evaluation dimensions

Depending on use, evaluation SHOULD consider:

- task quality and failure modes;
- robustness to realistic variation;
- security and prompt/input abuse risks;
- privacy and sensitive-data handling;
- bias or disparate impact relevant to the context;
- hallucination or unsupported-output risk;
- human oversight effectiveness;
- monitoring and rollback;
- model or provider change sensitivity.

## Evidence

Evaluation must use representative scenarios and documented acceptance
criteria. A small set of successful demonstrations is not sufficient evidence
for high-impact use.

## Change

Material model, prompt, retrieval, policy, data, or provider changes may
invalidate prior evaluation and should trigger reassessment proportional to
risk.

See [AI Risk Review SOP](../sop/AI_RISK_REVIEW_SOP.md).
