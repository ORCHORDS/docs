# Customer Sponsor Change

## Purpose

Establish an accountable, evidence-based approach to handling a change in a customer's executive sponsor — the senior individual on the customer side accountable for the engagement. The objective is to preserve continuity, refresh delegated authority, re-baseline commitments whose authority depended on the prior sponsor, and bring the new sponsor into a relationship that is honest about history, current state, and forward plan.

## Scope

This policy applies to any change in the customer executive sponsor for an active engagement, including permanent replacement, interim arrangement, dual-sponsor transitions, role reorganisation, secondment, and successor appointment following promotion or departure. It does not cover routine turnover of operational contacts within the customer's team (which is handled through contact-management practice), nor changes in the company's own account ownership.

## Requirements

- A sponsor change MUST be acknowledged in writing within a documented time bound. Acknowledgement MUST record the date the change became known, the source of the information, the prior sponsor's last day (if known), and the successor's appointment date.
- The customer-success lead MUST request a documented introduction to the new sponsor. The introduction MUST cover the engagement's purpose, history, current state, success plan, open commitments, risks, escalations, and the cadence and channels the customer can rely on.
- Delegated authority MUST be refreshed. Where the prior sponsor held delegated authority on behalf of the customer (to approve scope changes, accept deliverables, or commit resources), the customer's governance MUST issue new delegations, and the new delegations MUST be recorded in the system of record.
- Open commitments, escalations, and pending decisions MUST be re-stated to the new sponsor and re-affirmed or re-scoped by them. The re-statement MUST be in writing and MUST distinguish decisions already taken from decisions still open.
- The success-plan baseline MUST be re-baselined where its authority derived from the prior sponsor. Re-baselining MUST NOT silently rewrite history; the prior baseline and the re-baselined version MUST both be retained, with the change clearly dated.
- Information shared with the prior sponsor MUST be reviewed for confidentiality and minimum-necessary considerations before it is shared with the successor. Personal data of the prior sponsor MUST be removed or restricted to the documented retention period.
- The customer-success lead MUST invite the new sponsor to identify any continuity gaps, knowledge that was held by the prior sponsor, and any change in priority or scope that has not yet been communicated.
- The introduction MUST be coordinated with the company's account owner and other internal stakeholders (support, security, finance, legal) where they touch the engagement. Internal handover MUST be logged.
- Communication about the sponsor change MUST be handled with care in customer-facing materials and external references. Customer-success records MUST be updated to reflect the new sponsor; historical records MUST preserve the prior sponsor's role for audit purposes.
- The new sponsor MUST be offered an executive-business-review cadence appropriate to their engagement model. The cadence MAY remain at the prior cadence or be re-set with documented agreement; arbitrary changes without the customer's agreement are prohibited.
- A sponsor change that occurs in the middle of a renewal, expansion, or escalation MUST be flagged to the relevant owners. The continuity plan MUST address how in-flight commitments are handled and who within the customer has authority during the transition.
- A sponsor change that involves an internal reorganisation, a merger, an acquisition, or a divestiture MUST be treated with the additional care required by the customer-transition-readiness policy.
- The customer-success function MUST maintain a sponsor-change register that records the date of change, the prior and new sponsor, the actions taken, and the closure date of the re-baselining. The register MUST be reviewed at each governance cycle.
- A sponsor change MUST NOT be used to reset commercial terms, de-scope obligations, or re-open commitments that the prior sponsor closed in good faith. Where the customer wishes to do so, that re-opening is a separate negotiation governed by commercial policy.

## Workflow

1. The customer-success lead confirms the change through a documented source and records the acknowledgement.
2. A written introduction is sent to the new sponsor, framing the engagement honestly and offering a structured kickoff.
3. The kickoff convenes the relevant customer and internal stakeholders, reviews the success plan, the open commitments, and the escalations, and refreshes delegations.
4. The success-plan baseline is re-baselined where necessary, with both versions retained.
5. The sponsor-change record is closed when the new sponsor has acknowledged the engagement plan and the refreshed delegations are in place.
