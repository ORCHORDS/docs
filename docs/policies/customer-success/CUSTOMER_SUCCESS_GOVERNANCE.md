# Customer Success Governance

## Purpose

Create an accountable, evidence-based approach to helping customers achieve agreed outcomes after commercial handover.

## Requirements

Material accounts or engagements SHOULD identify success owner, desired outcomes, known commitments, onboarding status, dependencies, material risks, adoption signals, open escalations, and review cadence.

Customer-success activity must not invent commitments or bypass support, security, privacy, legal, or financial controls.

See [Customer Success Review SOP](../sop/CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_REVIEW_SOP.md).
