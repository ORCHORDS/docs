# Environmental Incident Review SOP

## Procedure
1. Record the incident scope, impact, owner, and immediate containment.
2. Identify applicable legal, contractual, supplier, or reporting obligations.
3. Determine root and contributing causes using available evidence.
4. Assign corrective actions with owners and due dates.
5. Verify completion and evaluate whether controls, training, or reporting need updates.
