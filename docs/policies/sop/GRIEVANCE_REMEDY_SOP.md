# Grievance and Remedy SOP

## Procedure
1. Receive and protect grievance information using appropriate confidentiality.
2. Triage severity, urgency, non-retaliation needs, and escalation paths.
3. Assign an impartial owner and investigate proportionately.
4. Determine corrective or remedy actions and responsible owners.
5. Track actions through closure and review effectiveness.
6. Preserve protected records without publishing identities or case details.
