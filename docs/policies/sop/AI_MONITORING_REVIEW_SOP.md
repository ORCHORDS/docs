# SOP: AI Monitoring Review

## Procedure

1. Confirm the monitored capability, intended use, and current risk assumptions.
2. Review quality/failure trends, safety/security indicators, complaints, tool/action failures, and material provider/model/configuration changes.
3. Check whether monitoring data remains representative and privacy-appropriate.
4. Compare observed behavior with acceptance criteria and known failure modes.
5. Trigger investigation, re-evaluation, restriction, rollback, or disablement when thresholds or risk assumptions are breached.
6. Record decision, owner, follow-up, and next review trigger.
