# Human Rights Due Diligence SOP

## Procedure
1. Define the activity, relationship, geography, and potentially affected groups.
2. Identify material human-rights risks and available evidence.
3. Assess severity, likelihood, leverage, and existing safeguards.
4. Define prevention, mitigation, monitoring, or remedy actions.
5. Assign owners and review triggers.
6. Record limitations and avoid unsupported public conclusions.
