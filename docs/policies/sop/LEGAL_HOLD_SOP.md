# SOP: Legal Hold

## Trigger
Use when authorized counsel/governance determines records must be preserved beyond ordinary retention.

## Procedure
1. Define authorized hold scope and effective date.
2. Identify record categories, systems, custodians, or suppliers as necessary.
3. Suspend normal deletion for affected records.
4. Communicate preservation instructions to relevant owners.
5. Confirm technical or procedural preservation controls.
6. Monitor material scope changes and compliance.
7. Keep hold information restricted to those who need it.
8. Release only on authorized instruction.
9. Reconcile records with normal retention after release.
