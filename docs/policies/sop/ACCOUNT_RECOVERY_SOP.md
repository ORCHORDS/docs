# SOP: Account Recovery

## Procedure

1. Identify the requested recovery action and its sensitivity.
2. Use approved recovery proof appropriate to the account risk.
3. Do not request passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or reusable login codes.
4. Escalate when normal proof is insufficient rather than weakening verification informally.
5. For high-risk recovery, record reviewer/approval evidence and protect sensitive recovery artifacts.
6. Complete the minimum required recovery action.
7. Notify through established channels where appropriate and record unusual indicators for security review.
