# Privacy Notice Applicability Review SOP

1. Compare current processing activities with published notice statements.
2. Review purposes, data categories, recipients, retention, rights, and material changes.
3. Identify stale or incomplete notice content.
4. Assign corrections, approvals, publication actions, and evidence retention.
