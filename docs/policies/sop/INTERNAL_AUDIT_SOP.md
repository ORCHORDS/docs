# SOP: Internal Audit

## Trigger
Use for an approved audit engagement in the risk-based internal audit plan or an authorized ad-hoc review.

## Procedure
1. Confirm auditor independence and competence.
2. Define objective, scope, period, criteria, stakeholders, and timing.
3. Identify key risks and controls.
4. Prepare evidence and sampling/test approach.
5. Conduct interviews, inspection, observation, reperformance, analysis, or other appropriate procedures.
6. Validate factual observations with relevant owners.
7. Rate material findings by impact and control significance.
8. Issue a clear report including scope limitations and management responses.
9. Track agreed actions and escalation needs.
10. Preserve workpapers in the authorized audit record location.
