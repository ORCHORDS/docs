# Refund Exception Aging SOP

1. Review material refund and credit exceptions by owner, age, reason, and status.
2. Escalate overdue or repeated patterns.
3. Determine corrective action, approval renewal, or closure.
4. Retain evidence without publishing customer or transaction details.
