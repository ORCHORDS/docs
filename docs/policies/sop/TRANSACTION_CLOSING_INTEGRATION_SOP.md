# Transaction Closing and Integration SOP

## Procedure
1. Confirm approved closing conditions, owners, dependencies, and unresolved exceptions.
2. Verify required handoffs, records, access, communications, and governance actions.
3. Establish integration or separation workstreams with accountable owners and milestones.
4. Track post-close obligations, risks, synergies, and corrective actions.
5. Conduct a post-close review and capture lessons without publishing confidential deal information.
