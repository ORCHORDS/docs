# Dependency Owner Recertification SOP

1. Identify material software dependencies due for review.
2. Confirm owner, criticality, lifecycle status, update responsibility, and unresolved risk.
3. Reassign ownership gaps and escalate unsupported or abandoned dependencies.
4. Record decisions and next review date.
