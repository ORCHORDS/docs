# SOP: Investment Review

## Procedure

1. Define decision, requested investment, and intended outcome.
2. Review strategic fit, evidence strength, cost, time to value, and opportunity cost.
3. Review key risks, dependencies, reversibility, and required functional input.
4. Test major assumptions and downside conditions.
5. Decide approve, conditionally approve, defer, re-scope, or reject.
6. Record rationale, conditions, owner, and review trigger.
