# SOP: Software and Service Acquisition

## Trigger
Use before introducing material third-party software, hosted services, plugins, APIs, or AI-enabled services.

## Procedure
1. Define business use and accountable owner.
2. Identify data, permissions, integrations, and user population.
3. Assess security, privacy, licensing, AI behavior, support, resilience, and portability.
4. Complete supplier due diligence proportional to risk.
5. Review contract and data-use terms.
6. Define deployment/integration, credential ownership, monitoring, renewal, and exit.
7. Obtain approvals before sensitive or production use.
8. Add the service to relevant inventory and review schedules.
