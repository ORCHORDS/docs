# SOP: Complaint Handling

## Trigger
Use for a complaint about products, services, support, accessibility, privacy, or company conduct that requires a tracked response.

## Procedure
1. Record the complaint and requested outcome.
2. Acknowledge receipt when appropriate.
3. Assess urgency, impact, privacy/safety issues, and conflicts of interest.
4. Gather relevant evidence and prior interactions.
5. Assign an appropriate reviewer, independent where required.
6. Decide remedy, explanation, escalation, or no-action outcome based on evidence.
7. Communicate the outcome and any further path available.
8. Protect complainant information and avoid retaliation.
9. Classify systemic issues for quality/CAPA or product review.
10. Preserve closure evidence.
