# SOP: Legal Request

## Trigger
Use when a formal legal, regulatory, court, law-enforcement, or preservation request is received.

## Procedure
1. Preserve the original request and receipt time.
2. Route it to an authorized legal/governance owner.
3. Verify authenticity, authority, jurisdiction, scope, and deadline.
4. Identify affected records and preservation needs.
5. Assess confidentiality, privacy, security, privilege, and contractual restrictions.
6. Collect only approved responsive information.
7. Obtain required response approval.
8. Deliver through the authorized channel and preserve delivery evidence.
9. Track follow-up, restrictions, and closure.

## Principle
Personnel should not improvise substantive legal responses outside their authority.
