# SOP: Supplier Evidence Review

1. Identify the assurance question and purchased-service scope.
2. Confirm evidence source/issuer, period, date, scope, independence, exceptions, and applicability.
3. Compare supplier evidence with actual data use, dependency, contract, architecture, and known incident context relevant to the service.
4. Record unsupported or stale claims and request clarification/evidence where material.
5. Decide whether residual uncertainty changes onboarding, monitoring, contract, renewal, or risk acceptance.
