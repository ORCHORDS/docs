# SOP: Evidence Collection

## Trigger

Use for periodic control review, audit preparation, risk closure, or validation
of an implementation claim.

## Procedure

1. Define the exact control or claim being evidenced.
2. Define period, scope, and freshness requirement.
3. Select the strongest practical source.
4. Capture enough context to establish provenance and meaning.
5. Remove or protect unnecessary sensitive information.
6. Record owner and collection date.
7. Validate that the evidence actually supports the claim.
8. Store it in the authorized evidence location.
9. Index the record without exposing secrets in public documentation.
10. Recollect when the evidence becomes stale or scope changes.
