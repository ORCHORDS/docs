# SOP: Benefits Review

## Procedure

1. Reconfirm the intended benefit and accountable benefit owner.
2. Compare observed outcomes with the stated baseline, target, or decision expectation where practical.
3. Review confounding factors, changed assumptions, adoption, operational cost, and unintended outcomes.
4. Distinguish evidence of delivery from evidence of benefit.
5. Decide whether to continue measurement, take corrective action, revise the benefit case, or close the benefit as achieved/not achieved.
6. Record lessons for future investment and project decisions.
