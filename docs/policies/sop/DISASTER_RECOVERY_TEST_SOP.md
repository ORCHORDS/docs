# SOP: Disaster Recovery Test

## Trigger

Use on the scheduled test cadence, after material recovery-path change, or when
an incident reveals uncertainty in restoration capability.

## Procedure

1. Define test objective and safe scope.
2. Record assumptions and success criteria.
3. Confirm roles, communication, and stop conditions.
4. Execute the recovery scenario without exposing production to unreasonable
   risk.
5. Verify restoration, integrity, access, dependencies, and usable operation.
6. Record elapsed times as evidence, not as promises.
7. Document deviations, manual workarounds, and failed steps.
8. Assign remediation owners and dates.
9. Retest material fixes when practical.
10. Close only after the outcome and residual risk are accepted.
