# Financial Approval Recertification SOP

1. Review material financial approval roles and business purpose.
2. Confirm current role, delegated authority, segregation, and continuing need.
3. Remove or correct obsolete and conflicting authority.
4. Record the decision without publishing limits, accounts, or payment instructions.
