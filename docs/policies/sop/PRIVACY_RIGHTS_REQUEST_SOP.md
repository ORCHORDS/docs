# SOP: Privacy Rights Request

## Trigger
Use when an individual submits a request concerning personal data and an applicable right may exist.

## Procedure
1. Record receipt and request scope.
2. Verify identity proportionally without excessive data collection.
3. Identify applicable obligation and response period.
4. Locate relevant records and suppliers where necessary.
5. Review lawful restrictions, security concerns, and third-party rights.
6. Perform approved access, correction, deletion, objection, portability, or other action as applicable.
7. Review response for completeness and sensitive-data exposure.
8. Respond through an appropriate channel.
9. Preserve decision and completion evidence.

## Escalation
Complex or disputed requests require privacy/legal review.
