# SOP: Audit Finding Follow-up

## Trigger
Use when an internal audit finding has an agreed management action, due date, or accepted risk requiring monitoring.

## Procedure
1. Confirm finding, owner, target date, and closure criteria.
2. Obtain remediation evidence from management.
3. Assess whether the evidence addresses the original risk and criteria.
4. Reperform or retest material controls where practical.
5. Mark closed, partially remediated, overdue, or risk-accepted.
6. Escalate significant overdue or ineffective remediation.
7. Preserve follow-up evidence and final conclusion.

## Principle
Internal audit verifies closure; it should not become the operational owner of remediation.
