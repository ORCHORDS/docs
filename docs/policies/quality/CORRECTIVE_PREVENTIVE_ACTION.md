# Corrective and Preventive Action

## Purpose

Ensure material failures lead to proportionate action that removes or reduces causes and verifies effectiveness.

## Sources

CAPA may be triggered by defects, incidents, complaints, audit findings, control failures, accessibility barriers, supplier failures, quality trends, or credible near misses.

## Requirements

A material CAPA record SHOULD identify:

- problem and evidence;
- immediate correction or containment;
- cause analysis appropriate to complexity;
- corrective/preventive actions;
- owners and review dates;
- effectiveness criteria;
- closure evidence.

## Quality

Adding training, reminders, or another checklist is not automatically a sufficient corrective action. Action should address the actual contributing causes.
