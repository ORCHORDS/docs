# Measurement Validation

## Purpose

Ensure metrics, dashboards, tests, and quality indicators are sufficiently trustworthy for the decisions they support.

## Requirements

Material measurements SHOULD define:

- purpose and decision supported;
- data source and population;
- calculation or test method;
- owner;
- known exclusions/limitations;
- expected freshness;
- validation or reconciliation method.

## Failure modes

Review for stale data, duplicated events, missing populations, sampling bias, changed definitions, broken instrumentation, time-window errors, and incentives that encourage gaming.

## Claims

A precise number is not necessarily an accurate measurement. Uncertainty and limitations should be disclosed when material.
