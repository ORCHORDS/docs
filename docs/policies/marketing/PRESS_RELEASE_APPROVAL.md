# Press Release Approval

## Purpose

A press release is a public statement that can move markets, influence stakeholders, and create legal exposure if it is inaccurate, premature, or improperly attributed. The approval workflow for press releases exists to ensure factual accuracy, claim substantiation, embargo discipline, spokesperson designation, and legal review. The objective is to publish confidently and quickly, on the record, without exposing the organisation to liability or embarrassing correction.

## Scope

This article applies to every public statement issued in press-release form — issued to media, posted to the corporate newsroom, distributed via wire services, shared with journalists under embargo, or published on the organisation's own channels. It covers product announcements, partnership announcements, financial results commentary, executive changes, incident statements, opinion pieces, and award announcements. It applies whether the release is drafted by Marketing, by Communications, by a third-party agency, or by a business unit.

## Requirements

- Every press release MUST be reviewed against the Claim Substantiation policy before distribution. Material claims (performance, ranking, financial outcome, customer outcome) MUST have a written substantiation record.
- The release MUST be factually accurate. Names, titles, dates, prices, terms, and quantitative figures MUST be verified against primary sources before approval.
- Every release MUST identify at least one named spokesperson. The spokesperson MUST have authorised the content and MUST be prepared to speak to the content with media.
- Embargoed releases MUST be clearly marked as such, MUST specify the embargo date and time in the timezone of the primary publication, and MUST be distributed only to recipients who have agreed to honour the embargo. Embargo breaches MUST be escalated to the Legal function.
- Forward-looking statements MUST be accompanied by a safe-harbour statement where required by securities regulation; the Legal function confirms whether the statement qualifies as forward-looking.
- Where the release references third parties (customers, partners, regulators), the reference MUST be reviewed and approved by the third party before publication; the customer case study consent rules apply where a customer is identified.
- Where the release references regulated information (financial, clinical, security), the Legal function and the relevant control owner MUST confirm that the release does not violate disclosure obligations, quiet periods, or selective-disclosure rules.
- The release MUST be reviewed for accessibility. A press-release page on the corporate website MUST be accessible; images MUST carry alt text; PDFs MUST be tagged; video MUST be captioned.
- The release MUST be reviewed for compliance with the Marketing Localisation policy where the release will be issued in multiple languages or markets.
- The release workflow MUST produce an approval record naming the drafter, the substantiation reviewer, the legal reviewer, the spokesperson, and the Marketing Lead. The record is retained per the Marketing Data Retention policy.
- The release MUST be version-controlled. After distribution, only corrections explicitly labelled as corrections MAY be issued; restatements of substance require a new release.
- After distribution, the Marketing Lead monitors pickup, social discussion, and any required clarifications; the Marketing Incident Response policy applies where the release generates a public incident.

## Workflow

1. The drafter submits the release draft, the substantiation package, the spokesperson, and the embargo details to the Communications team.
2. The Claim Substantiation Reviewer evaluates the material claims; the Legal function reviews the release for legal exposure, regulated-information compliance, and embargo handling.
3. The spokesperson confirms the content and signs off; the Marketing Lead confirms alignment with positioning and brand voice.
4. The release is finalised with a publication date and time, and the distribution list is confirmed.
5. At the publication moment, the release is distributed to the confirmed list; any pre-arranged media briefings are coordinated.
6. After distribution, pickup is monitored and any necessary corrections are issued through the same workflow.

## Controls

- The press release register records every release, version, approver, and publication date.
- Embargoed releases are tracked separately; the embargo window and recipient list are recorded.
- The Legal function maintains the regulated-information watchlist; new entries trigger a review of releases in flight.
- Corrections are tracked with the original release; the correction is published with the same reach as the original.
