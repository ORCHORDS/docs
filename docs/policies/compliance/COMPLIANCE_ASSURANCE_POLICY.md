# Compliance Assurance Policy

## Purpose

Define how legal, contractual, policy, and assurance obligations are translated
into owned controls and defensible evidence.

## Requirements

Material obligations MUST be:

1. identified from an authoritative source;
2. interpreted by an accountable owner;
3. mapped to one or more controls or explicit non-applicability decisions;
4. assigned evidence expectations;
5. reviewed when the obligation or operating context changes.

A control mapping is not evidence that the control operates effectively.

## Assurance levels

Assurance depth should reflect risk. It may include self-review, independent
internal review, technical verification, external assessment, or formal
attestation where appropriate.

## Gaps

Known gaps must have an owner and one of these outcomes:

- remediation;
- compensating control;
- accepted risk with expiry;
- documented non-applicability.

## Public claims

Any public compliance or assurance statement must follow
[Claims and Attestation](CLAIMS_AND_ATTESTATION.md).
