# Remediation Management

## Purpose

Ensure audit, risk, incident, vulnerability, privacy, accessibility, and control findings lead to tracked and verified improvement.

## Requirements

Material remediation items SHOULD identify finding source, impact, owner, target outcome, planned action, due or review date, dependencies, and closure evidence.

## Closure

A finding is not closed solely because an action was performed. Closure should verify that the intended risk reduction or control outcome was achieved.

## Extensions

Extensions require a reason, updated risk assessment, accountable approval, and revised date. Repeated extensions are a management signal and should not normalize permanent exception.

## Aggregation

Recurring findings across teams or periods should be analyzed for systemic causes.
