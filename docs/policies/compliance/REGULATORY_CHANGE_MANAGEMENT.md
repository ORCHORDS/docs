# Regulatory Change Management

## Purpose

Define how new or changed legal, regulatory, contractual, and standards-based obligations are assessed and translated into action.

## Process

Material changes SHOULD be recorded with source, effective or relevant date, affected scope, accountable interpreter, required control or documentation changes, owner, and evidence of completion.

Authoritative legal or regulatory text takes precedence over summaries. Draft proposals should be clearly identified as draft or pending and must not be represented as current obligations.

## Prioritization

Prioritize by applicability, effective date, potential harm, enforcement or contractual exposure, implementation lead time, and dependency impact.

## Closure

A regulatory-change item is complete only when applicability and required actions are documented and implementation evidence is available or an explicit non-applicability decision is approved.
