# Scenario and Assumption Management

## Purpose

Make strategic uncertainty visible before it becomes hidden commitment.

## Requirements

Material assumptions SHOULD have owner, rationale, evidence, confidence level or uncertainty statement, impact if wrong, and review trigger.

Scenario analysis should cover plausible conditions that materially alter decisions, not only optimistic and pessimistic labels.

When an assumption is invalidated, update dependent plans and decisions rather than preserving stale forecasts.
