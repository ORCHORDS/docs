# Strategy Evidence Review

## Purpose

Test strategic choices against current evidence and explicitly identify where decisions rely on uncertain assumptions.

## Review

A strategy review SHOULD distinguish observed facts, interpretations, forecasts, assumptions, constraints, and choices. Material assumptions should have signals or events that trigger reassessment.

Evidence that contradicts the preferred strategy must not be excluded merely because it is inconvenient. When evidence is weak, the decision should state the uncertainty and the cost of being wrong.
