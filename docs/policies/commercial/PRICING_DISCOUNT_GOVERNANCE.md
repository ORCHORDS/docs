# Pricing and Discount Governance

## Purpose

Ensure material pricing exceptions and discounts are intentional, authorized, and evaluated with the full commercial commitment.

## Requirements

Material discounts SHOULD document rationale, duration, approval authority, affected scope, non-price concessions, renewal assumptions, margin/delivery implications, and whether the exception creates precedent or operational burden.

Discount approval must not bypass legal, security, privacy, product, delivery, or finance review required by other commitments.

See [Pricing and Discount SOP](../sop/PRICING_DISCOUNT_SOP.md).
