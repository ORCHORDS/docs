# Commercial Forecast Integrity

## Purpose

Keep pipeline and forecast information decision-useful by separating evidence, probability, assumption, and aspiration.

## Requirements

Forecasts SHOULD use defined stages or confidence criteria, identify material dependencies, avoid double counting, record significant changes in assumptions, and distinguish contracted value from expected or potential value.

Pressure to meet a target must not justify moving an opportunity to a stronger stage without supporting evidence or hiding known delivery/renewal risk.

See [Commercial Forecast Review SOP](../sop/COMMERCIAL_FORECAST_REVIEW_SOP.md).
