# Partner-of-Record Change

## Purpose

Establish a controlled process for changing the partner-of-record (POR) on an existing commercial account so that partner compensation, customer ownership, and channel-conflict prevention are managed transparently and with proper customer notification.

## Scope

This article applies to all changes in partner-of-record on existing accounts, whether initiated by the partner, the customer, an internal team, or another partner. It covers changes in the partner who is recognized for compensation, in the partner who holds the commercial relationship with the customer, and in the partner who is recognized for renewals, escalations, or co-sell credit. It applies across all partner categories (reseller, distributor, systems integrator, managed-service provider, referral, agent).

## Requirements

- Partner-of-record changes SHOULD require documented eligibility for both the outgoing and incoming partners, evidence of customer notification or consent where required by contract, and approval per the deal-desk or partner-governance matrix.
- A partner-of-record change MUST NOT be used to bypass deal-desk review, discount approval, or contract-template controls.
- A partner-of-record change MUST NOT be used to retroactively reassign compensation in a manner that misrepresents the actual commercial history.
- A partner-of-record change SHOULD be transparent to the customer where the partner-of-record is material to the customer's relationship with the company; secret reassignment SHOULD be avoided.
- A partner-of-record change SHOULD preserve the contractual obligations of the customer and the integrity of any in-flight commitments.
- A partner-of-record change SHOULD be evaluated for sanctions, anti-bribery, and export-control implications of the incoming partner and SHOULD be screened accordingly.
- A partner-of-record change SHOULD be recorded with an effective date, evidence pack, and approval identifier; the change SHOULD be visible to finance, partner operations, and the account team.

## Workflow

A partner-of-record change is initiated by the requesting party with a complete request that includes: (a) the affected account and contract(s), (b) the outgoing partner, (c) the incoming partner, (d) the effective date, (e) the rationale, (f) the compensation treatment (e.g., split-period, full-transfer, no-compete, partner-buyout), (g) any customer-consent evidence or planned customer notification, and (h) any sanctions/anti-bribery screening status. The request is reviewed by partner operations, deal-desk, finance, and legal as appropriate. Where the change is contested by the outgoing partner or by the customer, the request SHOULD be escalated per the partner-channel governance policy. Approved changes are recorded with effective date and communicated to all stakeholders. The change SHOULD be reflected in the system of record before any new partner-attributed revenue or compensation is processed.

## Controls

- Partner-of-record changes SHOULD be subject to segregation of duties: the requester SHOULD NOT be the sole approver.
- The change SHOULD be approved by the partner-operations and finance functions in addition to commercial.
- The change SHOULD be visible to the customer where contractually required; the customer SHOULD be notified in writing.
- The change SHOULD be screened against applicable sanctions lists and SHOULD be subject to anti-bribery due diligence on the incoming partner.
- The change SHOULD be tracked in a register with effective date, evidence, and approval identifier, and SHOULD be retained per the records-retention schedule.
- The change SHOULD NOT be used to evade partner-channel governance, to manipulate compensation, or to obscure the actual commercial history of the account.

## Customer notification and consent

Where the partner-of-record is material to the customer's relationship (e.g., a reseller who provides first-line support, a systems integrator who delivers services), the customer SHOULD be notified in advance of any change. Where the underlying contract requires customer consent for partner-of-record change, that consent SHOULD be obtained in writing and retained. Customer notification SHOULD be clear, accurate, and SHOULD NOT misrepresent the reason for the change.

## Anti-bribery, sanctions, and export-control screening

The incoming partner SHOULD be screened against applicable sanctions lists (e.g., U.S. OFAC SDN, EU consolidated, UK OFSI, UN Security Council) before the change takes effect. The incoming partner SHOULD be subject to anti-bribery due diligence, including beneficial-ownership identification where appropriate. Where the incoming partner operates in a higher-risk geography or sector, heightened due diligence SHOULD be applied. The change SHOULD be declined where screening reveals unresolved risk and where risk-mitigation is not feasible.
