# Internal Audit Program

## Purpose

Define an independent, risk-based internal audit capability that evaluates governance, risk management, and controls without owning the controls being audited.

## Program principles

The audit program SHOULD:

- use a risk-informed audit universe or planning process;
- preserve auditor objectivity and appropriate organizational access;
- define scope, objectives, criteria, evidence, and reporting for each engagement;
- communicate significant issues to the appropriate authority;
- track agreed actions and overdue findings;
- evaluate audit quality and improve the audit process itself.

## Relationship to management

Management owns controls and remediation. Internal audit evaluates and advises but should not become the operational owner of the controls it later audits.

## References

The program is informed by the IIA Global Internal Audit Standards, effective January 9, 2025.
