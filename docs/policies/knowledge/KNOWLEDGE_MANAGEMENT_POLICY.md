# Knowledge Management Policy

## Purpose

Ensure material organizational knowledge is discoverable, owned, reviewed, transferable, and retired when obsolete.

## Principles

- capture knowledge at useful decision points, not only after problems;
- assign ownership to durable roles rather than individuals where practical;
- distinguish public, internal, confidential, and restricted knowledge;
- preserve rationale for material decisions;
- reduce single-person knowledge dependencies;
- review or retire stale content.

ISO 30401:2018 and its published amendment may inform knowledge-management design. The next edition remains under development and is not treated as final.
