# Decision Records

## Purpose

Preserve the rationale behind material decisions so future reviewers can understand context rather than only the final outcome.

## Record content

A material decision record SHOULD capture date, owner, context, constraints, options considered, decision, rationale, trade-offs, consequences, and review trigger if assumptions may change.

Decision records must respect information classification and legal privilege. Public repositories should contain only public-safe decisions.

Superseded decisions should remain traceable rather than silently rewritten.
