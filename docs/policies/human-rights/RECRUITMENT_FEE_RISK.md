# Recruitment Fee Risk

Worker-paid recruitment fees and debt-linked recruitment arrangements can create coercion and forced-labor risk.

Where relevant, diligence should identify fee practices, intermediaries, reimbursement arrangements, evidence, and remediation ownership.
