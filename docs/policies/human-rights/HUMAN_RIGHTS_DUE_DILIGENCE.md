# Human Rights Due Diligence

Due diligence should focus on severity, likelihood, leverage, affected groups, geographic and sector context, supplier practices, and credible evidence.

Material risks require ownership, treatment, monitoring, escalation, and a documented decision on whether the relationship or activity can continue responsibly.
