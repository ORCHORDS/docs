# Child Labor Principles

Business relationships should not rely on unlawful or harmful child labor.

Where age-related risk is material, due diligence should assess recruitment controls, identity verification, subcontracting, hazardous work restrictions, and remediation approaches that prioritize the affected young person’s interests.
