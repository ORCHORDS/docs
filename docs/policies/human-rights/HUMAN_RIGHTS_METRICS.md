# Human Rights Metrics

Human-rights metrics should avoid creating incentives to suppress grievances or underreport incidents.

Useful measures may include due-diligence coverage, open severe findings, remediation aging, effectiveness checks, training effectiveness, supplier evidence quality, and grievance accessibility, with clear definitions and limitations.
