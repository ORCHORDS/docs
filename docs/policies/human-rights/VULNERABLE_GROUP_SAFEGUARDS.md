# Vulnerable Group Safeguards

Human-rights assessments should consider whether impacts may fall disproportionately on groups with limited bargaining power, legal protection, access to remedy, or ability to exit a harmful situation.

Controls should avoid requiring unnecessary disclosure of sensitive personal characteristics.
