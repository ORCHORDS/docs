# Supplier Human Rights Expectations

Material suppliers may be expected to maintain lawful labor practices, worker grievance channels, non-retaliation, appropriate recruitment controls, incident escalation, and proportionate oversight of subcontractors.

Expectations should be reflected in due diligence and contracts where material.
