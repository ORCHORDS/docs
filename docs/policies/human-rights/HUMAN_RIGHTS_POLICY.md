# Human Rights Policy

ORCHORDS seeks to identify, prevent, mitigate, and address material human-rights risks connected to company activities and business relationships using proportionate, evidence-based governance.

Concerns should be escalated through appropriate channels and handled with confidentiality, non-retaliation, and respect for affected people.
