# IRS Electronic Business Records

## Purpose

Provide public governance guidance for maintaining business tax records electronically.

## Baseline

The IRS states that recordkeeping requirements applying to paper books and records also apply to electronic records maintained with accounting, point-of-sale, financial, or other electronic systems. Electronic systems should provide complete and accurate records that remain accessible to the IRS when required.

## Governance guidance

- Preserve completeness and accuracy when converting paper records to electronic form or migrating between systems.
- Maintain transaction history and supporting documentation needed to substantiate income, expenses, credits, payroll, assets, and other reportable items.
- Protect records against unauthorized alteration or deletion.
- Maintain backups and recovery procedures proportionate to the importance and retention period of the records.
- Ensure records remain readable and exportable after software, vendor, or format changes.
- Apply retention schedules consistently regardless of storage medium.

## Verification

Periodically test that retained electronic records can be located, opened, interpreted, and reconciled to the underlying books and tax reporting. Migration or backup success should not be assumed without retrieval testing.
