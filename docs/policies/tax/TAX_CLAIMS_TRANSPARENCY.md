# Tax Claims and Transparency

Public tax statements must be accurate, appropriately scoped, approved, and consistent with available evidence.

Policy commitments, tax expense, cash taxes, jurisdictional filings, and independently verified conclusions are different concepts and must not be presented as interchangeable.
