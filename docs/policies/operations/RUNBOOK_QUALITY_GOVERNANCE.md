# Runbook Quality Governance

## Purpose

Keep operational procedures usable under pressure rather than merely present.

## Requirements

Material runbooks SHOULD define purpose, prerequisites, safe checks, expected outcomes, stop/escalation conditions, recovery considerations, owner, and review trigger.

Runbooks should be tested or exercised when practical and updated after material failures or process changes.

Sensitive commands, credentials, or private topology do not belong in public documentation.
