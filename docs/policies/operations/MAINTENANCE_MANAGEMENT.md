# Maintenance Management

## Purpose

Govern planned operational maintenance that may affect availability, security, performance, or data integrity.

## Requirements

Material maintenance SHOULD define scope, owner, expected impact, prerequisites, communication needs, validation, rollback or containment, and completion criteria.

Maintenance must not become a recurring bypass around change review. Emergency work may use an expedited path but still requires evidence and retrospective review.

## Scheduling

Choose timing based on user impact, staffing, dependency availability, recovery options, and risk rather than a fixed universal maintenance window.

## Closure

Maintenance is complete only after expected health and security controls are verified and unresolved issues are tracked.
