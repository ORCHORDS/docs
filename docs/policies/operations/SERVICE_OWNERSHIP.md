# Service Ownership

## Purpose

Ensure material operational capabilities have clear accountability for reliability, security, change, support, and lifecycle decisions.

## Ownership record

A material service SHOULD have an accountable owner, user/business purpose, support state, important dependencies, escalation path, recovery expectations, and lifecycle status.

Ownership may be a team or role rather than a single permanent person, but responsibility must remain discoverable.

## Duties

Owners coordinate change, incidents, capacity, documentation, security findings, supplier dependencies, and deprecation decisions relevant to the service.

## Transfer

Ownership transfers require explicit acceptance and update of authoritative records.
