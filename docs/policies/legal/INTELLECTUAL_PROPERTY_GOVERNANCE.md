# Intellectual Property Governance

## Purpose

Protect company intellectual property while respecting third-party rights and lawful collaboration.

## Principles

Personnel MUST use software, media, code, trademarks, datasets, documentation, and other protected material only under appropriate rights or permissions.

Company-created material should have clear ownership or licensing expectations where relevant to employment, contractor, supplier, or open-source arrangements.

## External use

Before publishing or licensing material, review confidential information, third-party restrictions, patent/trademark concerns, attribution obligations, and intended reuse rights.

## Incidents

Suspected infringement, unauthorized disclosure, or ownership dispute should be escalated to the appropriate governance/legal owner rather than resolved informally.
