# Product Discovery Governance

## Purpose

Use discovery to reduce uncertainty before committing significant delivery effort while preserving ethical research and accurate evidence.

## Requirements

Material discovery SHOULD state the decision/question, assumptions, evidence needed, user/customer segment, constraints, risks, and what evidence would change the direction.

Discovery artifacts must distinguish observed user evidence from interpretation and solution preference.

Research involving participants follows [Research Governance](../research/README.md).
