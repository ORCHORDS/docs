# Launch Readiness

## Purpose

Define evidence required before a material capability is broadly released.

## Readiness areas

Depending on risk, launch review SHOULD cover acceptance criteria, testing, security, privacy, accessibility, operations, monitoring, support, documentation, suppliers, legal obligations, data migration, rollback or disable path, and user communication.

## Decision

A launch may be approved, staged, conditioned on specific controls, or delayed. Unresolved high-impact risk requires explicit accountable acceptance rather than silent assumption.

## Evidence

The launch record should identify reviewers, material evidence, known limitations, residual risk, and post-launch monitoring criteria.
