# Metrics and Experiment Integrity

## Purpose

Ensure product and operational metrics support decisions without creating misleading precision or incentives.

## Principles

Metrics SHOULD have defined meaning, owner, data source, calculation, population, known limitations, and review cadence when materially used for decisions.

Do not present estimates, targets, or incomplete samples as observed facts.

## Incentives

Review whether a metric encourages behavior that harms users, hides incidents, reduces reporting, or optimizes a proxy rather than the intended outcome.

## Experiments

Predefine primary decision metrics and important guardrails where practical. Interpret statistical or operational significance in context rather than relying on a single threshold.
