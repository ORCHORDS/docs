# Resilience Improvement Tracking

## Purpose

Ensure continuity and recovery gaps identified through incidents, exercises, audits, or dependency reviews are not lost after the immediate event.

## Requirements

Material improvements SHOULD identify source, risk, owner, target outcome, dependency, review date, and closure evidence.

Repeated deferral, recurrence, or scope reduction should trigger escalation rather than silent rollover.

Closure requires evidence that the intended resilience improvement was achieved or that residual risk was formally accepted.
