# Crisis Decision Logging

## Purpose

Preserve a defensible record of material decisions made during continuity, recovery, or crisis coordination.

## Requirements

Material decisions SHOULD record the issue, available evidence, assumptions, decision owner, time, chosen action, alternatives rejected where relevant, and follow-up required.

Decision records should distinguish verified facts from assumptions and evolving estimates.

The log should support later review without exposing sensitive operational detail in public documentation.
