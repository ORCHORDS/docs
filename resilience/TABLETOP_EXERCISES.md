# Tabletop Exercises

## Purpose

Use structured discussion exercises to test decision-making, coordination, assumptions, and escalation without intentionally disrupting live services.

## Design

A tabletop SHOULD define objective, scenario, participants, facilitator, known assumptions, injects, decision points, and completion criteria.

Scenarios should challenge more than the happy path and may include unavailable personnel, uncertain facts, supplier failure, security compromise, privacy impact, or conflicting recovery priorities.

## Conduct

Participants should respond as they realistically would, identify information they need, and state decisions or escalation paths. Facilitators should distinguish policy gaps from participant knowledge gaps.

## Learning

Findings require owners and follow-up. The purpose is to improve readiness, not to produce a perfect exercise score.
