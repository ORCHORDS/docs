# Technical Debt Governance

## Purpose

Make material engineering debt visible, owned, and prioritized before it becomes a reliability, security, delivery, or maintainability failure.

## Record expectations

Material debt SHOULD capture the affected capability, cause, consequence, workaround, risk if deferred, dependency on future work, accountable owner, and review trigger.

Debt should be prioritized using user/business impact, security exposure, change friction, incident contribution, operational cost, and compounding risk rather than age alone.

A deliberate short-term tradeoff is acceptable when the residual risk and repayment strategy are explicit.
