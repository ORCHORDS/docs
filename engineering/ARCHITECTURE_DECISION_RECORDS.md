# Architecture Decision Records

## Purpose

Define a lightweight record for material technical decisions without
publishing private system architecture in this public repository.

## When to record a decision

An architecture decision record (ADR) is useful when a decision materially
affects security boundaries, data handling, reliability, compatibility,
operational cost, supplier dependency, or long-term maintainability.

## Minimum record

An ADR should capture:

1. context and constraints;
2. decision;
3. material alternatives considered;
4. security, privacy, reliability, and operational consequences;
5. reversibility and migration considerations;
6. owner and decision date.

## Publication boundary

Public policy may describe the ADR method. Actual architecture records may be
private when they reveal topology, internal identifiers, sensitive trust
boundaries, or unannounced capabilities.
