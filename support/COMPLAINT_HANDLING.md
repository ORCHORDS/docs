# Complaint Handling

## Purpose

Provide an accessible, fair, traceable process for complaints about products, services, support, or company conduct.

## Principles

Complaint handling SHOULD be easy to access, acknowledge the issue, assess impact and requested resolution, avoid retaliation, protect privacy, and communicate an outcome or next step.

## Analysis

Complaints should be analyzed for recurring product, support, accessibility, quality, supplier, security, privacy, or communication problems.

## Independence

Complaints about a person or team may require independent review rather than sole disposition by the subject of the complaint.

## Standard

Process design is informed by ISO 10002:2018, which remains current as confirmed by ISO.
