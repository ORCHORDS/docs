# Support Data Handling

## Purpose

Minimize unnecessary collection and exposure of personal, customer, credential, payment, and diagnostic information during support.

## Requirements

Support SHOULD collect the minimum data needed, use authorized tools, follow classification/retention rules, and redact or delete unnecessary sensitive information when workflows permit.

Customers should not be instructed to post secrets or sensitive diagnostic bundles in public forums.

## Attachments and logs

Diagnostic files may contain tokens, identifiers, personal data, paths, messages, or configuration. Treat them according to sensitivity and restrict access.

## Sharing

Internal escalation should share only the information needed by the receiving team.
