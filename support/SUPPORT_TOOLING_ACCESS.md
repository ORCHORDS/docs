# Support Tooling Access

## Purpose

Restrict customer-support tooling and sensitive account actions according to least privilege and job need.

## Requirements

Support access SHOULD use named identities, strong authentication appropriate to risk, least-privilege roles, periodic access review, and logging of sensitive actions.

High-impact actions such as identity/security changes, financial adjustments, data exports, or broad customer-data access may require additional approval or specialized roles.

## Training

Access should be granted only after required security/privacy/support training for the role.

## Lifecycle

Role changes and departures require prompt access reassessment or removal.
