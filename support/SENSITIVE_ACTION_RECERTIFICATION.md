# Sensitive Support Action Recertification

## Purpose
Confirm the set of actions treated as sensitive or high impact remains accurate as support processes evolve.

## Requirements
- Sensitive-action classifications must have accountable owners and documented criteria.
- Periodic review must consider impact, abuse potential, reversibility, and customer harm.
- Changes must update approval, verification, logging, or dual-control expectations as appropriate.
- Deprecated actions must be removed from active control catalogs.

## Evidence
Maintain review records, classification decisions, and resulting control changes without publishing operational access details.
