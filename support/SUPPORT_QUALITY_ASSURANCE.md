# Support Quality Assurance

## Purpose

Evaluate support work for accuracy, safety, consistency, escalation quality, and customer outcome rather than speed alone.

## Review areas

Quality review SHOULD consider identity verification, sensitive-data handling, diagnostic accuracy, clarity, policy adherence, escalation decisions, action safety, complaint handling, and whether the user received an effective next step.

Metrics such as first-response time or closure rate must not incentivize premature closure or weaker verification.
