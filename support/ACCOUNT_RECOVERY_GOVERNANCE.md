# Account Recovery Governance

## Purpose

Recover legitimate user access without allowing support processes to bypass authentication controls.

## Requirements

Recovery strength MUST reflect the sensitivity of the account and requested action. Recovery should rely on approved proof mechanisms, avoid easily researched knowledge questions, protect recovery factors from disclosure, and create a reviewable record for high-risk recovery.

Support staff must not request passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or reusable login codes.

See [Account Recovery SOP](../sop/ACCOUNT_RECOVERY_SOP.md).
