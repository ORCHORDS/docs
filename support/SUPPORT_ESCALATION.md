# Support Escalation

## Purpose

Route issues to the right specialist process when ordinary support authority or expertise is insufficient.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate as appropriate for:

- suspected security compromise or vulnerability;
- privacy-rights requests or personal-data incidents;
- payment fraud or financial disputes;
- legal demands;
- safety or abuse concerns;
- accessibility blockers;
- repeated high-impact defects;
- outages or systemic incidents;
- unresolved identity verification.

## Handoff

Escalations SHOULD include customer impact, actions already taken, relevant evidence, urgency, and important unknowns without over-sharing sensitive data.
