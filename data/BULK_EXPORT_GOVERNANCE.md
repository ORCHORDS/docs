# Bulk Data Export Governance

## Purpose

Apply stronger review to exports whose scale can materially increase privacy, security, contractual, or operational risk.

## Requirements

Material bulk exports SHOULD identify purpose, requester, data categories, destination, access restrictions, retention, transfer method expectations, and deletion or return requirements where appropriate.

Export convenience is not sufficient justification for copying more information than the intended use requires.
