# Archive Storage Governance

## Purpose

Ensure archived information remains intentionally retained, protected, discoverable when needed, and deletable when its retention period ends.

## Requirements

Material archives SHOULD define owner, retained categories, reason for retention, access expectations, integrity needs, review or deletion trigger, and restoration expectations where applicable.

Archive status must not become a permanent exemption from retention, privacy, legal-hold, or disposal requirements.
