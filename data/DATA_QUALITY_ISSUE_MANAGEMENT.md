# Data Quality Issue Management

## Purpose

Treat material data-quality failures as owned issues with measurable correction and prevention.

## Requirements

Material issues SHOULD record affected data or decision use, impact, evidence, owner, containment, correction approach, recurrence risk, and closure criteria.

Repeated manual fixes should trigger root-cause analysis of source, transformation, workflow, or ownership problems.
