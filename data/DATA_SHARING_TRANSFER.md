# Data Sharing and Transfer

## Purpose

Set minimum controls before company information is shared outside its normal
authorized context.

## Requirements

Material sharing decisions SHOULD consider:

- purpose and necessity;
- classification and sensitivity;
- recipient authorization;
- minimum data required;
- transfer protection;
- onward-sharing risk;
- retention and deletion expectations;
- contractual or legal restrictions;
- ability to stop sharing when the purpose ends.

## Personal data

Sharing personal data also requires privacy review appropriate to the context.
Security approval alone does not resolve privacy or legal considerations.

## Public links

Public or broadly shareable links must not be used for restricted information
unless the information has been intentionally approved for public release.
