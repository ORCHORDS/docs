# Data Retention Schedule Policy

## Purpose

Define how retention periods are established without publishing sensitive
record inventories.

## Principles

Data should be retained no longer than necessary for legitimate business,
legal, contractual, security, or evidentiary purposes.

A retention rule SHOULD specify:

- record or data category;
- accountable owner;
- retention trigger;
- retention period or event-based rule;
- disposal method;
- legal-hold interaction;
- authoritative obligation or business rationale.

## Conflicts

When requirements conflict, applicable legal and contractual obligations take
priority, followed by approved governance decisions.

## Holds

A valid legal, investigation, security, or preservation hold suspends ordinary
deletion for the affected records until formally released.
