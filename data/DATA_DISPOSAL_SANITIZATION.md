# Data Disposal and Sanitization

## Purpose

Ensure information is deleted or sanitized when retention ends and no valid hold applies.

## Requirements

Disposal SHOULD reflect data classification, storage medium, backups, replicated copies, supplier-held data, legal holds, and the assurance required that data is no longer accessible.

Logical deletion may be sufficient in some contexts and insufficient in others. Media sanitization follows [Media Sanitization](../security/MEDIA_SANITIZATION.md).

## Verification

Material deletion programs should have evidence of execution and known limitations, including delayed backup expiration where applicable.

## Suppliers

Supplier deletion obligations should be addressed during offboarding and contract review.
