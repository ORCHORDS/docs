# Resource Efficiency

Material resource use should be considered in procurement, operating, maintenance, and retirement decisions.

## Expectations

- Prefer prevention of unnecessary consumption over downstream treatment.
- Use total lifecycle effects rather than purchase price alone when material.
- Track significant improvement initiatives with before-and-after evidence.
- Avoid shifting an impact to another lifecycle stage without evaluating the tradeoff.
