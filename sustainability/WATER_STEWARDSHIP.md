# Water Stewardship

Water use and water-related supplier exposure should be assessed where material to operations or resilience.

Reviews should consider local context, continuity risk, significant consumption, abnormal losses, supplier dependencies, and practical efficiency opportunities. Public claims must distinguish directly controlled use from supplier or estimated impacts.
