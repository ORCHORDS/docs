# Product and Service Lifecycle Considerations

Material environmental considerations should be evaluated across design, procurement, operation, maintenance, customer use where relevant, and retirement.

Reviews should identify significant tradeoffs and avoid optimizing one stage by creating a larger unmanaged impact elsewhere. Public lifecycle claims require a defined boundary and defensible method.
