# Environmental Aspect and Impact Review

Environmental reviews identify activities, purchases, services, and changes that may create material environmental impacts.

## Review criteria

Consider energy, emissions, materials, waste, water, travel, supplier activity, equipment lifecycle, and reasonably foreseeable abnormal conditions.

Significance decisions should record scope, evidence, magnitude, likelihood, controllability, stakeholder relevance, and the resulting control or monitoring decision.
