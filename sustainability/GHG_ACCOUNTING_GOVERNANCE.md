# Greenhouse Gas Accounting Governance

Any greenhouse-gas inventory or emissions statement must use a documented organizational boundary, reporting period, source hierarchy, calculation method, factor provenance, and uncertainty treatment.

## Controls

- Define inclusions, exclusions, estimation methods, and materiality rules.
- Preserve source records and calculation versions.
- Review factor changes and methodological changes separately from performance changes.
- Prevent double counting across categories.
- Label estimates and incomplete coverage clearly.
