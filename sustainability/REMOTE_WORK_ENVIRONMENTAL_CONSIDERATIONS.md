# Remote Work Environmental Considerations

Remote-work environmental impacts must not be assumed to be automatically lower or higher than office-based work.

Assessments should consider travel avoided, home energy, equipment duplication, workspace utilization, and data quality. Comparisons must state assumptions and avoid presenting modeled estimates as directly measured performance.
