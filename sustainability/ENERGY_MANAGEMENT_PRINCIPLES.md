# Energy Management Principles

Energy decisions should balance business need, reliability, cost, environmental impact, and evidence quality.

## Principles

- Identify material energy uses before setting improvement priorities.
- Prefer measured consumption over unsupported estimates where practical.
- Evaluate efficiency opportunities during significant procurement or lifecycle decisions.
- Avoid claims about renewable sourcing or efficiency gains without traceable evidence.
- Track material deviations and improvement actions.
