# Environmental Evidence and Records

Evidence supporting material environmental decisions and claims must be identifiable, attributable, dated, protected from inappropriate alteration, and retained according to applicable records requirements.

Key calculation inputs, source documents, approvals, methodological versions, corrections, and significant assumptions should be reproducible from retained records.
