# Electronic Equipment Lifecycle

Equipment decisions should consider useful life, repairability, energy use, redeployment, secure sanitization, responsible recycling, and supplier take-back options where relevant.

Premature replacement should require a documented business reason. End-of-life handling must preserve information-security and records obligations while supporting responsible disposition.
