# Sustainable Procurement

Material purchasing decisions should consider relevant environmental lifecycle factors alongside quality, security, privacy, resilience, commercial, and legal requirements.

Environmental criteria must be proportionate to the purchase and supported by verifiable evidence. Sustainability criteria must not override critical safety, security, legal, accessibility, or service-continuity requirements.
