# Supplier Environmental Expectations

Material suppliers may be asked to demonstrate proportionate environmental governance, legal compliance processes, incident escalation, evidence integrity, and improvement practices.

Supplier claims should be evaluated based on evidence relevant to the supplied service or product rather than labels alone. Requirements should reflect risk, leverage, and materiality.
