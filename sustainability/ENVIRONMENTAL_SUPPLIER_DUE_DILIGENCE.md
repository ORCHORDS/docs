# Environmental Supplier Due Diligence

Environmental due diligence should focus on material exposure rather than generic questionnaires.

Consider relevant legal history, environmental management, material energy or resource use, waste practices, climate exposure, subcontractor dependencies, evidence quality, and credible remediation of known issues. Findings should inform supplier risk and contract decisions.
