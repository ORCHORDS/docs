# Greenwashing Risk Governance

Sustainability communications are reviewed for risks including vague terminology, selective disclosure, irrelevant claims, hidden tradeoffs, unsupported comparisons, obsolete evidence, and confusing planned actions with achieved outcomes.

Material claims require documented review by appropriate subject-matter and communications owners before publication.
