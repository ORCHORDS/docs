# Environmental Governance Policy

ORCHORDS manages material environmental considerations through accountable ownership, evidence-based decisions, proportionate controls, and periodic review.

## Requirements

- Material environmental impacts must have an accountable owner.
- Decisions must consider relevant lifecycle, supplier, resource, and resilience impacts.
- Environmental claims must be traceable to current evidence and a defined methodology.
- Legal or contractual environmental obligations must be identified and reviewed.
- Significant issues and repeated control failures must be escalated and tracked to closure.

This policy does not assert a particular environmental performance level or certification.
