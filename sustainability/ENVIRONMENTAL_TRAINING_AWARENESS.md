# Environmental Training and Awareness

People with material environmental responsibilities should understand relevant obligations, controls, reporting expectations, evidence requirements, and escalation routes.

Training effectiveness should be assessed using behavior, decision quality, incident trends, review findings, or other relevant evidence rather than attendance alone.
