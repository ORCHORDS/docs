# Environmental Legal Obligation Review

Environmental obligations relevant to company activities, facilities, products, services, waste, or suppliers must be identified through the legal and compliance governance processes.

Reviews should record applicability, accountable owner, required evidence, change triggers, and unresolved interpretation issues. Public documentation must not provide jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
