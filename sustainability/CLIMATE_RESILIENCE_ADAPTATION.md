# Climate Resilience and Adaptation

Where climate-related physical risks are material, resilience planning should evaluate critical dependencies, geographic concentration, supplier exposure, recovery assumptions, and adaptation options.

Adaptation actions should be prioritized using risk and feasibility evidence and integrated with business-continuity and supplier-risk governance.
