# Emissions Reduction Governance

Reduction initiatives require a baseline, defined scope, accountable owner, implementation evidence, and a method for distinguishing real change from boundary or methodology changes.

Claims must identify the period, comparison basis, material exclusions, and whether changes are measured, estimated, avoided, or offset. Planned initiatives must not be reported as achieved reductions.
