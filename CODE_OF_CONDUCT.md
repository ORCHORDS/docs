# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in ORCHORDS public repositories should be professional,
constructive, and respectful.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving actionable feedback;
- respecting privacy and confidentiality;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- avoiding harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
  or sustained disruption;
- following maintainers' reasonable moderation directions.

## Scope

This applies to public repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where a person is representing ORCHORDS or participating in
an ORCHORDS-managed community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict
participation, lock discussions, or take other proportionate action.

Security-sensitive material may be removed immediately and redirected to the
private reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact `crm@orchords.com`. For security-sensitive
concerns, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
