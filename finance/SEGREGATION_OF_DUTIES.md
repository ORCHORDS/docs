# Segregation of Financial Duties

## Purpose

Reduce fraud and error risk by separating incompatible financial responsibilities where practical.

## Requirements

High-impact activities SHOULD separate request, approval, execution, reconciliation, and record-change authority when risk warrants it.

Where staffing or emergency conditions prevent full separation, compensating review must be explicit, independent where practical, and evidenced.

No individual should routinely be able to create an obligation, execute payment, alter the supporting record, and independently approve the result without meaningful oversight.
