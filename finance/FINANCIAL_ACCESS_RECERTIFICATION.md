# Financial Access Recertification

## Purpose
Maintain least-privilege access to financial records, workflows, and approval functions.

## Requirements
- Financial access must be attributable to a current role and accountable owner.
- Access must be reviewed periodically and after material role changes.
- Conflicting or excessive access must be corrected or supported by documented compensating review.
- Dormant, orphaned, or unnecessary access must be removed.

## Evidence
Retain dated review outcomes, reviewer identity, exceptions, and closure evidence without publishing account or banking details.
