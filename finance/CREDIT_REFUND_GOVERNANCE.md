# Credit and Refund Governance

## Purpose

Govern credits, refunds, write-offs, and similar financial adjustments so they are justified, authorized, and traceable.

## Requirements

Material adjustments SHOULD record reason, amount, affected transaction or obligation, approval basis, recipient or account, and evidence of completion.

High-risk or unusual adjustments warrant independent review and stronger verification of destination details.

Patterns of repeated refunds, credits, or write-offs should be reviewed for underlying product, service, fraud, or process issues.
