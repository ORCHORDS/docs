# Financial Approval Recertification

## Purpose
Ensure material financial approval authorities remain necessary, current, and appropriately separated.

## Requirements
- Approval authorities must have a named owner and defined business purpose.
- Material changes in role, responsibility, or risk must trigger review.
- Periodic recertification must confirm continued need, scope, and segregation of duties.
- Unnecessary or unclear approval authority must be removed or corrected promptly.

## Evidence
Evidence may include approval matrices, review records, exception decisions, and completed remediation actions. Public documentation must not expose private limits or payment instructions.
