# Refund Exception Aging

## Purpose
Prevent unresolved refund and credit exceptions from becoming permanent control gaps.

## Requirements
- Material exceptions must have an owner, rationale, opened date, and target resolution date.
- Aging must be reviewed at a risk-appropriate cadence.
- Overdue exceptions require escalation, refreshed justification, or closure.
- Repeated exception patterns must be evaluated for process or control improvement.

## Evidence
Retain exception registers, aging reviews, escalation records, and closure evidence. Do not publish customer or transaction details.
