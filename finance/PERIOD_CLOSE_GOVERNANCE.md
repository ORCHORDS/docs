# Financial Period Close Governance

## Purpose

Create a repeatable close process that produces timely, reviewable, and internally consistent financial records.

## Requirements

Material close activities SHOULD define responsibilities, expected reconciliations, cutoff assumptions, review points, significant estimates, unresolved exceptions, and final approval.

Late adjustments and manual journals should remain attributable and supported by evidence.

Recurring close exceptions should trigger process improvement rather than becoming accepted routine.
