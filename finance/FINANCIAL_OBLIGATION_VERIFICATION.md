# Financial Obligation Verification

## Purpose
Ensure material financial obligations are recorded, assigned, reviewed, and completed as intended.

## Requirements
- Material obligations must be traceable to an accountable owner and source record.
- Due dates and completion criteria must be recorded where relevant.
- Verification must distinguish completed, outstanding, disputed, and superseded obligations.
- Exceptions or missed obligations must be escalated according to risk.

## Evidence
Maintain obligation records, verification results, approvals, and corrective actions without exposing confidential amounts or counterparty details.
