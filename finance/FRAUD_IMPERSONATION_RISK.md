# Fraud and Impersonation Risk

## Purpose

Reduce losses caused by impersonation, compromised accounts, social engineering, forged documents, or manipulated payment workflows.

## Warning signs

Higher scrutiny is warranted for unusual urgency, secrecy requests, changed payment details, unexpected gift-card or cryptocurrency requests, new contact channels, inconsistent documentation, bypass of normal approvers, or pressure to ignore verification.

## Response

Personnel should stop or delay a suspicious transaction when safe, verify through independent trusted channels, preserve evidence, and escalate suspected fraud promptly.

## Culture

Good-faith verification of unusual financial requests must not be discouraged merely because it slows a transaction.
