# Financial Reconciliation Governance

## Purpose

Use reconciliations to detect incomplete, duplicated, misclassified, unauthorized, or stale financial records.

## Requirements

Material reconciliations SHOULD define scope, cadence, preparer, reviewer where appropriate, exception thresholds, retained evidence, and escalation for unresolved differences.

Matching totals are not sufficient when material timing differences, unmatched items, or unexplained adjustments remain.

Repeated differences should trigger root-cause analysis rather than permanent manual correction.
